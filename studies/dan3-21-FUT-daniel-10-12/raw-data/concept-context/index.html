    Raw Concept Context Output¶

    Daniel 11:36 — Concepts: FULFILLMENT, KING, TELOS¶

    Same Chapter (Daniel 11) — 27 verses with shared concepts¶

    Key matches: 1. Dan 11:6 [FULFILLMENT, KING, TELOS] — "in the end of years... the king's daughter of the south... the king of the north" 2. Dan 11:13 [KING, TELOS] — "king of the north shall return... after certain years" 3. Dan 11:16 [FULFILLMENT, TELOS] — "shall do according to his own will" (kir'tsono!) 4. Dan 11:17 [FULFILLMENT, KING] — "He shall also set his face" 5. Dan 11:27 [KING, TELOS] — "both these kings... the end shall be at the time appointed" 6. Dan 11:3 [FULFILLMENT, KING] — "do according to his will" (kir'tsono!) 7. Dan 11:4 [KING, TELOS] — "his kingdom shall be broken" 8. Dan 11:40 [KING, TELOS] — "at the time of the end... king of the south... king of the north"

    NOTE: The concept tool confirms the kir'tsono chain — Dan 11:3, 11:16, 11:36 all share FULFILLMENT concept through H6213 (asah, "do") + H7522 (ratson, "will"). Dan 11:40 shares KING + TELOS.

    Same Book (Daniel) — 47 verses¶

    Key: Dan 8:23 [KING, TELOS] — "king of fierce countenance" — connects the willful king to the ch. 8 figure.

    Daniel 12:1 — No theological concepts found by tool¶

    (The tool did not detect standard concepts in this verse, likely because the key terms — Michael, trouble, deliver — are not mapped to standard concept categories.)

    Daniel 12:2 — Concepts: LIFE¶

    Same Chapter (Daniel 12)¶

    1. Dan 12:7 [LIFE] — "swore by him that liveth for ever" — chayyey olam parallel

    Same Book (Daniel)¶

    1. Dan 8:4 [LIFE] — "no beasts might stand before him" — different usage

    Other Books — 1419 verses¶

    Key NT matches: - 1 Cor 15:19 [LIFE] — "If in this life only we have hope in Christ" - 1 Cor 15:45 [LIFE] — "first man Adam... living soul; last Adam... quickening spirit"
