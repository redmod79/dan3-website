    Daniel 7:9 — NT Parallels¶

    Reference Hybrid Sem Key Key Terms
    REV 1:14 0.474 0.51 0.70 burn, hair, head, like
    REV 4:4 0.392 0.55 0.40 dominion, garment, head, white
    HEB 1:11 0.344 0.51 0.30 garment, like, old
    REV 9:17 0.341 0.49 0.30 burn, head, like
    REV 4:5 0.339 0.56 0.20 burn, dominion
    MAT 28:3 0.298 0.40 0.30 like, snow, white
    REV 14:14 0.288 0.40 0.30 head, like, white

    Key Finding: Rev 1:14 is the strongest NT parallel to Dan 7:9 — hair white like wool/snow, combined with fire imagery. This is the Christological merger of Ancient of Days and Son of Man features.
