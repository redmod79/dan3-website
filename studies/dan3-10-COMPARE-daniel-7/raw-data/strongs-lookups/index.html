    Raw Strong's Lookup Data¶

    H1080 — בְּלָא (bela, wear out)¶

    • Transliteration: bᵉlâʼ
    • Pronunciation: bel-aw
    • Part of Speech: verb (v)
    • BLB Count: 1
    • Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to (but used only in a mental sense); to afflict: wear out
    • BDB: Pa. wear away, out (Biblical Hebrew בלה); Imperfect 3ms יְבַּלֵּא Daniel 7:25, figurative for harass continually (accusative of person)
    • Occurrences: 1 total — "shall wear out" (Dan 7:25)

    H8133 — שְׁנָא (shna, change/diverse)¶

    • Transliteration: shᵉnâʼ
    • Pronunciation: shen-aw
    • Part of Speech: verb (v)
    • BLB Count: 21
    • Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to: alter, change, (be) diverse

    Stem Distribution:¶

    Pe'al (intransitive: change, be different): - Dan 3:27 — Perfect 3mp שְׁנוֺ (garment by fire) - Dan 5:6 — suffix שְׁנוֺ֫הִי (brightness of face) - Dan 5:9 — Participle mp שָׁנַ֫יִן (brightness changed) - Dan 6:17 — Imperfect 3fs תִּשְׁנֵא (purpose) - Dan 7:3 — Participle fp שָֽׁנְיָן (diverse one from another) - Dan 7:19 — Participle fs שַׁנְיָא (different from all) - Dan 7:23 — Imperfect 3fs תִשְׁנֵא (diverse from all kingdoms) - Dan 7:24 — Imperfect 3ms יִשְׁנֵא (diverse from the first)

    Pa'el (transitive: change, frustrate): - Dan 3:28 — Perfect 3mp שַׁנִּי (frustrate word of king) - Dan 4:16 — Imperfect 3mp יְשַׁנּוֺן (change heart from man's) - Dan 7:7 — Passive participle fs מְשַׁנְּיָא (different from all)

    Ithpa'el (passive: be changed): - Dan 2:9 — Imperfect 3ms יִשְׁתַּנֵּא (time/conditions changed) - Dan 3:19 — Perfect 3mp אִשְׁתַּנּוֺ (countenance changed) - Dan 5:10 — Imperfect 3mp (brightness changed) - Dan 7:28 — Imperfect 3mp (countenance changed)

    Haph'el (causative: change, alter): - Dan 2:21 — Participle ms מְהַשְׁנֵא (HE [God] changes times and seasons) - Dan 6:8 — Imperfect 3ms (cannot be changed) - Dan 6:15 — Infinitive (cannot be changed) - Dan 7:25 — Infinitive לְהַשְׁנָיָה (think TO CHANGE times and law) - Ezra 6:11 — Imperfect 3ms יְהַשְׁנֵא (whoever alters decree) - Ezra 6:12 — Infinitive לְהַשְׁנָיָה (to alter/destroy)

    Translation Distribution: 26 total occurrences¶

    • be changed (4), was changed (3), changed (3), diverse (3), shall be diverse (2)
    • shall alter (1), to alter (1), changeth (1), to change (1), etc.

    Key Verses by Translation:¶

    • "changeth" = Dan 2:21 (God changes times — Haph'el Participle)
    • "diverse" = Dan 7:3, 7:7, 7:19 (beasts are different — Pe'al/Pa'el)
    • "to change" = Dan 7:25 (think to change — Haph'el Infinitive)
    • "be changed" = Dan 2:9, 4:16, 5:10, 6:17 (Ithpa'el/Pa'el)

    H1882 — דָּת (dat, law/decree)¶

    • Transliteration: dâth
    • Pronunciation: dawth
    • Part of Speech: feminine noun (n-f)
    • BLB Count: 14
    • Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to: decree, law

    BDB Categories:¶

    1. Decree of king: Dan 2:13, 2:15; Ezra 7:26; with suffix Dan 2:9
    2. Unchangeable law of Medes and Persians: Dan 6:9, 6:13, 6:16
    3. Law of God (in mouth of non-Jews): Dan 6:6; Ezra 7:12, 14, 21, 26; absolute Dan 7:25; laws of God Ezra 7:25

    Translation Distribution: 14 total¶

    • "according to the law" (3), "of the law" (2), "the law" (2)
    • "the laws" (1), "and the law" (1), "for you" (1), "And the decree" (1), "the decree" (1), "concerning the law" (1), "and laws" (1)

    Critical Note:¶

    BDB categorizes absolute דָת in Dan 7:25 under category 3 = LAW OF GOD (not decree of king or Medo-Persian law). The emphatic form דָּתָא appears elsewhere for specific decrees.

    H5452 — סְבַר (sbar, think/intend)¶

    • Transliteration: çᵉbar
    • Pronunciation: seb-ar
    • Part of Speech: verb (v)
    • BLB Count: 1 (HAPAX LEGOMENON)
    • Definition: (Aramaic) a primitive root; to bear in mind, i.e. hope: think
    • BDB: think, intend (Syriac cognate); Pe'al Imperfect 3ms יִסְבַּר Daniel 7:25, followed by infinitive
    • Occurrences: 1 — "and think" (Dan 7:25)

    H5732 — עִדָּן (iddan, time)¶

    • Transliteration: ʻiddân
    • Pronunciation: id-dawn
    • Part of Speech: masculine noun (n-m)
    • BLB Count: 13
    • Definition: (Aramaic) from a root corresponding to that of; a set time; technically, a year: time

    BDB Usage:¶

    1. General time (duration): Dan 2:8, 7:12
    2. Specific conditions: Dan 2:9, 2:21
    3. Point of time: Dan 3:5, 3:15
    4. Definite time = year: שִׁבְעָה ע׳ = seven years: Dan 4:13, 4:20, 4:22, 4:29
    5. Dan 7:25: עִדָּן וְעִדָּנִין וּפְלַג עִדָּן = time, times, half time (i.e. 3.5 years)

    Translation Distribution: 15 total¶

    • "times" (4), "the time" (2), "at" (2), "time" (2), "the times" (1), "and time" (1), "a time" (1), "and times" (1), "of time" (1)

    H1780 — דִּין (diyn, judgment, Aramaic)¶

    • Transliteration: dîyn
    • Pronunciation: deen
    • Part of Speech: masculine noun (n-m)
    • BLB Count: 5
    • Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to: judgement
