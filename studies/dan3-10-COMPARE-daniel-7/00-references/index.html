    Reference Gathering: Daniel 7 Three-Way Comparison and Evidence Classification¶

    Question¶

    What does Daniel 7 establish (E/N), and how do the three readings compare?

    Study Plan Context¶

    Plan entry: dan2-10-COMPARE (line 238 of FRESH-DANIEL-STUDY-PLAN-v3.md)

    Integrate list: - dan3-07-HIST-daniel-7 - dan3-08-PRET-daniel-7 - dan3-09-FUT-daniel-7 - dan3-06-COMPARE-daniel-2

    Key adjudications from plan: 1. Nine horn specifications: which identification meets ALL nine? (systematic test) 2. "Change times and law" -- Antiochus fit (PRET strongest argument) vs papal fit (HIST strongest argument) -- comparative analysis 3. Three uprooted horns -- historical evidence for each candidate set 4. Judgment timing -- does the text require pre-advent, final, or Maccabean? 5. Son of Man direction -- TO Ancient of Days (three Aramaic indicators) vs coming to earth 6. "Everlasting kingdom" language -- constrains PRET (no Maccabean fulfillment) 7. H1080 bela (wearing out) -- prolonged attrition vs brief persecution 8. H8133 shna lexical web (diverse/change) -- what does it constrain?

    Prior Studies¶

    From Study Plan (Integrate List)¶

    dan3-07-HIST-daniel-7: - Question: How does historicism read Daniel 7, and what is the textual basis for identifying the little horn as a centuries-long religio-political power? - Four beasts = Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome (sequential ordinal markers: qadmayeta, ochari tinyannah, ba'atar denah, revi'a'ah) - Fourth beast shares iron vocabulary with Dan 2:40 (d'qaq + parzel chain) - Aramaic A/A' chiastic structure (Dan 2-7) pairs the four-kingdom visions as complementary - Nine horn specifications identified with textual classification: - Spec 1 (arises from fourth beast): I-A(2) HIGH - Spec 2 (after ten, three fall): I-A(2) to I-B MED -- which three? Lists vary; papal agency indirect - Spec 3 (diverse from political kings): I-A(1) MED -- shanah = "different," not specifically "religious" - Spec 4 (eyes like a man): I-A(1) MED -- institutional leadership reading - Spec 5 (mouth speaking great things): E (spec) / I-A(1) (match) HIGH/MED - Spec 6 (speaks against Most High): E (spec) / I-A(2) (match) HIGH/MED - Spec 7 (wears out saints): E (spec) / I-A(2) (match) HIGH/MED -- bela Pa'el = prolonged attrition - Spec 8 (change times and law): I-A(1) (spec interpretation) / I-A(2) (Sabbath ID) MED - Spec 9 (3.5 times = 1,260 years): I-A(2) MED -- day-year principle required - Judgment scene: heavenly court BEFORE destruction; thrones "set/placed" (remiv Peil passive); Son of Man moves TOWARD Ancient of Days ('ad, meta, haqrebuhi) - Day of Atonement parallels: five shared elements (white garments, fire, cloud, records examined, exclusion) - Revelation connections: six verbal parallels between Dan 7 and Rev 13; Rev 14:7 krisis = Dan 7:10 dina; creator-worship formula echoes Exo 20:11 - Dan 2:21/7:25 shanah Haphel correspondence: horn usurps divine prerogative of changing times - Singular dat (H1882, emphatic) = "THE law" (BDB category 3: divine law) - Honest weaknesses: three-horn identification varies (Heruli/Vandals/Ostrogoths debated); 538 starting date contested (533, 554 alternatives); ten-kingdom list variability; "diverse" as religious character is inferred from activities, not from shanah alone - Tally: E-tier specs (5,6,7 as descriptions), N-tier (sequential order, bela semantics, Son of Man direction, 'iddan=year), I-A(1) (specs 3,4,8), I-A(2) (specs 1,2,6-match,7-match,9)

    dan3-08-PRET-daniel-7: - Question: How does the preterist school read Daniel 7, and what is the textual basis for identifying the little horn as Antiochus IV? - Four beasts: Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Greek successor states (Schema B forced by Dan 8:20) - Fourth beast = Seleucid dynasty; "devour the whole earth" read as hyperbolic (cf. Dan 2:39) - Ten horns = sequential Seleucid succession; qadmaye (H6933) supports "former" reading in 7:24 - Strongest argument: Dan 2:21/7:25 Haphel shanah parallel -- horn usurps divine prerogative - dat absolute = divine law (BDB); Antiochus targeted Torah specifically (Sabbath, circumcision, dietary laws) - sbar (H5452, hapax) = deliberate intention; formally decreed campaign (1 Macc 1:41-51) - 3.5-year literal time period: Dan 4 'iddan = year precedent; temple desecration to rededication ~3.0 years, ~155 days short of 3.5 years - Cross-vision consistency: Antiochus across Dan 7, 8, 11 (where identification is broadly accepted) - NT reception as constraint: Matt 24:15, 2 Thess 2:3-4, Rev 13:1-7, Rev 12:14 all apply Dan 7 imagery beyond Maccabean era - Specification-Match Table: ALL nine specs classified I-A with chain depth 2-3 - Spec 1: I-A(2) LOW -- fourth beast = Seleucids is itself I-tier; "devour the whole earth" unsatisfied - Spec 4: I-A(3) LOW -- aqar = forcible uprooting; Antiochus used subterfuge; triad varies - Spec 8: I-A(2) MED -- best-grounded textual argument; Haphel parallel + absolute dat - Spec 9: I-A(2) MED -- ~155 days short; NT quotation in non-Maccabean context - Honest weaknesses: (1) triple "everlasting kingdom" with no Maccabean fulfillment (I-B LOW); (2) escalating-scale problem for Seleucid fourth beast; (3) bela Pa'el favors prolonged attrition beyond 3.5 years; (4) aqar vocabulary vs Antiochus's method; (5) NT consistently applies Dan 7 beyond Antiochus; (6) beast slain but Seleucid empire continued 100 years; (7) sbar + lehashnayah implies institutional program beyond temporary prohibitions - Tally: 0 E-tier, 0 N-tier, 9 I-A (chain depth 1-3), 2 I-B, 1 I-C

    dan3-09-FUT-daniel-7: - Question: How does dispensationalist futurism read Daniel 7, and what is the textual basis for a future Antichrist from a revived Roman confederacy? - Four beasts = Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome (shared with HIST at I-A(1) HIGH) - d'qaq vocabulary chain reinforces fourth beast = Rome - Ten horns = future simultaneous ten-nation confederacy; Rev 17:12 oupo ("not yet") is strongest text - Little horn = future individual Antichrist; beast/horn grammatical distinction (7:23-24): beast = kingdom, horn = individual king - "Eyes like eyes of a man" (anasha emphatic): individual person with intelligence - "Son of perdition" chain: John 17:12 (Judas) + 2 Thess 2:3 (man of sin) + Rev 17:8,11 (beast into perdition) - Three-author NT convergence: Jesus (Matt 24:30, 26:64), Paul (2 Thess 2:3-8), John (Rev 1:7, 13:5-7) -- all treat Dan 7 as future - Rev 13:5 verbatim quotation of Dan 7:8 Theodotion -- direct textual dependence - Fiery destruction of Dan 7:11 has no historical parallel for Rome's dissolution (Western Empire dissolved gradually) - Progressive vs. classical dispensationalism: progressive FUT accommodates Matt 28:18 / Acts 2:30-36 with inaugural kingdom - Specification-Match Table: - Specs 1-2: I-A(2) + I-C MED -- depend on gap thesis - Specs 6-7: E (description) / I-A(1) (identification) HIGH - Spec 9: I-A(1) MED -- literal 3.5 years from Dan 4 precedent, but cross-genre transfer issue - Honest weaknesses: (1) gap thesis I-C LOW (no textual marker, tselem chad against it, six NT counter-passages to Israel/Church distinction); (2) no historical ten-kingdom simultaneity; (3) Dan 7:13 direction TOWARD God, not toward earth; (4) Matt 28:18 / Acts 2:30-36 suggest investiture already occurred; (5) 2 Thess 2:7 "already work"; (6) 1 John 2:18 "many antichrists" present; (7) pretribulation rapture = external framework I-C; (8) Rev 13:5 literary continuity argues ongoing relevance, not exclusive future - Tally: 3 E-tier (descriptions), 5 I-A(1), 6 I-A(2), 2 I-A(2)+I-C, 2 I-C

    dan3-06-COMPARE-daniel-2: - Question: What does Daniel 2 establish (E/N), and how do the three readings compare at the inference level? - Established methodology for COMPARE studies: compile perspective study classifications, compare, adjudicate - 12 E-tier statements (all classified ALL -- no position-specific E-items) - 6 N-tier statements (all ALL): Medo-Persia = 2nd, Greece = 3rd, Schema A eliminated, 4th follows Greece, stone divine origin, stone everlasting - Key I-B resolution: stone timing -- inaugurated-but-not-yet-consummated reading supported by Matt 21:44's internal bifurcation; HIST maintains stone action exclusively future - Fourth kingdom I-B resolution: Moderate toward Rome -- sequential logic + NT canonical evidence + iron vocabulary chain scope requirements vs. malkuyot vocabulary support for Greek successors - FUT gap thesis: I-C LOW (no textual marker, tselem chad against it) - Constraining effects documented: E9 eliminates PRET Schema A; E8 ka-chadah constrains PRET; batarakh succession language constrains PRET Schema B - HIST had shallowest inference chain (2 E, 2 N, 3 I-A(1), 1 I-A(2)) - PRET had highest chain depth for distinctive claims (2 E, 0 N, 8 I-tier) - FUT had highest inference burden with I-C framework items - This establishes the precedent: Dan 7 COMPARE should follow same methodology -- carry forward perspective study classifications, do not re-classify

    From Semantic Search (Additional)¶

    Shared Word Study Data¶

    All three perspective studies analyzed the same Aramaic terms. Key agreements and disagreements:

    • bela (H1080): All agree it is a hapax; Pa'el imperfect = intensive ongoing. HIST: prolonged attrition (decades/centuries). PRET: compressed intensive 3.5 years. FUT: compatible with either duration. Cognate balah (H1086) evidence leans toward extended duration.
    • shanah (H8133): All agree on the Pe'al/Haphel stem distinction and the Dan 2:21/7:25 Haphel parallel (horn usurps divine prerogative). This is shared ground across all positions. The debate is WHAT times and law are targeted.
    • dat (H1882): All agree absolute form = divine law per BDB. HIST: Sabbath specifically (via Exo 20:11, Isa 58:12-13, Rev 14:7 chain). PRET: Torah broadly (Antiochus targeted circumcision, Sabbath, dietary laws). FUT: divine law generally (future Antichrist restructures sacred time).
    • sbar (H5452): All agree it means intention/presumption. HIST: intent without actual success (God's law unchanged). PRET: deliberately decreed campaign. FUT: intention not accomplished fact.
    • 'iddan (H5732): All agree Dan 4 establishes 'iddan = year. HIST: applies day-year principle -> 1,260 years. PRET: literal 3.5 years. FUT: literal 3.5 years. The day-year principle is the dividing point.

    Key Adjudication Points Identified¶

    1. Nine-specification systematic test: HIST has shallowest chain depth on most specs; PRET has all specs at I-A(2-3); FUT has I-C dependency on specs 1-2. None have E-tier matches (text names neither the fourth beast nor the horn).

    2. "Change times and law" comparative: Both HIST and PRET ground this on the same Haphel shanah parallel and absolute dat. The difference is the IDENTIFICATION of the target: Sabbath (HIST, via Rev 14:7 chain) vs Torah broadly (PRET, via historical documentation of Antiochus's edicts). The textual data (Haphel parallel, absolute dat) is agreed ground; the identification is the inference.

    3. Three uprooted horns: HIST: Heruli/Vandals/Ostrogoths (debated within historicism, papal agency indirect). PRET: varies among scholars (no consensus triad). FUT: future military conquest (no historical verification yet). All three have weakness here.

    4. Judgment timing: HIST: pre-advent (be-edayin chronology, Son of Man direction, DOA parallels). PRET: eschatological hope / inaugurated at Christ's enthronement (Acts 2:33-36). FUT: final judgment at Second Coming (Rev 20 parallel). The Son of Man direction (TOWARD God) is a textual datum that HIST uses most directly.

    5. Son of Man direction: Three Aramaic indicators ('ad, meta, haqrebuhi) all point TOWARD the Ancient of Days. HIST reads this as heavenly investiture/pre-advent judgment. FUT argues NT reinterprets this as Second Coming (but must account for directional reversal). PRET offers three readings (collective Israel, angel Michael, messianic figure).

    6. "Everlasting kingdom": Triple declaration (Dan 7:14,18,27) with escalating emphasis constrains PRET most severely (no Maccabean fulfillment; Hasmonean state lasted 77 years). HIST and FUT both point to a consummated future kingdom.

    7. bela duration: Cognate evidence and Pa'el morphology favor prolonged attrition (HIST reading). PRET's compressed 3.5-year reading strains the semantic range. FUT's literal 3.5 years faces the same tension but argues intensity compensates for brevity.

    8. shanah lexical web: The Pe'al (intransitive "be different") / Haphel (causative "make change") stem shift in Dan 7:24-25 links the horn's distinctiveness to its law-changing activity. All three positions acknowledge this. The shanah web connects 7:3,7,19,23,24 (diversity descriptions) with 7:25 (active changing) and 2:21 (divine prerogative).
