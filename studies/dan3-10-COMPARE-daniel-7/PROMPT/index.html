    Bible Study: Daniel 7 Three-Way Comparison — Specification-Match Adjudication and Evidence Classification¶

    Question¶

    What does Daniel 7 establish (E/N), and how do the three readings (HIST, PRET, FUT) compare?

    Prior Research Summary¶

    From Perspective Studies (Integrate List)¶

    Three perspective studies have already been completed on Daniel 7:

    dan3-07-HIST-daniel-7: Historicist reading. Four beasts = Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome. Little horn = centuries-long religio-political power (papacy). Nine horn specifications mapped with E/I classifications. Judgment scene = pre-advent heavenly court. Son of Man moves TOWARD Ancient of Days. Day of Atonement parallels (five shared elements). Revelation verbal parallels (six). Shanah Haphel correspondence (Dan 2:21/7:25). Singular dat = divine law. Weaknesses: three-horn identification varies; 538 date contested; ten-kingdom list variability.

    dan3-08-PRET-daniel-7: Preterist reading. Four beasts = Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Greek successors (Schema B). Fourth beast = Seleucid dynasty. Little horn = Antiochus IV. Strongest argument: Haphel shanah parallel + absolute dat (Antiochus targeted Torah). 3.5 literal years (temple desecration ~155 days short). Weaknesses: triple "everlasting kingdom" with no Maccabean fulfillment (I-B LOW); escalating-scale problem; bela favors prolonged attrition; NT consistently applies Dan 7 beyond Antiochus.

    dan3-09-FUT-daniel-7: Futurist reading. Four beasts same as HIST through Rome. Ten horns = future simultaneous ten-nation confederacy. Little horn = future individual Antichrist. Rev 17:12 oupo strongest text. Three-author NT convergence (Jesus, Paul, John treat Dan 7 as future). Weaknesses: gap thesis I-C LOW (no textual marker); Dan 7:13 direction TOWARD God; Matt 28:18 / Acts 2:30-36 suggest investiture already occurred; 2 Thess 2:7 "already work."

    dan3-06-COMPARE-daniel-2: Established COMPARE methodology: compile perspective study classifications, compare, adjudicate. Fourth kingdom I-B resolution moderate toward Rome. FUT gap thesis I-C LOW. HIST had shallowest inference chain.

    From Companion Series Studies¶

    hist-02-daniel-7-beasts-little-horn-judgment: 28+ E-tier items from Dan 7 text; judgment = mechanism Daniel 2 lacked; Rev 4-5 parallels Dan 7:9-14; Rev 13 composite beast absorbs all four Dan 7 beasts.

    revs-41-daniels-beasts-revelation: Verbatim LXX quotation (stoma laloun megala Rev 13:5 = Dan 7:8,20 LXX); reversed animal order; Christological merger of Ancient of Days and Son of Man in Rev 1:13-14.

    EGW and Secrets Unsealed servers were not running during reference gathering. No external corpus claims to verify.
