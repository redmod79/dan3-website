    Raw Hebrew Parsing Output¶

    Dan 8:4¶

    רָאִיתִי אֶת־הָאַיִל מְנַגֵּחַ יָמָּה וְצָפֹונָה וָנֶגְבָּה וְכָל־חַיֹּות לֹא־יַעַמְדוּ לְפָנָיו וְאֵין מַצִּיל מִיָּדֹו וְעָשָׂה כִרְצֹנֹו וְהִגְדִּיל׃

Key words:
- כִרְצֹנֹו (kir'tsono) = "according to his will" — H7522 ratson + 3ms suffix
- וְהִגְדִּיל (wehigdil) = "and became great" — H1431 gadal, Hiphil.Perf.3ms

    Dan 8:8¶

    וּצְפִיר הָעִזִּים הִגְדִּיל עַד־מְאֹד וּכְעָצְמֹו נִשְׁבְּרָה הַקֶּרֶן הַגְּדֹולָה וַתַּעֲלֶנָה חָזוּת אַרְבַּע תַּחְתֶּיהָ לְאַרְבַּע רוּחֹות הַשָּׁמָיִם׃

Key words:
- הִגְדִּיל עַד־מְאֹד (higdil ad-me'od) = "waxed very great" — H1431 gadal, Hiphil.Perf.3ms + unto + might
- חָזוּת (chazut) = "conspicuous/notable" — Noun.fs.Abs — vision/appearance
- אַרְבַּע (arba) = "four"
- רוּחֹות (ruchot) = "winds" — Noun.fp.Cst — feminine plural

    Dan 8:9 (CRITICAL)¶

    וּמִן־הָאַחַת מֵהֶם יָצָא קֶרֶן־אַחַת מִצְּעִירָה וַתִּגְדַּל־יֶתֶר אֶל־הַנֶּגֶב וְאֶל־הַמִּזְרָח וְאֶל־הַצֶּבִי׃

Key words:
- מֵהֶם (mehem) = "from them" — Prep.+3mp — masculine plural suffix
- הָאַחַת (ha-achat) = "the one" — Art + Noun.fs.Abs — feminine singular
- קֶרֶן (qeren) = "horn" — H7161, Noun.fs.Abs — feminine
- אַחַת (achat) = "one" — Noun.fs.Abs — feminine (matching qeren)
- מִצְּעִירָה (mits'eirah) = "from littleness" — H4704, Noun.fs.Abs — HAPAX
- וַתִּגְדַּל (vattigdal) = "and it grew" — H1431 gadal, Qal.Wayq.3fs (NOTE: QAL, not Hiphil)
- יֶתֶר (yether) = "exceedingly/surplus" — H3499, Noun.ms.Abs
- הַנֶּגֶב (ha-negev) = "the south" — south
- הַמִּזְרָח (ha-mizrach) = "the east" — sunrise
- הַצֶּבִי (ha-tsevi) = "the beauty/pleasant [land]" — beauty/glory (= land of Israel)

CRITICAL OBSERVATIONS:
1. mehem is 3mp (masculine plural) — but both candidate antecedents are feminine: qeranot (horns) and ruchot (winds)
2. gadal is QAL here, vs. HIPHIL in 8:4 and 8:8 — stem shift
3. yether is NOT an adverb; it is a noun meaning "surplus, excess, preeminence"
4. mits'eirah is a hapax legomenon — the deliberate avoidance of common qatan (101x in OT)

    Dan 8:10¶

    וַתִּגְדַּל עַד־צְבָא הַשָּׁמָיִם וַתַּפֵּל אַרְצָה מִן־הַצָּבָא וּמִן־הַכֹּוכָבִים וַתִּרְמְסֵם׃

Key words:
- וַתִּגְדַּל (vattigdal) = "and it grew" — H1431 gadal, Qal.Wayq.3fs (QAL continues)
- צְבָא הַשָּׁמָיִם (tseva ha-shamayim) = "host of heaven" — H6635 + heavens
- וַתַּפֵּל (vattappel) = "and it cast down" — H5307 naphal, Hiphil.Wayq.3fs
- הַכֹּוכָבִים (ha-kokavim) = "the stars"
- וַתִּרְמְסֵם (vattirm'sem) = "and trampled them" — H7429 ramas, Qal.Wayq.3fs+3mp

    Dan 8:11 (CRITICAL — compare with 11:31)¶

    וְעַד שַׂר־הַצָּבָא הִגְדִּיל וּמִמֶּנּוּ הוּרַם הַתָּמִיד וְהֻשְׁלַךְ מְכֹון מִקְדָּשֹׁו׃

Key words:
- שַׂר־הַצָּבָא (sar ha-tsava) = "prince of the host" — H8269 + H6635
- הִגְדִּיל (higdil) = "magnified [himself]" — H1431 gadal, Hiphil.Perf.3ms (back to HIPHIL)
- מִמֶּנּוּ (mimmennu) = "from him" — Prep.+3ms
- הוּרַם (huram) = "was taken up/lifted" — H7311 rum, Hophal.Perf.3ms (PASSIVE of Hiphil)
- הַתָּמִיד (ha-tamid) = "the daily/continual" — H8548, Noun.ms.Abs
- מְכֹון (mekhon) = "place/foundation" — site
- מִקְדָּשֹׁו (miqdasho) = "his sanctuary" — H4720 miqdash + 3ms suffix

NOTE: Dan 11:31 uses הֵסִירוּ (hesiru) = Hiphil.Perf.3p of H5493 sur ("turn aside/remove")
Dan 8:11 uses הוּרַם (huram) = Hophal.Perf.3ms of H7311 rum ("lifted up")
The verbs are DIFFERENT — rum in 8:11 vs. sur in 11:31.
Both verses share הַתָּמִיד (ha-tamid).

    Dan 8:12¶

    וְצָבָא תִּנָּתֵן עַל־הַתָּמִיד בְּפָשַׁע וְתַשְׁלֵךְ אֱמֶת אַרְצָה וְעָשְׂתָה וְהִצְלִיחָה׃

Key words:
- צָבָא (tsava) = "host/army" — H6635
- תִּנָּתֵן (tinnatein) = "shall be given" — H5414 natan, Niphal.Impf.3fs (passive)
- הַתָּמִיד (ha-tamid) = "the daily" — H8548
- בְּפָשַׁע (be-fesha) = "by reason of transgression" — H6588 pesha
- אֱמֶת (emet) = "truth" — trustworthiness
- וְהִצְלִיחָה (vehitslichah) = "and prospered" — H6743 tsalach, Hiphil.Perf.3fs

    Dan 8:13¶

- וְהֵסִירוּ (ve-hesiru) = "and they shall take away" — H5493 sur, Hiphil.Perf.3p
  NOTE: This is Hiphil of SUR (remove), NOT Hophal of RUM as in 8:11
- הַתָּמִיד (ha-tamid) = "the daily" — H8548 (SAME as 8:11)
- הַשִּׁקּוּץ (ha-shiqquts) = "the abomination" — H8251
- מְשֹׁומֵם (meshomem) = "making desolate" — H8074 shamem, Piel.Ptcp.ms.Abs (Polel)

COMPARISON Dan 8:11 vs Dan 11:31:
Both share: ha-tamid (the daily), miqdash (sanctuary)
Differences:
- 8:11: huram (Hophal rum) = "was taken up" — PASSIVE
- 11:31: hesiru (Hiphil sur) = "they removed" — ACTIVE, different root entirely
- 8:11: mekhon miqdash ("place of sanctuary")
- 11:31: ha-miqdash ha-ma'oz ("sanctuary of fortress/strength")
- 8:13 uses pesha shomem; 11:31 uses shiqquts meshomem
