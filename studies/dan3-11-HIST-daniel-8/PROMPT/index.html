    Bible Study: Daniel 8 — The Historicist Reading¶

    Question¶

    How does historicism read Daniel 8, and what is the textual and grammatical basis for identifying the little horn as Rome?

    Prior Research Summary¶

    From Prior Studies (16+ studies)¶

    • hist-04 established: mehem (3mp suffix) disagrees with both feminine antecedents; GKC Section 145 documents constructio ad sensum; three-stage gadal progression with yether requiring horn to surpass both previous empires; verb stem shift Hiphil->Qal; directional indicators prove territorial expansion; hapax mits'eirah emphasizes rise from insignificance; az paniym/Deut 28:50 covenant-curse framework; Antiochus fails on yether, permanence, "time of the end," "Prince of princes," "broken without hand," 2300-day math
    • hist-06 established: nitsdaq = Niphal Perfect 3ms of tsadaq (H6663) — forensic/legal verb; Daniel deliberately chose tsadaq over taher/kaphar; LXX OG dikaiothesatai ("shall be justified") vs Theodotion katharisthesetai ("cleansed"); evening-morning = creation pattern (Gen 1), not DOA formula; Dan 7:9-10 = judicial PROCESS, Dan 8:14 = VERDICT; Rev 14:7 combines judgment + creation themes; kaphar -> tsadaq progression
    • rome-daniel-8-little-horn established: 24 specifications matched to Rome (23 STRONG, 1 MODERATE-STRONG, 0 failures); two phases under one symbol (pagan->papal); Dan 8:13 dual structure (ha-tamid VE ha-pesha' shomem); be'efes yad echoes Dan 2:34,45
    • sanc-25 established: four dimensions of vindication (people, character, plan, law); tsedeq/emeth -> dikaios/alethinos chain; Revelation's vindication quartet; Isa 53:11 -> Dan 8:14 -> Dan 12:3 tsadaq chain
    • dan3-03 (HIST Daniel 2) established: four-kingdom identification with Rome as I-A(1); stone "without hands" = Dan 8:25 be'efes yad; iron vocabulary links Dan 2:40 to Dan 7:7
    • dan3-07 (HIST Daniel 7) established: Dan 7/Dan 8 two-symbol vs one-symbol distinction; three-phase judgment structure; bela/shanah/dat vocabulary

    Grammar Studies¶

    • mehem grammar (v1, v2): Five grammar-textbook-verified findings; Reading D (constructio ad sensum for compass directions) rated "STRONGLY PERMITTED"; mixed-gender construction strongest syntactic indicator
    • gadal progression: Three-stage escalation; verb stem shift Hiphil->Qal; Antiochus ~3M km^2 vs Persia ~5.5-8M vs Alexander ~5.2M — only Rome qualifies
    • nitsdaq studies: Forensic Q&A structure; Dan 9:24 vocabulary bridge; chathak hapax; biyn chain across Dan 8-10
    • erev-boqer studies: Dan 8:14 grammatically UNIQUE; DOA has NO morning; Dan 8:26 back-references with definite articles; DOA connection via sanctuary context, not phrase

    External Corpus Leads¶

    • EGW/pioneers: Dan 8 little horn = Rome in both pagan and papal phases as ONE continuous symbol; 2300 days = 2300 years commencing 457 BC; sanctuary to be vindicated is heavenly; 490 years cut from 2300
    • Bohr: Dan 7 and Dan 8 little horns = same power; pagan-papal succession pattern consistent across all four vision cycles; resolution of "seeming discrepancy" through mehem grammar

    • DB argument: Dan 8:26 uses BOTH words in one sentence with different referents: "the mar'eh (H4758) of the evening and morning which was told is true: wherefore shut up the chazon (H2377)." Mar'eh = the 2300 evening-morning time prophecy (declared true). Chazon = the broader symbolic vision (sealed). The sealing command (shatam ha-chazon, "shut up the vision; for it shall be for many days") implies the 2300 period extends far beyond Daniel's lifetime — evidence for day-year. Gabriel in 9:23 returns to explain the mar'eh, not the chazon.

    20. Rev 13:6 Threefold Blasphemy Parallel to Dan 8:10-12¶

This three-to-three correspondence confirms Revelation's beast enacts the same attacks as Daniel's little horn.

    21. Psalm 119:142 Verbal Bridge (Tsedeq + Emeth)¶

The tsedeq + emeth pair maps to dikaios + alethinos in Rev 15:3, 16:7, 19:2.

    22. Dan 11:36 Self-Exaltation Vocabulary Link to Dan 8:25 and 2 Thess 2:4¶
