    Raw Hebrew Parsing Data¶

    Daniel 8:4 (Ram's gadal — Hiphil)¶

    רָאִ֣יתִי אֶת־הָאַ֡יִל מְנַגֵּחַ֩ יָ֨מָּה וְצָפֹ֜ונָה וָנֶ֗גְבָּה וְכָל־חַיֹּות֙ לֹֽא־יַֽעַמְד֣וּ לְפָנָ֔יו וְאֵ֥ין מַצִּ֖יל מִיָּדֹ֑ו וְעָשָׂ֥ה כִרְצֹנֹ֖ו וְהִגְדִּֽיל׃

Key: וְהִגְדִּֽיל = gadal, Verb.Hiphil.Perf.3ms ("be strong/great")
- Ram pushes westward/northward/southward
- Directional terms: ים (sea/west), צפון (north), נגב (south)
- Final verb: Hiphil Perfect 3ms of gadal — causative "became great"

    Daniel 8:8 (Goat's gadal — Hiphil + ad-me'od)¶

    וּצְפִ֥יר הָעִזִּ֖ים הִגְדִּ֣יל עַד־מְאֹ֑ד...

Key: הִגְדִּ֣יל עַד־מְאֹ֑ד = gadal Hiphil.Perf.3ms + ad-me'od ("exceedingly")
- Same Hiphil stem as 8:4 but modified with עד מאד
- Horn broken: נִשְׁבְּרָה֙ = Niphal.Perf.3fs of שׁבר (break)
- Four horns rise: תַּעֲלֶ֜נָה = Qal.Wayq.3fp of עלה (ascend)
- רוּחֹ֥ות = winds (feminine plural)

    Daniel 8:9 (Horn's gadal — Qal + yether; critical verse)¶

    וּמִן־הָאַחַ֣ת מֵהֶ֔ם יָצָ֥א קֶֽרֶן־אַחַ֖ת מִצְּעִירָ֑ה וַתִּגְדַּל־יֶ֛תֶר אֶל־הַנֶּ֥גֶב וְאֶל־הַמִּזְרָ֖ח וְאֶל־הַצֶּֽבִי׃

Key parsing:
- מֵהֶ֔ם = Prep.+3mp ("from them") — masculine plural suffix, both antecedents feminine
- יָצָ֥א = Qal.Perf.3ms of יצא ("go out") — masculine singular verb
- קֶֽרֶן = horn (feminine noun)
- מִצְּעִירָ֑ה = Noun.fs.Abs of צעירה (H4704, "littleness/youth") — HAPAX
- וַתִּגְדַּל = Qal.Wayq.3fs of גדל — NOTE: Qal (not Hiphil like 8:4, 8:8)
- יֶ֛תֶר = Noun.ms.Abs of יתר (H3499, "remainder/excess/surplus")
- Directional terms: נֶּ֥גֶב (south, ms), מִּזְרָ֖ח (sunrise/east, ms), צֶּֽבִי (beauty/glorious land, ms)
- All three directional terms are MASCULINE — constructio ad sensum
- Verb stem shift: Hiphil (8:4, 8:8) → Qal (8:9) — from causative to organic

    Daniel 8:10 (wattigdal — casting down host)¶

    וַתִּגְדַּ֖ל עַד־צְבָ֣א הַשָּׁמָ֑יִם וַתַּפֵּ֥ל אַ֛רְצָה מִן־הַצָּבָ֥א וּמִן־הַכֹּוכָבִ֖ים וַֽתִּרְמְסֵֽם׃

Key:
- וַתִּגְדַּ֖ל = Qal.Wayq.3fs of גדל (continues Qal from 8:9)
- צְבָ֣א הַשָּׁמָ֑יִם = "host of the heavens" (ms.Cst + mp.Abs)
- וַתַּפֵּ֥ל = Hiphil.Wayq.3fs of נפל ("cause to fall")
- כֹּוכָבִ֖ים = stars (mp)
- וַֽתִּרְמְסֵֽם = Qal.Wayq.3fs.+3mp of רמס ("trample them")

    Daniel 8:11 (higgdil — magnifying against Prince)¶

    וְעַ֥ד שַֽׂר־הַצָּבָ֖א הִגְדִּ֑יל וּמִמֶּ֨נּוּ֙ הוּרַ֣ם הַתָּמִ֔יד וְהֻשְׁלַ֖ךְ מְכֹ֥ון מִקְדָּשֹֽׁו׃

Key:
- שַֽׂר הַצָּבָ֖א = "prince of the host" (Noun.ms.Cst + Art + Noun.ms.Abs)
- הִגְדִּ֑יל = Hiphil.Perf.3ms of גדל — BACK TO HIPHIL (causative magnification)
- הוּרַ֣ם = Hophal.Perf.3ms of רום ("was taken away" — passive)
- הַתָּמִ֔יד = "the continual" (Art + Noun.ms.Abs) — substantive use with article
- הֻשְׁלַ֖ךְ = Hophal.Perf.3ms of שׁלך ("was cast down" — passive)
- מְכֹ֥ון מִקְדָּשֹֽׁו = "site of his sanctuary" (ms.Cst + ms.Abs.+3ms)

    Daniel 8:12 (pesha, emeth, tamid)¶

    וְצָבָ֛א תִּנָּתֵ֥ן עַל־הַתָּמִ֖יד בְּפָ֑שַׁע וְתַשְׁלֵ֤ךְ אֱמֶת֙ אַ֔רְצָה וְעָשְׂתָ֖ה וְהִצְלִֽיחָה׃

Key:
- תִּנָּתֵ֥ן = Niphal.Impf.3fs of נתן ("was given")
- הַתָּמִ֖יד = "the continual" (again, substantive with article)
- בְּפָ֑שַׁע = "in rebellion/transgression" (Prep + Noun.ms.Abs of פשׁע, H6588)
- אֱמֶת֙ = "truth/trustworthiness" (Noun.fs.Abs, H571)
- וְעָשְׂתָ֖ה = Qal.Perf.3fs of עשׂה ("and it practiced")
- וְהִצְלִֽיחָה = Hiphil.Perf.3fs of צלח ("and prospered")

    Daniel 8:13 (ha-tamid, ha-pesha', shomem — dual structure)¶

    ... עַד־מָתַ֞י הֶחָזֹ֤ון הַתָּמִיד֙ וְהַפֶּ֣שַׁע שֹׁמֵ֔ם תֵּ֛ת וְקֹ֥דֶשׁ וְצָבָ֖א מִרְמָֽס׃

Key DUAL STRUCTURE:
- הַתָּמִיד֙ = "THE continual" (Art + Noun.ms.Abs, H8548)
- וְ = "AND" (conjunction)
- הַפֶּ֣שַׁע = "THE transgression" (Art + Noun.ms.Abs, H6588)
- שֹׁמֵ֔ם = "desolating" (Qal.Ptcp.ms.Abs of שׁמם, H8074)
- TWO definite-article nouns connected by conjunction: ha-tamid VE ha-pesha'
- Both function as substantives with the article
- שֹׁמֵ֔ם modifies the second: "the transgression of desolation"
- תֵּ֛ת = Qal.InfCon of נתן ("to give/make")
- קֹ֥דֶשׁ = "holiness/sanctuary" (ms.Abs)
- מִרְמָֽס = "trampled land/trampling" (ms.Abs)

    Daniel 8:14 (erev-boqer, nitsdaq, qodesh)¶

    וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֵלַ֔י עַ֚ד עֶ֣רֶב בֹּ֔קֶר אַלְפַּ֖יִם וּשְׁלֹ֣שׁ מֵאֹ֑ות וְנִצְדַּ֖ק קֹֽדֶשׁ׃

Key:
- עֶ֣רֶב = "evening" (Noun.ms.Abs, H6153)
- בֹּ֔קֶר = "morning" (Noun.ms.Abs, H1242)
- NO conjunction between erev and boqer — they form a compound unit
- אַלְפַּ֖יִם = "two thousand" (Noun.d.Abs — dual form)
- שְׁלֹ֣שׁ מֵאֹ֑ות = "three hundred"
- וְנִצְדַּ֖ק = Niphal.Perf.3ms of צדק (H6663) — "shall be vindicated/justified"
  - ONLY Niphal of tsadaq in entire OT
  - Forensic/legal verb: NOT cleansing (taher) or atonement (kaphar)
- קֹֽדֶשׁ = "holiness/sanctuary" (ms.Abs, H6944)

    Daniel 8:17 (eth qets)¶

    Daniel 8:19 (acharit ha-za'am)¶

    Daniel 8:23 (az paniym, chiydah, biyn)¶

    וּֽבְאַחֲרִית֙ מַלְכוּתָ֔ם כְּהָתֵ֖ם הַפֹּשְׁעִ֑ים יַעֲמֹ֛ד מֶ֥לֶךְ עַז־פָּנִ֖ים וּמֵבִ֥ין חִידֹֽות׃

Key:
- מַלְכוּתָ֔ם = "their kingdom" (Noun.fs.Abs.+3mp) — FEMININE noun + MASCULINE suffix (malkutam)
  - Validates constructio ad sensum: Gabriel himself uses masc suffix on fem noun
- כְּהָתֵ֖ם = "when they are complete" (Prep + Hiphil.InfCon of תמם — "be complete")
- הַפֹּשְׁעִ֑ים = "the rebels/transgressors" (Art + Qal.Ptcp.mp.Abs of פשׁע)
  - Same root as pesha (H6588) in 8:12,13
  - IDENTICAL participle form to Isa 53:12 פֹּשְׁעִ֖ים
- עַז פָּנִ֖ים = "fierce of face/countenance" (Adj.ms.Cst + Noun.mp.Abs)
  - H5794 (az) + H6440 (paniym) — construct chain
  - SAME phrase in Deut 28:50: גֹּ֖וי עַ֣ז פָּנִ֑ים
- וּמֵבִ֥ין = Hiphil.Ptcp.ms.Abs of בין (H995) — "understanding"
- חִידֹֽות = "riddles/dark sentences" (Noun.fp.Abs of חידה, H2420)

    Daniel 8:24 (not by his own power)¶

    וְעָצַ֤ם כֹּחֹו֙ וְלֹ֣א בְכֹחֹ֔ו וְנִפְלָאֹ֥ות יַשְׁחִ֖ית...

Key:
- וְעָצַ֤ם כֹּחֹו֙ = "and his power shall be mighty" (Qal.Perf.3ms of עצם + ms.Abs.+3ms of כח)
- וְלֹ֣א בְכֹחֹ֔ו = "but NOT by his own power" (Neg + Prep + ms.Abs.+3ms of כח)
- נִפְלָאֹ֥ות = "wonderfully" (Niphal.Ptcp.fp.Abs of פלא)
- יַשְׁחִ֖ית = Hiphil.Impf.3ms of שׁחת ("destroy")
- עֲצוּמִ֖ים = "mighty ones" (Adj.mp.Abs)
- עַם קְדֹשִֽׁים = "holy people" (Noun.ms.Cst + Adj.mp.Abs)

    Daniel 8:25 (be'efes yad, bilbav, mirmah, sar sarim)¶

    וְעַל־שִׂכְלֹ֗ו וְהִצְלִ֤יחַ מִרְמָה֙ בְּיָדֹ֔ו וּבִלְבָבֹ֣ו יַגְדִּ֔יל וּבְשַׁלְוָ֖ה יַשְׁחִ֣ית רַבִּ֑ים וְעַ֤ל־שַׂר־שָׂרִים֙ יַעֲמֹ֔ד וּבְאֶ֥פֶס יָ֖ד יִשָּׁבֵֽר׃

Key:
- שִׂכְלֹ֗ו = "his insight/policy" (ms.Abs.+3ms of שׂכל)
- מִרְמָה֙ = "deceit/craft" (Noun.fs.Abs, H4820)
- וּבִלְבָבֹ֣ו יַגְדִּ֔יל = "in his heart he shall magnify" — Hiphil.Impf.3ms of gadal
  - NOTE: Hiphil+bilbav = personal arrogance (vs Qal in 8:9 = territorial)
- שַׁלְוָ֖ה = "ease/peace" (Noun.fs.Abs)
- שַׂר שָׂרִים֙ = "Prince of princes" (Noun.ms.Cst + Noun.mp.Abs, H8269)
  - Hebrew superlative construction (cf. qodesh qodashim, "Holy of Holies")
- בְאֶ֥פֶס יָ֖ד = "without hand" (Prep + Noun.ms.Cst of אפס + Noun.s.Abs of יד)
  - Parallels Dan 2:34,45 Aramaic di-la bidayin ("without hands")
- יִשָּׁבֵֽר = Niphal.Impf.3ms of שׁבר ("shall be broken" — passive, divine action)

    Daniel 8:26 (mar'eh vs chazon distinction)¶

    וּמַרְאֵ֨ה הָעֶ֧רֶב וְהַבֹּ֛קֶר אֲשֶׁ֥ר נֶאֱמַ֖ר אֱמֶ֣ת ה֑וּא וְאַתָּה֙ סְתֹ֣ם הֶֽחָזֹ֔ון כִּ֖י לְיָמִ֥ים רַבִּֽים׃

Key:
- מַרְאֵ֨ה = "sight/vision" (Noun.ms.Cst of מראה, H4758) — refers to 2300 evening-morning
- הָעֶ֧רֶב וְהַבֹּ֛קֶר = "THE evening AND THE morning" (with definite articles — back-reference to 8:14)
- אֱמֶ֣ת = "truth/trustworthiness" (Noun.fs.Abs) — declared TRUE
- סְתֹ֣ם = Qal.Impv.2ms of סתם ("shut up/seal")
- הֶֽחָזֹ֔ון = "THE vision" (Art + Noun.ms.Abs of חזון, H2377) — broader symbolic vision
- DISTINCTION: mar'eh (the 2300 time prophecy) = TRUE; chazon (broader vision) = SEALED

    Daniel 8:27 (three collapse verbs + shamam connection)¶

    וַאֲנִ֣י דָנִיֵּ֗אל נִהְיֵ֤יתִי וְנֶֽחֱלֵ֨יתִי֙ יָמִ֔ים... וָאֶשְׁתֹּומֵ֥ם עַל־הַמַּרְאֶ֖ה וְאֵ֥ין מֵבִֽין׃

Key three collapse verbs:
1. נִהְיֵ֤יתִי = Niphal.Perf.1s of היה ("I was undone/exhausted")
2. וְנֶֽחֱלֵ֨יתִי֙ = Niphal.Perf.1s of חלה ("I was sick/weak")
3. וָאֶשְׁתֹּומֵ֥ם = Hithpael.Wayq.1s of שׁמם (H8074, "I was appalled/desolated")
   - SAME ROOT as שֹׁמֵ֔ם (shomem) in 8:13 — sanctuary desolation
   - Daniel's personal desolation mirrors the sanctuary's desolation linguistically
- הַמַּרְאֶ֖ה = "the vision" (H4758 mar'eh — NOT chazon)
- מֵבִֽין = Hiphil.Ptcp.ms.Abs of בין (H995) — "understanding" (none understood)

    Daniel 11:36 (gadal Hithpael)¶
