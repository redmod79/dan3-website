    Raw Strong's Lookups¶

    H4437 — malkuw (kingdom, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 57. Translations: kingdom (44×), kingly, reign. All Daniel Aramaic sections.

    H4438 — malkuwth (kingdom, Hebrew)¶

    BLB Count: 91. Translations: kingdom (51×), royal (13×), reign (6×). Daniel Hebrew sections (8-12).

    H6523 — parzel (iron, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 20. Translations: iron (11×), the iron (4×), of iron (3×). All occurrences in Daniel: 2:33,34,35,40,41,42,43,45; 4:12,20; 5:4,23; 7:7,19.

    H1855 — d'qaq (break in pieces, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 10. Pe'al Perf 3mp: daquw (Dan 2:35). Haph'el: haddeqet (Dan 2:34,45), haddiquw (Dan 6:24), taddiq (Dan 2:40,44), mehaddéq (Dan 2:40), maddaqah (Dan 7:7,19), taddiqinnah (Dan 7:23).

    H8133 — shanah (change, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 21. Translations: changed (3×: Dan 3:27; 6:8; 7:28), diverse (3×: Dan 7:3,7,19). Full range: Dan 2:9,21; 3:19,27,28; 4:16; 5:6,9,10; 6:8,15,17; 7:3,7,19,23,24,25,28. CRITICAL: Haphel shanah in both Dan 2:21 and 7:25.

    H1882 — dat (law, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 14. Dan 2:9,13,15; 6:5,8,12,15; 7:25. Also Ezra 7:12,14,21,25,26.

    H5732 — 'iddan (time/year, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 13. Dan 2:8,9,21; 3:5,15; 4:16,23,25,32; 7:12,25(×2).

    H1080 — bela (wear out, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 1. HAPAX. Only Dan 7:25. Pa'el stem.

    H6132 — 'aqar (pluck up, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 1. Only Dan 7:8. Hithpe'el: "were plucked up by the roots."

    H7162 — qeren (horn, Aramaic)¶

    BLB Count: 14. Musical instrument: Dan 3:5,7,10,15 (cornet). Symbolic: Dan 7:7,8(×3),11,20(×2),21,24.

    H4704 — mits'eirah (littleness)¶

    BLB Count: 1. HAPAX. Only Dan 8:9 "a little [horn]."

    H1431 — gadal (be great)¶

    BLB Count: 117. Dan 8:4,8,9,10,11,25. Three-stage scale: gadal (8:4) → gadal me'od (8:8) → gadal yether (8:9).

    H3499 — yether (exceeding/remainder)¶

    BLB Count: 107. Dan 8:9 "exceeding great" — creates the scale problem for PRET.

    H8548 — tamid (continual/daily)¶

    BLB Count: 104. Translations: continually (53×), continual (9×), the daily (7×: Num 4:16; 29:6; Dan 8:11,12,13; 11:31; 12:11), always (6×), perpetual (2×). Pentateuchal tamid: Exo 29:38,42; Num 28:3,6,23.

    H7311 — rum (exalt)¶

    BLB Count: 194. Dan 8:11(×3), 8:13, 11:12, 11:36, 12:7. Dan 8:11 Hophal: "was taken away."

    H6588 — pesha (transgression)¶

    BLB Count: 93. Dan 8:12,13; 9:24. "Revolt, rebellion" — willful transgression.

    H5975 — 'amad (stand)¶

    BLB Count: 521. Daniel occurrences: 1:4; 8:18,22,23,25; 10:1,11,13,17,21; 11:1,2,3,4,7,11,13,14,17,20,21; 12:1,13.

    H4150 — mo'ed (appointed time)¶

    BLB Count: 223. Translations: "of the congregation" (146×), feasts, appointed time. Dan 8:19; 11:27,29,35; 12:7(×2).

    H2677 — chatsi (half)¶

    BLB Count: 125. Dan 9:27; 12:7.

    H6944 — qodesh (holy/sanctuary)¶

    BLB Count: 468. Dan 8:13; 9:16,20,24(×2); 11:28,30(×2),45; 12:7.

    H4720 — miqdash (sanctuary)¶

    BLB Count: 74. Dan 8:11 only Daniel occurrence. Also Amos 7:9,13.

    H6153 — 'erev (evening)¶

    BLB Count: 137. Dan 8:14,26; 9:21.

    H1242 — boqer (morning)¶

    BLB Count: 205. Dan 8:14,26(×2).

    H3607 — kala (finish/restrain)¶

    BLB Count: 18. Dan 9:24 "to finish the transgression."

    H2856 — chatham (seal)¶

    BLB Count: 27. Dan 9:24(×2), 12:4, 12:9.

    H4886 — mashach (anoint)¶

    BLB Count: 69. Dan 9:24.

    H4899 — mashiach (anointed one)¶
