    Reference Gathering: Steel-Man Compilation of the Complete Preterist Case Across All of Daniel¶

    Question¶

    What is the complete, strongest text-based case for the preterist interpretation across all of Daniel?

    Study Plan Context¶

    • Study ID: dan3-28-PRET-steelman (mapped from dan2-28-PRET in plan)
    • Type: Steel-man compilation — compile ALL PRET items (including CRIT variant), present as one coherent framework, honest weaknesses addressed and ranked by severity
    • Prior PRET studies to compile: dan3-04, dan3-08, dan3-12, dan3-16, dan3-20
    • Prior COMPARE studies for E/N/I classifications: dan3-06, dan3-10, dan3-14, dan3-18, dan3-22

    Prior PRET Perspective Studies¶

    dan3-04-PRET-daniel-2 — Preterist Reading of Daniel 2¶

    Question: How does the preterist school read Daniel 2, and what is the textual basis for alternative kingdom identifications?

    Schema A vs. Schema B: - Schema A (Babylon-Media-Persia-Greece) is ELIMINATED by Dan 8:20 (E-tier angel-interpreter identifies Media+Persia as one entity). Seven biblical texts confirm Medo-Persian unity (Dan 5:28; 6:8,12,15; 8:20; 9:1; Esth 1:19). Classification: I-D. - Schema B (Babylon-Medo-Persia-Greece-Greek successors) survives. Fourth kingdom = Seleucid/Ptolemaic/Antigonid/Lysimachid successor states. Textual basis: Dan 8:22 uses malkuyot ("kingdoms," H4438) for four Greek successors — same word family as malkuw (H4437) used throughout Dan 2.

    Key Arguments: 1. Iron-clay intermarriage (Dan 2:43): arab (H6151, hitpaal reflexive "mingle themselves") + dabaq ("cleave") with marriage connotations (Gen 2:24). Thematic match to Dan 11:6 (Berenice/Antiochus II) and 11:17 (Cleopatra I/Ptolemy V) — failed dynastic marriages. 2. Stone = Christ's inaugurated kingdom: likmao (G3039) link — only 2 NT occurrences (Matt 21:44; Luke 20:18), confirmed LXX presence in Dan 2:44. NT stone-Christ chain: Psa 118:22 quotations (Matt 21:42; Mark 12:10; Luke 20:17; Acts 4:11; 1 Pet 2:7), Isa 28:16 (1 Pet 2:6; Eph 2:20), Isa 8:14 (Rom 9:33; 1 Pet 2:8). Inaugurated-kingdom texts: Matt 12:28 (phthano aorist "HAS COME"), Col 1:13, Heb 12:28, Rom 14:17. 3. "Without hands" chain: Dan 2:34,45 (di-la bi-yedayin). NT develops via acheiropoietos (G886): Mark 14:58, 2 Cor 5:1, Col 2:11. 4. raz-mysterion-apokalypsis chain: Dan 2:18-19,28 (raz H7328) → LXX mysterion (G3466) → Paul's "mystery" (Eph 3:3-9, Col 1:26, Rom 16:25) → Rev 1:1 apokalypsis with dei genesthai formula. 5. Iron as quality descriptor: OT iron symbolism: Egypt (Deut 4:20; 1 Ki 8:51), Babylon (Jer 28:13-14), messianic rule (Ps 2:9; Rev 2:27; 12:5; 19:15), Israel's stubbornness (Isa 48:4). 6. kol-ar'a hyperbole: Dan 2:38, 2:39, 6:25 use "all the earth" for entities that did not literally rule globally. 7. "Great Sea" Mediterranean scope (Dan 7:2): yamma raba = Mediterranean in Num 34:6-7, Josh 1:4, 9:1, 23:4, Ezek 47:10,15,19,20. 8. Cross-vision consistency: Antiochus IV as climactic oppressor in every vision cycle.

    Specification-Match Tally: 2 E, 0 N, 3 I-A(1), 4 I-A(2), 1 I-A(3), 1 I-D. Zero N-tier items — distinctive PRET claims do not unavoidably follow from explicit statements.

    Honest Weaknesses (ranked by severity in the study): 1. ka-chadah simultaneous destruction (Dan 2:35): All five metals broken "together/at once." At first advent, Babylon/Persia/Greece no longer existed. PRET defense: image = unified system; simultaneity is dream-imagery not historical prediction. 2. gadal/yether scale problem (Dan 8:4,8,9): Three-stage progression requires horn to exceed both empires. Antiochus ruled one fragment. 3. batarakh succession language (Dan 2:39): "After you" implies categorically new world power. Greek successors were fragments of Greece. 4. Iron vocabulary chain constraint: parzel + d'qaq describes fourth kingdom as exceeding third in crushing power. 5. "Break in pieces and consume" language (Dan 2:44): Catastrophic, visible overthrow — first advent did not produce this. 6. Everlasting-kingdom test: le-almin/le-almayya eliminates Maccabean-revolt stone identification. Only inaugurated-kingdom reading survives. 7. Matt 24:15: Jesus cites Daniel's abomination as future (~200 years after Antiochus).

    Word Studies: d'qaq/parzel chain (exclusive to Daniel), arab hitpaal (5 occurrences all in Dan 2:41,43), likmao (2 NT occurrences), batarakh (succession strain), alam emphatic, malkuw/malkuwth cognate vocabulary.

    dan3-08-PRET-daniel-7 — Preterist Reading of Daniel 7¶

    Question: How does the preterist school read Daniel 7, and what is the textual basis for identifying the little horn as Antiochus IV?

    Four-Kingdom Schema (Schema B): Lion=Babylon, Bear=Medo-Persia, Leopard=Greece, Fourth beast=Seleucid dynasty/Diadochi system. Iron vocabulary chain links Dan 7:7 to Dan 2:40. "Diverse" (meshanneyah, Pa'el intensive of shanah H8133) = Hellenistic cultural imperialism as categorically different.

    Key Arguments: 1. Haphel shanah parallel (Dan 2:21 / 7:25): Identical Haphel causative stem of shanah. Dan 2:21 = God changes times/seasons. Dan 7:25 = horn "thinks to change" times and law. Horn usurps divine prerogative. Textually verifiable — one of the strongest PRET arguments. 2. dat absolute form (Dan 7:25): BDB classifies absolute dat as "law of God (in mouth of non-Jews)" at Dan 6:6, Ezra 7:12,14,21,26, Dan 7:25. = divine law (Torah), not civil legislation. Antiochus targeted Torah specifically: circumcision, Sabbath, dietary laws, Torah scrolls. 3. sbar (H5452) — deliberate intent: "Shall think/intend to change." Premeditated policy matching Antiochus's formal edicts (1 Macc 1:41-51). 4. Ten horns as sequential Seleucid succession: qadmaye (H6933) = "former" (as in Dan 7:4). yequmun (Pe'al Impf from qum) = sequential emergence. 5. "Diverse from the first" (Dan 7:24): Antiochus not legitimate heir (hostage in Rome, usurped throne). Self-deifying title "Theos Epiphanes." Unprecedented religious persecution. 6. "Eyes like eyes of man" (Dan 7:8): Personal descriptor — individual king with human intelligence, not impersonal institution. 7. 3.5 literal years: 'iddan (H5732) = year in Dan 4:16 (Nebuchadnezzar's madness). PRET rejects day-year principle as universal rule. Temple desecration ~167 BC to rededication ~164 BC ≈ 3.0 years (~155 days short of 3.5 years). 8. Dan 7:12 prolonged lives: First three empires' cultural continuation during Seleucid period. 9. Cross-vision consistency with Dan 8, 11 (Antiochus identification broadly accepted there).

    Son of Man: Three PRET interpretations — (a) collective Israel/saints (Dan 7:18,22,27), (b) Angel Michael (Collins), (c) individual messianic figure. NT data consistently supports messianic reading (Mark 14:62; Matt 26:64).

    Specification-Match Tally: 0 E, 0 N, 9 I-A (chain depths 2-3), 2 I-B, 1 I-C. Confidence: 0 HIGH, 5 MED, 4 LOW.

    Honest Weaknesses: 1. Triple "everlasting kingdom" (Dan 7:14,18,27): No Maccabean fulfillment. Hasmonean state = ~77 years. Classified I-B. 2. aqar (H6132) — forcible uprooting: Antiochus used subterfuge, not forcible military overthrow. Three uprooted kings vary among scholars. 3. bela Pa'el semantic range: Hapax. Pa'el intensive + imperfect = "sustained intensive wearing out." Cognate balah describes decades-long deterioration. 3.5-year period pushes against semantic range. 4. "Devour the whole earth" (Dan 7:23): Seleucid scale problem — the Seleucids were a fragment of Greece, not exceeding it. 5. Dan 7:11 — beast slain: The BEAST (not horn) is destroyed. Seleucid empire continued ~100 years after Antiochus. 6. dat absolute form tension: sbar + Haphel lehashnayah implies institutional program to alter law, not temporary suppression. 7. NT applies Dan 7 beyond Antiochus: Matt 24:15 (future to Jesus), 2 Thess 2:3-4 (future to Paul), Rev 13:1-7 (future to John), Rev 12:14 (non-Maccabean context).

    dan3-12-PRET-daniel-8 — Preterist Reading of Daniel 8¶

    Question: How does the preterist school read Daniel 8, and what is the textual basis for identifying the little horn as Antiochus IV?

    Key Arguments: 1. be-acharit malkutam timestamp (Dan 8:23): "-am" suffix (3mp possessive) on malkut → four kingdoms of 8:22. Horn's rise timestamped within Greek successor era. I-A(1) HIGH — most text-derivable PRET element. 2. Dan 8/Dan 11 vocabulary correspondence: tamid (8:11/11:31), mirmah (8:25/11:23), shalvah (8:25/11:21,24), miqdash (8:11/11:31), gadal (8:11,25/11:36,37), kir'tsono (8:4/11:3,16,36).     dan3-16-PRET-daniel-8-9 — Preterist Reading of Daniel 8-9 and the 70 Weeks¶

    Question: How does the preterist school read Daniel 8-9 and the 70 weeks, and what is the basis for disconnecting Dan 9 from Dan 8?

    Disconnection Thesis: Dan 9 responds to Jeremiah's 70-year exile prophecy (Jer 25:11-12; 29:10), not to Dan 8. Literary trigger = Jeremiah (named in 9:2). Prayer vocabulary (avon, chattat, shamem) matches Gabriel's response (9:24). eth qets absent from Dan 9. Genre: pesher exegesis (70 years → 70 x 7 = 490).

    PRET Concession: Dan 9:21 identifies Gabriel as "the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision (ba-chazon) at the beginning (ba-techillah)" — back-reference to Dan 8:16. PRET concedes lexical back-reference but argues SETTING (personnel) continuity, not CONTENT continuity.

    Mashiach Identifications: 1. mashiach nagid (9:25) = Joshua/Jeshua ben Jozadak (538 BC): mashiach applies to priests (Lev 4:3,5,16; 6:22). nagid applies to temple leaders (1 Chr 9:11; Neh 11:11). Anarthrous form = "an anointed one." Masoretic atnach after "seven weeks" separates periods. Chronological fit: 586 - 49 = 537 ≈ 538 BC. 2. mashiach yikkaret (9:26) = Onias III (murdered ~171 BC): Cross-chapter parallel with Dan 11:22 negid berith. ve-ein lo = dispossession. Semantic range: mashiach applies to priests, kings, Cyrus (Isa 45:1), Zedekiah (Lam 4:20). Only 2 of 39+ occurrences translated "Messiah" in KJV. 3. nagid ha-ba (9:26b) = Antiochus IV: Forces desecrate temple.

    gabar berith (Dan 9:27): ve-higbir (Hiphil of gabar H1396) = "cause to prevail." Concordance: gabar = "prevail" in 8/25 occurrences; "confirm" ONLY in Dan 9:27 KJV. NOT karath berith (standard covenant-making). Gen 7:18-24 uses gabar for flood waters "prevailing." PRET reads: Antiochus caused Hellenistic assimilation covenant (1 Macc 1:11-15) to prevail.

    Day of Atonement fingerprint: Lev 16:21 = ONLY Pentateuch verse with avon + pesha + chattat together; Dan 9:24 has the same triad. 490 = 10 jubilee cycles (Lev 25:8).

    Cross-vision consistency: Antiochus in Dan 7, 8, 9, 11.

    Specification-Match Tally: - I-A(1) HIGH: 4 (horn origin, tamid, broken without hand, abomination) - I-A(1) MED: 4 (nagid ha-ba, covenant, midweek, directional match) - I-A(2) MED: 3 (Prince of princes, mashiach nagid=Joshua, mashiach=Onias) - I-B LOW: 4 (gadal/yether magnitude, nitsdaq/2300, 70-week arithmetic, disconnection thesis)

    Honest Weaknesses: 1. 490-year arithmetic failure: No known starting decree yields Maccabean events. 538-490=48 BC, 605-490=115 BC, 586-490=96 BC — none match. Detailed subdivisions (7+62+1, mid-week) suggest arithmetic precision. 2. haben + mar'eh inclusio (8:16 // 9:23): Identical construction. Gabriel says "understand the mar'eh" BEFORE delivering content. Temporal-sequence problem. 3. chathak hapax "cut off FROM": BDB primary = "cut off." Daniel used charats for "determine" 3x in same context. No satisfactory candidate for "cut off from" within PRET framework. 4. biyn chain five-stage arc: Commission 8:16 → Failure 8:27 → Study 9:2 → Resumption 9:23 → Completion 10:1. Creates structural unity. 5. Matt 24:15 post-Maccabean application. 6. gadal/yether progression. 7. nitsdaq forensic sense.

    dan3-20-PRET-daniel-10-12 — Preterist Reading of Daniel 10-12¶

    Question: How does the preterist school read Daniel 10-12?

    Cosmic Framework (Dan 10): Patron-angel schema with sar (prince) for each empire. Michael = echad ha-sarim ha-rishonim ("one of the chief princes," 10:13) — PRET reads as partitive: one member of a group. Title progression (10:13 → 10:21 → 12:1) = increasing narrative prominence, not ontological escalation. Jude 1:9 = Michael defers to God's authority.

    Ptolemaic-Seleucid Identifications (Dan 11:2-20): Extraordinary precision. Essentially uncontested across positions. Jerome himself conceded the accuracy. Key correspondences: Xerxes (11:2), Alexander (11:3), fourfold division (11:4), Ptolemy I/Seleucus I (11:5), Berenice marriage (11:6), Battle of Raphia (11:11), Cleopatra I (11:17), Heliodorus (11:20).

    Antiochus IV Section (Dan 11:21-35): Strong correspondence. nivzeh/flatteries (11:21), negid berith = Onias III (11:22), small people (11:23), Egyptian campaigns (11:25-28), ships of Chittim/Day of Eleusis (11:30), temple desecration (11:31), maskilim persecution (11:33), purification triad (11:35).

    11:35-36 Transition — PRET argues continuity: No explicit subject-change marker. ha-melekh = anaphoric. kir'tsono = stock phrase (also in 11:3, 11:16). 2 Macc 9:12 — Antiochus acknowledged self-exaltation.

    11:36-39 Strain Zone: "Magnify himself above every god" strains Antiochene identification (Antiochus promoted Zeus, not total theological nihilism). "Desire of women" (chemdat nashim) = interpretive ambiguity (Tammuz/Adonis? personal indifference?). "God of forces" (eloah ma'uzzim) = Zeus Olympios — but "god of fortresses" not typical Zeus characterization.

    11:40-45 — FIVE-SPECIFICATION FAILURE: 1. No documented 3rd Egyptian campaign after Eleusis (168 BC) 2. No Antiochus control of Egypt, Libya, Ethiopia (11:42-43) 3. Edom/Moab/Ammon escape list — no Maccabean referent (11:41) 4. MOST DAMAGING: "Plant palace-tent between seas and glorious holy mountain" (11:45) = Jerusalem. Antiochus died in Persia (1 Macc 6:16; Josephus Ant. 12.9.2). 5. eth qets marker (11:40) may signal eschatological scope

    CRIT variant: 11:40-45 = failed prediction proving vaticinium ex eventu. Conservative variant = generalized eschatological projection.

    Dan 12:1-13 — Eschatological: - Dan 12:2 transcends Maccabean: Dual-outcome resurrection, dera'on hapax pair (H1860: ONLY in Dan 12:2 and Isa 66:24 — eschatological judgment), dual olam construct chains. National/collective reading (Ezk 37 model) fails: different from Ezk 37 in dual outcome, individual language, dera'on link. - Dan 12:13 — personal promise to Daniel: "Thou shalt rest and stand in thy lot at the end of the days." Individual bodily resurrection promise. Daniel dead centuries before Maccabean era. - Time periods: 1290 and 1335 days have NO identified Maccabean endpoints. 1260-day period ~155 days longer than desecration-to-rededication.

    maskilim chain (11:33 → 11:35 → 12:3 → 12:10): Strongest structural argument for narrative continuity. Same community from persecution through eschatological glorification.

    purification triad bracket (11:35 // 12:10): tsaraph + barar + laban co-occurrence unique to these two Daniel verses. Stem changes mark progression from purpose of suffering to achieved result.

    Typological reapplication defense: Hosea 11:1 // Matt 2:15 precedent — original historical referent reapplied by NT. Luke 21:20 replaces "abomination of desolation" with "Jerusalem compassed with armies."

    Specification-Match Tally (Dan 11:21-45): - I-A(1) HIGH: 7 (vile person, flatteries, scatter prey, Egyptian campaign, heart against covenant, internal division, maskilim persecution) - I-A(1) MED: 4 (prince of covenant, small people, little help, purification triad) - I-A(2) MED: 2 (willful king self-exaltation, god of forces) - I-A(2) LOW: 1 (desire of women) - I-A(3) LOW: 2 (time-of-end campaign, Edom/Moab/Ammon escape) - I-D LOW: 2 (Libya/Ethiopia control, death location)

    Progressive degradation: HIGH matches in 11:21-35, LOW/I-D in 11:36-45.
