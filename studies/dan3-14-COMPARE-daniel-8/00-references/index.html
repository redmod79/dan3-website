    Reference Gathering: Daniel 8 Three-Way Comparison¶

    Question¶

    What does Daniel 8 establish (E/N), and how do the three readings compare?

    Study Plan Context¶

    This is a COMPARE study reading the three perspective studies for Daniel 8: - dan3-11-HIST-daniel-8 - dan3-12-PRET-daniel-8 - dan3-13-FUT-daniel-8

    Prior COMPARE conclusions providing accumulated context: - dan3-06-COMPARE-daniel-2 - dan3-10-COMPARE-daniel-7

    Key adjudications from the plan (dan2-14-COMPARE): 1. mehem antecedent: grammar textbook evidence (GKC 135o, constructio ad sensum) -- does the 3mp suffix on two feminine antecedents resolve toward winds or horns? 2. gadal/yether progression: does the text REQUIRE the horn to exceed both named empires? 3. nitsdaq: forensic vindication vs. temple restoration -- lexical evidence (53/54 concordance distribution, LXX standard dikaioo) 4. 2300 full days vs. 1150 half-days: erev-boqer grammar (Gen 1 parallel vs. DOA formula) 5. 2300/1150 historical math test against the actual Antiochus period (~1105 days) 6. "Time of the end" (eth qets): what does the phrase require? (Danielic technical term, 5 occurrences)

    Prior Studies¶

    From Perspective Studies (Mandatory Reading)¶

    dan3-11-HIST-daniel-8: - Question: How does historicism read Daniel 8, and what is the textual/grammatical basis for identifying the little horn as Rome? - Horn identity: Rome (pagan + papal) under one symbol. Classification: I-A(1) for most specifications; no E-tier for the identification itself (text does not name Rome). - Gadal/yether progression: Ram gadal (Hiphil, unmodified), goat gadal ad-meod (Hiphil + intensifier), horn gadal-yether (Qal + surplus). The stem shift from Hiphil to Qal signals organic/inherent growth. yether (H3499) = surplus/preeminence. HIST argues this mathematically eliminates Antiochus (~3M km2 vs. Persia ~5.5-8M km2 and Greece ~5.2M km2). - Az paniym exclusive construct chain: Only Deut 28:50 and Dan 8:23 -- ties the horn to the covenant-curse framework. - Nitsdaq (H6663 Niphal): The ONLY Niphal of tsadaq in the OT. Forensic, not ritual. Daniel deliberately chose tsadaq over taher (94x) and kaphar (102x). Old Greek preserves dikaiothesatai (forensic); Theodotion shifts to katharisthesetai. - Eth qets chain: Dan 8:17; 11:35; 11:40; 12:4; 12:9 -- terminates at bodily resurrection (Dan 12:2). Cannot be confined to Maccabean era. - Dan 8-9 connection: Gabriel returns (9:21-23) to explain the mar'eh (8:26-27). Shared vocabulary: pesha (8:12-13, 9:24), tsadaq root (8:14, 9:24), qodesh (8:14, 9:24). Chathak hapax (H2852, 9:24) = "cut off from" larger period. - Vocabulary bridges: pasha-anomos bridge (Dan 8:23 -> Isa 53:12 -> 2 Thess 2:8); tsadaq chain (Isa 53:11 yatsdiq -> Dan 8:14 nitsdaq -> Dan 12:3 matsdiqey); Rev 13:6 three-to-three correspondence with Dan 8:10-12. - Two-phase reading: ha-tamid VE ha-pesha (two definite-article nouns) = two sequential desolating systems under one horn symbol. - Specification-match tally: 0 E, 0 N, 18 I-A(1) [11 HIGH, 7 MED], 5 I-A(2) [all MED], 0 I-C. Average chain depth ~1.3. - Honest weaknesses: chathak is hapax; Rome identification is inference; mehem grammar is ambiguous; several specs also fit Antiochus; heavenly sanctuary identification relies on Hebrews typology.

    dan3-12-PRET-daniel-8: - Question: How does preterism read Daniel 8, and what is the textual basis for identifying the little horn as Antiochus IV? - Horn identity: Antiochus IV Epiphanes. PRET's strongest argument: be-acharit malkutam (Dan 8:23) -- the -am suffix on malkut points back to the four Greek successor kingdoms (8:22), explicitly timestamping the horn's rise within the Hellenistic era. Classification: I-A(1) HIGH. - Dan 8/Dan 11 vocabulary correspondence: tamid, mirmah, shalvah, miqdash shared between Dan 8 and Dan 11:21-35 (near-consensus Antiochus context). BUT the parallel is thematic, not verbatim -- different verbs (rum Hophal vs. sur Hiphil), different desolation nouns (pesha vs. shiqquts). Classification: I-A(1) MED. - Directional match: South (Egypt), east (Persia/Parthia), pleasant land (Judea) match documented Antiochene campaigns. I-A(1) HIGH. - Activity matches: Tamid removed (I-A(1) HIGH), sanctuary desecrated (I-A(1) HIGH), host given by transgression (I-A(1) HIGH), truth cast to ground (I-A(1) MED), broken without hand = death by disease (I-A(1) HIGH). - 2300/1150 reading: Divides erev-boqer by 2 to get 1150 literal days. BUT ~1105 actual days (45-day shortfall, ~4%). I-A(2) LOW. - nitsdaq as temple restoration (Hanukkah): Depends on Theodotion's katharisthesetai rather than Hebrew/Old Greek forensic sense. I-B LOW -- competing E-LEX evidence. - gadal/yether as I-B: Antiochus geopolitically smaller than both named empires; PRET's shift to "theological greatness" lacks textual warrant. I-B LOW. - Specification-match tally: 14 specifications ranging from I-A(1) HIGH to I-B LOW. Two I-B items (gadal/yether and nitsdaq), both LOW. No E-tier for the Antiochus identification. - Honest weaknesses: gadal/yether progression (I-B), 2300/1150 arithmetic failure (45 days), eth qets chain to Dan 12:2 resurrection, nitsdaq forensic sense against ritual reading, three NT authors apply Daniel's imagery beyond Maccabean era, Dan 8/11 parallel is thematic not verbatim.

    dan3-13-FUT-daniel-8: - Question: How does dispensationalist futurism read Daniel 8, and what is the textual basis for a type/antitype reading? - Horn identity: Antiochus IV as type, future Antichrist as antitype. FUT accepts Antiochus as historical fulfillment for many specifications but projects ultimate fulfillment to eschatological Antichrist. - NT convergence (FUT's strongest argument): Three witnesses apply Daniel's horn imagery to a post-Maccabean figure -- Jesus (Matt 24:15), Paul (2 Thess 2:3-8), John (Rev 13:1-7). Dense vocabulary parallels. I-A(1) HIGH. BUT Matt 24:15 has competing AD 70 reading (Luke 21:20 parallel), introducing I-B element. - Eth qets chain: Same as HIST -- extends vision to bodily resurrection. I-A(1) HIGH. FUT and HIST share this argument. - Type/antitype framework: 1 John 2:18 provides direct warrant ("antichrist shall come, even now are there many antichrists"). Multiple NT typological precedents (Rom 5:14, 1 Cor 5:7, Heb 8:5). BUT Daniel 8 itself contains NO dual-fulfillment textual marker. - Literal 2300 days: ~6.3 years in 360-day prophetic calendar, fitting within 7-year tribulation framework from Dan 9:27. I-C LOW. - Third Temple dependency: 2 Thess 2:4 naos -- but Paul uses naos metaphorically for church in 1 Cor 3:16-17, 2 Cor 6:16, Eph 2:21. I-C LOW. - Dan 8-9 connection as counter-evidence against FUT: Gabriel's return creates #4a SIS link between 2300 and 70 weeks. If 70 weeks = 490 years, 2300 must also be years. FUT must sever this link or argue independent prophecies. - Horn-split problem: FUT assigns Dan 7 horn to future Antichrist (from Rome) and Dan 8 horn to Antiochus (from Greece) -- but extensive vocabulary overlap across the two suggests one figure. - Specification-match tally: "Type" identifications at I-A(1) MED-HIGH; "antitype" identifications at I-A(2) (depend on type/antitype framework); distinctive FUT claims (literal 2300, Third Temple) at I-C LOW. FUT's strongest items are shared with HIST (eth qets, NT convergence). - Honest weaknesses: Type/antitype hermeneutic reads later theology back into text; no dual-fulfillment marker in Dan 8; Antiochus still fails yether even as type; literal 2300 faces Dan 8-9 connection; Third Temple is I-C; horn-split creates internal tension.

    From Prior COMPARE Studies (Accumulated Verdicts)¶

    dan3-06-COMPARE-daniel-2: - E-tier items (12, all ALL): Babylon = head of gold; four sequential kingdoms; fourth = iron-crushing power; divided phase; mingling/non-cleaving; God's everlasting kingdom; stone without hands; ka-chadah simultaneous destruction; Dan 8:20 (Medo-Persia = one entity); Dan 8:21 (Greece); Dan 8:22 (four successor malkuyot); Dan 5:28. - N-tier items (6, all ALL): Medo-Persia = 2nd; Greece = 3rd; PRET Schema A eliminated by Dan 8:20; fourth follows Greece; stone divine origin; stone everlasting. - Fourth kingdom I-B resolution: Moderate toward Rome. Rome at I-A(1) HIGH vs. Greek successors at I-A(2) MED. Three constraints on PRET: batarakh succession language, gadal/yether scale, iron chain scope. - Stone timing I-B resolution: Moderate toward two-phase reading (inaugurated + future consummation). Matt 21:44 contains both present and future dimensions. - HIST aggregate: 2 E, 2 N, 6 I-A(1), 2 I-A(2). Average chain depth 1.25. 10 HIGH, 2 MED, 0 LOW. - PRET aggregate: 2 E, 2 I-A(1), 7 I-A(2), 1 I-A(3). Average chain depth 2.0. 3 HIGH, 7 MED, 2 LOW. - FUT aggregate: 2 E, 2 E+I-A(1), 2 I-A(1), 4 I-A(2), 2 I-A(2)+I-C. Average chain depth 1.75. 5 HIGH, 3 MED, 4 LOW. - Key constraining effects: Dan 8:20 eliminates PRET Schema A; ka-chadah constrains PRET stone timing; tselem chad (one image) constrains FUT gap thesis; "filled the whole earth" constrains inaugurated-only reading.
