    Bible Study: Methodology and Evidence Framework for the Dan3 Series¶

    Question¶

    What is the evidence classification system, what positions are being compared, and what analytical tools does this series use? Specifically, what is the BIBLICAL BASIS for the E/N/I evidence classification framework, the Scripture-interprets-Scripture principle, and the methodology of comparing interpretive positions (HIST/PRET/FUT/CRIT) against explicit textual data?

    Prior Research Summary¶

    From Prior Studies¶

    • hist-01-how-to-read-apocalyptic-prophecy: Established hermeneutical framework for the hist series. Key finding: Scripture provides its own interpretive keys -- semaino (G4591) in Rev 1:1 means "communicate by signs"; angel-interpreters decode symbols (beasts = kingdoms, horns = kings); four-kingdom succession is named and gap-free. 47 E-items classified, the largest single-study contribution.
    • hist-09-why-not-preterism-futurism-idealism: Systematic comparison of interpretive frameworks. Rev 12:5 breaks both preterism and futurism. Six convergent NT texts demolish Israel/Church distinction. 70-week gap theory classified as I-D. Demonstrates how E/N/I framework adjudicates between competing positions.
    • hist-19-comprehensive-synthesis: Shows E/N/I at scale -- 496 items across 18 studies. Structural asymmetry: Historicist = 100 items (42% at E/N tier); Anti-Historicist = 48 items (100% at inference level). Zero E-items or N-items support Anti-Historicist position.
    • genesis-6-explicit-vs-implied-evidence: Precursor to E/N/I system. Angel view: 2 explicit, 11 implied (15:85 ratio). Human view: 19 explicit, 1 implied (95:5 ratio). Demonstrates the "removal test" concept and structural difference between chain-of-inference and convergent-evidence.
    • true-false-prophets and testing-prophets-fulfilled-prophecy: Scripture provides explicit tests for prophetic validity (Deut 13:1-5; 18:20-22).

    From dan2-series-methodology.md (existing 392-line document)¶

    The complete E/N/I framework already exists: three tiers (Explicit, Necessary Implication, Inference with four subtypes I-A/I-B/I-C/I-D), five decision trees, Daniel-specific rules, hierarchy (E > N > I-A > I-B > I-C > I-D). The dan3 series inherits this with its own series tag.

    External Corpus Leads¶

    • EGW (TM 106.2): "The Bible must not be interpreted to suit the ideas of men... We should make the Bible its own expositor."
    • EGW (Ms 175, 1901): "The Bible is its own interpreter. With beautiful simplicity, one portion connects itself with the truth of another portion."
    • Loughborough (MPC 28.1): "An inference should not be made the basis for an argument. Great Scripture truths are plainly stated."
    • Bohr (ITB p. 68): "The Bible is self-contained and its own interpreter... the analogy of Scripture or Sola Scriptura."
    • Bohr (PPNB p. 62): "Does the method of Historicism come from Scripture itself or is it imposed upon Scripture by the interpreter?"
    • Bohr (KSBI p. 3): Day-year principle -- Num 14:34 and Ezek 4:6 as primary texts.

    Focus Areas¶

    1. Scripture's Self-Interpreting Principle (Biblical Foundation for SIS): Tool discoveries surface ISA 8:20 (law + testimony test), ISA 28:13 (precept upon precept, line upon line), 2PE 1:20-21 (no private interpretation because prophecy came by the Holy Spirit), LUK 24:27,45 (Christ expounding Himself from ALL the Scriptures), and 1CO 2:13 (comparing spiritual things with spiritual). These verses establish that Scripture provides its own interpretive framework rather than relying on external systems. The research agent should retrieve ISA 8:16-20 with full chapter context, 2PE 1:19-21 with full chapter context, LUK 24:25-47 with context, 1CO 2:9-16 with context, and ISA 28:9-13 with context. Run hebrew_parser.py on ISA 8:20 and ISA 28:13 to analyze the Hebrew structure of these foundational hermeneutical statements.

    2. The Berean Model -- Testing Claims Against Scripture (ACT 17:11): Nave's REASONING entry confirms ACT 17:2,11 as the primary model for Scripture-based evaluation. G350 (anakrino -- to scrutinize) appears in Acts 17:11. The concept_context.py output for ACT 17:11 confirms the WORD concept (G3056) as central. The research agent should retrieve ACT 17:10-14 with full chapter context, run greek_parser.py on ACT 17:11 to analyze the specific Greek vocabulary of "searched" (anakrino) and "whether those things were so." Also retrieve ACT 18:24-28 (Apollos corrected by Scripture) and ACT 28:23-24 (Paul reasoning from Scripture).

    3. Jesus' Use of Explicit Text as Argument (MAT 22:31-32): The WORD OF GOD entry under INSPIRATION lists MAT 22:31,32 -- Jesus argues to the Sadducees by citing what God explicitly "spoke." His argument derives a necessary implication (God IS, not WAS, the God of Abraham -- therefore resurrection) from an explicit statement. This is the biblical prototype for distinguishing E-tier from N-tier evidence. The research agent should retrieve MAT 22:23-33 with full chapter context, run greek_parser.py on MAT 22:31-32 to identify the precise logical structure, and then trace Jesus' other explicit-text arguments: MAT 4:4,7,10 (threefold "It is written"); MAT 19:4-6 ("Have ye not read"); MRK 12:24-27 (parallel). Run cross_testament_parallels_v2.py on MAT 22:31 (both directions).

    4. Evidence Laws in Scripture -- The Two-Witness Principle: The EVIDENCE and WITNESS entries converge on a consistent biblical standard: NUM 35:30; DEU 17:6; 19:15; MAT 18:16; JHN 8:17; 2CO 13:1; 1TI 5:19; HEB 10:28. This principle ("at the mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses") establishes that Scripture itself requires multiple independent attestations before a matter is established. This directly parallels the E/N/I framework's requirement that E-items should pass a "universal agreement test." The research agent should retrieve DEU 19:15-21 and DEU 17:6-7 with full context, JHN 8:13-18 (Jesus applies the two-witness principle to His own testimony), and 2CO 13:1 with context. Run hebrew_parser.py on DEU 19:15.

    5. Prophetic Interpretation Belongs to God -- The Angel-Interpreter Pattern: The INTERPRETER, PROPHETS, and DANIEL entries reveal a consistent pattern: GEN 40:8 ("Do not interpretations belong to God?"), DAN 2:28 ("there is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets"), DAN 7:16 (angel explains the vision), DAN 8:16 ("Gabriel, make this man to understand"), DAN 9:22 ("to give thee skill and understanding"), DAN 10:21 ("scripture of truth"). This pattern establishes that Daniel's visions include their own divinely-supplied interpretation -- the angel-interpreter provides E-tier identification of symbols. The research agent should retrieve DAN 7:15-28, DAN 8:15-26, DAN 9:20-27, and DAN 10:20-21 with full chapter context. Also retrieve ZEC 1:7-21 (parallel angel-interpreter pattern). Run hebrew_parser.py on DAN 7:16 and DAN 8:16 (Aramaic/Hebrew). Trace H6590 (peshar -- Aramaic "interpret") across Daniel.

    6. Semaino in Rev 1:1 -- God Communicates by Signs: G4591 (semaino) appears 6 times in the NT (JHN 12:33; 18:32; 21:19; ACT 11:28; 25:27; REV 1:1). The lexicon confirms it means "to indicate" from sema (a mark). In Rev 1:1, God "signified" (esemainen) the revelation -- communicating through signs/symbols. This word establishes that prophetic symbolism is not arbitrary but a divinely chosen communication method, and that the symbols themselves carry meaning. The research agent should retrieve all 6 verses where G4591 appears, run greek_parser.py on REV 1:1 and JHN 12:33, and run cross_testament_parallels_v2.py on REV 1:1 (both directions).

    7. The "Not Adding To" Principle -- Boundaries of Evidence: DEU 4:2 ("Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish"), PRO 30:5,6, and REV 22:18,19 establish a textual boundary principle. The research agent should retrieve DEU 4:1-8, PRO 30:5-6, and REV 22:18-19 with context. This principle directly supports the classification hierarchy: claims that add concepts not in the text (I-C, I-D) are lower-tier than claims derived from what the text says (E, N, I-A).

    8. False Doctrine Testing -- DEU 13 and DEU 18 Protocols: The PROPHETS entry identifies DEU 13:1-5 (consistency-with-revelation test) and DEU 18:20-22 (fulfillment test) as Scripture's own criteria for evaluating prophetic claims. These are the biblical prototypes for the dan3 methodology's "no position locks" approach -- testing each claim against textual data rather than presupposing correctness. The research agent should retrieve DEU 13:1-5 and DEU 18:15-22 with full context, GAL 1:6-9 (even an angel's teaching must be tested), and 1JN 4:1 ("try the spirits whether they are of God"). Run hebrew_parser.py on DEU 13:1-3 and DEU 18:20-22.

    9. Investigation and Right Division of the Word: INVESTIGATION (ECC 1:13-18; 2:1-12; 12:9-14), REASONING (ACT 17:2; ISA 1:18), and 2TI 2:15 ("Study to shew thyself approved... rightly dividing the word of truth") establish a biblical mandate for systematic investigation. The research agent should retrieve ECC 12:9-14 ("the preacher was wise; he still taught... he sought to find out acceptable words"), 2TI 2:15, and ISA 1:18 with context. Run greek_parser.py on 2TI 2:15 to analyze "rightly dividing" (orthotomeo -- G3718).

    10. Prophetic Certainty and Appointed Time: The PROPHECY entry establishes EZK 12:22-25,28 and HAB 2:3 as texts affirming that prophetic visions have appointed fulfillment times, and MAT 5:18 that not one jot or tittle shall fail. This grounds the methodology's treatment of time periods: stated periods are E-tier, while start/end identifications are I-tier. The research agent should retrieve EZK 12:21-28, HAB 2:1-4, MAT 5:17-18, and 2PE 3:3-9 with context. Also retrieve NUM 14:34 and EZK 4:4-6 (the day-year formula texts). Run hebrew_parser.py on HAB 2:3 and NUM 14:34.
