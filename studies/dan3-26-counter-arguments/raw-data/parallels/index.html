    Reference Score Key Terms

    Daniel 7:14 0.279 lips
    NOTE: Dan 7:8 "a mouth speaking great things" is the primary verbal link (stoma laloun megala)
    42 months = 1260 days = time/times/half time — seven canonical expressions:
    1. Dan 7:25 — iddan ve-iddanin u-felag iddan (time, times, dividing of time)
    2. Dan 12:7 — mo'ed mo'adim va-chetsi (time, times, half)
    3. Rev 11:2 — 42 months (Gentiles tread holy city)
    4. Rev 11:3 — 1260 days (two witnesses prophesy)
    5. Rev 12:6 — 1260 days (woman nourished in wilderness)
    6. Rev 12:14 — time, times, half a time (woman flees from serpent)
    7. Rev 13:5 — 42 months (beast's authority)
    Contextual differentiation: 42 months = hostile/oppressive periods; 1260 days = preservation/witness
