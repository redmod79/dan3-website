    Raw Hebrew/Aramaic Parsing Data¶

    Daniel 7:2 — "Great Sea" (Directive A)¶

    עָנֵה דָנִיֵּאל וְאָמַר חָזֵה הֲוֵית בְּחֶזְוִי עִם־לֵילְיָא וַאֲרוּ אַרְבַּע רוּחֵי שְׁמַיָּא מְגִיחָן לְיַמָּא רַבָּא

Key terms:
- יַמָּא (yamma) = sea [Noun.ms] from ים
- רַבָּא (rabba) = great [Adj.ms] from רב
- מְגִיחָן (megichan) = stir up [Verb.haf.Ptcp.fp] from גוח
- רוּחֵי (ruche) = winds [Noun.fp.Cst] from רוח
- שְׁמַיָּא (shmayya) = heaven [Noun.mp] from שׁמין

    Daniel 7:3 — "diverse one from another" (shanyan)¶

    וְאַרְבַּע חֵיוָן רַבְרְבָן סָלְקָן מִן־יַמָּא שָׁנְיָן דָּא מִן־דָּא

Key terms:
- שָׁנְיָן (shanyan) = be different [Verb.peal.Ptcp.fp.Abs] from שׁנה
  Pe'al participle = intransitive "being different"
- חֵיוָן (cheyvan) = animals [Noun.fp.Abs] from חיוה
- רַבְרְבָן (rabrevan) = great [Adj.fp.Abs] from רב
- סָלְקָן (saleqan) = go up [Verb.peal.Ptcp.fp.Abs] from סלק

    Daniel 7:7 — Fourth beast¶

    Key terms:
- רביעיה (revi'ayah) = fourth [Adj.fs.Abs] from רביעי
- דְּחִילָה (dechilah) = fear/dreadful [Verb.peal.Ptcp.fs.Abs] from דחל
- אֵימְתָנִי (eimtani) = terrible [Adj.ms.Abs]
- תַקִּיפָא (taqqipha) = strong [Adj.fs.Abs] from תקיף
- יַתִּירָא (yattira) = excessive/exceedingly [Adj.fs.Abs] from יתיר
- שִׁנַּיִן דִּי־פַרְזֶל (shinnayin di-pharzel) = teeth of iron
- מְשַׁנְּיָה (meshanneya) = be different [Verb.PAEL.Ptcp.fs.Abs] from שׁנה
  NOTE: Pa'el (intensive) participle — stronger than Pe'al in 7:3
- קַרְנַיִן עֲשַׂר (qarnayyin 'asar) = ten horns [Noun.fd.Abs + Noun.s.Abs]

    Daniel 7:8 — Little horn, three uprooted¶

    Key terms:
- קֶרֶן אָחֳרִי זְעֵירָה (qeren achori ze'irah) = horn another small [Noun.fs.Abs + Adj.fs.Abs + Adj.fs.Abs]
- סִלְקָת (silqat) = go up [Verb.peal.Perf.3fs] from סלק
- קַרְנַיָּא קַדְמָיָתָא (qarnayya qadmayata) = the former horns [Noun.fd + Adj.fp]
  H6933 qadmay = "first/former" — same root as qadmayeta in 7:4
- אתעקרו (et'aqaru) = pluck out [Verb.ETPE'EL.Perf.3fp] from עקר (H6132)
  Hithpe'el = reflexive/passive "were rooted up"
- עַיְנִין כְּעַיְנֵי אֲנָשָׁא (aynin ke-ayney anasha) = eyes like the eyes of mankind
- פֻם מְמַלִּל רַבְרְבָן (pum memallil rabrevan) = mouth speaking great [things]
  מְמַלִּל = Pa'el Ptcp ms from מלל = "speaking"
  רַבְרְבָן = Adj fp from רב = "great" (reduplicated form = H7260)

    Daniel 7:9 — Judgment scene¶

    Key terms:
- כָרְסָוָן רְמִיו (karsavan remivu) = thrones thrown/placed [Noun.mp.Abs + Verb.peil.Perf.3mp]
  NOTE: רְמִיו from רמה "throw" — thrones were "cast/placed"
- עַתִּיק יוֹמִין (Attiq Yomin) = Ancient of Days [Adj.ms.Cst + Noun.mp.Abs]
- יְתִב (yetiv) = sit [Verb.peal.Perf.3ms] from יתב
- לְבוּשֵׁהּ כִּתְלַג חִוָּר (levusheh ki-telag chivvar) = garment like snow white
- שְׂעַר רֵאשֵׁהּ כַּעֲמַר נְקֵא (se'ar resheh ka-'amar neqe) = hair of his head like pure wool
- כָּרְסְיֵהּ שְׁבִיבִין דִּי־נוּר (karseyeh shvivin di-nur) = his throne flames of fire
- גַּלְגִּלֹּוהִי נוּר דָּלִק (galgillohiy nur daliq) = his wheels burning fire

    Daniel 7:11 — Beast slain (Directive D)¶

    Key terms:
- קְטִילַת חֵיוְתָא (qetilat cheyveta) = slain was the beast [Verb.PEIL.Perf.3fs]
  Peil = passive "was killed"
- הוּבַד גִּשְׁמַהּ (huvad gishmah) = perished/destroyed her body [Verb.Hophal.Perf.3ms + Noun.ms.Abs+3fs]
  Hophal = causative passive "was caused to perish"
- יהִיבַת לִיקֵדַת אֶשָּׁא (yehivat li-yeqedat esha) = given to firebrand of fire

    Daniel 7:12 — Rest of the beasts (Directive B)¶

    Key terms:
- שְׁאָר חֵיוָתָא (she'ar cheyvata) = rest of the beasts [Noun.ms.Cst + Noun.fp]
- הֶעְדִּיו שָׁלְטָנְהוֹן (he'ddivu sholtanhon) = taken away their dominion [Verb.haf.Perf.3mp + Noun.ms.Abs+3mp]
- אַרְכָה בְחַיִּין (arkhah be-chayyim) = prolonging in life [Noun.fs.Abs + Prep + Noun.mp.Abs]
- יְהִיבַת לְהוֹן (yehivat lehon) = given to them [Verb.peil.Perf.3fs + Prep+3mp]
- עַד זְמַן וְעִדָּן ('ad zeman ve-'iddan) = until a season and a time [Prep + Noun.ms.Abs + Conj + Noun.ms.Abs]

    Daniel 7:13 — Son of Man¶

    Key terms:
- עִם עֲנָנֵי שְׁמַיָּא ('im 'ananey shmayya) = with clouds of heaven
  עִם = Prep "with" (NOT "upon" — the motion is described separately)
  עֲנָנֵי = clouds [Noun.mp.Cst] from ענן (H6050)
- כְּבַר אֱנָשׁ (ke-var enash) = like son of mankind [Prep + Noun.ms.Cst + Noun.ms.Abs]
