    Bible Study: How Revelation Develops Daniel's Prophetic Themes¶

    Question¶

    How does Revelation develop Daniel's prophetic themes, and what does the literary dependence establish?

    Prior Research Summary¶

    From Prior Studies (14+ studies)¶

    The dan3 series and companion studies have established extensive groundwork:

    1. Verbatim Greek quotation chains documented across Daniel LXX and Revelation: stoma laloun megala (Dan 7:8,20 LXX = Rev 13:5), ha dei genesthai (Dan 2:28 LXX = Rev 1:1; 22:6), sphragizo reversal (Dan 12:4 -> Rev 22:10), ten horns = ten kings vocabulary chain (daniel-7-8-9-revelation-parallels, hist-07, dan3-24)
    2. Composite beast absorption validated as Strong pattern: Rev 13 absorbs all four Dan 7 beasts in reverse order (leopard-bear-lion), with seven heads as arithmetic composite 1+1+4+1=7 (revs-41, rev-11-sea-beast)
    3. sphazO counterfeit pattern: same verb G4969, same tense (perfect passive participle), same particle (hOs) for Lamb (Rev 5:6) and beast (Rev 13:3) -- documented in rev-11-sea-beast and revs-41
    4. edothE divine passive appears 4x in Rev 13:5-7, paralleling Dan 8:24 "not by his own power" (rev-11-sea-beast)
    5. Christological merger: Rev 1:13-14 collapses Dan 7:9 (Ancient of Days: white hair) and Dan 7:13 (Son of Man) into one figure (revs-41, dan3-24, dan3-22)
    6. Sealed-to-unsealed arc (SP090): Dan 12:4 sealed -> Rev 5-8 scroll opened -> Rev 10:4 thunders sealed -> Rev 22:10 NOT sealed (revs-39)
    7. Dan 12:5-7 / Rev 10:5-7 oath scene parallel with 5-6 shared elements, content reversal from duration to termination (revs-39, dan3-24)
    8. Time-period chain across three languages: iddan (Aramaic) -> moed (Hebrew) -> kairos (Greek), with 3.5 years = 42 months = 1260 days documented (daniel-7-8-9-revelation-parallels)
    9. nitsdaq (Dan 8:14) established as forensic vindication, not ritual cleansing -- tsadaq forensic in 53/54 KJV instances (dan3-14)
    10. 2 Thess 2 / Rev 13 composite: Paul's man of sin draws from Dan 7+8+11 simultaneously (hist-07, dan3-24)
    11. Three angels' messages positioned after beast's 42-month authority; Rev 14:7 krisis = LXX of Aramaic dina from Dan 7:10 (hist-12)
    12. Kingdom-transfer climax (SP109): Dan 7 -> Rev 5:10 -> Rev 11:15 -> Rev 20:4 -> Rev 22:5 (revs-41)

    NEW Investigation Areas (not yet formally studied)¶

    • Vindication Quartet: Dan 8:14 nitsdaq distributed across four Revelation declarations (plan topic #19)
    • God's temporal formula inverted: "is, was, is to come" (Rev 1:4,8; 4:8) vs "was, is not, yet is" (Rev 17:8) (plan topic #10)
    • Eight-element trinitarian parody structure (Dragon/Beast/False Prophet) (plan topic #9)
    • 70th week as Revelation tribulation backbone (FUT reading) (plan topic #11)
    • Olivet-seals correspondence (plan topic #16)
    • Dan 3 / Rev 13 image-worship-penalty pattern (Bohr claim)

    External Corpus Leads¶

    • EGW identifies Rev 10's sealed book as specifically Daniel's prophecy (1MR 43.3)
    • Froom documents Protestant consensus that Dan 7 and Rev 13 beasts are "identical" (PFF3 95)
    • Bohr argues reverse beast order reflects temporal direction: Daniel forward, John backward (TFOD p.31)
    • Bohr connects Rev 13:11-18 earth beast to Daniel 3 image-worship-penalty pattern (3AM p.100)
    • Bohr treats Dan 12 / Rev 10 oath scenes as structurally parallel (GPOT2V1 p.355)
    • Bohr connects Daniel, Revelation, and sanctuary as unified theme via vocabulary chains (HWIS p.4)
