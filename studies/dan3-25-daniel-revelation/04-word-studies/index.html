            • G4969 -- sphazo (to slaughter/slay) -- CRITICAL: Counterfeit Pattern
              • Translations (8 KJV occurrences)
              • Full BLB Occurrence List
              • Key Verses for Counterfeit Pattern
            • G988 -- blasphemia (blasphemy) -- Beast's Speech
              • Translations (18 KJV occurrences)
              • Revelation Occurrences
              • Key Verses (all G988 "blasphemy" translations)
            • G4633 -- skene (tabernacle) -- Sanctuary Connection
              • Translations
              • Key Verses (G4633 "tabernacle")
              • Critical: Rev 13:6 connects beast's blasphemy to tabernacle/sanctuary attack (cf. Dan 8:11)
            • G4637 -- skenoo (to tent/dwell) -- Rev 13:6 "Them That Dwell in Heaven"
              • Critical use: Rev 13:6 -- "them that dwell [skenountas] in heaven" = those tabernacling in heaven
            • G3485 -- naos (temple/shrine) -- Temple Nexus
              • Translations
              • Revelation Occurrences (naos "temple")
              • 2 Thessalonians 2:4 -- "he as God sitteth in the temple [naon] of God"
              • Temple Convergence: Rev 11:1-2 / Dan 9:27 / Dan 8:11 / 2 Thess 2:4
            • G5281 -- hypomone (patience/endurance) -- Inclusio Pattern
              • Translations (29 KJV occurrences)
              • Revelation Occurrences (ALL)
              • Inclusio structure: Rev 13:10 -> 14:12 brackets the beast narrative with saints' endurance call
            • G2920 -- krisis (judgment/decision) -- Vindication Chain
              • Translations (34 KJV occurrences)
              • Revelation Occurrences
              • LXX Connection: Dan 7:10 Aramaic dina -> LXX krisis; Dan 7:26 -> krisis; this is the vindication vocabulary chain
              • G2920 "judgment" verses (all 18)
            • G4973 -- sphragis (seal/signet) -- Sealed-Unsealed Progression
              • Translations
              • Key progression: Dan 12:4 sealed (sphragizo) -> Rev 5-8 seals (sphragis) opened -> Rev 10:4 thunders sealed -> Rev 22:10 NOT sealed
            • G601 -- apokalypto (to reveal/disclose) -- Contrast with Sealing
              • Translations
              • Key theological contrast: Daniel = sphragizo (seal up); Revelation = apokalypto (unveil)
            • G946 -- bdelygma (abomination) -- Daniel-to-Revelation Chain
              • Translations
              • Chain: Dan 11:31 shiqquts -> LXX bdelygma -> Matt 24:15 bdelygma -> Rev 17:4-5 bdelygma
              • Matt 24:15 explicitly cites "Daniel the prophet" for the abomination connection
            • G5480 -- charagma (mark/stamp) -- Mark of the Beast
              • Translations
              • Rev 13:16-17 -- the mark system; counterfeits the seal (sphragis) of God (Rev 7:3-4)
            • G4127 -- plege (plague/wound/stripe) -- Beast's Death-Wound
              • Translations
              • Rev 13:3 -- "his deadly wound [plege]" (plege tou thanatou)
              • Rev 13:12 -- "whose deadly wound was healed"
              • Rev 13:14 -- "which had the wound by a sword, and did live"
            • G684 -- apoleia (perdition/destruction) -- Perdition Chain
              • Translations (17 KJV occurrences)
              • Perdition Chain (G684 "perdition")
              • This chain links the man of sin (2 Thess 2) to the beast (Rev 17) through shared "son of perdition" designation
              • G684 "destruction" verses
            • G1325 -- didomi (to give) -- Divine Passive (edothe)
              • Critical Pattern: "was given" (edothe) divine passive in Rev 13:5-7
              • Connection to Dan 8:24 -- "his power shall be mighty, but not by his own power"
            • G5585 -- psephizo (to count/calculate) -- 666 Gematria
              • Occurrences
              • PRET argument: Neron Kaisar in Hebrew letters = 50+200+6+50 + 100+60+200 = 666; variant 616 = Latin "Nero"
            • H6663 -- tsadaq (to be/make right) -- Dan 8:14 Vindication
              • Translations (54 KJV occurrences)
              • Dan 8:14 -- nitsdaq (Niphal of tsadaq) = "be vindicated/justified" -- unique Niphal form
              • LXX Mapping (PMI-weighted)
              • Vindication chain: H6663 tsadaq -> G1344 dikaioo / G2920 krisis -> Rev 14:7 krisis; Rev 16:5-7 dikaiai; Rev 19:2 dikaiai
            • H4720 -- miqdash (sanctuary) -- Dan 8:11
              • Translations
              • LXX Mapping (PMI-weighted)
              • Key: H4720 miqdash -> LXX G40 hagios connects Dan 8:11 sanctuary to Rev 13:7 hagioi (saints) and Rev 15:5 "temple of the tabernacle of the testimony"
            • H5732 -- iddan (time, Aramaic) -- Time-Period Chain
              • Translations (15 KJV occurrences)
              • Key verse: Dan 7:25 -- "a time [iddan] and times [iddanin] and the dividing of time [iddan]" = 3.5 years
              • LXX Mapping (PMI-weighted)
              • Vocabulary chain: H5732 iddan (Aramaic) -> LXX G2540 kairos -> Rev 12:14 kairos (time, times, half a time)
            • H4150 -- moed (appointed time) -- Time-Period Chain
              • Translations (229 KJV occurrences)
              • Key verses: Dan 8:19; 11:27; 11:29; 11:35; 12:7 (2x)
              • LXX Mapping (PMI-weighted)
              • Three-language chain: H5732 iddan (Aramaic) -> H4150 moed (Hebrew) -> G2540 kairos (Greek)
            • H8548 -- tamiyd (continual/daily) -- Dan 8:11-13 Daily Sacrifice
              • Translations
              • Key: Dan 8:11 "the daily [tamiyd] sacrifice was taken away" -- the word translated "sacrifice" is not in the Hebrew; tamiyd alone is the noun meaning "the continual (service)"
            • H6663 tsadaq LXX -> Revelation Vindication Quartet Summary

    H6663 tsadaq LXX -> Revelation Vindication Quartet Summary¶

    The forensic chain from Dan 8:14 to Revelation's four vindication declarations:

    1. Rev 14:7 -- "the hour of his judgment [kriseos, G2920] is come" (cf. Dan 7:10 LXX krisis)
    2. Rev 15:3 -- "just [dikaiai, G1342] and true are thy ways" (cf. tsadaq -> dikaios)
    3. Rev 16:5-7 -- "Righteous [Dikaios] art thou... true and righteous [dikaiai] are thy judgments [kriseis]"
    4. Rev 19:1-2 -- "true and righteous [dikaiai] are his judgments [kriseis]"

    All four use Greek derivatives of the same semantic field as H6663 tsadaq: dikaios (G1342), krisis (G2920). The LXX mapping confirms tsadaq's primary Greek equivalent is dikaioo (G1344), whose adjective form dikaios (G1342) appears in all four Revelation passages.
