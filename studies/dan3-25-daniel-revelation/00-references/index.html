    Reference Gathering: Daniel-Revelation Connections (dan3-25)¶

    Question¶

    How does Revelation develop Daniel's prophetic themes, and what does the literary dependence establish?

    Study Plan Context¶

    From FRESH-DANIEL-STUDY-PLAN-v3.md (dan2-25): - Integrate list: daniel-7-8-9-revelation-parallels, daniel-references-in-revelation-lxx, hist-07, rev-11-sea-beast, revs-39, revs-41 - Key topics: composite beast, sphazO counterfeit, edothE divine passive, hypomonE inclusio, Christological merger, three angels' messages and Dan 8:14, trinitarian parody, sealed/unsealed progression, time-period equivalences, vocabulary chains (Aramaic/Hebrew -> LXX Greek -> NT Greek), angel oath scene parallels, Olivet-seals correspondence, 2 Thess 2 / Rev 13 parallel, Vindication Quartet - Note: daniel-references-in-revelation-lxx does not exist as a study. Its content is largely covered by daniel-8-9-revelation-parallels and hist-07.

    Prior Studies¶

    From Study Plan (Integrate List)¶

    daniel-7-8-9-revelation-parallels: - Comprehensive master parallel chart mapping Dan 7, 8, 9 themes to Revelation - Five most significant parallels identified: (1) composite beast (Dan 7 -> Rev 13), (2) "time, times, half a time" identical phrase across three languages (Aramaic, Hebrew, Greek), (3) throne/judgment scene (Dan 7:9-10 -> Rev 4-5 + 20:11-12), (4) verbatim stoma laloun megala (Dan 7:8,20 LXX = Rev 13:5), (5) sealed vs. unsealed book (Dan 12:4 -> Rev 22:10) - Vocabulary chain documented: qeren/keras, qaddishin/hagioi, chewah/therion, sholtan/exousia, mashiach/Christos, shiqquts/bdelygma, iddan/kairos - Key conclusion: "The LXX created the Greek vocabulary that Revelation uses, establishing linguistic continuity from Daniel through the NT" - Time period equivalences mapped: 3.5 years = 42 months = 1260 days - Day-year principle applied: 1260 prophetic days = 1260 literal years (538-1798 AD)

    daniel-8-9-revelation-parallels: - Extensive parallel chart: time periods, beast/kingdom imagery, horn symbolism, abomination of desolation, Son of Man with clouds, sealed/unsealed book - Key word studies: mashiach (H4899) -> Christos, shiqquts (H8251) -> bdelygma, shamem (H8074) -> eremosis, tsadaq (H6663) as forensic vindication (not ritual cleansing) - Theological implication: Revelation COMPLETES Daniel -- sealed becomes unsealed, successive beasts become composite, brief judgment scene expands - Common antichrist pattern: arises from political succession, speaks blasphemy, persecutes saints, limited time, destroyed supernaturally

    hist-07-nt-connects-daniel-7-12 (companion series): - Established that NT authors reproduce Daniel's Greek vocabulary verbatim, combine elements from multiple Daniel chapters into single descriptions, and structurally frame Revelation as Daniel's fulfillment-era counterpart - dei genesthai formula: Rev 1:1 retains Dan 2:28 LXX verbatim ("ha dei genesthai"), changing only temporal qualifier from "in the last days" to "shortly" -- forms inclusio with Rev 22:6 - Sealed-to-unsealed arc: Dan 12:4 sphragison ("seal!") reversed to Rev 22:10 me sphragises ("do not seal!") -- same verb, opposite command - Olivet Discourse draws from Dan 7, 8-9, 11-12 simultaneously: Matt 24:15 = Dan 8-12 abomination; Matt 24:21 = Dan 12:1 tribulation; Matt 24:30 = Dan 7:13 Son of Man - Paul's man of sin (2 Thess 2:3-8) is a Dan 7+8+11 composite: anomia = Dan 7:25 law-changing; naos usurpation = Dan 8:11 sanctuary; hyperairo above every god = Dan 11:36 - Rev 13 composite beast absorbs all four Dan 7 beasts in reverse order -- proves Revelation treats Dan 7 as a complete system - Time-period chain across three languages: iddan (Aramaic) -> moed (Hebrew) -> kairos (Greek) - "Son of perdition" (apoleia G684) connects 2 Thess 2:3, Rev 17:8,11, and John 17:12 - Already/not yet temporal framework attested by Paul (2 Thess 2:7) and John (1 John 2:18; 4:3) independently - Evidence: 37 E-items, 6 N-items, 7 I-items

    rev-11-sea-beast-daniels-composite (companion series -- slug differs from plan): - SP108 composite absorption validated as Strong pattern: 4/4 Daniel beasts contribute features, order reversal documented, shared vocabulary for each pair - Rev 13:6 threefold blasphemy (name, tabernacle, heaven-dwellers) corresponds to Dan 8:10-12 threefold attack (prince, sanctuary, host) - sphazO counterfeit: same verb (G4969), same tense (perfect passive participle), same particle (hOs) for Lamb (Rev 5:6) and beast (Rev 13:3) -- beast counterfeits Christ's death-and-resurrection - edothE divine passive appears 4x in Rev 13:5-7, paralleling Dan 8:24 "not by his own power" - Rev 13:7 counterfeits Dan 7:14: identical fourfold universal-authority formula - hypomonE inclusio: Rev 13:10 + 14:12 bracket the beast narrative - 42-month chain documented as the 7th reference in the 1260-day chain spanning three languages - Rev 13:6 skEnE (G4633) connects to Dan 8:11 sanctuary attack and Hebrews' tabernacle theology

    revs-39-quickly-shortly-daniel-contrast (companion series -- slug differs from plan): - en tachei (G5034) semantic range documented: manner-of-action ("with speed") to temporal nearness ("shortly") - Daniel-Revelation seal progression (SP090, Strong validation): sealed (Dan 12:4) -> scroll opened by Lamb (Rev 5-8) -> thunders sealed (Rev 10:4) -> book NOT sealed (Rev 22:10) - ha dei genesthai derives from LXX Dan 2:28; Revelation adds "en tachei" - Parallel moral-fixedness declarations at Dan 12:10 and Rev 22:11 - Parallel beatitude structures at Dan 12:12 and Rev 1:3/22:7 - hypomonE 7x in Revelation co-occurring with quickly/shortly framework - Rev 10:5-7 replicates Dan 12:5-7 oath scene with 6 shared elements but reverses content: duration -> termination

    revs-41-daniels-beasts-revelation (companion series -- slug differs from plan): - Reversed beast order (TM204): leopard-bear-lion (Rev) vs lion-bear-leopard (Dan) - Seven heads = arithmetic composite: 1+1+4+1 = 7 from Daniel's four beasts (TM206) - stoma laloun megala verbatim LXX quotation (TM205) - Christological merger (SP110, Moderate): Rev 1:14 collapses Dan 7:9 (Ancient of Days) and Dan 7:13 (Son of Man) into one figure - Kingdom-transfer climax (SP109, Strong): Dan 7 -> Rev 5:10 -> Rev 11:15 -> Rev 20:4 -> Rev 22:5 - Beast-parody structure: identical sphazo + hOs construction for Lamb and beast - Five new verbal parallels documented: VP193-VP197 (blasphemous speech, throne approach, beast destruction, saint judgment, myriades myriadon) - Dan 7:12 provides theological rationale for composite: earlier beasts' lives "prolonged for a season"

    From Dan3 Series (Accumulated Verdicts)¶

    dan3-14-COMPARE-daniel-8: - gadal/yether progression: horn must surpass both Medo-Persia and Greece in territorial greatness -- resolved Strong against Antiochus identification on this specification - nitsdaq (Dan 8:14) is forensic vindication, not ritual cleansing -- tsadaq is forensic in 53/54 KJV instances; Old Greek dikaiothesatai confirms - eth qets chain (8:17; 11:35; 11:40; 12:4; 12:9) extends vision scope to bodily resurrection - Three NT authors (Matt 24:15, 2 Thess 2:3-8, Rev 13:1-7) apply Daniel's horn imagery beyond Maccabean era (I10, ALL, HIGH) - HIST operates at shallowest average inference chain depth

    dan3-18-COMPARE-daniel-8-9: - biyn chain five-stage arc: COMMISSION (8:16) -> FAILURE (8:27) -> STUDY (9:2) -> RESUMPTION (9:23) -> COMPLETION (10:1) - haben + mar'eh grammatical inclusio (8:16 // 9:23) establishes Dan 8-9 connection - chathak hapax (9:24): "cut off" primary meaning, lexically distinct from charats ("determine") - DOA triad (pesha + chattat + avon) in 9:24 connects to Lev 16:21 - gabar beriyth (9:27) is NOT karath beriyth -- unique collocation supports strengthening existing covenant - Dan 9 disconnection thesis resolved Strong against

    dan3-22-COMPARE-daniel-10-12: - Purification triad bracket (11:35 // 12:10) frames Dan 11:36-12:9 as literary unit - dera'on hapax pair locks Dan 12:2 to permanent eschatological judgment (Isa 66:24) - Dan 12:13 personal resurrection promise requires bodily resurrection for Daniel - Three-party pronoun structure in 11:40 distinguishes willful king from both KoN and KoS - Dan 10:5-6 six-element description parallels Rev 1:13-16 (linen, gold girdle, lightning face, fiery eyes, bronze limbs, multitude voice) -- E157 - Angel oath scene: Dan 12:7 mo'ed mo'adim va-chetsi = same period as Dan 7:25 and Rev 11-13

    dan3-24-nt-use-of-daniel: - Confirms NT authors treat Daniel 7-12 as unified prophetic corpus - Verbatim Greek parallels documented: stoma laloun megala, ha dei genesthai, ten horns = ten kings, sphragizo reversal - Vocabulary chains crossing three NT authors: anomia, apoleia, bdelygma - Paul's naos tou theou in 2 Thess 2:4: every Pauline use is metaphorical (church) -- E204 - 1 John 3:4 "sin IS anomia" (G458) = same word as 2 Thess 2:7 -- E203 - Christological merger: Rev 1:13-14 merges Dan 7:13 (Son of Man) with Dan 7:9 (Ancient of Days: white hair) into one figure -- E207 - Rev 14:7 "hour of his judgment is come" -- krisis is LXX equivalent of Aramaic dina from Dan 7:10,26 -- E209 - Rev 10:5-7 replicates Dan 12:5-7 oath scene with 5 shared elements but reverses content: duration -> termination -- E210 - AD 70 exhaustion thesis resolved Strong against: Matt 24:21-31 language exceeds any first-century event - Composite beast of Rev 13 represents same sequential system as Dan 7, with sea beast occupying position of Dan 7's little horn (I10, ALL, HIGH) - Already/not yet framework requires long-enduring entity (I9, HIST, HIGH)
