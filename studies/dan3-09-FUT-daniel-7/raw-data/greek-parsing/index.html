    Raw Greek Parsing Output¶

    Matthew 26:64 — Jesus Quoting Dan 7:13 at Trial¶

    Greek: λέγει αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς Σὺ εἶπας· πλὴν λέγω ὑμῖν, ἀπ' ἄρτι ὄψεσθε τὸν Υἱὸν τοῦ ἀνθρώπου καθήμενον ἐκ δεξιῶν τῆς δυνάμεως καὶ ἐρχόμενον ἐπὶ τῶν νεφελῶν τοῦ οὐρανοῦ.

    Word Lemma Strong Parsing
    ὄψεσθε ὁράω G3708 Fut Mid Ind 2P — "you shall see"
    τὸν Υἱὸν υἱός G5207 Acc Sg M — "the Son"
    τοῦ ἀνθρώπου ἄνθρωπος G444 Gen Sg M — "of man"
    καθήμενον κάθημαι G2521 Pres M/P Ptcp Acc Sg M — "sitting"
    ἐκ δεξιῶν δεξιός G1188 Gen Pl N — "at [the] right hand"
    τῆς δυνάμεως δύναμις G1411 Gen Sg F — "of power"
    ἐρχόμενον ἔρχομαι G2064 Pres M/P Ptcp Acc Sg M — "coming"
    ἐπὶ τῶν νεφελῶν νεφέλη G3507 Gen Pl F — "upon the clouds"
    τοῦ οὐρανοῦ οὐρανός G3772 Gen Sg M — "of heaven"

    KEY: ap' arti (ἀπ' ἄρτι) = "from now / hereafter" — temporal marker indicating future visibility KEY: opsesthe (Fut Mid Ind 2P) = "you SHALL SEE" — future tense, indicating what lies ahead KEY: kathemenon (Pres M/P Ptcp) = "sitting" — present participle: simultaneous with seeing KEY: erchomenon (Pres M/P Ptcp) = "coming" — present participle: simultaneous with seeing KEY: epi ton nephelon = "upon/with the clouds" — preposition epi + genitive NOTE: Two simultaneous actions: sitting at God's right hand AND coming with clouds. The high priest would understand this as a Dan 7:13 claim combined with Ps 110:1.

    Revelation 1:7 — "Behold He Cometh with Clouds"¶

    Greek: Ἰδοὺ ἔρχεται μετὰ τῶν νεφελῶν, καὶ ὄψεται αὐτὸν πᾶς ὀφθαλμὸς καὶ οἵτινες αὐτὸν ἐξεκέντησαν, καὶ κόψονται ἐπ' αὐτὸν πᾶσαι αἱ φυλαὶ τῆς γῆς.

    Word Lemma Strong Parsing
    Ἰδοὺ ὁράω G3708 Aor Mid Impv 2S — "Behold!"
    ἔρχεται ἔρχομαι G2064 Pres M/P Ind 3S — "he comes"
    μετὰ τῶν νεφελῶν νεφέλη G3507 Gen Pl F — "with the clouds"
    ὄψεται ὁράω G3708 Fut Mid Ind 3S — "shall see"
    πᾶς ὀφθαλμὸς ὀφθαλμός G3788 Nom Sg M — "every eye"
    ἐξεκέντησαν ἐκκεντέω G1574 Aor Act Ind 3P — "pierced"
    κόψονται κόπτω G2875 Fut Mid Ind 3P — "shall mourn/wail"
    πᾶσαι αἱ φυλαὶ φυλή G5443 Nom Pl F — "all the tribes"
    τῆς γῆς γῆ G1093 Gen Sg F — "of the earth"

    KEY: erchetai (Pres Ind 3S) = "he comes/is coming" — PRESENT tense used for vivid future (futuristic present) KEY: meta ton nephelon = "WITH the clouds" — preposition meta + genitive = accompaniment KEY: opsetai... pas ophthalmos = "every eye shall see him" — universal visibility KEY: hoi tines auton exekentesan = "those who pierced him" — aorist: a past action (the crucifixion) KEY: kopsontai (Fut Mid Ind 3P) = "they shall mourn" — future mourning at the visible return NOTE: Combines Dan 7:13 (coming with clouds) with Zech 12:10 (they shall look on him whom they pierced). FUT's strongest text for Dan 7:13 = the visible, physical Second Coming.

    2 Thessalonians 2:3 — Man of Sin, Son of Perdition¶

    Greek: μή τις ὑμᾶς ἐξαπατήσῃ κατὰ μηδένα τρόπον· ὅτι ἐὰν μὴ ἔλθῃ ἡ ἀποστασία πρῶτον καὶ ἀποκαλυφθῇ ὁ ἄνθρωπος τῆς ἀνομίας, ὁ υἱὸς τῆς ἀπωλείας,

    Word Lemma Strong Parsing
    ἐξαπατήσῃ ἐξαπατάω G1818 Aor Act Subj 3S — "deceive"
    ἔλθῃ ἔρχομαι G2064 Aor Act Subj 3S — "come"
    ἡ ἀποστασία ἀποστασία G646 Nom Sg F — "the apostasy/falling away"
    πρῶτον πρῶτος G4412 Adv — "first"
    ἀποκαλυφθῇ ἀποκαλύπτω G601 Aor Pass Subj 3S — "be revealed"
    ὁ ἄνθρωπος ἄνθρωπος G444 Nom Sg M — "the man"
    τῆς ἀνομίας ἀνομία G458 Gen Sg F — "of lawlessness/sin"
    ὁ υἱὸς υἱός G5207 Nom Sg M — "the son"
    τῆς ἀπωλείας ἀπώλεια G684 Gen Sg F — "of perdition/destruction"

    KEY: he apostasia (Nom Sg F with article) = "THE apostasy/falling away" — a specific, definite event KEY: apokalyphthe (Aor Pass Subj 3S) = "be revealed" — passive: he is revealed by God's permission at the appointed time KEY: ho anthropos tes anomias = "THE man of lawlessness" — articular: a specific individual KEY: ho huios tes apoleias = "THE son of perdition" — same title as Judas in John 17:12 KEY: Two prerequisites for the Day of Christ: (1) the apostasy must come first (proton), (2) the man of sin must be revealed NOTE: FUT argues the article (ho, "the") before both anthropos and huios identifies a specific individual, not a succession of individuals or an institution.

    Revelation 13:1 — Beast from the Sea¶

    Greek: Καὶ εἶδον ἐκ τῆς θαλάσσης θηρίον ἀναβαῖνον, ἔχον κέρατα δέκα καὶ κεφαλὰς ἑπτά...

    Word Lemma Strong Parsing
    εἶδον ὁράω G3708 Aor Act Ind 1S — "I saw"
    ἐκ τῆς θαλάσσης θάλασσα G2281 Gen Sg F — "out of the sea"
    θηρίον θηρίον G2342 Acc Sg N — "a beast" (neuter)
    ἀναβαῖνον ἀναβαίνω G305 Pres Act Ptcp Acc Sg N — "rising up"
    κέρατα κέρας G2768 Acc Pl N — "horns"
    δέκα δέκα G1176 "ten"
    κεφαλὰς κεφαλή G2776 Acc Pl F — "heads"
    ἑπτά ἑπτά G2033 "seven"
    διαδήματα διάδημα G1238 Acc Pl N — "diadems/crowns"
    ὀνόματα βλασφημίας βλασφημία G988 Gen Sg F — "names of blasphemy"

    NOTE: The beast (therion) is NEUTER, yet Rev 13:2 uses MASCULINE pronouns for it (autou, auto). This gender inconsistency is grammatical constructio ad sensum — the beast is conceptualized as a male person despite the neuter noun.

    Revelation 13:5 — Mouth Speaking Great Things, 42 Months¶

    Greek: καὶ ἐδόθη αὐτῷ στόμα λαλοῦν μεγάλα καὶ βλασφημίας, καὶ ἐδόθη αὐτῷ ἐξουσία ποιῆσαι μῆνας τεσσεράκοντα δύο.

    Word Lemma Strong Parsing
    ἐδόθη δίδωμι G1325 Aor Pass Ind 3S — "was given" (x2)
    στόμα στόμα G4750 Nom Sg N — "a mouth"
    λαλοῦν λαλέω G2980 Pres Act Ptcp Nom Sg N — "speaking"
    μεγάλα μέγας G3173 Acc Pl N — "great things"
    βλασφημίας βλασφημία G988 Acc Pl F — "blasphemies"
    ἐξουσία ἐξουσία G1849 Nom Sg F — "authority"
    ποιῆσαι ποιέω G4160 Aor Act Inf — "to act/do"
    μῆνας τεσσεράκοντα δύο μήν G3376 Acc Pl M — "forty-two months"

    KEY: edothe (Aor Pass Ind 3S x2) = "was given" — divine passive: God permits the activity KEY: stoma laloun megala = "a mouth speaking great things" — VERBATIM echo of Dan 7:8 LXX/Theodotion KEY: exousia poiesai menas tesserakonta duo = "authority to act [for] 42 months" — specific time limit NOTE: Prior study (nt-ties-daniel-7-12) established this quotes Dan 7:8 Theodotion verbatim. FUT reads the 42 months as a literal future 3.5-year tribulation period.

    Revelation 13:7 — War with Saints, Authority over Nations¶

    Greek: καὶ ἐδόθη αὐτῷ ποιῆσαι πόλεμον μετὰ τῶν ἁγίων καὶ νικῆσαι αὐτούς...

    Word Lemma Strong Parsing
    ἐδόθη δίδωμι G1325 Aor Pass Ind 3S — "was given"
    ποιῆσαι πόλεμον πόλεμος G4171 Acc Sg M — "to make war"
    μετὰ τῶν ἁγίων ἅγιος G40 Gen Pl M — "with the saints"
    νικῆσαι νικάω G3528 Aor Act Inf — "to overcome/conquer"
    αὐτούς αὐτός G846 Acc Pl M — "them"
    ἐξουσία ἐξουσία G1849 Nom Sg F — "authority"
    πᾶσαν φυλὴν φυλή G5443 Acc Sg F — "every tribe"
