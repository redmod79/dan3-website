    Raw Hebrew Parsing Output¶

    Daniel 8:4 — Gadal Hiphil (Ram becomes great)¶

    רָאִ֣יתִי אֶת־הָאַ֡יִל מְנַגֵּחַ֩ יָ֨מָּה וְצָפֹ֜ונָה וָנֶ֗גְבָּה וְכָל־חַיֹּות֙ לֹֽא־יַֽעַמְד֣וּ לְפָנָ֔יו וְאֵ֥ין מַצִּ֖יל מִיָּדֹ֑ו וְעָשָׂ֥ה כִרְצֹנֹ֖ו וְהִגְדִּֽיל׃

Key terms:
- כִרְצֹנֹ֖ו (kirtsonoh) — ratson H7522, Noun.ms.Abs.+3ms "pleasure/will" — "according to his will"
- וְהִגְדִּֽיל (wehigdil) — gadal H1431, Verb.Hiphil.Perf.3ms "be strong/great"

NOTE: Hiphil stem = causative. Ram "caused itself to be great" / "made itself great"
NOTE: kirtsonoh marks structural transition (also at Dan 11:3, 11:16, 11:36)

    Daniel 8:8 — Gadal Hiphil + ad-meod (Goat becomes very great)¶

    וּצְפִ֥יר הָעִזִּ֖ים הִגְדִּ֣יל עַד־מְאֹ֑ד

Key terms:
- הִגְדִּ֣יל (higdil) — gadal H1431, Verb.Hiphil.Perf.3ms "be strong/great"
- עַד־מְאֹ֑ד (ad-meod) — "unto exceedingly/very"
- נִשְׁבְּרָה֙ (nishberah) — shabar H7665, Verb.Niphal.Perf.3fs "break" — great horn broken
- חָז֤וּת (chazut) — Noun.fs.Abs "vision" (four notable horns)

NOTE: Same Hiphil stem as 8:4. Modifier escalation: 8:4 = just gadal; 8:8 = gadal ad-meod

    Daniel 8:9 — Gadal Qal + Yether (Horn exceeds exceedingly)¶

    וּמִן־הָאַחַ֣ת מֵהֶ֔ם יָצָ֥א קֶֽרֶן־אַחַ֖ת מִצְּעִירָ֑ה וַתִּגְדַּל־יֶ֛תֶר אֶל־הַנֶּ֥גֶב וְאֶל־הַמִּזְרָ֖ח וְאֶל־הַצֶּֽבִי׃

Key terms:
- מִצְּעִירָ֑ה (mitseirah) — H4704, Noun.fs.Abs "youth/littleness" — HAPAX LEGOMENON
- וַתִּגְדַּל (vattigdal) — gadal H1431, Verb.Qal.Wayq.3fs "be strong/great"
- יֶ֛תֶר (yether) — H3499, Noun.ms.Abs "remainder/excess/surplus"
- אֶל־הַנֶּ֥גֶב (el-ha-negeb) — "toward the south"
- וְאֶל־הַמִּזְרָ֖ח (ve-el-ha-mizrach) — "toward the east/sunrise"
- וְאֶל־הַצֶּֽבִי (ve-el-ha-tsebi) — "toward the beauty/pleasant [land]"

NOTE: STEM SHIFT: Ram (8:4) and Goat (8:8) use HIPHIL; Horn uses QAL.
NOTE: Yether modifies gadal = "exceedingly beyond" — horn surpasses both predecessors
NOTE: Directional markers: south, east, pleasant land — FUT argues Seleucid geography

    Daniel 8:13 — Ha-tamid ve-ha-pesha shomem (Vision question)¶

    עַד־מָתַ֞י הֶחָזֹ֤ון הַתָּמִיד֙ וְהַפֶּ֣שַׁע שֹׁמֵ֔ם תֵּ֛ת וְקֹ֥דֶשׁ וְצָבָ֖א מִרְמָֽס׃

Key terms:
- הֶחָזֹ֤ון (hechazon) — chazon H2377, Art.+Noun.ms.Abs "the vision"
- הַתָּמִיד֙ (hattamid) — tamid H8548, Art.+Noun.ms.Abs "the continuity/daily"
- וְהַפֶּ֣שַׁע (veha-pesha) — pesha H6588, Conj+Art.+Noun.ms.Abs "and the rebellion"
- שֹׁמֵ֔ם (shomem) — shamam H8074, Verb.Qal.Ptcp.ms.Abs "be desolate"
- מִרְמָֽס (mirmas) — H4823, Noun.ms.Abs "trampled land/trampling"

NOTE: Both ha-tamid AND ha-pesha have the definite article (ha-).
NOTE: The conjunction ve connects the two articular nouns.
NOTE: FUT reads this as one question about one desolating system; HIST reads two separate entities.

    Daniel 8:14 — Nitsdaq (Sanctuary cleansed/justified)¶

    וַיֹּ֣אמֶר אֵלַ֔י עַ֚ד עֶ֣רֶב בֹּ֔קֶר אַלְפַּ֖יִם וּשְׁלֹ֣שׁ מֵאֹ֑ות וְנִצְדַּ֖ק קֹֽדֶשׁ׃

Key terms:
- עֶ֣רֶב (erev) — H6153, Noun.ms.Abs "evening"
- בֹּ֔קֶר (boqer) — H1242, Noun.ms.Abs "morning"
- אַלְפַּ֖יִם (alpayim) — H505, Noun.d.Abs "thousand" (dual = two thousand)
- וּשְׁלֹ֣שׁ מֵאֹ֑ות (ushlosh meot) — "and three hundred"
- וְנִצְדַּ֖ק (venitsdaq) — tsadaq H6663, Verb.Niphal.Perf.3ms "be just/justified"
- קֹֽדֶשׁ (qodesh) — H6944, Noun.ms.Abs "holiness/sanctuary"

NOTE: ONLY Niphal of tsadaq in entire OT. All other passive forms are forensic/courtroom.
NOTE: Erev-boqer = "evening-morning" — same order as Gen 1 day formula

    Daniel 8:17 — Le-eth qets (Time of the end)¶

    הָבֵ֣ן בֶּן־אָדָ֔ם כִּ֖י לְעֶת־קֵ֥ץ הֶחָזֹֽון׃

Key terms:
- הָבֵ֣ן (haven) — bin H995, Verb.Hiphil.Impv.2ms "understand"
- לְעֶת־קֵ֥ץ (le-eth-qets) — "to/for the time of end"
  - עֶת (eth) — H6256, Noun.s.Cst "time"
  - קֵ֥ץ (qets) — H7093, Noun.ms.Abs "end"
- הֶחָזֹֽון (hechazon) — chazon H2377, Art.+Noun.ms.Abs "the vision"

NOTE: le-eth-qets = "for the time of the end" — construct chain
NOTE: Same phrase at 11:35, 11:40, 12:4, 12:9 — eth qets chain

    Daniel 8:19 — Acharit ha-zaAm, le-moed qets¶

    הִנְנִ֣י מֹודִֽיעֲךָ֔ אֵ֥ת אֲשֶׁר־יִהְיֶ֖ה בְּאַחֲרִ֣ית הַזָּ֑עַם כִּ֖י לְמֹועֵ֥ד קֵֽץ׃

Key terms:
- בְּאַחֲרִ֣ית (be-acharit) — "in the end/latter time"
