    Primary Verses — Core Daniel 8-9 Prophecy¶

    Daniel 8 (Full Chapter)¶

    360-Day Year Evidence¶

    Genesis 7:11; 8:3-4 (Flood Chronology)¶

    7:11 In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the great deep broken up... 8:3 And the waters returned from off the earth continually: and after the end of the hundred and fifty days the waters were abated. 8:4 And the ark rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat.

    NOTE: 2nd month 17th day to 7th month 17th day = 5 months = 150 days. This yields 30 days/month. FUT argues this proves a prophetic 360-day year (12 x 30). But the text only proves 30-day months for a 5-month flood period.

    Revelation 12:6,14; 13:5; 11:2-3 (Prophetic Time Equations)¶

    Rev 12:6 ...a thousand two hundred and threescore days. Rev 12:14 ...a time, and times, and half a time. Rev 13:5 ...forty and two months. Rev 11:2 ...forty and two months. Rev 11:3 ...a thousand two hundred and threescore days.

    NOTE: 42 months = 1260 days = time, times, half-time. This yields 30 days/month. FUT uses this to support 360-day prophetic year for the 70-weeks calculation.
