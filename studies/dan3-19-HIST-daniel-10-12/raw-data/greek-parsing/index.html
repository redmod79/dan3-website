KEY VERBAL PARALLEL WITH DAN 11:36:
  Dan 11:36 (Heb): ve-yitromem ve-yitgaddel 'al kol 'el = "exalt and magnify himself above every god"
  2 Thess 2:4 (Gk): ὑπεραιρόμενος ἐπὶ πάντα λεγόμενον Θεόν = "exalting himself above every so-called god"
  NEAR-VERBATIM correspondence: self-exaltation + above + every/all + god

  Dan 11:36: 'al 'el 'elim yedabber niphla'ot = "against God of gods speaks extraordinary things"
  2 Thess 2:4: ἀποδεικνύντα ἑαυτὸν ὅτι ἔστιν Θεός = "displaying himself that he is God"

  KEY: naos tou theou (G3485) = "temple/sanctuary of God"
  In every Pauline usage, naos = the church (1 Cor 3:16-17; 6:19; 2 Cor 6:16; Eph 2:21)
