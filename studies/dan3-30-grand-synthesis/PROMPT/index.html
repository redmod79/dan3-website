    Bible Study: Grand Synthesis of Daniel's Prophetic Visions (dan3-30)¶

    Question¶

    Taking all evidence from the prior dan3 studies, what does Daniel establish (E/N), suggest (I-A), and leave disputed (I-C/I-D)?

    Prior Research Summary¶

    Evidence Database Foundation¶

    The dan3-evidence.db contains 399 classified items across 12 studies:

    Tier ALL CRIT FUT HIST PRET PRET/FUT(prog) Total
    E 210 0 0 0 0 0 210
    N 63 0 0 0 0 0 63
    I-A 7 0 22 38 33 1 101
    I-B 1 0 1 2 9 0 13
    I-C 1 1 4 1 0 0 7
    I-D 0 0 4 0 1 0 5
    TOTAL 282 1 31 41 43 1 399

    Prior Study Architecture¶

    • 5 COMPARE studies: dan3-06 (Dan 2), dan3-10 (Dan 7), dan3-14 (Dan 8), dan3-18 (Dan 8-9/70 weeks), dan3-22 (Dan 10-12)
    • 4 cross-cutting studies: dan3-23 (day-year principle), dan3-24 (NT use of Daniel), dan3-25 (Daniel-Revelation literary dependence), dan3-26 (counter-arguments)
    • 3 steel-man studies: dan3-27 (HIST), dan3-28 (PRET), dan3-29 (FUT)

    Key Quantitative Observations from 00-references.md¶

    • 273 items (68%) are position-neutral (ALL at E/N tier) — the shared textual foundation
    • No position achieved E or N tier for any position-specific claim — all distinctive claims are inferences
    • HIST: 0 I-D overrides, 38 I-A items, shallowest chain depth, all 24 I-B items resolved favorably
    • PRET: 1 I-D override, 9 I-B tensions, FATAL weaknesses in Dan 11:40-45 and Dan 12:2/12:13
    • FUT: 4 I-D overrides, 4 I-C framework items (gap, rapture, Third Temple, six purposes)
    • Counter-argument asymmetry: PRET faces most E/N-grounded opposition (10/15)
    • Day-year principle is I-A(1) HIGH based on nine text-derived lines
    • Dan 8-9 connection established at E/N tier (biyn chain, haben+mar'eh inclusio, chathak hapax)
    • eth qets chain extending to bodily resurrection is N-tier

    External Corpus Observations¶

    EGW/Froom/Smith and Bohr materials all reflect historicist tradition. The biblical verification of their claims has already been completed in prior studies — the sequential-empire logic, 2300-day/year connection, little horn identification constraints, and Dan 8-9 have all been independently classified in the evidence DB.

    Focus Areas¶

    1. What CAN Be Said: The Position-Neutral Textual Foundation (E + N)¶

    WHAT: Compile the complete set of 273 E+N items from the evidence DB, organized by vision cycle (Dan 2, 7, 8-9, 10-12) and by cross-cutting theme. WHY: Tool searches confirm the breadth of Daniel's textual data — Nave's DANIEL entry alone references Dan 1-12 plus Mat 24:15. The HORN entry connects Dan 7:7-24 and 8:3-9,20 with Rev 5:6; 12:3; 13:1,11; 17:3-16. These N-tier connections form the shared textual base all positions must build upon. HOW: Query the evidence DB for all items with classification=ALL at tier E and N. Retrieve full verse text for the foundational passages: Dan 2:31-45; 7:1-28; 8:1-27; 9:20-27; 10:1-21; 11:1-45; 12:1-13. Organize by vision cycle and note which E/N items constrain which positions.

    2. HIST Position Profile¶
