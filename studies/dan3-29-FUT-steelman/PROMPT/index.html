    Bible Study: Steel-Man -- The Complete Futurist Case Across All of Daniel¶

    Question¶

    What is the complete, strongest text-based case for the futurist interpretation across all of Daniel?

    Prior Research Summary¶

    Five prior FUT perspective studies (dan3-05, dan3-09, dan3-13, dan3-17, dan3-21) and five COMPARE studies (dan3-06, dan3-10, dan3-14, dan3-18, dan3-22) have been fully extracted. Across them, ~70+ distinct FUT arguments have been identified, ranked in four tiers by evidential strength. The reference agent compiled these into a tiered synthesis:

    Tier 1 (Strongest): Three-author NT convergence (Jesus, Paul, John all treat Daniel's figures as future); Rev 13:5 verbatim quotation of Dan 7:8; eth qets chain terminating at bodily resurrection (Dan 12:2); four-kingdom schema shared with HIST.

    Tier 2 (Strong): Dan 7:11 fiery destruction with no historical Roman parallel; Rev 17:12 oupo ("not yet"); Dan 11:36 double Hithpael escalation; za'am bracket (8:19 + 11:36); Dan 11:45 unfulfilled geography; literal 3.5 years via Dan 4 iddan precedent; beast/horn grammatical distinction.

    Tier 3 (Moderate): Type/antitype hermeneutic for Dan 8; gadal/yether progression; feet-timing argument; kir'tsono chain; three-party structure in Dan 11:40; progressive dispensationalism's already/not-yet; prophetic telescoping precedents.

    Tier 4 (Framework): Gap thesis / Israel-Church distinction; pretribulation rapture; Anderson-Hoehner calculation; Third Temple requirement; clay = democracy; 360-day prophetic year.

    Honest weaknesses (16+) are compiled and ranked: Critical (gap thesis lacks textual basis, Israel/Church distinction faces 6 NT counter-passages, Mark 1:15 peplērotai), Severe (Dan 7:13 direction toward God, kingdom-already-present texts, stone/cornerstone chain, la-rabbim echo), Moderate (break at 11:36 inferred, kir'tsono continuity, "mystery already works").

    External corpus leads: Froom documents futurism's origin with Jesuit Ribera (~1585); EGW/Smith argue 70 weeks "cut from" 2300 days (457 BC start); Elliott argues Rome-as-capital refutes Jerusalem temple theory; Bohr argues Dan 7 judgment = pre-advent heavenly court rather than FUT's final-judgment reading.
