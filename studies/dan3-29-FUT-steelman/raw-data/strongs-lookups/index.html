    Raw Strong's Lookups¶

    H1855 — דְּקַק (d'qaq) — crush, break in pieces¶

    • Transliteration: dᵉqaq | POS: verb | BLB Count: 10
    • Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to H1854; to crumble or (trans.) crush: break to pieces
    • BDB: Pe. Perfect 3mp דָּקוּ Dan 2:35. Haph. break in pieces: Perf 3fs הַדֶּקֶת Dan 2:34,45; 3mp הַדִּקוּ Dan 6:25; Impf 3fs תַּדִּק Dan 2:40,44; suffix תַּדֱּקִנַּהּ Dan 7:23; Ptcp active מְהַדֵּק Dan 2:40, fem מַדֱּקָה Dan 7:7,19
    • Occurrences: 13 total — all in Daniel (2:34,35,40,44,45; 6:25; 7:7,19,23)
    • Key translations: brake in pieces, breaketh in pieces, broken to pieces, break in pieces and bruise

    H2852 — חָתַךְ (chathak) — cut off / determine¶

    • Transliteration: châthak | POS: verb | BLB Count: 1 (HAPAX)
    • Definition: a primitive root; properly, to cut off, i.e. (figuratively) to decree: determine
    • Only occurrence: Dan 9:24 — "Seventy weeks are determined (נֶחְתַּךְ, Niphal Perf 3ms)"
    • CRITICAL: BDB says "properly, to cut off" then "figuratively, to decree." The root meaning supports both "determine/decree" AND "cut off from [a larger period]"

    H1396 — גָּבַר (gabar) — be strong, prevail, confirm¶

    • Transliteration: gâbar | POS: verb | BLB Count: 25
    • Definition: a primitive root; to be strong; by implication, to prevail, act insolently: exceed, confirm, be great, be mighty, prevail, put to more (strength), strengthen, be stronger, be valiant
    • Key occurrence: Dan 9:27 — וְהִגְבִּיר (Hiphil Perf 3ms) "And he shall confirm" — Hiphil = causative "make strong/prevail"
    • Other occurrences: Gen 7:18,19,20,24 (flood waters prevailed); Exo 17:11 (prevailed); Zech 10:6,12 (strengthen)
    • FUT significance: Hiphil + berith can mean "impose/enforce covenant" (political treaty), not just "confirm existing covenant"

    H2195 — זַעַם (za'am) — indignation, wrath¶

    • Transliteration: zaʻam | POS: masculine noun | BLB Count: 22
    • Definition: from H2194; strictly froth at the mouth, i.e. (figuratively) fury (especially of God's displeasure with sin): angry, indignation, rage
    • Key occurrences in Daniel: Dan 8:19 and Dan 11:36 — the "za'am bracket"
    • Other notable: Isa 10:5,25; 26:20; Zep 3:8 (eschatological wrath contexts)
    • FUT significance: The identical word in 8:19 ("last end of the indignation") and 11:36 ("till the indignation be accomplished") brackets Dan 8–11 as a unified eschatological period

    H5732 — עִדָּן (iddan) — time, year¶

    • Transliteration: ʻiddân | POS: masculine noun | BLB Count: 13
    • Definition: (Aramaic) from a root corresponding to that of H5708; a set time; technically, a year: time
    • BDB: 1. General = time, duration (Dan 2:8; 7:12). 2. Definite time = year: שִׁבְעָה עִדָּנִין = seven years (Dan 4:13,20,22,29); עִדָּן וְעִדָּנִין וּפְלַג עִדָּן = 3½ years (Dan 7:25)
    • Key occurrences: Dan 2:8,9,21; 3:5,15; 4:13,16,20,22,23,25,29,32; 7:12,25
    • FUT significance: Dan 4 establishes iddan = literal year (Nebuchadnezzar's seven years of madness), supporting literal reading of Dan 7:25 (3½ literal years)

    H8548 — תָּמִיד (tamid) — continual, daily¶

    • Transliteration: tâmîyd | POS: masculine noun | BLB Count: 104
    • Definition: from an unused root meaning to stretch; properly, continuance; used as adjective/adverb: always, continual, daily, perpetual. Elliptically: the regular (daily) sacrifice
    • Daniel occurrences: Dan 8:11,12,13; 11:31; 12:11 — "the daily [sacrifice]"
    • Broader usage: Overwhelmingly cultic/sacrificial — Exo 25:30 (showbread); 27:20 (lamp); 29:38,42 (daily offering); Num 28:3,6,10,15,23,24,31; 29:6,11,16,19,22,25,28,31,34,38
    • FUT significance: While HIST connects tamid to heavenly sanctuary ministry, FUT reads it as referring to literal temple sacrifice taken away by Antichrist

    H1860 — דְּרָאוֹן (dera'on) — contempt, abhorrence¶

    • Transliteration: dᵉrâʼôwn | POS: masculine noun | BLB Count: 2 (near-HAPAX pair)
    • Only occurrences: Dan 12:2 ("everlasting contempt") and Isa 66:24 ("an abhorring unto all flesh")
    • FUT significance: This exclusive verbal link between Dan 12:2 and Isa 66:24 connects the resurrection to final judgment, supporting eschatological reading

    H643 — אַפֶּדֶן (appeden) — palace, pavilion¶

    • Transliteration: ʼappeden | POS: masculine noun (Persian origin) | BLB Count: 1 (HAPAX)
    • Only occurrence: Dan 11:45 — "he shall plant the tabernacles of his palace"
