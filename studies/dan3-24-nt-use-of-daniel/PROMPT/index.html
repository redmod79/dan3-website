    Bible Study: NT Authors' Use of Daniel -- Do They Treat Daniel 7-12 as a Unified Prophetic Corpus?¶

    Question¶

    How do NT authors use Daniel, and do they treat Daniel 7-12 as a unified prophetic corpus?

    Prior Research Summary¶

    Eight prior studies provide substantial groundwork:

    1. nt-ties-daniel-7-12-together -- Established that Jesus' Olivet Discourse weaves Dan 8-9 (abomination), Dan 12 (tribulation), Dan 7 (Son of Man) into one speech; Mark 13:14 hestekota (masculine) for neuter bdelygma = constructio ad sensum; Paul fuses Dan 7:25 + 8:11 + 11:36 into one figure; Rev 13:2 reverses all four Dan 7 beasts; time formula traverses three languages; sealed-to-unsealed arc (Dan 12:4 to Rev 22:10); "son of perdition" chains across three authors; Dan 7:13 is top OT match for at least nine NT passages.

    2. 2-thessalonians-2-man-of-sin -- Established naos tou theou = church in every Pauline usage; apostasia (G646) = religious defection in both NT uses and LXX; anomia (G458) definitionally = law-transgression (1 John 3:4); "mystery of iniquity already work" parallels 1 John 4:3 and 2:18 (two-author attestation); man of sin = long-enduring entity.

    3. hist-07-nt-connects-daniel-7-12 -- Established Rev 1:1 "ha dei genesthai" from LXX Dan 2:28 (Rev 22:6 repeats = inclusio); eremosis (G2050) exclusively in three Synoptic Olivet passages; "ten horns = ten kings" verbatim Dan 7:24/Rev 17:12 (parallel score 0.500); composite beast = Revelation treats Dan 7 as complete system; Paul presupposes Dan 7+8+11 describe same power. Tally: 37 E, 6 N, 7 I items.

    4. abomination-of-desolation-grammar -- shiqquts (H8251) exclusively idolatrous in all 28 OT uses; meshomem (Piel participle) is causative = "the one causing desolation"; Hebrew phrase varies across Dan 8:13, 9:27, 11:31, 12:11 but Jesus treats all as one concept.

    5. revs-46-man-of-lawlessness-beast -- Seven-point functional parallel between Paul's man of sin and Revelation's beast; Daniel 7 is demonstrable common source; Revelation distributes across two beasts what Paul presents as one figure.

    6. revs-45-olivet-discourse-seals -- Seven-element sequential correspondence between Olivet Discourse and Revelation's seal sequence.

    7. hist-13-olivet-discourse-spans-history -- Olivet Discourse contains duration markers spanning apostolic era to Second Coming.

    8. Three prior COMPARE studies (dan3-14, dan3-18, dan3-22) established accumulated position verdicts on Dan 8, 8-9, and 10-12.

    External corpus leads (EGW, Bohr, pioneer writers): EGW claims apostasy manifested in apostolic days developing into man of sin; Litch identifies Daniel's little horn as identical to Paul's man of sin; Guinness argues Daniel and Revelation are "two parts of a single prophecy"; Froom identifies Rev 13 powers as identical to Dan 7; Bohr argues Rev 13 beast occupies same sequential position as Dan 7 little horn (reverse-order observation); Bohr connects anomia across 2 Thess 2:7 and 1 John 3:4.

    Focus Areas¶

    1. Jesus' Olivet Discourse as a Daniel Synthesis (Matt 24; Mark 13; Luke 21): WHAT -- Map which Daniel chapters Jesus draws from in a single discourse, and whether he distinguishes between them or treats them as unified. WHY -- Tool discoveries confirm that eremosis (G2050) appears exclusively in three Synoptic Olivet passages (Matt 24:15; Mark 13:14; Luke 21:20), functioning as a lexical "Daniel marker."
