    Raw Hebrew Parsing Data¶

    Dan 10:1¶

    • biyn (H995) Qal Perf 3ms = "understood" + binah (noun) = "understanding"
    • mar'eh (H4758) = "sight/vision" — same term as Dan 8:26-27 linking this vision back to Dan 8
    • davar (H1697) = "word/matter" revealed, not chazon (vision)

    Dan 10:13¶

    • Michael echad ha-sarim ha-rishonim: echad (H259) Noun.s.Cst + ha-sarim (H8269) Noun.mp.Abs + ha-rishonim (H7223) Adj.mp.Abs
    • Partitive: "one of the chief/first princes" OR superlative: "the first of the chief princes"
    • amad (H5975) Qal Ptcp = "standing" (opposing the speaker)

    Dan 10:14¶

    • acharit ha-yamim: acharit (H319) Noun.fs.Cst + ha-yamim = "the latter days"
    • lehivin-kha: biyn Hiphil InfCon = "to cause you to understand" (biyn chain)
    • chazon (H2377) = "vision" + la-yamim = "for many days"

    Dan 11:16¶

    • kir'tsono: ki (prep "as") + ratson (H7522) Noun.ms.Abs + 3ms suffix = "according to his pleasure/will"
    • ha-ba (H935) Qal Ptcp = "the one coming" (against him)
    • ya'amod (H5975) Qal Impf 3ms = "shall stand" in erets ha-tsevi = "the glorious land"
    • kalah (H3617) Noun.fs = "destruction" in his hand

    Dan 11:22¶

    • zero'ot ha-sheteph: zero'ot (H2220) Noun.fp.Cst = "arms of" + sheteph (H7858) Noun.ms = "the flood"
    • yishshatephu (H7857) Niphal Impf 3mp = "shall be overflowed"
    • yishshaberu (H7665) Niphal Impf 3mp = "shall be broken"
    • negid (H5057) Noun.ms.Cst = "prince/ruler of" + berith (H1285) Noun.fs = "covenant"

    Dan 11:31¶

    • zero'im (H2220) = "arms" + mimmennu = "from him"
    • ya'amodu (H5975) Qal Impf 3mp = "shall stand"
    • chillelu (H2490) Piel Perf 3p = "profaned/defiled" + ha-miqdash (H4720) = "the sanctuary" + ha-ma'oz (H4581) = "the fortress/stronghold"
    • hesiru (H5493) Hiphil Perf 3p = "removed/took away" + ha-tamid (H8548) = "the continual/daily"
    • natenu (H5414) Qal Perf 3p = "placed/set up" + ha-shiqquts (H8251) = "the abomination" + meshomem (H8074) Piel Ptcp = "that makes desolate"

    Dan 11:33¶

    • maskiley (H7919) Hiphil Ptcp mp Cst = "the wise ones of / those who cause to understand"
    • yavinu (H995) Hiphil Impf 3mp = "shall cause to understand" (biyn chain continues)
    • la-rabbim = "the many"

    Dan 11:35¶

    • ha-maskilim (H7919) Hiphil Ptcp mp = "the wise/understanding ones"
    • yikkashelu (H3782) Niphal Impf 3mp = "shall stumble/fall"
    • li-tseroph (H6884) Qal InfCon = "to refine/smelt" — purification triad member 1
    • u-le-barer (H1305) Piel InfCon = "and to purify/clarify" — purification triad member 2
    • ve-la-lebben (H3835) Hiphil InfCon = "and to make white" — purification triad member 3
    • ad eth qets = "until the time of the end"
    • ki od la-mo'ed = "for yet for the appointed time"

    Dan 11:36¶

    • kir'tsono (H7522) = "according to his will" — 4th occurrence in chain (8:4, 11:3, 11:16, 11:36)
    • ha-melekh (H4428) = "THE king" — definite article, anaphoric
    • yitromem (H7311) Hithpael Impf 3ms = "shall exalt himself" (reflexive)
    • yitgaddel (H1431) Hithpael Impf 3ms = "shall magnify himself" — gadal stem progression: Qal (8:4,8) -> Hiphil (8:9,25) -> Hithpael (11:36,37)
    • al kol el = "above every god"
    • al el elim = "against the God of gods"
    • yedabber (H1696) Piel Impf 3ms = "shall speak" + nipla'ot (H6381) Niphal Ptcp fp = "wonderful/marvelous things"
    • hitsliyach (H6743) Hiphil Perf 3ms = "shall prosper"
    • ad kalah (H3615) za'am (H2195) = "until the indignation is accomplished" — za'am bracket (8:19 + 11:36)
    • necheratsah (H2782) Niphal Ptcp fs = "that which is determined" — links to Dan 9:26,27
    • ne'esatah (H6213) Niphal Perf 3fs = "shall be done"

    Dan 11:37¶

    • elohey avotayv = "the god(s) of his fathers"
    • lo yavin (H995) Qal Impf 3ms = "shall not regard" (lit. "understand/discern")
    • chemdat (H2532) nashim = "the desire of women"
    • al kol eloah = "upon any god"
    • yitgaddal (H1431) Hithpael Impf 3ms = "shall magnify himself" — 2nd Hithpael of gadal

    Dan 11:40¶

    • u-be-eth qets: be + eth (H6256) Noun.s.Cst + qets (H7093) = "at the time of the end" — eth qets chain (8:17, 11:35, 11:40, 12:4, 12:9)
    • yitnagach (H5055) Hithpael Impf 3ms = "shall push/gore at him" — melekh ha-negev = king of the south
    • yista'er (H8175) Hithpael Impf 3ms = "shall storm/whirl against him" — melekh ha-tsaphon = king of the north
    • be-rekhev u-ve-pharashim u-ba-oniyyot rabboth = "with chariots, horsemen, and many ships"
    • u-va va-aratsot = "and he shall enter into the countries"
    • ve-shataf ve-avar = "and shall overflow and pass over"

    Dan 11:44¶

    • shemu'ot (H8052) Noun.fp = "reports/tidings" — feminine plural of shemu'ah
    • yevahalhuhu (H926) Piel Impf 3mp + 3ms suffix = "shall trouble him"
    • mi-mizrach u-mi-tsaphon = "from the east and from the north"
    • be-chemah gedolah = "with great fury"
    • le-hashmid (H8045) Hiphil InfCon = "to destroy"
    • u-le-hacharim (H2763) Hiphil InfCon = "and to utterly destroy/devote to destruction"

    Dan 11:45¶

    • ve-yitta (H5193) Qal Impf 3ms = "and he shall plant"
    • ohaley (H168) appadno (H643): "tents of his palace/pavilion" — appeden is HAPAX LEGOMENON (only here in OT)
    • ben yammim = "between the seas"
    • le-har tsevi qodesh = "toward the glorious holy mountain"
    • u-va ad qitso = "and he shall come to his end"
    • ve-en ozer lo = "and none shall help him"

    Dan 12:1¶

    • u-ba-eth ha-hi = "and at that time" — temporal connector linking to 11:45
    • ya'amod (H5975) Qal Impf 3ms = "shall stand up" — Michael
    • Miyka'el ha-sar ha-gadol = "Michael the great prince"
    • ha-omed al beney ammekha = "who stands for the children of your people" — Qal Ptcp
    • ve-hayetah eth tsarah = "and there shall be a time of trouble"
    • asher lo nihyetah = "such as never was" (Niphal Perf)
    • me-heyot goy = "since there was a nation"
    • yimmalet ammekha = "your people shall be delivered" (Niphal Impf)
    • kol ha-nimtsa kathuv ba-sepher = "everyone found written in the book"

    Dan 12:2¶

    • ve-rabbim = "and many" (partitive debate: "many of" vs. "the multitude of")
    • mi-yesheney admat aphar = "from those sleeping in the dust of the ground"
    • yaqitsu (H6974) Hiphil Impf 3mp = "shall awake"
    • elleh le-chayyey olam = "some to everlasting life"
    • ve-elleh la-charafot = "and some to shame"
    • le-dir'on (H1860) olam = "to everlasting abhorrence/contempt" — HAPAX PAIR with Isa 66:24

    Dan 12:3¶

    • ve-ha-maskilim (H7919) Hiphil Ptcp mp = "and the wise" (maskilim chain)
    • yazhiru (H2094) Hiphil Impf 3mp = "shall shine"
    • ke-zohar ha-raqia = "as the brightness of the firmament"
