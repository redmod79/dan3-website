    Raw Strong's Lookups Output¶

    H2852 — chathak (cut off/determine)¶

    Transliteration: châthak Part of Speech: verb (v) BLB Count: 1 Definition: a primitive root; properly, to cut off, i.e. (figuratively) to decree:—determine. Single Occurrence: Dan 9:24 — "Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people"

    H2782 — charats (determine/decide)¶

    Transliteration: chârats Part of Speech: verb (v) BLB Count: 12 Definition: a primitive root; properly, to point sharply, i.e. (literally) to wound; figuratively, to be alert, to decide:—bestir self, decide, decree, determine, maim, move. Verses with "determined": Job 14:5 Daniel occurrences: Dan 9:26 ("desolations are determined"), Dan 9:27 ("that determined shall be poured"), Dan 11:36 ("that which is determined shall be done")

    H1396 — gabar (prevail/strengthen)¶

    Transliteration: gâbar Part of Speech: verb (v) BLB Count: 25 Definition: a primitive root; to be strong; by implication, to prevail, act insolently:—exceed, confirm, be great, be mighty, prevail, put to more (strength), strengthen, be stronger, be valiant. Verses with "prevail": Gen 7:20 (3 times), 1Sa 2:9, Psa 65:3 Key verse: Dan 9:27 — "he shall confirm [gabar] the covenant with many for one week"

    H1285 — beriyth (covenant)¶

    Transliteration: bᵉrîyth Part of Speech: feminine noun (n-f) BLB Count: 284 Definition: from H1262 (in the sense of cutting); a compact (because made by passing between pieces of flesh):—confederacy, (con-)feder(-ate), covenant, league. Key collocations: karath berith (cut covenant), gabar berith (make prevail/confirm covenant in Dan 9:27) Translations: 264x "covenant", plus "league," "confederacy," "confederate"

    H3772 — karath (cut off/make covenant)¶

    Transliteration: kârath Part of Speech: verb (v) BLB Count: 288 Definition: a primitive root; to cut (off, down or asunder); by implication, to destroy or consume; specifically, to covenant (i.e. make an alliance or bargain, originally by cutting flesh and passing between the pieces):—be chewed, be con-(feder-)ate, covenant, cut (down, off), destroy, fail, felling, be freed, hew (down), make a league ((covenant)), X lose, perish, X utterly, X want. Verses with "cut off": Deu 23:1, 1Sa 2:33, 1Sa 5:4, 1Sa 20:15, 1Ki 18:4, Psa 109:13, Joe 1:16, Nah 1:15 Key distinction from chathak: karath is the standard covenant-cutting verb; chathak is the hapax in Dan 9:24

    H4899 — mashiyach (anointed/Messiah)¶

    Transliteration: mâshîyach Part of Speech: adjective (adj) BLB Count: 39 Definition: from H4886; anointed; usually a consecrated person (as a king, priest, or saint); specifically, the Messiah:—anointed, Messiah. Translations: 21x "anointed" (57.9%), 4x "his anointed" (10.5%), 2x "the anointed" (5.3%), 2x "thine anointed" (5.3%), 2x "mine anointed" (5.3%), plus "Messiah" (only Dan 9:25-26), "anointed one" Semantic range: Applied to kings (1Sa 2:10,35; 24:6; 2Sa 1:14), priests (Lev 4:3,5,16), patriarchs (Psa 105:15), Cyrus (Isa 45:1), Messiah (Dan 9:25,26)

    H5057 — nagiyd (prince/ruler)¶

    Transliteration: nâgîyd Part of Speech: masculine noun (n-m) BLB Count: 44 Definition: from H5046; a commander (as occupying the front), civil, military or religious; generally (abstractly, plural), honorable themes:—captain, chief, excellent thing, (chief) governor, leader, noble, prince, (chief) ruler. Translations: 18x "ruler", 10x "prince", 5x "captain", 4x "leader", 3x "chief" Key construct chains: mashiach nagiyd (Dan 9:25) vs nagiyd habba (Dan 9:26)

    H6663 — tsadaq (be right/vindicated)¶

    Transliteration: tsâdaq Part of Speech: verb (v) BLB Count: 41 Definition: a primitive root; to be (causatively, make) right (in a moral or forensic sense):—cleanse, clear self, (be, do) just(-ice, -ify, -ify self), (be turn to) righteous(-ness). Verses with "righteous": Gen 38:26, Psa 19:9 Key verse: Dan 8:14 — nitsdaq (Niphal Perfect 3ms) — "then shall the sanctuary be cleansed [vindicated]" LXX mapping: - G1344 dikaioo (count 21, PMI 8.73) — PRIMARY mapping: "to render just, justify" - G1342 dikaios (count 22, PMI 5.47) — "equitable, just" - G273 amemptos (count 4) — "blameless" - G2919 krino (count 8) — "to judge, decide" NOTE: dikaioo (forensic justification) is the dominant LXX rendering, NOT katharizo (ritual cleansing). KJV "cleansed" follows Theodotion, not the Hebrew or Old Greek.

    H6664 — tsedeq (righteousness)¶

    Transliteration: tsedeq Part of Speech: masculine noun (n-m) BLB Count: 116 Definition: from H6663; the right (natural, moral or legal); also (abstractly) equity or (figuratively) prosperity:—even, (that which is altogether) just(-ice), (un-)right(-eous)(cause, -ly, -ness). Translations: 27x "righteousness", 10x "in righteousness", 9x "of righteousness", 7x "just", 5x "with righteousness" Verses with "righteousness": 27 occurrences including Psa 45:7, Psa 85:10, Psa 97:2, Isa 11:5, Isa 45:8, Isa 45:19, Dan 9:24, Hos 10:12, Zep 2:3 Key verse: Dan 9:24 — le-havi tsedeq olamim ("to bring in everlasting righteousness")

    H4758 — mar'eh (appearance/vision)¶

    Transliteration: mar'eh Part of Speech: masculine noun (n-m) BLB Count: 103 Definition: from H7200; a view (the act of seeing); also an appearance (the thing seen), whether (real) a shape (especially if handsome, comeliness; often plural the looks), or (mental) a vision:—X apparently, appearance(-reth), X as soon as beautiful(-ly), countenance, fair, favoured, form, goodly, to look (up) on (to), look(-eth), pattern, to see, seem, sight, visage, vision. Translations: 4x "the vision" (Eze 11:24, Dan 8:16, Dan 8:27, Dan 9:23), 2x "countenance" (1Sa 17:42, 2Sa 14:27) Daniel distribution: Dan 8:16 ("make this man to understand the vision [mar'eh]"), Dan 8:26 ("the vision [mar'eh] of the evening and the morning"), Dan 8:27 ("I was astonished at the vision [mar'eh]"), Dan 9:23 ("consider the vision [mar'eh]"), Dan 10:1 ("had understanding of the vision [mar'eh]") Key finding: mar'eh consistently refers to the specific 2300 erev-boqer revelation and the 70-weeks explanation — NOT the broader ram-goat vision.

    H2377 — chazon (vision/revelation)¶

    Transliteration: châzôwn Part of Speech: masculine noun (n-m) BLB Count: 35 Definition: from H2372; a sight (mentally), i.e. a dream, revelation, or oracle:—vision. Translations: 9x "vision", 10x "the vision" Verses with "the vision": Eze 7:13, Dan 8:13, Dan 8:15, Dan 8:17, Dan 8:26, Dan 9:24, Dan 10:14, Dan 11:14, Hab 2:2, Hab 2:3 Daniel distribution: Dan 8:1 ("a vision [chazon] appeared unto me"), Dan 8:2 ("I saw in a vision [chazon]"), Dan 8:13 ("How long shall be the vision [chazon]"), Dan 8:15 ("had seen the vision [chazon]"), Dan 8:17 ("at the time of the end shall be the vision [chazon]"), Dan 8:26 ("shut thou up the vision [chazon]"), Dan 9:24 ("seal up the vision [chazon] and prophecy"), Dan 10:14 ("the vision [chazon] is for many days") Key finding: chazon refers to the broader symbolic vision (ram, goat, horn sequence) while mar'eh refers to the specific auditory revelation (2300 erev-boqer, 70 weeks).

    H995 — biyn (understand/consider)¶

    Transliteration: bîyn Part of Speech: verb (v) BLB Count: 170 Definition: a primitive root; to separate mentally (or distinguish), i.e.(generally) understand:—attend, consider, be cunning, diligently, direct, discern, eloquent, feel, inform, instruct, have intelligence, know, look well to, mark, perceive, be prudent, regard, (can) skill(-ful), teach, think, (cause, make to, get, give, have) understand(-ing), view, (deal) wise(-ly, man). Verses with "understand": 13 occurrences including Isa 6:9, Isa 6:10, Isa 28:19, Dan 10:11, Hos 4:14 Verses with "understood": Isa 40:21, Isa 44:18, Dan 8:27, Dan 9:2 Five-stage biyn chain in Daniel 8-10: 1.
