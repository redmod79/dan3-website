    Raw Greek Parsing Output¶

    Matthew 24:15 -- bdelygma eremoseos¶

    Greek: Ὅταν οὖν ἴδητε τὸ βδέλυγμα τῆς ἐρημώσεως τὸ ῥηθὲν διὰ Δανιὴλ τοῦ προφήτου ἑστὸς ἐν τόπῳ ἁγίῳ, ὁ ἀναγινώσκων νοείτω,

Key parsing:
- βδέλυγμα (bdelygma) G946 = [N-ASN] Accusative Singular Neuter "abomination"
- ἐρημώσεως (eremoseos) G2050 = [N-GSF] Genitive Singular Feminine "of desolation"
- τὸ ῥηθὲν (to rhethen) = [T-ASN + V-APP-ASN] "the thing spoken" -- Aorist Passive Participle
- διὰ Δανιὴλ τοῦ προ��ήτου = "through Daniel the prophet" -- Jesus explicitly attributes to Daniel
- ἑστὸς (hestos) = [V-2RAP-ASN] Perfect Active Participle Accusative Singular NEUTER
- ἐν τόπῳ ἁγίῳ = "in a holy place"
- ὁ ἀναγινώσκων νοείτω = "the one reading, let him understand" -- Present Active Imperative
    NOTE: In Matthew, hestos is NEUTER (matching bdelygma). Jesus treats Daniel as prophecy still future ("when you shall SEE"). The parenthetical "whoso readeth, let him understand" signals the reader should study Daniel to understand.

    Mark 13:14 -- hestekota (masculine participle for neuter noun)¶

    Greek: Ὅταν δὲ ἴδητε τὸ βδέλυγμα τῆς ἐρημώσεως ἑστηκότα ὅπου οὐ δεῖ, ὁ ἀναγινώσκων νοείτω,

Key parsing:
- βδέλυγμα (bdelygma) G946 = [N-ASN] Accusative Singular NEUTER "abomination"
- ἑστηκότα (hestekota) = [V-RAP-ASM] Perfect Active Participle Accusative Singular MASCULINE
- ὅπου οὐ δεῖ = "where it ought not" -- standing where it should not be
    NOTE: The critical grammatical anomaly -- bdelygma is NEUTER but hestekota is MASCULINE. This constructio ad sensum (agreement by sense rather than grammar) indicates the "abomination" is a PERSON (hence masculine), not merely a thing. Mark deliberately uses the masculine form to signal a personal power behind the abomination. This supports the HIST reading of the horn as a personal/institutional entity.

    2 Thessalonians 2:3 -- ho anthropos tes anomias, ho huios tes apoleias¶

    Greek: μή τις ὑμᾶς ἐξαπατήσῃ κατὰ μηδένα τρόπον· ὅτι ἐὰν μὴ ἔλθῃ ἡ ἀποστασία πρῶτον καὶ ἀποκαλυφθῇ ὁ ἄνθρωπος τῆς ἀνομίας, ὁ υἱὸς τῆς ἀπωλείας,

Key parsing:
- ἀποστασία (apostasia) G646 = [N-NSF] "the falling away/apostasy" -- with definite article = THE apostasy
- πρῶτον (proton) = "first" -- apostasy must come BEFORE the Day of Christ
- ἀποκαλυφθῇ (apokalyphthe) = [V-APS-3S] Aorist Passive Subjunctive "be revealed" -- passive = God reveals
- ἄνθρωπος τῆς ἀνομίας = "the man of lawlessness" -- genitive of quality/characteristic
- ἀνομίας (anomias) G458 = [N-GSF] "of lawlessness" -- anomia = violation of law
- υἱὸς τῆς ἀπωλείας = "the son of perdition" -- same title as Judas (John 17:12)
- ἀπωλείας (apoleias) G684 = [N-GSF] "of destruction/perdition"
    NOTE: "man of lawlessness" echoes Dan 7:25 "think to change times and LAW" -- the connection between anomia and dat/torah. The title "son of perdition" links to John 17:12 (Judas) and Rev 17:8,11 (the beast) -- three authors, one title for the betrayer-from-within.

    2 Thessalonians 2:4 -- self-exaltation, sitting in temple¶

    Greek: ὁ ἀντικείμενος καὶ ὑπεραιρόμενος ἐπὶ πάντα λεγόμενον Θεὸν ἢ σέβασμα, ὥστε αὐτὸν εἰς τὸν ναὸν τοῦ Θεοῦ καθίσαι, ἀποδεικνύντα ἑαυτὸν ὅτι ἔστιν Θεός.

Key parsing:
- ἀντικείμενος (antikemenos) G480 = [V-PNP-NSM] "the one opposing" -- Present Middle/Passive Participle
- ὑπεραιρόμενος (hyperairomenos) G5229 = [V-PPP-NSM] "the one exalting himself above" -- Present Passive Ptcp
- ἐπὶ πάντα λεγόμενον Θεὸν = "above every so-called god" -- above ALL
- σέβασμα (sebasma) G4574 = "object of worship"
- ναὸν (naon) G3485 = "temple/inner shrine" -- NOT hieron; naos = inner sacred space
- καθίσαι (kathisai) = [V-AAN] Aorist Active Infinitive "to sit" -- takes his seat
- ἀποδεικνύντα (apodeiknynta) G584 = [V-PAP-ASM] "displaying/demonstrating"
- ἑαυτὸν ὅτι ἔστιν Θεός = "himself that he is God"
    NOTE: Seven structural parallels with Dan 7: 1. Speaking against the Most High (Dan 7:25) = exalts above every god (2 Thess 2:4) 2. Wearing out saints (Dan 7:25) = opposes (2 Thess 2:4) 3. Changing times and law (Dan 7:25) = man of lawlessness (2 Thess 2:3) 4. Revealed/arises (Dan 7:8) = revealed (2 Thess 2:3,8) 5. Until judgment (Dan 7:26) = consumed by brightness of His coming (2 Thess 2:8) 6. From within God's people (implied) = sits in temple of God (2 Thess 2:4) 7. Religious authority (Dan 7:25 dat) = shows himself as God (2 Thess 2:4)

    2 Thessalonians 2:7 -- mystery of iniquity ALREADY working¶

    Greek: τὸ γὰρ μυστήριον ἤδη ἐνεργεῖται τῆς ἀνομίας· μόνον ὁ κατέχων ἄρτι ἕως ἐκ μέσου γένηται.

Key parsing:
- μυστήριον (mysterion) G3466 = [N-NSN] "mystery"
- ἤδη (ede) G2235 = [ADV] "already" -- present tense, c. AD 51
- ἐνεργεῖται (energeitai) G1754 = [V-PMI-3S] Present Middle Indicative "is working"
- τῆς ἀνομίας (tes anomias) = "of lawlessness"
- κατέχων (katechon) G2722 = [V-PAP-NSM] Present Active Participle "the one restraining"
- ἄρτι (arti) G737 = [ADV] "now/at this moment"
- ἕως ἐκ μέσου γένηται = "until he be taken out of the way"
    NOTE: "ALREADY working" (ede energeitai) in AD 51 rules out: - A purely future individual (FUT) -- it was already active - A purely past figure ending at AD 70 (PRET) -- Paul says it continues until Christ's coming - Only a PROCESS power developing over centuries fits: already active in Paul's day, growing, to be fully revealed, finally destroyed at the Second Coming.

    Revelation 13:5 -- stoma laloun megala (mouth speaking great things)¶

    Greek: καὶ ἐδόθη αὐτῷ στόμα λαλοῦν μεγάλα καὶ βλασφημίας, καὶ ἐδόθη αὐτῷ ἐξουσία ποιῆ��αι μῆνας τεσσεράκοντα δύο.

Key parsing:
- ἐδόθη (edothe) G1325 = [V-API-3S] Aorist Passive Indicative "was given" -- divine passive
- στόμα (stoma) G4750 = [N-NSN] "mouth"
- λαλοῦν (laloun) G2980 = [V-PAP-NSN] Present Active Participle "speaking"
- μεγάλα (megala) G3173 = [A-APN] "great things"
- βλασφημίας (blasphemias) G988 = [N-APF] "blasphemies"
- ἐξουσία (exousia) G1849 = [N-NSF] "authority/power"
- μῆνας τεσσεράκοντα δύο = "forty-two months"
    NOTE: "stoma laloun megala" is verbatim from Dan 7:8,20 LXX -- "a mouth speaking great things." This is a direct literary quotation. The 42 months (= 1260 days = time/times/half time) connects to Dan 7:25 and Dan 12:7. Rev 13:5-7 reproduces Dan 7:25 point by point: - mouth speaking great things = Dan 7:8 (stoma laloun megala) - blasphemy against God and His tabernacle = Dan 7:25 "words against the Most High" - war with saints and overcome them = Dan 7:21 "made war with saints and prevailed" - 42 months = time/times/half time of Dan 7:25

    Revelation 13:6 -- three-to-three blasphemy correspondence¶

    Greek: καὶ ἤνοιξεν τὸ στόμα αὐτοῦ εἰς βλασφημίας πρὸς τὸν Θεόν, βλασφημῆσαι τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ καὶ τὴν σκηνὴν αὐτοῦ, τοὺς ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ σκηνοῦντας.

Key parsing:
- βλασφημῆσαι (blasphemesai) G987 = [V-AAN] Aorist Active Infinitive "to blaspheme"
- Three objects of blasphemy:
  1. τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ = "His name" (God's identity/authority)
  2. τὴν σκηνὴν αὐτοῦ = "His tabernacle" (God's dwelling/sanctuary)
  3. τοὺς ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ σκηνοῦντας = "those who dwell in heaven" (God's people/angels)
- σκηνὴν (skenen) G4633 = "tent/tabernacle" -- heavenly sanctuary
- σκηνοῦντας (skenountas) G4637 = [V-PAP-APM] "those tabernacling/dwelling"
    NOTE: Three-to-three correspondence with Dan 7:25: Dan 7:25a: "speak against the Most High" = Rev 13:6a "blaspheme His name" Dan 7:25b: "wear out the saints" = Rev 13:6c "blaspheme those dwelling in heaven" Dan 7:25c: "think to change times and law" = Rev 13:6b "blaspheme His tabernacle" (where the law is kept)

    Revelation 14:7 -- hora tes kriseos (hour of judgment) + creation language¶

    Greek: λέγων ἐν φωνῇ μεγάλῃ· Φοβήθητε τὸν Θεὸν καὶ δότε αὐτῷ δόξαν, ὅτι ἦλθεν ἡ ὥρα τῆς κρίσεως αὐτοῦ, καὶ προσκυνήσατε τῷ ποιήσαντι τὸν οὐρα��ὸν καὶ τὴν γῆν καὶ θάλασσαν καὶ πηγὰς ὑδάτων.

Key parsing:
- ἦλθεν (elthen) G2064 = [V-2AAI-3S] Aorist Active Indicative "came/has come"
- ὥρα (hora) G5610 = [N-NSF] "hour"
- κρίσε��ς (kriseos) G2920 = [N-GSF] "of judgment"
- προσκυνήσατε (proskynesate) G4352 = [V-AAM-2P] Aorist Active Imperative "worship!"
- τῷ ποιήσαντι (to poiesanti) G4160 = [V-AAP-DSM] Aorist Active Participle Dative "the One who made"
- τὸν οὐρανὸν καὶ τὴν γῆν καὶ θάλασσαν καὶ πηγὰς ὑδάτων = "heaven and earth and sea and fountains of waters"
    NOTE: This verse UNIQUELY converges two themes: 1. JUDGMENT: "the hour of His krisis" -- krisis traces via LXX to Dan 7:10 (dina = krisis). This bridges from Dan 7 judgment scene to Dan 8:14 sanctuary vindication. 2. CREATION/SABBATH: "worship Him who made heaven and earth and the sea" -- verbatim echo of Exo 20:11 (Fourth Commandment). This connects judgment with Sabbath/creation. The convergence: Rev 14:7 calls for worship of the Creator (Exo 20 language) because the hour of judgment (Dan 7/8 language) has come. This is the HIST identification verse: the judgment of Dan 8:14 triggers a Sabbath-creation call to worship.

    Revelation 1:1 -- esemainen (signified) + ha dei genesthai¶

    Greek: Ἀποκάλυψις Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ, ἣν ἔδωκεν αὐτῷ ὁ Θεός, δεῖξαι τοῖς δούλοις αὐτοῦ ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι ἐν τάχει, καὶ ἐσήμανεν ἀποστείλας διὰ τοῦ ἀγγέλου αὐτοῦ, τῷ δούλῳ αὐτοῦ Ἰωάνῃ,

Key parsing:
- Ἀποκάλυψις (apokalypsis) G602 = [N-NSF] "revelation/unveiling"
- ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι (ha dei genesthai) = "things which must come to pass" -- δεῖ [V-PAI-3S] "it is necessary" + γενέσθαι [V-2ADN] Aor Mid Inf "to happen"
- ἐν τάχει (en tachei) = "in/with speed" -- NOT "immediately" but "when they begin, they come quickly"
- ἐσήμανεν (esemainen) G4591 = [V-AAI-3S] Aorist Active Indicative "He signified/communicated through signs"
- ἀποστείλας (aposteilas) G649 = [V-AAP-NSM] Aorist Active Participle "having sent"
    NOTE: (1) ha dei genesthai is VERBATIM from Dan 2:28-29,45 LXX ("the things that must come to pass"). In Daniel 2, this content spans Nebuchadnezzar's day to the end of time. John deliberately frames Revelation with Daniel's trans-historical scope. (2) esemainen = "communicated through signs/symbols" -- every NT occurrence of semaino involves indirect sign-mediated communication (John 12:33; 18:32; 21:19; Acts 11:28; 25:27). This establishes from the opening verse that Revelation's beasts, horns, time periods, etc. are symbolic codes. (3) en tachei cannot restrict Revelation to first-century events given the Danielic framing.

    Revelation 12:5 -- eteken, herpasthe (aorist past-tense argument)¶

    Greek: καὶ ἔτεκεν υἱόν, ἄρσεν, ὃς μέλλει ποιμαίνειν πάντα τὰ ἔθνη ἐν ῥάβδῳ σιδηρᾷ· καὶ ἡρπάσθη τὸ τέκνον αὐτῆς πρὸς τὸν Θεὸν καὶ πρὸς τὸν θρόνον αὐτοῦ.

Key parsing:
- ἔτεκεν (eteken) G5088 = [V-2AAI-3S] Aorist Active Indicative "she brought forth" -- PAST completed
- ἄρσεν (arsen) G730 = [A-ASN] "male" -- NEUTER agreeing with teknon
- ὃς (hos) G3739 = [R-NSM] Relative Pronoun MASCULINE -- agreement by sense with "son/child"
- μέλλει ποιμαίνειν (mellei poimainein) = "is about to rule/shepherd" -- future from the vision's perspective
- ῥάβδῳ σιδηρᾷ (rhabdo sidera) = "with a rod of iron" -- echoes Ps 2:9 and Rev 2:27; 19:15
- ἡρπάσθη (herpasthe) G726 = [V-API-3S] Aorist Passive Indicative "was caught up/snatched away" -- PAST completed
- τέκνον (teknon) G5043 = [N-NSN] "child" -- neuter
    NOTE: TWO aorist indicative verbs describe COMPLETED PAST events: Christ's birth (eteken) and ascension (herpasthe). If Rev 4-22 were entirely future (as FUT requires), these past events should not appear within the prophetic narrative. The rod-of-iron allusion to Ps 2:9 identifies the child as Christ. The passive herpasthe ("was snatched away") = divine passive, indicating God's protective intervention at the ascension.

    Revelation 13:11 -- arnion (lamb-like beast)¶

    Greek: Καὶ εἶδον ἄλλο θηρίον ἀναβαῖνον ἐκ τῆς γῆς, καὶ εἶχεν κέρατα δύο ὅμοια ἀρνίῳ, καὶ ἐλάλει ὡς δράκων.

Key parsing:
- ἄλλο (allo) G243 = [A-ASN] "another (of the same kind)" -- another beast
- ἀναβαῖνον (anabainon) G305 = [V-PAP-ASN] Present Active Participle "coming up/rising"
- ἐκ τῆς γῆς (ek tes ges) = "out of the earth" -- NOT from the sea (Rev 13:1; 17:15 sea = peoples)
- κέρατα δύο (kerata duo) = "two horns"
- ὅμοια ἀρνίῳ (homoia arnio) G3664 + G721 = "like a lamb" -- arnion = Christological diminutive
- ἐλάλει (elalei) G2980 = [V-IAI-3S] Imperfect Active Indicative "was speaking" -- ongoing speech
- ὡς δράκων (hos drakon) = "as a dragon" -- dragon-like speech
    NOTE: arnion (G721) appears 30x in Revelation -- 29x for Christ, 1x here for the beast's imitation. The lamb-like horns WITHOUT crowns (contrast 12:3; 13:1 which have crowns) suggest principles rather than monarchies. Rising from "earth" (ge) vs "sea" = sparsely populated territory. The imperfect elalei ("was speaking") = ongoing/developing speech -- initially lamb-like, progressively dragon-like.

    Revelation 11:19 -- naos, kibotos, theophany¶

    Greek: καὶ ἠνοίγη ὁ ναὸς τοῦ Θεοῦ ὁ ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ, καὶ ὤφθη ἡ κιβωτὸς τῆς διαθήκης αὐτοῦ ἐν τῷ ναῷ αὐτοῦ, καὶ ἐγένοντο ἀστραπαὶ καὶ φωναὶ καὶ βρονταὶ καὶ σεισμὸς καὶ χάλαζα μεγάλη.

Key parsing:
- ἠνοίγη (enoige) G455 = [V-2API-3S] Aorist Passive Indicative "was opened"
- ναὸς (naos) G3485 = [N-NSM] "temple/inner shrine" -- NOT hieron (temple precincts)
- ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ (en to ourano) = "in heaven" -- the HEAVENLY temple
- ὤφθη (ophthe) G3708 = [V-API-3S] Aorist Passive Indicative "was seen/appeared"
- κιβωτὸς (kibotos) G2787 = [N-NSF] "ark/chest" -- THE ark of the covenant
- διαθήκης (diathekes) G1242 = [N-GSF] "of the covenant/testament"
- ἀστραπαί, φωναί, βρονταί, σεισμός, χάλαζα = lightnings, voices, thunders, earthquake, hail
    NOTE: The ark of the covenant -- the repository of the Ten Commandments -- is seen in the heavenly temple. Revelation uses naos (inner shrine) 16x and NEVER hieron. This is the Most Holy Place of the heavenly sanctuary. The theophany formula (lightnings + voices + thunders + earthquake + hail) at Rev 11:19 is the third escalation stage (after 4:5 and 8:5), marking the transition to the MHP phase.

    Revelation 2:25 -- achri hou an hexo (till I come)¶

    Greek: πλὴν ὃ ἔχετε κρατήσατε ἄχρι οὗ ἂν ἥξω.

Key parsing:
- πλὴν (plen) G4133 = [ADV] "nevertheless/only"
- κρατήσατε (kratesate) G2902 = [V-AAM-2P] Aorist Active Imperative "hold fast!"
- ἄχρι (achri) G891 = [ADV] "until"
- οὗ (hou) G3739 = [R-GSM] Relative Pronoun "which" (= the time when)
- ἂν (an) G302 = [PRT] contingency particle
- ἥξω (hexo) G2240 = [V-AAS-1S] Aorist Active Subjunctive "I come"
    NOTE: achri hou an hexo = "until whenever I come" -- an indefinite temporal clause extending to Christ's return. This appears at the FOURTH of seven churches (Thyatira). If the seven churches represent sequential historical eras, this clause extends the sequence to the Second Coming. Rev 3:3 adds "I will come as a thief" at the fifth church (Sardis). These single verses refute confining all seven churches to the first century alone.

    Luke 23:56 -- kata ten entolen (according to THE commandment)¶

Key parsing:
- σάββατον (sabbaton) G4521 = [N-ASN] "sabbath"
- ἡσύχασαν (hesychasan) G2270 = [V-AAI-3P] Aorist Active Indicative "they rested"
- κατὰ (kata) G2596 = [PREP] "according to"

    2 Thessalonians 2:8 -- ho anomos (the lawless one)¶

    Greek: καὶ τότε ἀποκαλυφθήσεται ὁ ἄνομος, ὃν ὁ Κύριος Ἰησοῦς ἀνελεῖ τῷ πνεύματι τοῦ στόματος αὐτοῦ καὶ καταργήσει τῇ ἐπιφανείᾳ τῆς παρουσίας αὐτοῦ,

Key parsing:
- ἀποκαλυφθήσεται (apokalyphthesetai) G601 = [V-FPI-3S] Future Passive Indicative "will be revealed"
- ὁ ἄνομος (ho anomos) G459 = [T-NSM + A-NSM] "THE lawless one" -- substantival adjective with article
- ἀνελεῖ (anelei) G337 = [V-2FAI-3S] Future Active Indicative "will destroy/consume"
- τῷ πνεύματι τοῦ στόματος (to pneumati tou stomatos) = "by the breath/spirit of His mouth"
- καταργήσει (katargesei) G2673 = [V-FAI-3S] Future Active Indicative "will render inoperative/abolish"
- τῇ ἐπιφανείᾳ (te epiphaneia) G2015 = [N-DSF] "by the brightness/appearing"
- τῆς παρουσίας (tes parousias) G3952 = [N-GSF] "of the coming/presence"
    NOTE: ho anomos = "the lawless one" (a-nomos = without-law/against-law). Paul uses TWO verbs for the destruction: anelei ("consume/slay" by the breath of His mouth) and katargesei ("render inoperative/abolish" by the brightness of His coming). The double destruction verb suggests a two-stage process: progressive weakening + final elimination at the parousia. This parallels Dan 7:26 "they shall take away his dominion, to consume and to destroy it unto the end."

    Revelation 5:1 -- katasphragismenon (sealed with seven seals)¶

    Greek: βιβλίον γεγραμμένον ἔσωθεν καὶ ὄπισθεν, κατεσφραγισμένον σφραγῖσιν ἑπτά.

Key parsing:
- βιβλίον (biblion) G975 = [N-ASN] "scroll/book"
- γεγραμμένον (gegrammenon) G1125 = [V-RPP-ASN] Perfect Passive Participle "having been written"
- ἔσωθεν καὶ ὄπισθεν (esothen kai opisthen) = "inside and on the back" -- completely full
- κατεσφραγισμένον (katesphragismenon) G2696 = [V-RPP-ASN] Perfect Passive Participle "having been sealed up"
- σφραγῖσιν ἑπτά (sphragisin hepta) = "with seven seals"
    NOTE: katasphragizo (G2696) is an NT HAPAX -- found only here. The kata- prefix intensifies the sealing: "sealed up completely." This is the third stage in the sealed-to-unsealed arc: (1) Dan 8:26 satham (Qal); (2) Dan 12:4 satham + chatham (double seal); (3) Rev 5:1 katasphragizo (intensified NT hapax). The perfect passive participle indicates a STATE: the scroll IS sealed and REMAINS sealed until the Lamb opens it.

    Revelation 22:10 -- me sphragises (do not seal)¶

    Greek: Μὴ σφραγίσῃς τοὺς λόγους τῆς προφητείας τοῦ βιβλίου τούτου· ὁ καιρὸς γὰρ ἐγγύς ἐστιν.

Key parsing:
- Μὴ (me) G3361 = [PRT-N] negative particle
- σφραγίσῃς (sphragises) G4972 = [V-AAS-2S] Aorist Active Subjunctive "you should seal"
- τοὺς λόγους (tous logous) = "the words"
- τῆς προφητείας (tes propheteias) = "of the prophecy"
- ὁ καιρὸς ἐγγύς ἐστιν (ho kairos engys estin) = "the time is near"
    NOTE: me + aorist subjunctive = prohibitive subjunctive = "do not even BEGIN to seal." This is the fifth and final stage of the sealed-to-unsealed arc: (1) Dan 8:26 shut up; (2) Dan 12:4 double-sealed; (3) Rev 5:1 intensified sealing; (4) Rev 10:2 open (eneogmenon, perfect passive = "having been opened and remaining open"); (5) Rev 22:10 command not to seal. The arc reversal proves Revelation's content is now accessible -- Daniel's sealed prophecies are UNSEALED through Revelation.

    Revelation 7:3 -- sphragis (seal of God)¶

    Greek: ἄχρι σφραγίσωμεν τοὺς δούλους τοῦ Θεοῦ ἡμῶν ἐπὶ τῶν μετώπων αὐτῶν.

Key parsing:
- σφραγίσωμεν (sphragisomen) G4972 = [V-AAS-1P] Aorist Active Subjunctive "we seal"
- δούλους (doulous) G1401 = [N-APM] "servants/slaves"
- μετώπων (metopon) G3359 = [N-GPN] "foreheads" -- FOREHEAD only (no hand)
    NOTE: God's seal is placed on the FOREHEAD only -- location of thought/allegiance. Compare Rev 13:16 where the mark (charagma) is placed on forehead OR hand. This contrast (seal = forehead only; mark = forehead or hand) suggests the seal involves conscious conviction while the mark includes either conviction or coerced compliance.

    Revelation 13:16 -- charagma (mark)¶

    Greek: ἵνα δῶσιν αὐτοῖς χάραγμα ἐπὶ τῆς χειρὸς αὐτῶν τῆς δεξιᾶς ἢ ἐπὶ τὸ μέτωπον αὐτῶν,

Key parsing:
- χάραγμα (charagma) G5480 = [N-ASN] "mark/stamp/brand" -- from charasso = engrave
- ἐπὶ τῆς χειρὸς (epi tes cheiros) = "upon the hand"
- τῆς δεξιᾶς (tes dexias) = "the right (hand)"
- ἢ (e) G2228 = [PRT] "OR"
- ἐπὶ τὸ μέτωπον (epi to metopon) = "upon the forehead"
    NOTE: charagma = an engraved stamp/brand (used of images on coins, imperial seals). The OR between hand and forehead means the mark involves EITHER conscious allegiance (forehead) OR external compliance/action (hand). This contrasts with God's seal which is forehead ONLY -- requiring genuine conviction.
