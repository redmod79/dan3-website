    Raw Strong's Lookups¶

    H1855 - d'qaq (break in pieces)¶

    • Transliteration: dᵉqaq
    • POS: verb
    • BLB Count: 10
    • Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to; to crumble or (trans.) crush: break to pieces.
    • BDB: Pe. Perfect 3mp daquw Dan 2:35. Haph. break in pieces: Perfect 3fs haddeqet Dan 2:34,45; 3mp haddiquw Dan 6:24. Imperfect 3fs taddiq Dan 2:40 (absolute), Dan 2:44 (accusative); suffix taddᵉqinnah Dan 7:23; Participle active mehaddeq Dan 2:40 (accusative), feminine maddᵉqah Dan 7:7, Dan 7:19.
    • 13 total occurrences; translations: brake in pieces, breaketh in pieces, broken to pieces, etc.
    • KEY: Appears exclusively in Dan 2 (2:34,35,40,44,45) and Dan 7 (7:7,19,23) and Dan 6:24 -- binding chapters 2 and 7 together

    H8548 - tamid (continual/daily)¶

    • Transliteration: tawmeed
    • POS: masculine noun
    • BLB Count: 104
    • Definition: from an unused root meaning to stretch; properly continuance; the regular (daily) sacrifice
    • 105 total occurrences; 53x "continually," 17x "the continual," 9x "continual," 7x "the daily"
    • Daniel occurrences: Dan 8:11, 8:12, 8:13, 11:31, 12:11 (all as "the daily")
    • KEY: Links Dan 8, 11, and 12 -- same power removes the tamid in all three contexts

    H6663 - tsadaq (be just/righteous/cleansed)¶

    • Transliteration: tsaw-dak
    • POS: verb
    • BLB Count: 41
    • Definition: a primitive root; to be (causatively, make) right (in a moral or forensic sense): cleanse, clear self, (be, do) just(-ice, -ify, -ify self), (be turn to) righteous(-ness)
    • 54 total occurrences; Dan 8:14 is the ONLY Niphal occurrence, translated "be cleansed"
    • KEY: nitsdaq (Niphal) in Dan 8:14 is forensic, not ritual -- "be declared righteous/vindicated"

    H2852 - chathak (determine/cut off) -- HAPAX¶

    • Transliteration: khaw-thak
    • POS: verb
    • BLB Count: 1
    • Definition: a primitive root; properly to cut off, i.e. (figuratively) to decree: determine
    • ONLY occurrence: Dan 9:24
    • KEY: Daniel used charats (H2782) 3x elsewhere for "determine" (Dan 9:26,27; 11:36). His deliberate choice of chathak (="cut off") in 9:24 is an authorial signal that the 70 weeks are "cut off" from a larger period (the 2300 days of Dan 8:14)

    H2782 - charats (decide/determine/decree)¶

    • Transliteration: khaw-rats
    • POS: verb
    • BLB Count: 12
    • Definition: to point sharply; figuratively to be alert, to decide: bestir self, decide, decree, determine, maim, move
    • Daniel occurrences: Dan 9:26 ("determined"), Dan 9:27 ("determined"), Dan 11:36 ("determined")
    • KEY: Daniel's normal word for "determine" -- contrast with chathak (H2852) in Dan 9:24

    H5057 - nagiyd (prince/ruler/commander)¶

    • Transliteration: naw-gheed
    • POS: masculine noun
    • BLB Count: 44
    • Definition: from; a commander (as occupying the front); captain, chief, governor, leader, noble, prince, ruler
    • Daniel occurrences: Dan 9:25 (Messiah the Prince), Dan 9:26 (prince that shall come), Dan 11:22 (prince of the covenant)
    • KEY: Prince chain -- nagiyd bridges Messiah (9:25) and the opposing prince (9:26)

    H8269 - sar (prince/captain/chief)¶

    • Transliteration: sar
    • POS: masculine noun
    • BLB Count: 421
    • Definition: a head person (of any rank or class): captain, chief, general, governor, prince, ruler, steward
    • Daniel occurrences: Dan 1:7-11,18 (prince of eunuchs); Dan 8:11 (sar ha-tsaba, prince of the host); Dan 8:25 (sar sarim, Prince of princes); Dan 9:6,8 (our princes); Dan 10:13 (prince of Persia, Michael one of the chief princes); Dan 10:20 (prince of Greece); Dan 10:21 (Michael your prince); Dan 11:5 (his princes); Dan 12:1 (Michael, ha-sar ha-gadol, the great prince)
    • KEY: Michael chain -- sar used for both earthly and supernatural princes; Michael progresses from "one of the chief princes" (10:13) to "your prince" (10:21) to "the great prince" (12:1)

    H1080 - bela (wear out) -- HAPAX in Aramaic¶

    • Transliteration: bel-aw
    • POS: verb (Aramaic)
    • BLB Count: 1
    • Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to (Biblical Hebrew balah); to afflict: wear out
    • ONLY occurrence: Dan 7:25
    • BDB: Pael Imperfect 3ms yeballe -- figurative for "harass continually" (acc. of person)
    • KEY: Pael imperfect indicates prolonged, continuous wearing-out action -- not a single event but sustained attrition over time

    H5732 - 'iddan (time/year)¶

    • Transliteration: id-dawn
    • POS: masculine noun (Aramaic)
    • BLB Count: 13
    • Definition: (Aramaic) from a root corresponding to that of; a set time; technically, a year: time
    • BDB: 1. in general, time as duration (Dan 2:8, 7:12). 2. definite time = year: "seven 'iddanin = seven years" (Dan 4:13,20,22,29); 'iddan we-'iddanin u-felag 'iddan (Dan 7:25, i.e. 3.5 years)
    • KEY: BDB explicitly glosses 'iddan = year in Dan 4. This establishes the day-year principle: if 'iddan = year in Dan 4 (literal), the same word in Dan 7:25 should also = year.

    H8251 - shiqquts (abomination/detestable thing)¶

    • Transliteration: shik-koots
    • POS: masculine noun
    • BLB Count: 28
    • Definition: disgusting; especially idolatrous; concretely an idol: abominable filth, detestable thing
    • Daniel occurrences: Dan 9:27, 11:31, 12:11
    • KEY: shiqquts links Dan 9, 11, and 12 -- same "abomination" appears in all three

    H4941 - mishpat (judgment/verdict)¶

    • Transliteration: mish-pawt
    • POS: masculine noun
    • BLB Count: 421
    • Definition: from; properly a verdict pronounced judicially; including the act, the place, the suit, the crime, and the penalty; abstractly, justice
    • 448 total occurrences
    • KEY: Forensic judgment vocabulary -- the foundational Hebrew term for judicial proceedings

    H1780 - diyn (judgment, Aramaic)¶

    • Transliteration: deen
    • POS: masculine noun (Aramaic)
    • BLB Count: 5
    • Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to; judgment
    • Occurrences: Dan 4:34 (justice of God's ways), Dan 7:10 (dina = judges/court), Dan 7:22 (judgment given), Dan 7:26 (judgment shall sit), Ezra 7:26
