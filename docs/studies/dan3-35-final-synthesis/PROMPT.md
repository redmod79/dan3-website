### Focus Areas

1. **Framework-evidence alignment verification**: Does each position's framework-level analysis (dan3-31 through dan3-34) match its evidence-level performance (dan3-30 tallies)? WHAT: Compare the hermeneutical engine of each position with its tally profile. WHY: This determines whether the evidence asymmetry is a detail artifact or a structural feature. HOW: Read all 8 CONCLUSIONs; cross-reference HIST's 38:0 I-A:I-D ratio with its "text-extending" framework characterization; cross-reference PRET's 33:1 ratio and ~20+ constraints with its "scope-cap" characterization; cross-reference FUT's 22:4 ratio with its "two-tier" characterization.

2. **PRET survival analysis**: Can PRET survive its three FATAL weaknesses? WHAT: Evaluate whether any modification to PRET preserves its genuine insights while resolving Dan 11:40-45, Dan 12:2, and Dan 12:13. WHY: PRET's contributions (Antiochus identification, vocabulary chains) are textually real. HOW: Examine whether type/antitype (Antiochus as Greek-era type of a larger power) or near-term fulfillment layer integrates PRET's insights into a broader framework.

3. **FUT survival analysis**: What remains of FUT when I-C and I-D items are removed? WHAT: Evaluate FUT stripped of gap thesis, Third Temple, pretribulation rapture, and sharp Israel/Church distinction. WHY: FUT's strongest items overlap with HIST. HOW: List FUT's surviving claims and compare them against HIST's framework — do they substantially overlap?

4. **The convergence observation**: Is there a unified reading of Daniel? WHAT: Assess whether HIST + PRET's near-term layer + FUT's eschatological insistence converge toward a single comprehensive reading. WHY: Dan3-34 noted that FUT minus dispensational additions ≈ HIST, and PRET's Antiochus work as a near-term layer integrates into HIST's type/antitype framework. HOW: Construct the convergent reading and test it against the 399 evidence items.

5. **Plain-language conclusion for first-time readers**: WHAT: Write an accessible summary of what Daniel's prophetic visions establish, suggest, and leave open. WHY: This is the study a new reader encounters first. HOW: Use the "What CAN be said / What CANNOT be said" framework from dan3-30; avoid technical jargon; explain the evidence classification system briefly.

6. **HIST weakness assessment**: Where does HIST still need work? WHAT: Evaluate HIST's MODERATE weaknesses — Dan 11:40-45 sub-positions, 457 BC starting point, 1290/1335 calculations, day-year below N-tier. WHY: Honest assessment requires acknowledging where the strongest framework still has gaps. HOW: Draw from dan3-27's weakness ranking.

7. **The christological question**: Where is Christ in each reading? WHAT: Compare the christological integration across the three frameworks. WHY: Dan3-34 identified a measurable gradient (HIST > PRET > FUT). HOW: Trace the tsadaq chain (Isa 53:11 → Dan 8:14 → Dan 9:24 → Dan 12:3), the mashiach identification, and the sealed-to-unsealed arc.

8. **The NT integration question**: How well does each framework account for the canonical NT use of Daniel? WHAT: Evaluate each framework's ability to integrate Matt 24:15, 2 Thess 2:3-8, Rev 13:1-5, and the already/not-yet framework. WHY: Three independent NT authors treating Daniel's imagery as ongoing/future is textual evidence constraining interpretation. HOW: Review dan3-24 and dan3-25 findings.
