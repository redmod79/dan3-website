# Cross-Testament Parallels Summary — dan3-35

This synthesis study draws on parallel data already gathered across the dan3 series. Key cross-testament connections documented in prior studies:

## Daniel 2:44 → NT Stone/Kingdom
- Matt 21:44; Luke 20:18 (likmao chain from Dan 2:44 LXX)
- Matt 12:28 (ephthasen — kingdom "has come")
- Col 1:13 (metestēsen — "translated into the kingdom")
- Heb 12:28 ("receiving a kingdom which cannot be moved")

## Daniel 7:13-14 → NT Son of Man
- Matt 24:30; 26:64 (Jesus applies to himself)
- Rev 1:7 (every eye shall see him)
- Rev 1:13-14 (Christological merger — fusing Dan 7:9 and 7:13)

## Daniel 12:2 → Resurrection Passages
- Isa 66:24 (dera'on hapax pair — permanent eschatological judgment)
- John 5:28-29 (dual-outcome resurrection)
- 1 Thess 4:16 (resurrection voice convergence with Dan 12:1-2)

## Daniel 7:8,25 → Revelation 13:5-6
- Rev 13:5 verbatim quotes Dan 7:8 LXX (stoma laloun megala)
- Rev 13:1-2 composite beast absorbs all four Dan 7 beasts in reverse order
- Rev 1:1 quotes Dan 2:28 LXX verbatim (ha dei genesthai)

## Daniel 11:36 → 2 Thessalonians 2:3-4
- hyperairomenos (2 Thess 2:4) maps to yitromem (Dan 11:36)
- Both describe self-exaltation above all that is called God
- 2 Thess 2:7 "already works" — present-tense attestation c. AD 51
