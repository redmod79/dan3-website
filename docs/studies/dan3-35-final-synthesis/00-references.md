## Prior Studies

### From Study Plan (Integrate List)

**dan3-31-HIST-framework:**
- HIST operates via three interlocking text-derived principles: sequential kingdoms, continuous fulfillment, day-year conversion
- These three principles generate the entire HIST reading — none is imported from outside the text
- HIST's evidence profile (38 I-A, 0 I-D, 0 ALL constraints, 0 I-B against) is a structural consequence of the hermeneutical method itself
- The narrative arc tells a theodicy: God-given authority → deterioration → religious apostasy → christological pivot → divine judgment → everlasting kingdom
- Weaknesses are all history-mapping (Dan 11:40-45, 1290/1335, 457 BC) — not text-level contradictions
- Christological center: 70 weeks (first advent), Dan 8:14 (heavenly ministry), Dan 12 (return)
- The tsadaq chain (Isa 53:11 → Dan 8:14 → Dan 9:24 → Dan 12:3) makes Christ the interpretive axis

**dan3-32-PRET-framework:**
- PRET is generated by a single master axiom: the Maccabean-era temporal scope cap
- From this axiom, every distinctive claim follows: fourth kingdom = Greece, horn = Antiochus, literal time, single referent
- PRET's genuine strengths: Dan 8 (5 I-A(1) HIGH specification matches), Dan 11:2-35 (7 I-A(1) HIGH, essentially uncontested)
- Progressive degradation: I-A(1) HIGH in Dan 11:21-35 → I-D LOW in Dan 11:40-45
- Three FATAL weaknesses are framework problems, not detail problems: Dan 11:40-45 (I-D), Dan 12:2 (N-tier), Dan 12:13 (N-tier)
- ~20+ ALL constraints are 20 manifestations of one structural incompatibility — scope cap too narrow
- Internal contradiction: vocabulary chains prove too much (maskilim extends into eschatological section)
- Permanent contributions survive: Antiochus identification, vocabulary chain analysis, lexical arguments

**dan3-33-FUT-framework:**
- FUT is a two-tier system: text-derived foundation (shared with HIST) + dispensational superstructure (I-C LOW)
- Distinctive items (gap, Israel/Church, rapture, Third Temple) uniformly I-C LOW or I-D
- Dependency chain: Israel/Church → gap → Antichrist reading → Third Temple → tribulation → rapture
- Six NT counter-texts challenge the Israel/Church keystone
- When FUT's distinctive superstructure is removed, what remains is essentially HIST's foundation
- FUT's genuine contributions: NT convergence argument, unfulfilled Dan 11:45 geography, eschatological insistence, 2 Thess 2:4 mapping

**dan3-34-framework-comparison:**
- Three structurally distinct hermeneutical architectures, not three equally weighted alternatives
- HIST extends the text → 0 constraints; PRET truncates the text → ~20+ constraints; FUT imports into the text → ~10+ constraints
- The asymmetry is structural: text-extending vs. text-truncating vs. text-importing frameworks
- Christological gradient: HIST (Christ as continuous center) > PRET (Christ as secondary resolution) > FUT (Christ at endpoints with gap)
- Falsifiability: PRET already falsified at framework level by N-tier evidence; FUT structurally undermined at I-B/I-C; HIST's weaknesses are application-level
- Convergence insight: PRET's Antiochus work shows a near-term fulfillment layer; FUT's eschatological urgency captures something real; HIST's continuous-history reading best preserves scope markers
