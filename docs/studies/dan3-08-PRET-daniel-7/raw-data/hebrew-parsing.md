## Daniel 4:16 (Directive G — 'iddan literal years)
```
NOTE: Dan 4:16 in Hebrew parser = Dan 4:19 in English KJV numbering
The verse parsed does not contain the "seven times" formula.
The relevant verse is Dan 4:13 (Aramaic) = 4:16 (English):
"Let his heart be changed from man's, and let a beast's heart be given unto him; and let seven times pass over him."
- שִׁבְעָה עִדָּנִין (shiv'ah 'iddanin) = seven times/years
  עִדָּנִין = plural of עדן (H5732) = times/years
  BDB explicitly: "definite time = year" in Dan 4:13,20,22,29
  This establishes that 'iddan = literal year in Dan 4
```
