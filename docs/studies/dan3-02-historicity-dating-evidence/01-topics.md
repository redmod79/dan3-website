# Topics Research

## Question
What historical and linguistic evidence bears on Daniel's composition date and historical reliability?

## Topics

### DANIEL
- A Jewish captive, also called BELTESHAZZAR
  - Educated at king's court DAN 1
  - Interprets visions DAN 2; 4; 5
  - Promotion and executive authority of DAN 2:48,49; 5:11,29; 6:2
  - Conspiracy against, cast into the lions' den DAN 6
  - Prophecies of DAN 4:8,9; 7; 8; 9; 10; 11; 12; MAT 24:15
  - Abstinence of DAN 1:8-16
  - Wisdom of DAN 1:17; EZK 28:3
  - Devoutness of DAN 2:18; 6; 9; 10; 12; EZK 14:14
  - Courage and fidelity of DAN 4:27; 5:17-23; 6:10-23
  - Worshiped by Nebuchadnezzar DAN 2:6
- David's son, also called CHILEAB (2SA 3:3; 1CH 3:1)
- A descendant of Ithamar, companion of Ezra (EZR 8:2; NEH 10:6)

### BELSHAZZAR
- King of Babylon DAN 5:1-30
- NOTE: Nave's has only one entry -- the entire narrative of Daniel 5 is the sole biblical data source for Belshazzar.

### DARIUS
1. The Mede, king of Persia -- DAN 5:31; 6; 9:1
2. King of Persia (emancipator of Jews) -- EZR 5; 6; HAG 1:1,15; ZEC 1:1
3. The Persian -- NEH 12:22
- NOTE: Three distinct Darius figures are listed. The Darius of Daniel (entry 1) is distinguished from the Darius who emancipated the Jews (entry 2) and "the Persian" (entry 3).

### BABYLON
1. CITY OF -- Built by Nimrod (GEN 10:10); tower of (GEN 11:1-9); capital of the kingdom (DAN 4:30; 2KI 25:13; 2CH 36:6,7,10,18,20). Prophecies concerning: PSA 87:4; 137:8,9; ISA 13; 14:4-26; 21:1-10; 46:1,2; 47; 48:14,20; JER 21:4-10; 25:12-14; 50; 51; DAN 2:21-38; 4:10-26; 5:25-29; 7; HAB 1:5-11; ZEC 2:7-9
2. EMPIRE OF -- Founded by Nimrod (GEN 10:10); called LAND OF SHINAR (DAN 1:2); divisions of (DAN 3:1; 2KI 17:24); extent at time of Nebuchadnezzar (DAN 2:37,38; 4:1; 6:1). Government: a limited monarchy (EST 1:13-19; 8:8; DAN 6:8,14,17); tyrannical (DAN 3).

### PERSIA
- Extended from India to Ethiopia, 127 provinces (EST 1:1; DAN 6:1)
- Government restricted by constitutional limitations (EST 8:8; DAN 6:8-12)
- Princes as advisors in administration (DAN 6:1-7)
- Rulers: Darius (DAN 5:31; 6; 9:1); Artaxerxes I (EZR 4:7-24); Artaxerxes II (EZR 7; NEH 2; 5:14); Cyrus (2CH 36:22,23; EZR 1; ISA 44:28; 45:1-4,13)
- Prophecies concerning: ISA 13:17; 21:1-10; JER 49:34-39; 51:11-64; DAN 2:31-45; 5:28; 7; 8; 11:1-4

### CYRUS
- King of Persia. Issues decree for emancipation of Jews and rebuilding the temple: 2CH 36:22,23; EZR 1; 3:7; 4:3; 5:13,14; 6:3
- Prophecies concerning: ISA 13:17-22; 21:2; 41:2; 44:28; 45:1-4,13; 46:11; 48:14,15

### NEBUCHADNEZZAR
- Also called NEBUCHADREZZAR. King of Babylon (JER 21:2).
- Administration: DAN 1; 2; 3; 4
- Conquests: Jerusalem (2KI 24; 25; 1CH 6:15; 2CH 36:5-21; EZR 1:7; JER 39); Egypt (2KI 24:7; JER 46:2); Tyre (EZK 29:18)
- Instrument of God's judgments (JER 27:8)

### CAPTIVITY
- Foretold: LEV 26:33; DEU 28:36
- Of Judah in Babylon, prophecy: ISA 39:6; JER 13:19; 20:4; 25:2-11; 32:28
- Fulfilled: 2KI 24:11-16; 25; 2CH 36; JER 52:28-30
- Return: EZR 2; 3; 8
- 70-year prophecy: JER 25:11,12; DAN 9:2

### PROPHECY
- Inspired: ISA 28:22; LUK 1:70; 2TI 3:16; 2PE 1:21
- Sure fulfillment: EZK 12:22-25,28; HAB 2:3; MAT 5:18; 24:35; ACT 13:27,29
- Captivity predicted and fulfilled: JER 25:11,12; 29:10,14; 32:3-5; DAN 9:2; with 2KI 25:1-8; EZR 1

### CHALDEA
- Southern portion of Babylonia, often interchangeable with Babylon
- Abraham a native of: GEN 11:28,31; 15:7
- Founded by the Assyrians: ISA 23:13

### BELTESHAZZAR
- A name given Daniel: DAN 1:7

### RULERS
- Appointed and removed by God; chastised (DAN 4)
- Daniel listed among righteous rulers
- Wicked rulers in Daniel: Nebuchadnezzar destroying wise men (DAN 2:1-13); three Hebrews to furnace (DAN 3:1-23); Belshazzar in sacrilege (DAN 5:22); Darius deifying himself (DAN 6:7,9); princes conspiring against Daniel (DAN 6:1-9)

## Related Topics Discovered
- CHRONOLOGY -- EXO 12:2 (limited relevance)
- EZEKIEL -- connected through references to Daniel (EZK 14:14; 28:3); Ezekiel was a contemporary exile
- The connection between CAPTIVITY and PROPHECY is significant: the 70-year prophecy (JER 25:11,12) is explicitly referenced in DAN 9:2 as something Daniel himself studied
