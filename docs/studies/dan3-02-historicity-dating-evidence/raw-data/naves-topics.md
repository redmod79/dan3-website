# Raw Nave's Topic Data

## DANIEL
- A Jewish captive, also called BELTESHAZZAR
  - Educated at king's court DAN 1
  - Interprets visions DAN 2; 4; 5
  - Promotion and executive authority of DAN 2:48,49; 5:11,29; 6:2
  - Conspiracy against, cast into the lions' den DAN 6
  - Prophecies of DAN 4:8,9; 7; 8; 9; 10; 11; 12; MAT 24:15
  - Abstinence of DAN 1:8-16
  - Wisdom of DAN 1:17; EZK 28:3
  - Devoutness of DAN 2:18; 6; 9; 10; 12; EZK 14:14
  - Courage and fidelity of DAN 4:27; 5:17-23; 6:10-23
  - Worshiped by Nebuchadnezzar DAN 2:6
- David's son, also called CHILEAB 2SA 3:3; 1CH 3:1
- A descendant of Ithamar, companion of Ezra EZR 8:2; NEH 10:6

## BELSHAZZAR
- King of Babylon DAN 5:1-30

## DARIUS
1. The Mede, king of Persia DAN 5:31; 6; 9:1

3. The Persian NEH 12:22

## BABYLON

   - Built by Nimrod GEN 10:10
   - In the land of Shinar GEN 10:10; 11:2
   - Tower of GEN 11:1-9
   - Capital of the kingdom of Babylon DAN 4:30; 2KI 25:13; 2CH 36:6,7,10,18,20
   - Gates of ISA 45:1,2; JER 51:58
   - Walled JER 51:44,58
   - Splendor of ISA 14:4
   - Peter writes from 1PE 5:13
   - Prophecies concerning PSA 87:4; 137:8,9; ISA 13; 14:4-26; 21:1-10; 46:1,2; 47; 48:14,20; JER 21:4-10; 25:12-14; 27:1-11; 28:14; 32:28; 34:2,3; 42:11,12; 43; 46:13-26; 49:28-30; 50; 51; EZK 21:19; 26; 29:17-20; 30:10; 32:11; DAN 2:21-38; 4:10-26; 5:25-29; 7; HAB 1:5-11; ZEC 2:7-9
   - FIGURATIVE REV 14:8; 16:19; 17; 18
2. EMPIRE OF
   - Founded by Nimrod GEN 10:10
   - Called LAND OF SHINAR GEN 10:10; 11:2; 14:1,9; ISA 11:11; DAN 1:2; ZEC 5:11
   - SHESHACH JER 25:26; 51:41
   - MERATHAIM JER 50:21
   - Divisions of 2KI 17:24; 24:7; ISA 23:12,13; DAN 3:1; ACT 7:4
   - Extent at time of Nebuchadnezzar DAN 2:37,38; 4:1; 6:1
   - At time of Ahasuerus EST 1:1; 8:9; 9:30
   - Government: A limited monarchy EST 1:13-19; 8:8; DAN 6:8,14,17
   - Tyrannical EST 3:7-15; DAN 3

## PERSIA
- Extended from India to Ethiopia, 127 provinces EST 1:1; DAN 6:1
- Government restricted by constitutional limitations EST 8:8; DAN 6:8-12
- Municipal governments with dual governors NEH 3:9,12,16-18
- Princes as advisors in administration DAN 6:1-7
- Rulers: Ahasuerus EST 1:3; Darius DAN 5:31; 6; 9:1; Artaxerxes I EZR 4:7-24; Artaxerxes II EZR 7; NEH 2; 5:14; Cyrus 2CH 36:22,23; EZR 1; 3:7; 4:3; 5:13,14,17; 6:3; ISA 41:2,3; 44:28; 45:1-4,13; 46:11; 48:14,15
- Prophecies concerning ISA 13:17; 21:1-10; JER 49:34-39; 51:11-64; EZK 32:24,25; 38:5; DAN 2:31-45; 5:28; 7; 8; 11:1-4

## CYRUS
- King of Persia
- Issues decree for emancipation of Jews and rebuilding temple 2CH 36:22,23; EZR 1; 3:7; 4:3; 5:13,14; 6:3
- Prophecies concerning ISA 13:17-22; 21:2; 41:2; 44:28; 45:1-4,13; 46:11; 48:14,15

## CAPTIVITY

## PROPHECY
- Inspired ISA 28:22; LUK 1:70; 2TI 3:16; 2PE 1:21

- Captivity of the Jews predicted and fulfilled JER 25:11,12; 29:10,14; 32:3-5; DAN 9:2; with 2KI 25:1-8; EZR 1

## CHALDEA
- Southern portion of Babylonia, often used interchangeably with Babylon
- Abraham a native of GEN 11:28,31; 15:7
- Founded by the Assyrians ISA 23:13
- Character of its people HAB 1:6

## BELTESHAZZAR
- A name given Daniel DAN 1:7

## RULERS
- Appointed and removed by God
- Chastised DAN 4
- Daniel listed as righteous ruler
- Wicked examples: Nebuchadnezzar commanding to destroy wise men DAN 2:1-13; commanding three Hebrews to furnace DAN 3:1-23; Belshazzar in drunkenness and committing sacrilege DAN 5:22; Darius in deifying himself DAN 6:7,9; Princes conspiring against Daniel DAN 6:1-9
