### Focus Areas

**1. gadal/yether Progression and Territorial Greatness (Against PRET #1)**
- **WHAT:** The Dan 8:9-11 horn "waxed exceeding great" (gadal, H1431) with comparative yether, requiring territorial surpassing of both Medo-Persia and Greece. Antiochus controlled ~3M km2 vs. Persia's ~5.5-8M km2.
- **WHY:** This is the single strongest textual argument against PRET. The COMPARE study classified this I-B resolved STRONG against Antiochus. PRET response likely appeals to spiritual or military greatness rather than territorial.
- **HOW:** Retrieve DAN 8:9-12 with full chapter context. Run hebrew_parser.py on DAN 8:9-11 to confirm gadal form and yether usage. Look up [H1431](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1431/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (gadal) and [H3499](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3499/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (yether) with search_strongs.py --lexicon. Trace gadal across Daniel (DAN 8:4,8,9,10,11,25) to establish the comparative chain.

**2. Everlasting Kingdom vs. Maccabean Fulfillment (Against PRET #2)**
- **WHAT:** Dan 2:44 uses triple emphasis ("shall never be destroyed... shall not be left to other people... shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms... shall stand for ever"). Dan 7:14,18,27 repeat "everlasting" (alam, H5957). The Hasmonean dynasty lasted ~77 years.
- **WHY:** Tool discoveries show KINGDOM OF HEAVEN (score 0.69) connects to MAT 25:1-13, JHN 18:36, LUK 1:32,33 -- all describing a kingdom that exceeds any temporal Maccabean state. Nave's RESURRECTION entry ties DAN 12:2,3,13 to eschatological scope.
- **HOW:** Retrieve DAN 2:44-45, DAN 7:13-14,18,27 with chapter context. Run hebrew_parser.py on DAN 7:14 and DAN 7:27 to parse alam/ad constructions. Look up [H5957](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5957/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (alam Aramaic) and [H5703](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5703/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (ad) with search_strongs.py --lexicon. Compare LUK 1:32-33 which echoes DAN 7:14.

**3. 2300/1150 Days Mathematical Problem (Against PRET #3)**
- **WHAT:** PRET reads erev-boqer as 1150 literal days (2300 half-units). But Antiochus's actual desecration period was ~1105 days (167-164 BC), not 1150 -- a 45-day shortfall. Additionally, the COMPARE study argues erev-boqer functions as a single unit (like Gen 1 "evening and morning").
- **WHY:** DAY topic entry (score 0.35) lists DAN 8:14 as "Prophetic" alongside DAN 9:24-27; 12:11,12; REV 11:3. Nave's categorization treats these as a set of prophetic time periods, not literal days.
- **HOW:** Retrieve DAN 8:13-14,26 with chapter context. Run hebrew_parser.py on DAN 8:14 to parse erev-boqer construct. Compare GEN 1:5,8,13,19,23,31 (erev-boqer as single unit in creation account). Retrieve 1 Maccabees timeline data if available in apocrypha corpus.

**4. Dan 11:40-45 Historical Failure (Against PRET #4)**
- **WHAT:** Five specifications in Dan 11:40-45 fail historically for Antiochus: (1) no third Egyptian campaign, (2) wrong death location, (3) no conquest of Libya/Ethiopia, (4) no alarming tidings from north and east, (5) death between seas and holy mountain does not match Tabae/Gabae.
- **WHY:** COMPARE study dan3-22 documents PRET progressive degradation from I-A(1) HIGH in 11:21-35 to I-D LOW in 11:40-45. PRET either concedes this as critical weakness or labels Dan 11:40-45 a "failed prediction."
- **HOW:** Retrieve DAN 11:40-45 with full chapter context. Retrieve DAN 12:1-4 for the continuity into resurrection. Run hebrew_parser.py on DAN 11:40-45 to identify the three-party pronoun structure. Cross-reference DAN 11:45 with DAN 12:1 for vocabulary links.

**5. Day-Year Principle: Textual Basis and Limits (Against HIST #1)**
- **WHAT:** The counter-argument: no verse states "in all apocalyptic prophecy, days = years." Num 14:34 and Ezek 4:6 use yom lashshanah formula but in unique divine judgment contexts, not as universal hermeneutical prescriptions.
- **WHY:** DAY entry lists prophetic day-counts together (DAN 8:14; 9:24-27; 12:11,12; REV 11:3; 12:6). dan3-23 classified day-year I-A(1) HIGH with 7+ converging lines but acknowledged it cannot reach N-tier. The AGAINST arguments prevent universalization.
- **HOW:** Retrieve NUM 14:34, EZK 4:5-6 with chapter context. Run hebrew_parser.py on both to parse the yom lashshanah formula. Retrieve DAN 10:2-3 (shabuim yamim = "weeks of days" -- authorial signal distinguishing literal from symbolic). **6. Dan 9:25 Decree Identification: Ezra 7 vs. Nehemiah 2 (Against HIST #7 / Against FUT #3,6)**
- **WHAT:** HIST argues for 457 BC (Ezra 7:12-26) as the decree to "restore and build." FUT argues for 444 BC (Neh 2:1-8). The counter: Dan 9:25 says "restore AND build" -- Neh 2 addresses building walls but not judicial restoration; Ezra 7 includes judicial authority (EZR 7:25-26).
- **WHY:** JERUSALEM entry lists EZR 7; NEH 2; DAN 9:25 together. The COMPARE study dan3-18 noted that Dan 9:25 says "restore AND build" and that Neh 2 is narrower, while Anderson-Hoehner calculation (444 BC + 360-day years) was classified I-A(3) LOW.
- **HOW:** Retrieve EZR 7:11-26 and NEH 2:1-8 with full chapter context. Run hebrew_parser.py on DAN 9:25 to parse shuwb (restore) and banah (build). Compare EZR 7:25-26 (judicial/administrative authority) vs. NEH 2:5-8 (building permission only). Also retrieve EZR 6:14 which mentions a composite decree.

The distinction between gabar and karath is a critical exegetical argument. COVENANT entry lists DAN 9:27 under "To be confirmed."
- **HOW:** Retrieve DAN 9:26-27 with full chapter context. Run hebrew_parser.py on DAN 9:27 to parse gabar. **8. Gap Thesis and Continuous Image (Against FUT #1,5)**

**9. Six NT Texts Against Israel/Church Distinction (Against FUT #9)**
