# Topics Research

## Question
What does Daniel 7 establish (E/N), and how do the three readings (HIST, PRET, FUT) compare?

### ANCIENT OF DAYS
An appellative of Jehovah: **DAN 7:9,13,22**
Only three occurrences in Scripture, all in Daniel 7.

### HORN
- **Used for anointing oil:** 1SA 16:1; 1KI 1:39
- **Figurative of divine protection:** 2SA 22:3
- **Figurative of power:** 1KI 22:11; PSA 89:24; 92:10; 132:17
- **SYMBOLICAL:** DAN 7:7-24; 8:3-9,20; AMO 6:13; MIC 4:13; HAB 3:4; ZEC 1:18-21; REV 5:6; 12:3; 13:1,11; 17:3-16

Horn symbolism in Daniel spans chs. 7 and 8 with explicit interpretation in 8:20-22 (ram = Medo-Persia, goat = Greece). The Revelation passages (12:3; 13:1; 17:3-16) extend horn symbolism with ten horns and seven heads.

### KINGDOM OF HEAVEN
- Compared to: sowing (MAT 13:24-30), mustard seed (MAT 13:31-32), leaven (MAT 13:33), treasure (MAT 13:44), pearl (MAT 13:45), net (MAT 13:47-50), ten virgins (MAT 25:1-13), talents (MAT 25:14-30; LUK 19:12-27)
- "My kingdom is not of this world" (JHN 18:36)
- Does not consist of meat and drink (ROM 14:17)
- Rich people cannot enter (MAT 19:23-24)

### BLASPHEMY

### ANTICHRIST

### BOOK (of Life / of Judgment)
- **OF LIFE:** EXO 32:32,33; PSA 69:28; 87:6; **DAN 12:1**; LUK 10:20; PHP 4:3; REV 3:5; 13:8; 17:8; 20:12,15; 21:27; 22:19
- **OF REMEMBRANCE:** PSA 56:8; 139:16; **MAL 3:16**

The "books opened" in Dan 7:10 connects to both the book of life and the books of works/remembrance. Rev 20:12 explicitly shows both: "the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life."

### CLOUD
- **SYMBOLICAL:** REV 14:14
- **PILLAR OF:** EXO 13:21,22; 14:19,24; 19:9,16; **LEV 16:2**; 1KI 8:10,11; EZK 10:3,4,18; MAT 17:5; LUK 9:34,35
- **APPEARANCES IN EZEKIEL'S VISION:** EZK 10:3,4,18; 11:22,23

Cloud = theophanic presence of God. The pillar of cloud guided Israel, filled the temple, and appeared on the Day of Atonement over the mercy seat (Lev 16:2). Dan 7:13 "clouds of heaven" carries this theophanic significance.

## Related Topics Discovered

### Connection: ISA 24:5 and Dan 7:25
Nave's ORDINANCE entry lists ISA 24:5 which contains the phrase "changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant." This provides a Hebrew parallel to the Aramaic "think to change times and laws" of Dan 7:25. Both describe human presumption to alter divine decrees.

### Connection: Blasphemy Chain (Dan 7:25 -> 2 Thess 2:3-4 -> Rev 13:5-6)
Nave's BLASPHEMY entry explicitly groups DAN 7:25, 2TH 2:3,4, and REV 13:1,5,6 together — recognizing these as parallel descriptions of the same blasphemous activity.

### Connection: Antichrist "Already Present"
Nave's ANTICHRIST entry includes 1JN 2:18 ("even now are there many antichrists") and 4:3 ("even now already is it in the world"), which is significant for the HIST reading — the antichrist principle was operative in the first century, not exclusively future.
