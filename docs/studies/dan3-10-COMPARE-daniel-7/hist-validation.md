## Layer 1: Representation Issues

### Issue 1.1: HIST I-A item count discrepancy (minor)

**Section:** CONCLUSION.md, Positional Tally (lines 249, 255)

**Problem:** The tally table lists HIST I-A = 5, but the note below it enumerates six HIST I-A items: "I1 (shared), I3, I6, I9, I12, I17." If I1 is shared between HIST and FUT and counted once for each, HIST should have 6 I-A items (or the note should say 5 unique + 1 shared). As written, the number 5 and the list of 6 create a minor internal inconsistency. This does not misrepresent the HIST position's substance, but it could confuse a reader trying to verify the count.

**What needs to change:** Either the tally should read HIST I-A = 6 (counting I1 for both HIST and FUT, which would also raise FUT's count), or the note should clarify that I1 is counted only once and allocated to one position in the tally. The total I-A of 15 should then be rechecked for consistency.

## Layer 2: Grounding Issues

### Issue 2.1: HIST DB records a stronger papacy-agency claim for the three horns than the COMPARE study attributes (minor)

**Section:** CONCLUSION.md, Specification-Match Matrix commentary (line 291); 03-analysis.md, Adjudication Point 6 (lines 185-190)

**Problem:** The COMPARE study states that for Spec 2 (three displaced horns), "papal agency was indirect (Byzantine military action under Justinian)." This is accurate as a description of what the dan3-07 perspective study said in its Honest Weaknesses section. However, the HIST position DB record `hist-def-dan7-three-horn-identification` (from-negative-fill.jsonl) provides a stronger defense than the COMPARE study acknowledges: it specifically argues that (a) all three were Arian kingdoms whose removal cleared the path for papal supremacy, (b) the Arian theological factor unifies the identification, and (c) aqar (H6132) means uprooting by the roots — all three kingdoms were destroyed as political entities, not merely defeated. The gap-verification report also confirms EGW support for the specific Heruli/Vandals/Ostrogoths identification (BIAD 111.3; FUMP 31.3).

The COMPARE study's characterization of "papal agency indirect" is not wrong — it faithfully reproduces the dan3-07 weakness section — but it does not capture the full HIST defense that the DB provides. Since the COMPARE study is compiling what perspective studies said (not re-researching), this is a very minor issue: the weakness statement is accurate to the source, but the HIST position has stronger defenses available than the COMPARE acknowledges. This is more of a "the perspective study could have been stronger" note than a misrepresentation in the COMPARE.

**What needs to change:** No mandatory change. If desired, the COMPARE could add a parenthetical noting HIST's Arian-theological-unification defense, but since the COMPARE is faithfully carrying forward the dan3-07 classifications, this is acceptable as-is.
