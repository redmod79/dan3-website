# Topics Research

## Question
How does dispensationalist futurism read Daniel 8-9 and the 70 weeks, and what is the basis for the gap between weeks 69 and 70?

## Topics

### SEVENTY
- The senate of the Israelites composed of seventy elders (Exo 24:1,9; Num 11:16,24,25)
- The seventy disciples sent forth by Jesus (though the best Greek mss. have "seventy-two") (Luk 10:1-17)
- The Jews in captivity in Babylon for seventy years (Jer 25:11,12; 29:10; Dan 9:2; Zec 1:12; 7:5)
- 70 Weeks in the vision of Daniel (Dan 9:24)

### ANTICHRIST
- General scriptures concerning: Mat 24:5,23,24,26; Mrk 13:6,21,22; Luk 21:8; 2Th 2:3-12; 1Jn 2:18,22; 4:3; 2Jn 1:7
- To be destroyed: Rev 19:20; 20:10,15

### COVENANT
- Sacred (Jos 9:18-21; Gal 3:15)
- Binding (Jos 9:18-20; Jer 34:8-21; Ezk 17:14-18; Gal 3:15)
- Blood of (Exo 24:8)
- Book of (Exo 24:7)
- The Mosaic law called a covenant (Exo 34:28)
- **OF GOD WITH MEN**
  - Confirmed with an oath (Gen 22:16; 26:3; 50:24; Psa 89:35; 105:9; Luk 1:73; Heb 6:13,17,18)
  - Binding (Lev 26; Jer 11:2,3; Gal 3:15)
  - Everlasting (Gen 8:20-22; 9:1-17; Psa 105:8,10; Isa 54:10; 61:8)
  - God faithful to (Lev 26:44,45; Deu 4:31; 7:8,9; Jdg 2:1; 1Ki 8:23; Psa 105:8-11; 106:45; 111:5; Mic 7:20)
  - Repudiated by God on account of Jews' idolatry (Jer 44:26,27; Heb 8:9)
  - Broken by the Jews (Jer 22:9; Ezk 16:59; Heb 8:9)
- **INSTANCES OF**
  - With Abraham (Gen 12:1-3; 15; 17:1-22; Exo 6:4-8; Psa 105:8-11; Rom 9:7-13; Gal 3)
  - With David (2Sa 7:12-16; 1Ch 17:11-14; 2Ch 6:16)
  - With David and his house (2Sa 23:5; Psa 89:20-37; Jer 33:21)
  - With his people (Isa 55:3; 59:21)
  - **To be confirmed (Dan 9:27)**
- **THE SECOND COVENANT** (Jer 31:31-34; Heb 8:4-13; 12:18-24; 13:20)

### DANIEL
- A Jewish captive, also called BELTESHAZZAR
  - Educated at king's court (Dan 1)
  - Interprets visions (Dan 2; 4; 5)
  - Prophecies of (Dan 4:8,9; 7; 8; 9; 10; 11; 12; Mat 24:15)
  - Abstinence of (Dan 1:8-16)
  - Wisdom of (Dan 1:17; Ezk 28:3)
  - Devoutness of (Dan 2:18; 6; 9; 10; 12; Ezk 14:14)
  - Courage and fidelity of (Dan 4:27; 5:17-23; 6:10-23)

### NEHEMIAH
- Son of Hachaliah (Neh 1:1)
  - The cupbearer of Artaxerxes, a very trusted position (Neh 1:11; 2:1)
  - Is grieved over the desolation of his country (Neh 1)
  - Is sent by the king to rebuild Jerusalem (Neh 2:1-8)
  - Register of the people whom he led from Babylon (Neh 7)
  - Register of the priests and Levites (Neh 12:1-22)
  - Rebuilds Jerusalem (Neh 2; 3; 4; 5; 6)
  - His administration as ruler of the people (Neh 5; 6; 8; 9; 10; 11; 13)

### JERUSALEM
- Called: Jebus, Zion, City of David, Salem, Ariel, City of God, City of the Great King, Holy Mountain (Dan 9:16,20), Holy City (Neh 11:1,18; Mat 4:5)
- The capital of David's kingdom by divine appointment (1Ki 15:4; 2Ki 19:34; 2Ch 6:6; 12:13)
- Annual feasts kept at (Ezk 36:38; with Deu 16:16)
- Rebuilding of, ordered by proclamation of Cyrus (2Ch 36:23; Ezr 1:1-4)
- Rebuilt by Nehemiah under the direction of Artaxerxes (Neh 2; 3; 4; 5; 6)
- Wall of, dedicated (Neh 12:27-43)
- Prophecies against (Isa 3:1-8; Jer 9:11; 19:6,15; 21:10; 26:9,11; Dan 9:2,27; Mic 1:1; 3:12)
- Of the destruction of (Jer 7:32-34; 26:18; 32:29,31,32; Dan 9:24-27)
- Destruction foretold by Jesus (Mat 23:37,38; 24:15; Mrk 13:14-23; Luk 13:35; 17:26-37; 19:41-44; 21:20-24)
- Prophecies of the rebuilding of (Isa 44:28; Jer 31:38-40; Ezk 48:15-22; Dan 9:25; Zec 14:8-11)
- Of final restoration of (Jol 3:20,21; Zec 2:2-5; 8)

### ARTAXERXES
- A Persian king probably identical with AHASUERUS
  - Prohibits the rebuilding of Jerusalem (Ezr 4:7-24)
- King of Persia. Decree of, in behalf of the Jews (Ezr 7; Neh 2; 5:14)

### EZRA
- A famous scribe and priest (Ezr 7:1-5,6,10,21; Neh 12:36)
  - Appoints a fast (Ezr 8:21)
  - Commissioned by Artaxerxes, returns to Jerusalem with a large group of Jews (Ezr 7:8)
  - His charge to the priests (Ezr 8:29)
  - Exhorts people to put away their heathen wives (Ezr 9; 10:1-17)
  - Reads the law (Neh 8)
  - Reforms corruptions (Ezr 10; Neh 13)
  - Dedicates the wall of Jerusalem (Neh 12:27-43)

### SABBATIC YEAR
- (A rest that reoccurs every seventh year)
- Called YEAR OF RELEASE (Deu 15:9; 31:10)
- Ordinances concerning (Exo 23:9-11; Lev 25)
- Israelite bondservants set free in (Exo 21:2; Deu 15:12; Jer 34:14)
- Creditors required to release debtors in (Deu 15:1-6,12-18; Neh 10:31)
- Ordinances concerning instruction in the law during (Deu 31:10-13; Neh 8:18)
- Punishment to follow violation of the ordinances concerning (Lev 26:34,35; with 26:32-41; Jer 34:12-22)

### ABOMINATION

  - Uncleanness (Lev 18:22; 20:13; Deu 24:4)

- Unclassified scriptures: Deu 22:5; Pro 3:32; 6:16-19; 8:7; 11:20; 12:22; 15:8,9,26; 16:5; 17:15; 20:10,23; 21:27; 24:9; 28:9; 29:27
