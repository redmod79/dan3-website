## [H1431](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1431/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} — gadal (גָּדַל) — "be great, magnify"
- Part of Speech: verb
- BLB Count: 115
- Definition: to twist, i.e. to be (causatively make) large: advance, boast, exceed, great, grow up, increase, magnify, promote.
- Daniel 8 occurrences with stem analysis:
  - 8:4 הִגְדִּֽיל = Hiphil.Perf.3ms (Ram: causative "became great")
  - 8:8 הִגְדִּ֣יל = Hiphil.Perf.3ms + עד מאד (Goat: causative "exceedingly great")
  - 8:9 וַתִּגְדַּל = Qal.Wayq.3fs + יתר (Horn: organic "grew exceeding" — STEM SHIFT)
  - 8:10 וַתִּגְדַּ֖ל = Qal.Wayq.3fs (continues organic growth)
  - 8:11 הִגְדִּ֑יל = Hiphil.Perf.3ms (back to Hiphil: against Prince)
  - 8:25 יַגְדִּ֔יל = Hiphil.Impf.3ms + bilbav ("magnify in heart")
  - 11:36 יִתְגַּדֵּל = Hithpael.Impf.3ms (reflexive: "magnify HIMSELF")
