### 13. Eth Qets — "The Time of the End" (Dan 8:17, 8:19)

Dan 12:4,9 use the same formula in the context of bodily resurrection (12:2), making it impossible to confine eth qets to Antiochus. ### 14. Sar/Nagid Prince Chain (Dan 8:11, 8:25, 9:25, 11:22, 12:1)
- **DB argument:** Five prince titles in Daniel's prophetic chapters all refer to Christ: Messiah the Prince / nagid (9:25) -> Prince of the Host / sar ha-tsaba (8:11) -> Prince of princes / sar sarim (8:25) -> Prince of the Covenant (11:22) -> Michael the Great Prince / ha-sar ha-gadol (12:1). Sar sarim is a Hebrew superlative (GKC section 133), parallel to qodesh qodashim. The horn's war is specifically directed against THIS figure.
- **Action:** Look up [H8269](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8269/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (sar) with --lexicon and --verses, tracing all Daniel occurrences. Run hebrew_parser.py on Dan 8:11 (sar ha-tsaba) and Dan 8:25 (sar sarim). Retrieve Dan 9:25, Dan 11:22, Dan 12:1 for the complete chain. Document how the five titles unify Daniel's Christology.

### 18. Dan 8:27 Collapse as Day-Year Evidence
- **DB argument:** Dan 8:27 va-eshtomem uses the Hithpael of shamam (H8074) — the SAME ROOT as the sanctuary desolation (shomem in 8:13). A man ranked with Noah and Job (Ezek 14:14) does not collapse over ~6.3 literal years; only 2300 YEARS explains the severity. Three verbs describe the collapse: nihyeiti (Niphal of hayah, "I was undone"), necheleti (Niphal of chalah, "I was sick"), and va-eshtomem (Hithpael of shamam, "I was appalled/desolated").
- **Action:** Run hebrew_parser.py on Dan 8:27 (parsing all three collapse verbs). Verify that shamam (H8074) root appears in both 8:13 (shomem) and 8:27 (va-eshtomem). Retrieve Ezek 14:14 for the Noah/Job ranking. Document the shamam root connection as internal literary evidence.

### 19. Dan 8:26 Sealing Command and Mar'eh vs. Chazon Distinction
- **DB argument:** Dan 8:26 uses BOTH words in one sentence with different referents: "the mar'eh (H4758) of the evening and morning which was told is true: wherefore shut up the chazon (H2377)." The sealing command (shatam ha-chazon, "shut up the vision; for it shall be for many days") implies the 2300 period extends far beyond Daniel's lifetime — evidence for day-year. Gabriel in 9:23 returns to explain the mar'eh, not the chazon.
- **Action:** Run hebrew_parser.py on Dan 8:26.
