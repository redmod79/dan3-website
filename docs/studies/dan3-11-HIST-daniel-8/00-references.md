### From Study Plan (Integrate List)

**hist-04-daniel-8-little-horn-identified:**
- Question: What does Daniel 8's vision show? Does the Hebrew grammar of Daniel 8:8-9 require the little horn to come from the four Greek horns? How does the yether progression prove the little horn must exceed Greece?
- Key finding 1: mehem (3mp suffix) disagrees with both feminine antecedents (horns, winds); GKC Section 145 documents constructio ad sensum; Gabriel's own malkutam (8:23) validates masculine-for-feminine pattern
- Key finding 2: Three-stage gadal progression — Ram unmodified higgdil (8:4), Goat higgdil ad-me'od (8:8), Horn wattigdal yether (8:9); yether (H3499) = "surplus/excess" requiring horn to surpass BOTH empires
- Key finding 3: Verb stem shift from Hiphil (Ram/Goat, causative "caused to be great") to Qal (Horn, organic growth); when Daniel means personal pride he adds bilbav ("in heart," 8:25)
- Key finding 4: Directional indicators (negev, mizrach, tsebi) are all masculine geographic terms proving territorial expansion, not boasting
- Key finding 5: Hapax mits'eirah (H4704) = "from littleness," emphasizing rise from insignificance; Rome from city-state to greatest empire
- Key finding 6: az paniym (Dan 8:23) cross-reference to Deut 28:50 — only TWO OT passages with this construct chain; universally identified as Rome
- Key finding 7: Antiochus fails on yether requirement, directional permanence, "time of the end," "Prince of princes," "broken without hand," 2300-day math, "for many days," prophetic pattern
- Key finding 8: Every Daniel vision spans from prophet's time to eschatological end; Antiochus identification would break this pattern uniquely for Dan 8
- Classification: E/N-tier for named identifications and gadal progression; I-A for Rome identification (one step from three named kingdoms)

**hist-06-sanctuary-vindicated-1844:**
- Question: What does nitsdaq mean in Dan 8:14? Why forensic vocabulary instead of cleansing? What begins in 1844?
- Key finding 1: nitsdaq = Niphal Perfect 3ms of tsadaq (H6663) — forensic/legal verb meaning "shall be vindicated"; all 9 Niphal/passive occurrences in courtroom contexts; zero ritual cleansing
- Key finding 2: Daniel's deliberate vocabulary choice — taher (H2891, 94x "cleanse") and kaphar (H3722, 102x "atone") were available; Daniel uses kaphar in 9:24; chose tsadaq for 8:14 deliberately
- Key finding 3: LXX anomaly — OG dikaiothesatai ("shall be justified") vs Theodotion katharisthesetai ("cleansed"); standard LXX tsadaq -> dikaioo (21 co-occurrences, PMI 26.99); forensic meaning lost at Theodotion stage -> Jerome -> KJV
- Key finding 4: Evening-morning = creation pattern (Gen 1), NOT DOA formula (Lev 23:32 lacks morning component); Dan 8:26 back-references 8:14 with definite articles; Daniel uses yamim in same verse, proving deliberate choice
- Key finding 5: Dan 7:9-10 = judicial PROCESS (thrones placed, court convened, books opened); Dan 8:14 = VERDICT (nitsdaq); shared forensic domain (dina/mishpat ch.7; tsadaq/nitsdaq ch.8)
- Key finding 6: Son of Man comes TO Ancient of Days (three Aramaic directional indicators: 'ad + metah + haqrebuwhi) = approach to throne, opposite of second coming direction
- Key finding 7: Rev 14:7 combines BOTH themes — judgment (krisis = nitsdaq) AND creation (erev-boqer echoing Gen 1); creation catalogue echoes Exo 20:11
- Key finding 8: kaphar -> tsadaq progression traceable: Lev 16:30 (kaphar -> taher), Dan 9:24 (lekhapper -> tsedeq olamim), Isa 53:11 (bearing iniquities -> yatsdiq), Rom 3:25-26 (hilasterion -> dikaios)

**rome-daniel-8-little-horn:**

- Key finding 1: Systematic examination of ALL 24 specifications in Dan 8:9-14 and 8:23-25 — Rome matches 23 STRONG, 1 MODERATE-STRONG, 0 failures

- Key finding 3: Dan 8:13 dual structure — ha-tamid VE ha-pesha' shomem = two nouns with definite articles connected by conjunction = paganism AND papacy as two sequential desolating powers
- Key finding 4: "Broken without hand" (be'efes yad) echoes Dan 2:34,45 stone "without hands" — verbal parallel connecting horn's destruction to eschatological divine act
- Key finding 5: Antiochus fails eight critical specifications including yether (surpassing greatness), permanent directional growth, "time of the end," "Prince of princes," "broken without hand," 2300 days, "many days," prophetic pattern

**sanc-25-daniel-8-14-vindicated:**

### Grammar Studies (Integrate List)

**daniel-8-9-grammar-origin-little-horn (v1):**
- mehem = 3mp suffix; both antecedents (horns/winds) are feminine; strict agreement satisfied by neither
- Gabriel parallel: malkutam (8:23) = feminine noun + masculine suffix — same phenomenon
- Both readings of mehem support Rome: (a) from horns via Pergamum bequest/absorption; (b) from winds = western direction; (c) from broader world-scene
- Verb gender switch: yatsa (3ms) -> wattigdal (3fs) — masculine anticipates real-world melek confirmed in 8:23

**daniel-8-9-grammar-origin-little-horn-v2:**
- Five grammar-textbook-verified findings: (1) mehem is 3mp, (2) both antecedents feminine, (3) verb gender switch GKC 145t, (4) Gabriel's malkutam parallel GKC 135o, (5) all directional terms masculine
- Mixed-gender construction (ha-achat feminine + mehem masculine) = strongest syntactic indicator that "them" does not refer to four horns
- Reading D (constructio ad sensum for compass directions) rated "STRONGLY PERMITTED" — best conceptual fit with strongest textbook support
- Grammar does NOT decide identification; removes objection that "grammar requires Greek-horn origin"

**daniel-8-great-progression:**
- Three-stage gadal escalation with yether (H3499) = "excess/surplus/preeminence"
- Verb stem shift: Hiphil (causative, effort) for Ram/Goat -> Qal (organic/innate) for Horn
- Territorial vs personal pride: directional indicators (negev, mizrach, tsebi) prove territorial expansion; bilbav ("in heart," 8:25) marks personal pride as distinct
- Antiochus was ~3M km^2; Persia ~5.5-8M; Alexander ~5.2M — yether requires surpassing both; only Rome qualifies

**daniel-8-14-cleansed-hebrew-meaning:**
- nitsdaq = forensic vindication across all 54 tsadaq occurrences; KJV "cleansed" is the ONLY time tsadaq is rendered with cleansing vocabulary
- Forensic Q&A structure: question (8:13) uses injustice vocabulary (pesha, shomem, mirmac); answer (8:14) uses justice vocabulary (nitsdaq)
- Dan 9:24 vocabulary bridge: uses kaphar AND tsadaq root (tsedeq olamim), pesha (same as 8:12-13), qodesh (same as 8:14)
- chathak (H2852, hapax) = "cut off" — 70 weeks cut from 2300 days; biyn chain (8:16 -> 8:27 -> 9:2 -> 9:22 -> 9:23 -> 10:1)

**daniel-8-14-cleansed-word-study:**
- Confirms forensic meaning: every Niphal/passive of tsadaq in courtroom context (Job 9:2; 25:4; 13:18; Psa 51:4; 143:2; Isa 43:9,26; 45:25)
- Dan 9:24 bridge: "to finish THE transgression" (happesha, same as 8:13) + "everlasting righteousness" (tsedeq olamim, same root as 8:14)

**daniel-8-14-grammar-study:**
- erev boqer = one day cycle; grammatically closest to Genesis 1 (both include evening AND morning)
- DOA formula (Lev 23:32 me-erev ad-erev) fundamentally different — prepositions and NO morning
- Dan 8:26 back-references 8:14 with definite articles (ha-erev ve-ha-boqer) = anaphoric reference
- venitsdaq qodesh = Niphal Perfect 3ms + masculine singular absolute; passive forensic verdict

**daniel-8-14-evening-morning-vs-day-of-atonement:**
- Dan 8:14 is grammatically UNIQUE — two bare nouns without conjunction, prepositions, verbs, or yom
- Three critical findings: (1) DOA has NO morning, (2) Daniel AVOIDED using yom (uses yamim in 12:11), (3) Dan 8:14 construction is unique but echoes Genesis 1
- DOA connection to Dan 8-9 exists through other evidence (sanctuary context, nitsdaq theme, Dan 9:24 kaphar vocabulary, confession prayer, karat language), NOT through erev-boqer phrase

**daniel-8-23-deuteronomy-parallel:**
- az paniym (H5794 + H6440) as construct chain appears in only Deut 28:50 and Dan 8:23 — Nave's indexes only these two verses
- Daniel 9:11 explicitly cites "the curse written in the law of Moses" — Daniel writing within Mosaic covenant framework
- tamam/pasha word bridge: Dan 8:23 (kehatem + happosheim) mirrors Dan 9:24 (ulehatem + happesha) — identical forms; problem -> solution pairing
- pasha triangle: Dan 8:23 happosheim = Isa 53:12 posheim (identical Qal participle); LXX bridge pasha -> anomos -> Paul's ho anomos (2 Thess 2:8)
- chiydah motif: fierce king as perversion of wisdom tradition; Jesus fulfills chiydah (Mat 13:35 / Psa 78:2); fierce king uses chiydah for deception
- biyn chain: 6-link narrative across Dan 8-10; LXX biyn = noieo = Mat 24:15 "let him understand"
- Dan 8:24 "not by his own power" matches Deut 28:48 "enemies which the LORD shall send" — covenant-curse agent, not autonomous

**daniel-8-14-sanctuary-cleansed:**
- Confirms forensic meaning via Job connection: cosmic courtroom drama (Job 1-2 heavenly court parallels Dan 8 vision)
- Dan 7 judgment scene (7:9-10) = procedural context for 8:14 verdict; shared vocabulary domain
