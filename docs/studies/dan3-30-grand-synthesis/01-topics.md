# Topics — Grand Synthesis of Daniel's Prophetic Visions (dan3-30)

## Topic Coverage by Vision Cycle

### Daniel 2 (Metallic Image)
- **PROPHECY** — Four-kingdom succession; stone kingdom; divine sovereignty over history
- **KINGDOM OF HEAVEN** — Stone = God's everlasting kingdom (Dan 2:44); inauguration vs. consummation debate
- **COVENANT** — Batarakh succession language; iron-clay marriage imagery (arab H6151)
- **Covered in:** dan3-06-COMPARE-daniel-2

### Daniel 7 (Four Beasts and Judgment)
- **ANTICHRIST** — Little horn identity: speaks against Most High, wears out saints, changes times/law
- **BLASPHEMY** — Dan 7:25 mouth speaking great things; shanah parallel to Dan 2:21
- **PERSECUTION** — bela Pa'el (wearing out saints); duration semantics
- **KINGDOM OF HEAVEN** — Everlasting kingdom given to saints (Dan 7:14,18,27); triple le-'alamayya
- **Covered in:** dan3-10-COMPARE-daniel-7

### Daniel 8 (Ram, Goat, Little Horn)
- **HORN** — gadal/yether progression; mits'eirah hapax; three-directional expansion
- **SANCTUARY** — 2300 erev-boqer; nitsdaq (Niphal of tsadaq, forensic); tamid removal
- **ABOMINATION** — pesha shomem (transgression of desolation, Dan 8:13)
- **MESSIAH** — Prince of the host (sar ha-tsaba); Prince of princes (sar sarim)
- **Covered in:** dan3-14-COMPARE-daniel-8

### Daniel 8-9 (Seventy Weeks)
- **ATONEMENT** — Dan 9:24 six purposes; DOA triad (avon + pesha + chattat = Lev 16:21)
- **MESSIAH** — mashiach nagid (9:25); mashiach yikkaret (9:26); gabar berith (9:27)
- **COVENANT** — gabar (H1396) vs. karath; la-rabbim echo of Isa 53:11
- **PROPHECY** — chathak hapax; haben + mar'eh inclusio; biyn chain
- **Covered in:** dan3-18-COMPARE-daniel-8-9

### Daniel 10-12 (Final Vision)
- **ANTICHRIST** — Willful king (11:36); double Hithpael self-exaltation; kir'tsono chain
- **ABOMINATION** — shiqquts meshomem (Dan 11:31; 12:11); tamid chain continuity
- **PERSECUTION** — maskilim chain (11:33->11:35->12:3->12:10); purification triad bracket
- **PROPHECY** — eth qets chain (8:17; 11:35; 11:40; 12:4; 12:9); za'am bracket
- **Covered in:** dan3-22-COMPARE-daniel-10-12
