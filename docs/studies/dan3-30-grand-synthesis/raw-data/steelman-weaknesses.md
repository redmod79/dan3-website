## PRET Honest Weaknesses (dan3-28-pret-steelman)

### Key Strengths Summary
- Dan 8: Five I-A(1) HIGH specification matches (three-directional growth, tamid, sanctuary, host/transgression, broken without hand)
- Dan 11:2-35: Seven I-A(1) HIGH specification matches (extraordinary, essentially uncontested historical precision)
- Dan 8/Dan 11 five-point verbal correspondence (thematic rather than verbatim)
- Haphel shanah parallel (Dan 2:21 // 7:25) classified N-tier
- be-acharit malkutam timestamp (Dan 8:23) grammatically timestamped within Greek successor era

### Weaknesses (Ranked by Severity)

**FATAL:**

**F1. Dan 11:40-45 Five-Specification Failure (I-D LOW)**
- No documented third Egyptian campaign after Day of Eleusis (168 BC)
- No Antiochus control of Egypt, Libya, Ethiopia (11:42-43)
- Edom/Moab/Ammon escape list has no Maccabean referent (11:41)
- Death-location contradiction: Dan 11:45 says "between seas and holy mountain" but Antiochus died in Persia (I-D — explicit override)
- eth qets marker in 11:40 connects to Dan 12:2 resurrection
- CRIT variant concedes failed prediction; CONS variant appeals to prophetic foreshortening

**F2. Dan 12:2 Transcends Maccabean Framework (N-tier)**
- Dual-outcome bodily resurrection
- dera'on hapax pair locks to Isa 66:24 eschatological judgment
- Ezekiel 37 metaphorical defense fails (dual vs. single outcome; dera'on anchor)

**F3. Dan 12:13 Personal Resurrection Promise (N-tier)**
- "Thou shalt rest and stand in thy lot at the end of the days" — individual promise to Daniel
- Daniel died centuries before Maccabean era
- No adequate PRET response exists

**SEVERE:**

**S1. gadal/yether Scale Problem (I-B Strong against)**
- Antiochus ~3M km2 vs. Persia 5.5-8M km2, Greece 5.2M km2
- PRET redefines "greatness" as theological/cultural impact
- Text's progressive intensification with directional indicators naturally implies scope increase

**S2. nitsdaq Forensic Sense (I-B Strong toward forensic)**
- tsadaq forensic in 53/54 occurrences
- Old Greek dikaiothesatai forensic
- PRET depends on later Theodotion against Hebrew and Old Greek

**S3. 490-Year Arithmetic Failure (I-B Moderate against)**
- No starting decree yields Maccabean events: 538-490=48 BC; 605-490=115 BC; 586-490=96 BC
- Subdivisions (7+62+1) resist pure symbolism

**S4. Dan 8-9 Disconnection Thesis (I-B Strong against)**
- haben+mar'eh inclusio (E-tier), ba-chazon ba-techillah back-reference, chathak hapax, six-root vocabulary network

**S5. Triple Everlasting Kingdom (I-B Strong against Maccabean)**
- Dan 7:14,18,27 triple le-'alamayya 'almayya
- Hasmonean dynasty lasted ~77 years

**S6. NT Authors Consistently Apply Daniel Beyond Antiochus**
- Three independent authors (Jesus, Paul, John) extend Daniel's imagery ~200+ years beyond Antiochus

**MODERATE:**

**M1-M8:** eth qets chain, ka-chadah simultaneous destruction, batarakh succession language, bela Pa'el semantics, Dan 7:11 beast slain, time period imprecision, chathak "cut off FROM," six purposes exceed Maccabean

## FUT Honest Weaknesses (dan3-29-fut-steelman)

### Key Strengths Summary
- NT convergence: Three authors (Jesus, Paul, John) independently treating Daniel's figures as future
- eth qets chain extending to bodily resurrection (N-tier, shared)
- Rev 13:5 verbatim quotation of Dan 7:8 (E-tier literary connection)
- Shared ground with HIST: Rome = fourth kingdom at I-A(1) HIGH
- 2 Thess 2:4 hyperairomenos correspondence with Dan 11:36 yitromem
- Dan 11:45 unfulfilled geography (Antiochus demonstrably did not die at Jerusalem)

### Weaknesses (Ranked by Severity)

**CRITICAL:**

**1. Gap Thesis Lacks Textual Marker in Daniel (I-C LOW)**
- Legs-to-feet transition uses identical grammatical structure to every other body-part transition
- tselem chad ("one image") emphasizes organic unity
- Horns grow from beast's head (organic connection)
- No temporal conjunction, disjunctive clause, or narrative signal for a break
- Gap imported from dispensational framework, not text-derived

**2. Israel/Church Distinction Faces Six Convergent NT Counter-Passages (I-C LOW)**
- Gal 3:28-29, Rom 9:6-8, Rom 11:17-24, Eph 2:14-16, 1 Pet 2:9, Rom 2:28-29
- Three NT authors, six independent texts describe one unified people of God
- FUT's participation-vs.-replacement defense distinguishes soteriological from programmatic identity
- I-B Unresolved

**3. Mark 1:15 peplērotai Declares Timetable Fulfilled, Not Paused**
- Both perfects (peplērotai, ēngiken) indicate completed action
- Gal 4:4 parallels with "fulness of the time was come"
- FUT must explain declared-fulfilled timetable suspended 2000+ years

**SEVERE:**

**4. Dan 7:13 Direction: Son of Man Toward God, Not Toward Earth**
- Aramaic prepositions (we-'ad, meta', qodamohi, haqrebuhi) describe approach TO Ancient of Days
- FUT's equation with Second Coming requires NT reinterpretation argument

**5. Kingdom-Already-Present Texts (Five Independent Witnesses)**
- Matt 12:28 (ephthasen, aorist), Col 1:13 (metestesen, aorist), Heb 12:28, Rom 14:17, Mark 1:15
- Progressive dispensationalism accommodates but weakens strict gap thesis

**6. Stone/Cornerstone Chain Identifies Stone with First-Advent Work**
- Seven texts, five authors: Psa 118:22, Isa 8:14, Isa 28:16, Matt 21:42-44, Acts 4:11, Rom 9:33, 1 Pet 2:4-8

**7. la-rabbim Echo Connecting Dan 9:27 to Isaiah 53, Not Antichrist**
- Matt 26:28 ("shed for many [peri pollon]") confirms messianic reading

**MODERATE:**

**8-16:** No break marker at 11:36, no gap precedent in numbered countdown, Paul's naos = church, type/antitype no textual marker in Dan 8, "mystery of iniquity already works," pretribulation rapture absent pre-19th century, Anderson-Hoehner compounding assumptions, 360-day year inconsistency, Daniel's prayer against delay (al-te'achar)
