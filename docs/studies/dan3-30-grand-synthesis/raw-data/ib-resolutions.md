# I-B Resolution Summary — Compiled from All Studies

## What Is an I-B Item?

An I-B (Competing-Evidence) item represents a genuine textual tension where E/N-tier evidence exists on both sides. The resolution protocol (SIS) evaluates clarity, weight, and direction to determine how the tension resolves.
