## Layer 1: Representation Issues

### Issue 1: Honest Weaknesses Section Omits Four of Eight dan3-27 Weaknesses (Minor)

**Section:** CONCLUSION.md, HIST Position Summary (lines 172-177) and Position Profile Comparison table (line 160)

**Nature of Problem:** The synthesis lists four HIST honest weaknesses:
1. Dan 11:40-45 internal disagreement (MODERATE)
2. 508 AD starting point (LOW-MED)
3. 1290/1335 starting points (LOW)
4. Close of probation mechanism (LOW)

However, dan3-27's "Honest Weaknesses Ranked by Severity" (lines 518-536) lists EIGHT weaknesses:
1. KoN/KoS in Dan 11:40+ (Greatest Weakness)
2. Three-horn identification (I-A(2) MED)
3. chathak hapax limitation
4. 457 BC starting point
5. Heavenly sanctuary depends on Hebrews 8-9
6. AD 31 crucifixion date
7. 1290/1335 starting point (508 AD)
8. Close of probation (I-A(3))

The synthesis omits: three-horn identification (#2), chathak hapax limitation (#3), 457 BC starting point (#4), heavenly sanctuary dependence on Hebrews (#5), and AD 31 crucifixion date (#6). It also merges #2 and #7 (508 AD and 1290/1335).

**Assessment:** This is a MINOR issue rather than a strawmanning concern because the synthesis correctly notes that HIST's weaknesses "are concentrated at the history-mapping level" and correctly reports that HIST has 0 FATAL, 0 CRITICAL, 0 SEVERE weaknesses. The omitted weaknesses are all at the MED-to-LOW level and do not change the overall picture. However, to be precise, the "4 MODERATE" count in the Position Profile Comparison table should reflect the fact that dan3-27 identified 8 weaknesses. The synthesis appears to have condensed them rather than omitted them, since items #2/#4/#6 are history-mapping weaknesses and items #3/#5 are methodology-level weaknesses. The condensed count still does not misrepresent the severity level.

**Recommendation:** Consider a footnote or parenthetical noting "8 weaknesses total documented in dan3-27, 4 highest-level summarized here" or adjusting the weakness count in the Position Profile Comparison table. Not urgent.

## Layer 2: Grounding Issues

### Issue 1: Severity Taxonomy Mapping for HIST Weaknesses (Minor)

**Section:** Position Profile Comparison table (CONCLUSION.md, lines 157-160)

**Nature of Problem:** The FATAL/CRITICAL/SEVERE/MODERATE taxonomy was introduced in dan3-28 (PRET steelman) and dan3-29 (FUT steelman). The dan3-27 (HIST steelman) used a ranked list (Rank 1-8) without these severity categories. The synthesis mapped HIST's weaknesses to "4 MODERATE" in the Position Profile Comparison table. This mapping is reasonable — none of HIST's weaknesses reach the severity of PRET's FATAL items (I-D overrides of textual data) or FUT's CRITICAL items (framework dependencies that are I-C LOW). But the mapping is editorially imposed, not drawn from the source study.

**Assessment:** MINOR. The mapping is defensible based on the actual severity of HIST's weaknesses. Dan3-27's Rank 1 weakness (KoN/KoS) operates at I-A(2-3) LOW-MED — this does not rise to SEVERE or CRITICAL level since it involves internal disagreement on a specific identification rather than contradiction with textual data. The remaining weaknesses are at history-mapping or hapax-limitation levels. The "4 MODERATE" classification is a reasonable synthesis judgment, though it would be more transparent to note the cross-study mapping.

**Recommendation:** No change required. Could optionally add a note that "HIST's 8 honest weaknesses (dan3-27 Ranks 1-8) are classified here as MODERATE because none involve textual contradictions (FATAL), framework-level failures (CRITICAL), or multiple-E/N-grounded tensions (SEVERE)."
