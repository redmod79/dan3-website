# Topics Research

## Question
Does the Bible claim genuine predictive prophecy? Examine every passage where God claims prophetic foreknowledge as evidence of His deity. Does the Bible's own argument for God's deity rest on predictive prophecy being genuine? If so, what does that mean for the preterist presupposition that genuine predictive prophecy doesn't occur?

## Topics

**Sure fulfillment:** EZK 12:22-25,28; HAB 2:3; MAT 5:18; 24:35; ACT 13:27,29

### PROPHETS
**Inspiration of:** The PROPHETS entry provides an extensive catalog of prophetic inspiration, including AMO 3:7,8; 2PE 1:21; HEB 1:1; 1PE 1:10,11; REV 10:7. Key passages establishing prophetic inspiration as proceeding from God rather than human initiative.

**False Prophets:** The entry distinguishes true from false prophets (1KI 13:18; JER 23:25-27; DEU 13:1-3; DEU 18:20), confirming that Scripture has a clear test for genuine vs. false prophecy.

### IDOLATRY (Folly of)
The IDOLATRY entry's "Folly of" section directly links the inability to predict with the folly of idol worship:
- ISA 41:23,24,26-29 -- idols challenged to predict; they cannot
- ISA 43:9 -- who among the nations/gods can declare?
- ISA 44:9-20 -- satire on idol manufacture
- ISA 45:20 -- idols cannot save; no knowledge
- ISA 46:1,2,6,7 -- Bel and Nebo collapse; idols must be carried
- ISA 47:12-15 -- Babylon's astrologers cannot save
- JER 10:3-16 -- idols are "the work of errors"
- HAB 2:18,19 -- "What profiteth the graven image?"

This classification confirms that the Bible itself treats inability to predict as a mark of false deity and ability to predict as a mark of true deity.

### CYRUS
- Issues decree for emancipation of Jews and rebuilding the temple: 2CH 36:22,23; EZR 1; 3:7; 4:3; 5:13,14; 6:3
- Prophecies concerning: ISA 13:17-22; 21:2; 41:2; 44:28; 45:1-4,13; 46:11; 48:14,15

The CYRUS entry directly links the historical decree (fulfillment) with the prophetic texts (prediction), treating them as a single prediction-fulfillment sequence.

### PREDESTINATION
General scriptures include ISA 44:1,2,7 (linked to the trial speeches), JER 1:4,5, ACT 2:23 (crucifixion foreordained), EPH 1:4,5,9-11, 1PE 1:2,20 (Christ foreordained before the foundation of the world).

### TESTIMONY (Religious)
- ISA 43:10 -- "Ye are my witnesses, saith the LORD"
- ISA 44:8 -- "ye are even my witnesses"
- ISA 45:24 -- testimony to God's righteousness

God calls Israel as "witnesses" specifically in the context of the trial speeches where He demonstrates His predictive power against the idols. Israel's witness-role is tied to God's demonstrated ability to predict.
