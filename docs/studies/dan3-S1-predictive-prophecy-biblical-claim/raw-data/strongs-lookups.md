# Raw Strong's Lookup Data

## [H5046](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5046/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - nagad (declare, report, announce, predict)
- Transliteration: naw-gad
- Part of Speech: verb
- BLB Count: 370
- Definition: a primitive root; properly, to front, i.e. stand boldly out opposite; by implication (causatively), to manifest; figuratively, to announce (always by word of mouth to one present); specifically, to expose, predict, explain, praise
- Translations: bewray, certainly, certify, declare(-ing), denounce, expound, fully, messenger, plainly, profess, rehearse, report, shew (forth), speak, surely, tell, utter
- As "declare" (11x): Jdg 14:12, Jdg 14:13, Job 38:4, Job 38:18, Psa 9:11, Psa 97:6, Isa 42:9, Isa 42:12, Isa 48:6, Jer 42:20, Eze 40:4
- Key Isaiah occurrences: Isa 3:9; 7:2; 19:12; 21:2; 40:21; 42:12; 44:7; 48:6; 57:12; 58:1

## [H7225](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h7225/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - reshith (beginning, first)

## [G4293](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4293/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - prokatangello (to announce beforehand)
- Transliteration: prok-at-ang-ghel-lo
- Part of Speech: verb
- BLB Count: 4 (5 total occurrences)
- Definition: from pro and katangello; to announce beforehand, i.e. predict, promise
- 5 occurrences, 5 unique translations:
  - "before had shewed" (Act 3:18)
  - "have" (?)
  - "foretold" (Act 3:24)
  - "shewed before" (Act 7:52)
  - "whereof ye had notice before" (2Co 9:5)
- CRITICAL: This verb combines pro- ("before") + kat-angello ("announce publicly") = "announce publicly beforehand" = predict

## [G4303](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4303/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - promarturomai (to testify beforehand)
