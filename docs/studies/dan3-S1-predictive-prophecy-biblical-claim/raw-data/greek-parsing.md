# Raw Greek Parsing Data

## 2 Peter 1:19
Greek: καὶ ἔχομεν βεβαιότερον τὸν προφητικὸν λόγον, ᾧ καλῶς ποιεῖτε προσέχοντες ὡς λύχνῳ φαίνοντι ἐν αὐχμηρῷ τόπῳ, ἕως οὗ ἡμέρα διαυγάσῃ καὶ φωσφόρος ἀνατείλῃ ἐν ταῖς καρδίαις ὑμῶν·

Key words:
- ἔχομεν (echo) [G2192](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2192/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Pres Act Ind 1P - "we have" (present tense - currently possess)
- βεβαιότερον (bebaios) [G949](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g949/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Acc Sg M Comparative - "more sure/firm" (COMPARATIVE degree -- more confirmed)
- προφητικὸν (prophetikos) [G4397](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4397/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Acc Sg M - "prophetic" (pertaining to a foreteller)
- λόγον (logos) [G3056](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3056/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Acc Sg M - "word"
- προσέχοντες (prosecho) [G4337](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4337/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Pres Act Ptcp Nom Pl M - "giving heed to"
- λύχνῳ (lychnos) [G3088](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3088/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Dat Sg M - "a lamp"
- φαίνοντι (phaino) [G5316](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5316/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Pres M/P Ptcp Dat Sg M - "shining"
- ἐν αὐχμηρῷ τόπῳ - "in a dark/dry place"

NOTE: The "prophetic word" (prophetikon logon) is characterized as "more sure" (bebaioteron) -- comparative form meaning more confirmed/established than something else (context: than even the eyewitness experience of the Transfiguration, vv. 16-18). Peter elevates prophetic Scripture ABOVE eyewitness testimony.

## 2 Peter 1:20

- γινώσκοντες (ginosko) [G1097](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1097/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Pres Act Ptcp Nom Pl M - "knowing"

- γραφῆς (graphe) [G1124](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1124/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Gen Sg F - "of Scripture"

- ἐπιλύσεως (epilysis) [G1955](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1955/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Gen Sg F - "interpretation/explanation"

NOTE: "No prophecy of Scripture is of private interpretation (epilysis)" -- the word epilysis means "unloosing, release, explanation." Prophecy does not originate from human interpretation/explanation.

## 2 Peter 1:21
Greek: οὐ γὰρ θελήματι ἀνθρώπου ἠνέχθη προφητεία ποτέ, ἀλλὰ ὑπὸ Πνεύματος Ἁγίου φερόμενοι ἐλάλησαν ἀπὸ Θεοῦ ἄνθρωποι.

Key words:
- θελήματι (thelema) [G2307](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2307/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Dat Sg N - "by [the] will" (dative of means)
- ἀνθρώπου (anthropos) [G444](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g444/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Gen Sg M - "of man"
- ἠνέχθη (phero) [G5342](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5342/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Aor Pass Ind 3S - "was brought/carried" (AORIST PASSIVE -- prophecy was brought BY SOMETHING EXTERNAL, not self-generated)
- προφητεία (propheteia) [G4394](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4394/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Nom Sg F - "prophecy"
- ποτέ (pote) [G4218](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4218/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - "at any time/ever"
- ὑπὸ (hypo) [G5259](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5259/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - "by" (agent marker - by the Holy Spirit)
- Πνεύματος Ἁγίου (pneuma hagios) [G4151](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4151/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}/G40 - Gen Sg N - "of [the] Holy Spirit"
- φερόμενοι (phero) [G5342](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5342/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Pres Pass Ptcp Nom Pl M - "being carried/borne along" (PRESENT PASSIVE PARTICIPLE -- continuous passive action)
- ἐλάλησαν (laleo) [G2980](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2980/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Aor Act Ind 3P - "they spoke" (aorist -- specific historical events)
- ἀπὸ Θεοῦ (apo theos) - "from God"

CRITICAL: Two uses of phero (G5342):
1. ἠνέχθη (passive aorist) - prophecy was "brought/carried" -- not by human will
2. φερόμενοι (passive present participle) - men were "being carried/borne along" by the Holy Spirit
The metaphor is of a ship carried along by wind. Prophets were carried along by the Holy Spirit -- they did not generate the prophecy themselves. This makes prophecy EXTERNAL to human knowledge/will.

## 1 Peter 1:10
Greek: περὶ ἧς σωτηρίας ἐξεζήτησαν καὶ ἐξηραύνησαν προφῆται οἱ περὶ τῆς εἰς ὑμᾶς χάριτος προφητεύσαντες,

Key words:
- σωτηρίας (soteria) [G4991](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4991/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Gen Sg F - "salvation"
- ἐξεζήτησαν (ekzeteo) [G1567](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1567/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Aor Act Ind 3P - "sought out/inquired diligently"
- ἐξηραύνησαν (exeraunao) [G1830](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1830/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Aor Act Ind 3P - "searched out diligently"
- προφῆται (prophetes) [G4396](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4396/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Nom Pl M - "prophets"
- προφητεύσαντες (propheteuo) [G4395](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4395/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Aor Act Ptcp Nom Pl M - "having prophesied"
- χάριτος (charis) [G5485](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5485/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Gen Sg F - "grace"
- εἰς ὑμᾶς - "for/unto you" (the grace destined for the NT readers)

NOTE: The prophets themselves "inquired and searched diligently" (two compound verbs with ek-/ex- prefix = intensive) about the salvation they prophesied. This implies they delivered prophecies whose full meaning they did not understand -- they prophesied something beyond their own comprehension.

## 1 Peter 1:11
Greek: ἐραυνῶντες εἰς τίνα ἢ ποῖον καιρὸν ἐδήλου τὸ ἐν αὐτοῖς Πνεῦμα Χριστοῦ προμαρτυρόμενον τὰ εἰς Χριστὸν παθήματα καὶ τὰς μετὰ ταῦτα δόξας·

Key words:
- ἐραυνῶντες (eraunao) [G2045](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2045/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Pres Act Ptcp Nom Pl M - "searching"
- τίνα ἢ ποῖον καιρὸν - "what or what manner of time" (they searched for WHEN and HOW)
- ἐδήλου (deloo) [G1213](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1213/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Impf Act Ind 3S - "was indicating/making clear" (imperfect = ongoing past action)
- τὸ...Πνεῦμα Χριστοῦ - "the Spirit of Christ" (subject -- the Spirit was doing the indicating)
- προμαρτυρόμενον (promarturomai) [G4303](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4303/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Pres M/P Ptcp Nom Sg N - "TESTIFYING BEFOREHAND"
- τὰ εἰς Χριστὸν παθήματα - "the sufferings unto/for Christ"
- τὰς μετὰ ταῦτα δόξας - "the glories after these things"

CRITICAL: [G4303](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4303/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} promarturomai = pro (before) + marturomai (testify/witness). Present middle/passive participle. The Spirit of Christ was "testifying beforehand" (promartyromenon) about:
1. The sufferings of Christ (ta eis Christon pathemata)
2. The glories that would follow (tas meta tauta doxas)
This is explicit advance testimony -- the Spirit predicted specific future events through the prophets.

## Acts 3:18
Greek: ὁ δὲ Θεὸς ἃ προκατήγγειλεν διὰ στόματος πάντων τῶν προφητῶν, παθεῖν τὸν Χριστὸν αὐτοῦ ἐπλήρωσεν οὕτως.

Key words:
- Θεὸς (theos) [G2316](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2316/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Nom Sg M - "God" (subject)
- ἃ (hos) [G3739](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3739/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Acc Pl N - "the things which" (relative pronoun, neuter plural)
- προκατήγγειλεν (prokatangello) [G4293](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4293/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Aor Act Ind 3S - "HE ANNOUNCED BEFOREHAND"
- διὰ στόματος (dia stoma) - "through the mouth of"
- πάντων τῶν προφητῶν (pas prophetes) - "ALL the prophets"
- παθεῖν (pascho) [G3958](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3958/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Aor Act Inf - "to suffer" (content of the announcement)
- τὸν Χριστὸν (Christos) [G5547](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5547/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Acc Sg M - "the Christ"
- ἐπλήρωσεν (pleroo) [G4137](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4137/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - Aor Act Ind 3S - "he fulfilled" (AORIST - completed action)
- οὕτως (houto) [G3779](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3779/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - "thus/in this way"

CRITICAL: [G4293](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4293/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} prokatangello = pro (before) + kat-angello (announce publicly). God "announced publicly beforehand" (prokatenggeilen) through the mouth of ALL the prophets that Christ would suffer -- and then HE FULFILLED (eplerosen) it. The structure is: prior announcement → subsequent fulfillment. This is the NT's explicit claim about the nature of OT prophecy.
