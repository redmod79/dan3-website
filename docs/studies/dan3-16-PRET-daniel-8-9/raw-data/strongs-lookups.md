# Raw Strong's Lookups

## [H4899](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4899/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} mashiach (anointed one)
- BLB Count: 39
- Transliteration: mâshîyach
- POS: masculine noun
- Definition: from; anointed; usually a consecrated person (as a king, priest, or saint); specifically, the Messiah
- Translations: "anointed" 11x, "that is anointed" 4x, "his anointed" 4x, "of thine anointed" 4x, "mine anointed" 3x, "the anointed" 3x, "to his anointed" 3x, "of his anointed" 2x, "thine anointed" 2x, "the Messiah" 1x (DAN 9:25), "Messiah" 1x (DAN 9:26)
- KEY: Only 2 of 44 occurrences translate as "Messiah" -- both in Dan 9:25-26
- All others translate as "anointed" applied to priests, kings, or God's people
- Occurrences: LEV 4:3,5,16; 6:22; 1SA 2:10,35; 12:3,5; 16:6; 24:6,10; 26:9,11,16,23; 2SA 1:14,16,21; 19:21; 22:51; 23:1; 1CH 16:22; 2CH 6:42; PSA 2:2; 18:50; 20:6; 28:8; 84:9; 89:38,51; 105:15; 132:10,17; ISA 45:1; LAM 4:20; DAN 9:25,26; HAB 3:13
- LXX Translation: primarily Christos (G5547) 34x

## [H2852](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2852/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} chathak (cut off / determine)
- BLB Count: 1 (HAPAX LEGOMENON)
- Transliteration: châthak
- POS: verb
- Definition: a primitive root; properly, to cut off, i.e. (figuratively) to decree
- Only occurrence: DAN 9:24
- LXX: No alignments found
- CRITICAL: Primary meaning is "cut off" not "determine"; figurative extension to "decree"

## [H2782](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2782/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} charats (decide / determine)
- BLB Count: 12
- Transliteration: chârats
- POS: verb
- Definition: a primitive root; properly, to point sharply, i.e. (literally) to wound; figuratively, to be alert, to decide
- Translations: "even determined" 2x, "determined" 1x, "decreed" 1x, "are determined" 1x, "that determined" 1x, "that that is determined" 1x, plus others (move, maim, bestir, decide)
- KEY Daniel occurrences: DAN 9:26, 9:27, 11:36 -- Daniel used this word for "determine/decree" THREE TIMES, yet chose the hapax chathak (H2852) for 9:24

## [H4758](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4758/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} mar'eh (vision / appearance)
- BLB Count: 103
- Transliteration: marʼeh
- POS: masculine noun
- Definition: a view (the act of seeing); also an appearance; or a vision
- Range: "appearance" (physical looks), "vision" (prophetic), "sight" (general)
- KEY Daniel occurrences: 1:4,13,15 (appearance); 8:15,16,26,27 (vision); 9:23 (understand the mar'eh); 10:1,6,18
- NOTE: 8:26 uses BOTH mar'eh (the evening-morning vision) and chazon (seal up the vision) in the SAME verse with DIFFERENT referents

## [H2377](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2377/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} chazon (vision)
- BLB Count: 35
- Transliteration: châzôwn
- POS: masculine noun
- Definition: a sight (mentally), i.e. a dream, revelation, or oracle
- KEY Daniel occurrences: 1:17; 8:1,2,13,15,17,26; 9:21,24; 10:14; 11:14
- NOTE in 8:26: "shut up the chazon" (the broad prophetic vision); "the mar'eh of the evening and morning is true" (the specific time element)
- NOTE in 9:21: "whom I had seen in the chazon at the beginning" (reference back to Dan 8)
- NOTE in 9:24: "seal up chazon and prophet" (one of the six purposes)

## [H1396](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1396/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} gabar (strengthen / prevail)
- BLB Count: 25 (31 occurrences)
- Transliteration: gâbar
- POS: verb
- Definition: to be strong; by implication, to prevail, act insolently
- Translations: "prevailed" 8x, "prevail" 3x, "are mighty" 1x, "shall confirm" (DAN 9:27) = only instance as "confirm"
- KEY: "And he shall confirm" (DAN 9:27) = ve-higbir (Hiphil perf 3ms) -- "and he shall make strong/prevail"
- GEN 7:18-24: waters "prevailed" upon the earth (gabar = physical overwhelming force)
- Only DAN 9:27 translates as "confirm" -- all other uses mean "prevail/strengthen/be mighty"

## [H3772](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3772/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} karath (cut off / make covenant)
- BLB Count: 288
- Definition: to cut (off, down or asunder); by implication, to destroy or consume; specifically, to covenant (i.e. make an alliance or bargain, originally by cutting flesh and passing between the pieces)
- KEY: This is the STANDARD Hebrew verb for "make a covenant" (karath berith = "cut a covenant")
- It is NOT used in Dan 9:27 -- gabar berith is used instead, unique in Scripture

## LXX Mappings

### [H4899](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4899/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} mashiach LXX translations:
- [G5547](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5547/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} Christos (anointed/Christ): 34 occurrences, PMI 9.06 -- DOMINANT translation
- [G2018](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2018/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} epiphero: 6 occ, PMI 8.04
- [G4991](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4991/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} soteria (salvation): 4 occ, PMI 4.57
- [G1138](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1138/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} Dabid (David): 10 occ, PMI 2.80
- KEY: The LXX consistently translates mashiach as Christos, including in Dan 9:25-26 (Theodotion). This is significant: the LXX translators understood mashiach as "the anointed one" (Christos), the same word the NT uses for Jesus.

### [H2852](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2852/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} chathak LXX translation:

- NOTE: The LXX translation of Daniel is notoriously divergent from the MT. The Old Greek (OG) Daniel is so different that the church replaced it with Theodotion's translation. The absence of alignment data for this hapax is expected.
