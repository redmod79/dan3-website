### Leviticus 16:15-34 (Day of Atonement)

> **16:15** Then shall he kill the goat of the sin offering [chattat], that is for the people, and bring his blood within the vail...
> **16:16** And he shall make an atonement for the holy place, because of the uncleanness of the children of Israel, and because of their **transgressions [pesha, H6588] in all their sins [chattat, H2403]**: and so shall he do for the tabernacle of the congregation, that remaineth among them in the midst of their uncleanness.
> **16:20** And when he hath made an end of reconciling the holy place...
> **16:21** And Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat, and confess over him all the **iniquities [avon, H5771]** of the children of Israel, and all their **transgressions [pesha, H6588] in all their sins [chattat, H2403]**, putting them upon the head of the goat...
> **16:22** And the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited...
> **16:30** For on that day shall the priest make an atonement for you, to cleanse you, that ye may be clean from all your sins before the LORD.
> **16:33** And he shall make an atonement for the holy sanctuary, and he shall make an atonement for the tabernacle of the congregation, and for the altar...
> **16:34** And this shall be an everlasting statute unto you, to make an atonement for the children of Israel for all their sins once a year.

**CRITICAL NOTE:** LEV 16:21 is the ONLY Pentateuch verse containing all three sin-words together: avon (H5771) + pesha (H6588) + chattat (H2403). DAN 9:24 contains the same three: pesha ("finish the transgression") + chattat ("make an end of sins") + avon ("make reconciliation for iniquity"). This is a Day of Atonement liturgical fingerprint in Daniel's 70-weeks prophecy.

> **2:5** And I said unto the king... that thou wouldest send me unto Judah, unto the city of my fathers' sepulchres, that **I may build it**.

### PRD-7: Genesis 7:18-24 (gabar = "prevailed")
> **7:18** And the waters **prevailed** [gabar], and were increased greatly upon the earth; and the ark went upon the face of the waters.
> **7:19** And the waters **prevailed exceedingly** upon the earth; and all the high hills, that were under the whole heaven, were covered.
> **7:20** Fifteen cubits upward did the waters **prevail**; and the mountains were covered.
> **7:24** And the waters **prevailed** upon the earth an hundred and fifty days.

NOTE: gabar in Genesis flood = physical overwhelming force/dominance. This is the most common usage of gabar -- "prevail/dominate" -- not "confirm." DAN 9:27 is the ONLY place gabar is translated "confirm."
