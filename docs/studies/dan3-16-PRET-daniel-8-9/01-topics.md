# Topics Research

## Question
How does the preterist school read Daniel 8-9 and the 70 weeks, and what is the basis for disconnecting Dan 9 from Dan 8?

## Topics

### GABRIEL
- A messenger of God
- Appeared to: Daniel (DAN 8:16; 9:21), Zacharias (LUK 1:11-19), Mary (LUK 1:26-29)
- Note: Gabriel appears in ONLY these four passages in Scripture. His appearance in both Dan 8:16 and 9:21 creates an inherent textual link between the two chapters.

### DANIEL
- Jewish captive, also called Belteshazzar
- Prophecies of: DAN 4:8,9; 7; 8; 9; 10; 11; 12; MAT 24:15
- Wisdom of: DAN 1:17; EZK 28:3
- Devoutness of: DAN 2:18; 6; 9; 10; 12; EZK 14:14
- Note: Jesus references Daniel's prophecy by name in MAT 24:15 regarding the abomination of desolation.

### SEVENTY
- The senate of the Israelites composed of seventy elders: EXO 24:1,9; NUM 11:16,24,25
- The seventy disciples sent forth by Jesus: LUK 10:1-17
- **The Jews in captivity in Babylon for seventy years:** JER 25:11,12; 29:10; DAN 9:2; ZEC 1:12; 7:5
- **70 Weeks in the vision of Daniel:** DAN 9:24
- Note: The direct literary connection between Jeremiah's 70 years and Daniel's 70 weeks is established by DAN 9:2 explicitly referencing JER 25:11-12 and 29:10.

### COVENANT
- Sacred: JOS 9:18-21; GAL 3:15
- Binding: JOS 9:18-20; JER 34:8-21; EZK 17:14-18
- Blood of: EXO 24:8
- The Mosaic law called a covenant: EXO 34:28
- OF GOD WITH MEN:
  - Confirmed with an oath: GEN 22:16; PSA 89:35; HEB 6:13,17,18
  - Everlasting: GEN 8:20-22; 9:1-17; PSA 105:8,10; ISA 54:10; 61:8
  - To be confirmed: DAN 9:27
- THE SECOND COVENANT: JER 31:31-34; HEB 8:4-13; 12:18-24; 13:20
- Note: Nave's links DAN 9:27 "confirm the covenant" to God's covenant, not a foreign treaty. The standard Hebrew for "make covenant" is karath berith (H3772 + H1285); Dan 9:27 uses gabar berith (H1396 + H1285) -- unique in Scripture.

### ATONEMENT
- For tabernacle and furniture: LEV 16:15-20,33
- DAY OF:
  - Time: EXO 30:10; LEV 23:27; 25:9; NUM 29:7
  - How observed: LEV 16:2-34; 23:27-32; HEB 5:3; 9:7,19,22
- MADE BY ANIMAL SACRIFICES: LEV 1:4; 4:20,22-35; 16:6-34; 17:11; HEB 9:22
- MADE BY JESUS: typified by LEV 16:30,34 with HEB 9:7,12,28
- UNCLASSIFIED: **DAN 9:24-27**; ISA 53:4-12; ROM 3:24-26; HEB 9:12-15,25,26
- Note: Nave's classifies DAN 9:24-27 under ATONEMENT, connecting the six purposes of 9:24 to the atonement theology of the Bible.

### ANOINTING
- IN CONSECRATION:
  - OF HIGH PRIESTS: EXO 29:7,29; 40:13; LEV 6:20; 8:12; 16:32
  - OF PRIESTS: EXO 28:41; 30:30; 40:15; LEV 4:3; 8:30
  - OF KINGS: Saul (1SA 9:16; 10:1), David (1SA 16:3,12,13; 2SA 2:4; 5:3), Solomon (1KI 1:39), Jehu (1KI 19:16; 2KI 9:1-3)
  - Cyrus: ISA 45:1
  - OF PROPHETS: 1KI 19:16
- FIGURATIVE:
  - **Of Christ's kingly and priestly office:** PSA 45:7; 89:20; ISA 61:1; **DAN 9:24**; LUK 4:18; ACT 4:27; 10:38; HEB 1:9
  - Of spiritual gifts: 2CO 1:21; 1JN 2:20,27
- Note: The anointing language spans priests, kings, and even a pagan king (Cyrus). The PRET argues mashiach without the definite article means "an anointed one" (generic), not "THE Messiah" (specific).

### VISION
- A mode of revelation: NUM 12:6; 1SA 3:1; DAN 1:17; HOS 12:10; JOL 2:28; HAB 2:2
- Of Daniel:
  - Of the four beasts: DAN 7
  - Of the Ancient of Days: DAN 7:9-27
  - **Of the ram and the he-goat: DAN 8**
  - Of the angel: DAN 10
- Note: DAN 9 is NOT listed separately as a "vision" in Nave's -- it is categorized under Daniel's prophecies rather than visions, which is interesting given that 9:23 uses the word mar'eh.

### SACRILEGE
- Profaning holy things
- Forbidden: LEV 19:8; 1CO 3:17
- INSTANCES: Uzziah (2CH 26:16-21), Ahaz (2CH 28:24), money-exchangers in the temple (MAT 21:12,13)
- Note: Temple desecration is a key element of the PRET reading -- Antiochus IV's installation of the Zeus altar in 167 BC as the abomination of desolation.

### ABOMINATION
- Things that are, to God: Idolatry (DEU 7:25; 27:15; 32:16)
- UNCLASSIFIED: PRO 6:16-19; 11:20; 15:8,9,26; 16:5; 17:15
- Note: The Hebrew shiqquts (H8251, "detestable thing/idol") in DAN 9:27 and 11:31 is distinct from to'ebah (the more common "abomination" word). Shiqquts specifically refers to idolatrous objects.

### CAPTIVITY
- Foretold: LEV 26:33; DEU 28:36
- Of Judah in Babylon, prophecy: ISA 39:6; JER 13:19; 20:4; 25:2-11; 32:28
- Fulfilled: 2KI 24:11-16; 25; 2CH 36; JER 52:28-30
- Jews return from: EZR 2; 3; 8
- As a judgment: EZR 5:12; 9:7; ISA 5:13; JER 29:17-19
- FIGURATIVE: ISA 61:1; ROM 7:23

### SABBATIC YEAR
- A rest recurring every seventh year
- Called YEAR OF RELEASE: DEU 15:9; 31:10
- Ordinances: EXO 23:9-11; LEV 25
- Punishment for violation: **LEV 26:34,35** with 26:32-41; JER 34:12-22
- Note: 2CH 36:21 explicitly connects the 70-year captivity to the sabbatic year violations -- "until the land had enjoyed her sabbaths." This provides the sabbatical-year framework for interpreting the 70 x 7 structure.

### JUBILEE
- Called THE ACCEPTABLE YEAR OF THE LORD: ISA 61:2
- Called THE YEAR OF LIBERTY: EZK 46:17
- LAWS CONCERNING: LEV 25:8-55; 27:17-24; NUM 36:4
- Note: LEV 25:8 -- "seven sabbaths of years... seven times seven years = 49 years." The 70 weeks (490 years) = 10 jubilee cycles, providing the mathematical framework for the PRET "schematic periodization" argument.

### PROPHECY
- Inspired: ISA 28:22; 2TI 3:16; 2PE 1:21
- Sure fulfillment: EZK 12:22-25; HAB 2:3; MAT 5:18; 24:35
- CONCERNING THE MESSIAH: Extensive list of OT passages quoted in NT
  - PSA 2:1,2 with ACT 4:25,26
  - ISA 53:1 with JHN 12:38; ROM 10:16
  - JER 31:31-34 with HEB 8:8-12; 10:16,17
- Captivity predicted and fulfilled: **JER 25:11,12; 29:10,14; DAN 9:2** with 2KI 25:1-8; EZR 1
- Note: Nave's confirms the literary dependence between Jeremiah's 70-year prophecy and Daniel's 70-week vision.
