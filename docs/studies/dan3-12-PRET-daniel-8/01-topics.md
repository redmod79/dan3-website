# Topics Research

## Question
How does the preterist school read Daniel 8, and what is the textual basis for identifying the little horn as Antiochus IV?

## Topics

### HORN
- Used to contain the anointing oil: 1 Sam 16:1; 1 Kgs 1:39
- Used for a trumpet (See TRUMPET)
- **FIGURATIVE:** Of divine protection 2 Sam 22:3; Of power 1 Kgs 22:11; Psa 89:24; 92:10; 132:17
- **SYMBOLICAL:** Dan 7:7-24; 8:3-9,20; Amos 6:13; Mic 4:13; Hab 3:4; Zec 1:18-21; Rev 5:6; 12:3; 13:1,11; 17:3-16
- Horns of the altar (See ALTAR)

### DANIEL
- **1. A Jewish captive, also called BELTESHAZZAR**
  - Educated at king's court: Dan 1
  - Interprets visions: Dan 2; 4; 5
  - Promotion and executive authority: Dan 2:48,49; 5:11,29; 6:2
  - Conspiracy against, cast into the lions' den: Dan 6
  - Prophecies of: Dan 4:8,9; 7; 8; 9; 10; 11; 12; Matt 24:15
  - Abstinence of: Dan 1:8-16
  - Wisdom of: Dan 1:17; Ezek 28:3
  - Devoutness of: Dan 2:18; 6; 9; 10; 12; Ezek 14:14
  - Courage and fidelity of: Dan 4:27; 5:17-23; 6:10-23
  - Worshiped by Nebuchadnezzar: Dan 2:6
- **2. David's son** (Also called CHILEAB): 2 Sam 3:3; 1 Chr 3:1
- **3. A descendant of Ithamar**, companion of Ezra: Ezra 8:2; Neh 10:6

### PROPHECY
- Concerning Jesus (See JESUS)
- Concerning the church (See CHURCH)
- Inspired: Isa 28:22; Luke 1:70; 2 Tim 3:16; 2 Pet 1:21
- The word of the Lord came to various prophets (Elijah, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Amos, Jonah, Haggai, Zechariah)
- Publicly proclaimed: Jer 11:6
- Exemplified in pantomime: Ezek 4; 5:1-4; Acts 21:11
- Proof of God's foreknowledge: Isa 43:9
- Sure fulfillment: Ezek 12:22-25,28; Hab 2:3; Matt 5:18; 24:35; Acts 13:27,29
- Cessation of: Lam 2:9
- Of apostasy: 1 John 2:18; Jude 1:17,18

### SANCTUARY
- The Holy of Holies
- In the tabernacle: Heb 9:2
- Divine dwelling place: Exo 25:8
- Reverence for: Lev 19:30; 26:2
- In the charge of high priest: Exo 27:21; Lev 24:3; Num 18:5
- The Holy Place in the temple: Lam 2:7,20; Ezek 42:20
- **FIGURATIVE:** Ezek 11:16
- **Symbolical:** Heb 8:2,5

### TEMPLE
- **SOLOMON'S** (Also called TEMPLE OF THE LORD, HOLY TEMPLE, HOUSE OF GOD, etc.)
- Greatness of: 2 Chr 2:5,6; Beauty of: Isa 64:11; Holiness of: 1 Kgs 8:10; 9:3; Lam 1:10; Matt 23:17
- David undertakes the building: 2 Sam 7:2,3; 1 Chr 22:7; 28:2; Psa 132:2-5
- Forbidden to David because he was a man of war: 2 Sam 7:4-12; 1 Kgs 5:3
- Built by Solomon: Acts 7:47
- Pattern and building of: 1 Kgs 6; 7:13-51; 1 Chr 28:11-19; 2 Chr 3; 4
- **Prophecy of destruction:** Dan 8:11-15; 11:30,31
- **Prophecy of restoration:** Dan 8:13,14

### PERSIA
- An empire extending from India to Ethiopia (127 provinces): Est 1:1; Dan 6:1
- Government restricted by constitutional limitations: Est 8:8; Dan 6:8-12
- Israel captive in: 2 Chr 36:20
- Rulers: Ahasuerus, Darius, Artaxerxes I & II, Cyrus
- Prophecies concerning: Isa 13:17; 21:1-10; Jer 49:34-39; 51:11-64; Ezek 32:24,25; 38:5; Dan 2:31-45; 5:28; 7; 8; 11:1-4

### GREECE
- Inhabitants called "Gentiles": Mark 7:26; John 7:35; Rom 2:10
- Schools of philosophy in Athens: Acts 19:9
- Philosophy of: 1 Cor 1:22,23
- Prophecies concerning: Dan 10:20; Zec 9:13

### ABOMINATION
- Things that are, to God:
  - Idolatry: Deut 7:25; 27:15; 32:16
  - Unjust weights and measures: Deut 25:13-16; Prov 11:1; 20:1,23
  - Uncleanness: Lev 18:22; 20:13; Deut 24:4
  - Offering children in sacrifice: Deut 18:10
  - Sorcery and necromancy: Deut 18:10,11
- Unclassified scriptures: Deut 22:5; Prov 3:32; 6:16-19; 8:7; 11:20; 12:22; 15:8,9,26; 16:5; 17:15

### SACRIFICES
- **FIGURATIVE:** Isa 34:6; Ezek 39:17; Zeph 1:7,8; Rom 12:1; Phil 2:17; 4:18
- Of self-denial: Phil 3:7,8
- Of praise: Psa 116:17; Jer 33:11; Hos 14:2; Heb 13:15
