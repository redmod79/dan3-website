# Topics Research

## Question
How does the preterist school read Daniel 2, and what is the textual basis for alternative kingdom identifications?

## Topics

### PERSIA
An empire which extended from India to Ethiopia, comprising 127 provinces (EST 1:1; DAN 6:1). Government restricted by constitutional limitations (EST 8:8; DAN 6:8-12). Rulers include Ahasuerus, Darius (DAN 5:31; 6; 9:1), Artaxerxes I, Artaxerxes II, and Cyrus (2CH 36:22,23; EZR 1; ISA 41:2,3; 44:28; 45:1-4,13). Prophecies concerning: ISA 13:17; 21:1-10; JER 49:34-39; DAN 2:31-45; 5:28; 7; 8; 11:1-4.

### BABYLON
City built by Nimrod (GEN 10:10). Capital of the kingdom of Babylon (DAN 4:30). Empire founded by Nimrod; extent at the time of Nebuchadnezzar (DAN 2:37,38; 4:1; 6:1). Government: a limited monarchy (EST 1:13-19; DAN 6:8,14,17). Prophecies: PSA 87:4; ISA 13; 14:4-26; 21:1-10; 47; JER 50; 51; DAN 2:21-38; 4:10-26; 5:25-29; 7; HAB 1:5-11.

### GREECE
Inhabitants called "Gentiles" (MRK 7:26; JHN 7:35; ROM 2:10). Accept the Messiah (ACT 17:2-4). Prophecies concerning (DAN 10:20; ZEC 9:13).

### MEDES
Inhabitants of Media. Israelites distributed among them (2KI 17:6; 18:11). An essential part of the Medo-Persian Empire (EST 1:1,19). Supremacy of, in the Chaldean Empire (DAN 5:28,31; 9:1; 11:1). **Note: Nave's categorizes Medes as part of the Medo-Persian Empire, not as a separate empire.**

### CORNERSTONE
Of Christ: PSA 118:22; ISA 28:16; MAT 21:42; MRK 12:10; LUK 20:17; ACT 4:11; 1CO 3:11; EPH 2:20; 1PE 2:6.

### STONES (figurative/symbolical)
- Of temptation, "Stone of Stumbling": ISA 8:14; ROM 9:33; 1PE 2:8
- Of Christ, "a Tested Stone," "a Sure Foundation": ISA 28:16
- Of Christ's rejection, the rejected Cornerstone: PSA 118:22; MAT 21:42-44; MRK 12:10; LUK 20:17,18; ACT 4:11; 1PE 2:4
- The True Foundation: ISA 28:16; MAT 16:18; 1CO 3:11; EPH 2:20; REV 21:14
- **SYMBOLICAL: Of the kingdom of Christ DAN 2:34,45** — Nave's explicitly classifies Dan 2's stone as symbolizing Christ's kingdom

### KINGDOM OF HEAVEN
Compared to: sowed good seed (MAT 13:24-30); mustard seed (MAT 13:31,32); leaven (MAT 13:33); treasure (MAT 13:44); pearl (MAT 13:45); net (MAT 13:47-50); marriage feast (MAT 22:2-14); ten virgins (MAT 25:1-13). "My kingdom is not of this world" (JHN 18:36). Does not consist of meat and drink (ROM 14:17).

### IRON
First use: GEN 4:22. Key articles: Furnace (DEU 4:20; 1KI 8:51; JER 11:4), Rods for scourging (PSA 2:9; REV 2:27; 12:5; 19:15), Yokes (DEU 28:48; JER 28:13,14). Idols (DAN 2:33; 5:4,23). Figurative: 2SA 23:7; JER 15:12; 1TI 4:2.

### CLAY
Man formed from (JOB 33:6). Used by potter (ISA 29:16; 45:9). Figurative: ISA 64:8; JER 18:6; ROM 9:21. **SYMBOLICAL: DAN 2:33-41.**

### HORN (symbolical)
DAN 7:7-24; 8:3-9,20; AMO 6:13; MIC 4:13; HAB 3:4; ZEC 1:18-21; REV 5:6; 12:3; 13:1,11; 17:3-16.

### STUMBLING (stone of)
ISA 8:14; ROM 9:32,33; 1PE 2:8 (variant reading "1:8" in Nave's). JER 6:21; EZK 3:20; 14:3,4,7.

### DANIEL
Jewish captive (BELTESHAZZAR). Interprets visions (DAN 2; 4; 5). Prophecies of (DAN 7; 8; 9; 10; 11; 12; MAT 24:15).

### ROMAN EMPIRE
Ruled by Augustus Caesar (LUK 2:1), Tiberius Caesar (LUK 3:1), Claudius Caesar (ACT 18:2), Nero (PHP 4:22). Citizenship by birth and purchase (ACT 22:28). Rights of citizens (ACT 16:37; 22:25-29; 25:10,16,21).

### VISION
Mode of revelation (NUM 12:6; DAN 1:17). Daniel's visions: four beasts (DAN 7), Ancient of Days (DAN 7:9-27), ram and he-goat (DAN 8), angel (DAN 10).

### ABOMINATION
Things abominable to God: idolatry (DEU 7:25; 27:15), unjust weights (DEU 25:13-16), uncleanness (LEV 18:22), sorcery (DEU 18:10,11).
