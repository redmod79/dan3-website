# Topics Research

## Question
How do NT authors use Daniel, and do they treat Daniel 7-12 as a unified prophetic corpus?

## Topics

### PROPHECY
- **Inspired**: ISA 28:22; LUK 1:70; 2TI 3:16; 2PE 1:21
- **Sure fulfillment**: EZK 12:22-25,28; HAB 2:3; MAT 5:18; 24:35; ACT 13:27,29
- **Of apostasy**: 1JN 2:18; Jude 1:17,18
- **False teachers**: 2PE 2; 3
- **Messianic fulfillment chain**: ISA 9:7 with DAN 7:14,27 with LUK 1:32,33 (kingdom prophecy)
- **Messianic OT-to-NT passages**: PSA 2:1,2 with ACT 4:25,26; PSA 110:1 with MAT 22:44; HEB 1:13; ZEC 12:10 with JHN 19:37; MAL 3:1 with MAT 11:10; MRK 1:2; LUK 7:27
- **Relevance**: Establishes the pattern of OT prophecy fulfilled in NT. DAN 7:14,27 explicitly linked to LUK 1:32,33 in the Nave's entry.

### APOSTASY
- **Described**: DEU 13:13; HEB 3:12
- **Caused by persecution**: MAT 24:9,10; LUK 8:13
- **Shall abound in latter days**: MAT 24:12; 2TH 2:3; 1TI 4:1-3
- **Unclassified scriptures**: DEU 32:15; 1CH 28:9; ISA 1:28; JER 17:5,6; EZK 3:20; MAT 13:20,21; 24:10,12; MRK 4:5-17; LUK 8:13; 11:24-26; JHN 15:6; ACT 7:39-43; 1CO 9:27; 2TH 2:3,11,12; 1TI 4:1,2; 2TI 3:1-9; 4:3,4; HEB 6:4-8; 10:26-29; 2PE 2:1,15,17,20-22; 3:17; Jude 1:4-6
- **Instances**: Judas MAT 26:14-16; 27:3-5; ACT 1:16-18
- **Relevance**: 2 Thess 2:3 is classified under "shall abound in latter days." The apostasy precedes the man of sin. Matt 24:12 "anomia shall abound" also classified here — linking Olivet anomia to 2 Thess 2 anomia.

### DANIEL
- **Prophecies of**: DAN 4:8,9; 7; 8; 9; 10; 11; 12; MAT 24:15
- **Wisdom of**: DAN 1:17; EZK 28:3
- **Devoutness of**: DAN 2:18; 6; 9; 10; 12; EZK 14:14
- **Relevance**: MAT 24:15 is directly listed under Daniel's prophecies — Nave's recognizes Jesus as citing Daniel. The listing of Dan 7-12 as a continuous block of prophecies is itself relevant to the unified corpus question.

### ABOMINATION
- **Idolatry**: DEU 7:25; 27:15; 32:16
- **Unjust weights**: DEU 25:13-16; PRO 11:1
- **Unclassified**: PRO 3:32; 6:16-19; 8:7; 11:20; 12:22; 15:8,9,26; 16:5; 17:15; 20:10,23; 21:27; 24:9; 28:9; 29:27
- **Relevance**: The core meaning of shiqquts (Hebrew) is idolatry/detestable thing. The abomination of desolation in Daniel carries this fundamental meaning of idolatrous intrusion. Rev 17:4-5 uses bdelygma (G946) in a context linking "ABOMINATIONS" to the harlot system.

### REVELATION
- **God reveals himself**: EXO 3:1-6,14
- **The sonship of Jesus revealed**: MAT 3:17; 16:17; 17:5
- **Relevance**: The concept of divine revelation (apokalypsis) connects the genre of Daniel's visions to John's Apocalypse. Both are "revelations" of future events.

### GODLESSNESS
- **General scriptures**: DEU 7:10; 32:15; ROM 1:21,22,28; EPH 4:18; HEB 10:26,27; JAS 4:4
- **DAN 5:23**: Daniel-specific entry — Belshazzar's godlessness
- **Relevance**: The trajectory from godlessness to full apostasy is the backdrop for the man of sin.

### DECEIT
- **Characteristic of antichrist**: 2JN 1:7
- **Characteristic of the apostasy**: 2TH 2:10
- **False teachers are workers of**: 2CO 11:13
- **Relevance**: Nave's explicitly connects deceit to both antichrist (2 John 1:7) and the apostasy (2 Thess 2:10). This reinforces the vocabulary chain across Johannine and Pauline writings.
