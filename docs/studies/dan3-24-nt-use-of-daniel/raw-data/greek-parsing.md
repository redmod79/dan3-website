**CRITICAL: hestekota is MASCULINE (ASM), but bdelygma is NEUTER. ## Hebrew Parsing (Daniel source texts)

### Daniel 7:8 (Aramaic)
- פֻ֖ם (pum) = "mouth" — corresponds to Greek stoma (G4750)
- מְמַלִּ֥ל (memalil) = Pael Ptcp "speaking" — corresponds to Greek laloun
- רַבְרְבָֽן (rabrevan) = "great things" — corresponds to Greek megala (G3173)
- **stoma laloun megala = pum memalil rabrevan — verbatim Greek rendering of Aramaic**

### Daniel 7:25 (Aramaic)
- מִלִּ֗ין (millin) = "words" — speaks words against
- לְצַ֤ד עִלָּאָה֙ (l'tsad illaa) = "against the Most High"
- יְבַלֵּ֑א (y'balle) = Pael Impf "wear out" — the saints
- לְהַשְׁנָיָה֙ (l'hashnayah) = Hafel InfCon "to change" — times and law
- זִמְנִ֣ין (zimnin) = "times"
- דָ֔ת (dat) = "law" (singular)
- עִדָּ֥ן וְעִדָּנִ֖ין וּפְלַ֥ג עִד��ּֽן (iddan v'iddanin u'f'lag iddan) = "time, times, and half a time"

### Daniel 9:27 (Hebrew)
- שִׁקּוּצִים֙ (shiqquts-im) = "idols/abominations" (PLURAL) — Note: different form from 11:31
- מְשֹׁמֵ֔ם (meshomem) = Piel Ptcp "one causing desolation" (causative)
- כְּנַ֤ף (k'naph) = "wing" — "upon the wing of abominations"

### Daniel 11:31 (Hebrew)
- הַשִּׁקּ֥וּץ (ha-shiqquts) = "THE abomination" (singular with article)
- מְשֹׁומֵֽם (meshomem) = Piel Ptcp "one causing desolation"
- הַמִּקְדָּ֤שׁ הַמָּעֹוז֙ = "the sanctuary, the fortress"

### Daniel 11:36 (Hebrew)
- יִתְרֹומֵ֤ם (yitromem) = Hithpael "exalt himself" — cf. 2 Thess 2:4 hyperairomenos
- יִתְגַּדֵּל֙ (yitgaddel) = Hithpael "magnify himself" — above every god
- עַל כָּל אֵל (al kol el) = "above every god" — cf. 2 Thess 2:4 "above all called God"

### Daniel 12:4 (Hebrew)
- סְתֹ֧ם (s'tom) = Qal Impv "stop up/close up"
- חֲתֹ֥ם (chatom) = Qal Impv "seal" — cf. Greek sphragizo (G4972) in Rev 22:10
- עַד עֵ֣ת קֵ֑ץ = "until the time of the end"

### Daniel 12:11 (Hebrew)
- שִׁקּ֣וּץ (shiqquts) = "abomination" (singular, no article)
- שֹׁמֵ֑ם (shomem) = Qal Ptcp "being desolate/desolating"
- **Note different from 11:31 meshomem (Piel) vs 12:11 shomem (Qal)**

### Daniel 8:13 (Hebrew)
- הַפֶּ֣שַׁע (ha-pesha) = "THE transgression/rebellion" — NOT shiqquts!
- שֹׁמֵ֔ם (shomem) = Qal Ptcp "causing desolation"
- **Dan 8:13 uses PESHA (rebellion), not shiqquts (idol). Different term from other abomination passages.**

### Daniel 8:11 (Hebrew)
- שַֽׂר הַצָּבָ֖א (sar ha-tsava) = "prince of the host"
- הִגְדִּ֑יל (higdil) = Hiphil "magnified [himself]"
- הַתָּמִ֔יד (ha-tamid) = "the continual [offering]"
- מְכֹ֥ון מִקְדָּשֹֽׁו (m'khon miqdash-o) = "place of his sanctuary"

**ABOMINATION VOCABULARY COMPARISON (Litch's observation verified):**
| Verse | Term | Stem | Meaning |
|-------|------|------|---------|
| Dan 8:13 | pesha shomem | Qal | "transgression causing desolation" |
| Dan 9:27 | shiqquts-im meshomem | Piel | "abominations causing desolation" |
