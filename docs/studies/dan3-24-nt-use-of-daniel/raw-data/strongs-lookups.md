## [G3485](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3485/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}: ναός (naos) — temple/shrine
- Part of Speech: masculine noun
- BLB Count: 46
- Definition: a fane, shrine, temple (inner sanctuary)
- All Pauline uses: 1 Cor 3:16,17; 6:19; 2 Cor 6:16(2x); Eph 2:21; 2 Thess 2:4
- Revelation uses: Rev 3:12; 7:15; 11:1,2,19; 14:15,17; 15:5,6,8(2x); 16:1,17; 21:22
- Gospel uses: Matt 23:16,17,21,35; 26:61; 27:5,40,51; Mark 14:58; 15:29,38; Luke 1:9,21,22; 23:45; John 2:19,21
- Acts: 7:48; 17:24; 19:24
- Translations: "temple" (35x), "the temple" (4x), "temples" (2x), "shrines" (1x), "hath the temple" (1x)
- **KEY PAULINE PATTERN:**
  - 1 Cor 3:16-17: "ye are the temple of God" (church = naos)
  - 1 Cor 6:19: "your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost" (body = naos)
  - 2 Cor 6:16: "ye are the temple of the living God" (church = naos)
  - Eph 2:21: "groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord" (church = naos)
  - 2 Thess 2:4: "he as God sitteth in the temple of God" (man of sin in naos)
  - **Every Pauline use of naos tou theou = believers/church. Never literal Jerusalem temple.**
