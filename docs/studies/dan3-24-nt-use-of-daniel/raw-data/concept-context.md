## Hebrew Parsing Summary for External Corpus Verification

### Litch's Multiple Abominations Claim (VERIFIED)
The Hebrew parser confirms vocabulary variation across Daniel's abomination passages:

| Verse | Hebrew Term | Root | Stem | English |
|-------|------------|------|------|---------|
| Dan 8:13 | הַפֶּשַׁע שֹׁמֵם | פשׁע + שׁמם | Noun + Qal Ptcp | "the transgression causing desolation" |
| Dan 9:27 | שִׁקּוּצִים מְשֹׁמֵם | שׁקוץ + שׁמם | Noun.mp + Piel Ptcp | "abominations causing desolation" |
| Dan 11:31 | הַשִּׁקּוּץ מְשֹׁומֵם | שׁקוץ + שׁמם | Noun.ms + Piel Ptcp | "THE abomination causing desolation" |
| Dan 12:11 | שִׁקּוּץ שֹׁמֵם | שׁקוץ + שׁמם | Noun.ms + Qal Ptcp | "abomination desolating" |

Key differences:
1. Dan 8:13 uses pesha ("transgression/rebellion"), NOT shiqquts ("idol/abomination")
2. Dan 9:27 uses shiqquts-im (PLURAL), while 11:31 and 12:11 use singular
3. Dan 11:31 has the article (ha-shiqquts), others do not
4. The desolation participle varies: Piel (causative agent) in 9:27/11:31 vs Qal (stative/intransitive) in 8:13/12:11
5. Yet Jesus treats all as one concept under "the abomination of desolation spoken of by Daniel"

### Dan 11:36 to 2 Thess 2:4 Connection (VERIFIED)

- 2 Thess 2:4: hyperairomenos (hyperairo = "exalt above") + "above all called God or worshipped"
- Both describe self-exaltation above all deity — Hebrew reflexive Hithpael parallels Greek middle/passive voice
