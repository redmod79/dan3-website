### From Study Plan (Integrate List) — Key Standalone Findings

**daniel-8-15-connection-to-daniel-9:**
- Question: "Does Daniel 8:15 connect to Daniel 9?"
- Conclusive finding: biyn chain traces a five-stage narrative arc: COMMISSION (8:16 haben+ha-mar'eh) → FAILURE (8:27 ein mebiyn+ha-mar'eh) → STUDY (9:2 binoti) → RESUMPTION (9:22-23 va-yaben+binah, vehaven+ba-mar'eh) → COMPLETION (10:1 biyn+mar'eh)
- The identical haben+mar'eh construction at 8:16 and 9:23 (same verb, stem, form, object, speaker, recipient) forms a grammatical inclusio
- Gabriel's ch. 9 arrival at "the time of the evening oblation" connects to the sanctuary system under attack in ch. 8
- chathak (H2852) hapax: primary meaning "cut off" with "determine" as figurative extension; Daniel had charats available and used it in 9:26, 9:27, 11:36

**daniel-8-14-cleansed-hebrew-meaning:**
- nitsdaq = Niphal Perfect of tsadaq (H6663), forensic/legal term meaning "to be vindicated, justified"
- tsadaq appears 54 times in OT; LXX renders it with dikaio (21x), dikaios (22x), krino (8x) — never katharizo
- The Q&A structure: injustice vocabulary (pesha, shamem, mirmac in 8:13) answered by justice vocabulary (nitsdaq in 8:14)
- KJV "cleansed" derives from LXX/Theodotion (katharisthesetai), not from the Hebrew
- Daniel had taher (94x) and kaphar (102x) available for ritual cleansing but chose tsadaq

**daniel-9-24-weeks-grammar:**
- shabuwa (H7620) = "a period of seven," flexible for days or years
- Genesis 29:27-28: shabuah = seven years (Jacob's service)
- Daniel's grammatical distinction: 10:2-3 adds yamim ("days") to shabuim; 9:24 omits yamim — same author, same book, different constructions
- chathak confirmation: if God meant only "decreed," common words exist (gazar, charats)

**daniel-jeremiah-70-years:**
- Daniel was a student of Scripture who "understood by books" (binoti basepharim)
- Sabbath-year mathematics: 70 missed sabbaths × 7 = 490 years of violation; 70 weeks = 490 years = identical duration
- Shared vocabulary between Jeremiah and Daniel 9 proves direct literary dependence (shib'im shanah, male, chorbah)
- The 70→70×7 numerical echo: Gabriel extends Jeremiah's prophetic framework

**daniel-8-14-grammar-study:**
- erev boqer = one day cycle (parallels Genesis 1 creation pattern)
- Dan 8:26 back-references 8:14 with definite articles: "THE evening and THE morning" (anaphoric reference)
- The Day of Atonement formula (Lev 23:32) is "evening TO evening" — no morning component; fundamentally different from Dan 8:14
- Each erev boqer = one day; 2300 erev-boqer = 2300 days (not 1150)

**daniel-8-9-grammar-origin-little-horn:**
- Hebrew grammar of Dan 8:9: mehem uses MASCULINE plural suffix, but both "horns" (chazut) and "winds" (ruchot) are FEMININE
- Strict gender agreement would require feminine suffix mehen; masculine suffix points to broader geographic/directional concept
- Gabriel's own interpretive language (8:22-23) uses masculine suffix -am for feminine malkuyot — same grammatical phenomenon
- The verb gender switch (yatsa masculine, wattigdal feminine) reveals Daniel conceives horn as both feminine horn and masculine king/power

**daniel-8-23-deuteronomy-parallel:**
- az paniym (H5794+H6440) appears in exactly 2 OT verses: Deut 28:50 and Dan 8:23 — exclusive construct chain
- Daniel explicitly cites "the curse written in the law of Moses" (Dan 9:11)
- LXX translates pasha as anomos (G459, "lawless"), creating lexical bridge to Paul's "lawless one" (2 Thess 2:8)
- The tamam/pesha vocabulary link (8:23→9:24) connects the fierce king's rise to the Messianic solution

**daniel-8-great-progression:**
- Three-stage gadal escalation: ram = gadal (Hiphil), goat = gadal ad-me'od (Hiphil), horn = gadal yether (Qal)
- yether (H3499) = surplus/preeminence in all 101 occurrences
- Antiochus (~3M km2) cannot satisfy yether when Persia (~5.5-8M km2) and Alexander (~5.2M km2) were larger
- Rome satisfies the yether requirement by surpassing both predecessors

**daniel-8-9-sanctuary-day-of-atonement:**
- Israel's 70-year exile was calculated punishment for 490 years of sabbatical violation (2 Chr 36:21)
- The sanctuary represented atonement, God's presence, national identity, covenant relationship
- Dan 9:24 uses DOA vocabulary (kaphar) while Dan 8:14 uses courtroom vocabulary (tsadaq) — together they reveal the Messiah would accomplish what DOA typified
- Daniel himself became "desolated" (shamem root) at seeing the sanctuary made desolate

**hist-03-70-weeks-jesus-fulfills-timeline:**
- Day-year principle proven primarily by Daniel's internal evidence: fainting at 2300 (inexplicable for ~6.3 years) + 70 weeks must be year-weeks (483 literal days cannot span a Persian decree to the Messiah)
- 457 BC (Artaxerxes decree, Ezra 7) → AD 27 (69 weeks = Messiah anointed, Luke 3:1-2 synchronism)
- Crucifixion "in the midst of the week" → AD 30-33 (all within 70th week)
- 70 weeks = continuous period: 7+62+1=70, no gap stated between any segment
- Mark 1:15 peplērotai ("time is fulfilled") and Gal 4:4 plēroma tou chronou confirm prophetic time completed at first advent

**hist-05-daniel-8-9-connected-2300-days:**
- Six converging lines: biyn chain, mar'eh/chazon distinction, chathak hapax, shared vocabulary, Ezra 7 decree, NT confirmation
- Ezra 7 is the only decree satisfying "restore AND build" — includes judicial authority (judges, magistrates, legal enforcement)
- Ezra 6:14 composite reading: "the commandment of Cyrus, and Darius, and Artaxerxes" (singular "commandment" for three decrees)
- If 70 weeks cut from 2300, both begin 457 BC → 2300 terminate AD 1844

**hist-06-sanctuary-vindicated-1844:**
- nitsdaq = vindicated (forensic), not cleansed (ritual). Every Niphal/passive tsadaq in OT appears in courtroom/judgment context
- KJV "cleansed" from LXX Theodotion (katharisthesetai), anomalous — LXX's own standard for tsadaq is dikaioo (21x)
- The judgment scene of Dan 7:9-10 provides the procedural framework; Dan 8:14 declares the forensic outcome
- erev boqer parallels Genesis 1 creation day-cycle, not DOA formula (Lev 23:32)

**abomination-of-desolation-grammar:**
- shiqquts (H8251): exclusively idolatrous in all 28 OT occurrences
- Piel participle meshomem (Dan 9:27, 11:31): unambiguously causative — "the one CAUSING desolation" (personal agent)
- Mark 13:14 masculine participle hestekota modifying neuter bdelygma = constructio ad sensum (personal figure behind the abomination)
- Four Daniel occurrences vary: 8:13 uses pesha ("transgression"), 9:27 uses plural shiqqutsim, 11:31 has definite article, 12:11 is anarthrous
