## [G3039](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3039/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} — likmao — winnow/grind to powder
- Transliteration: likmao
- Part of Speech: verb
- BLB Count: 2 (4 total translation occurrences)
- Definition: from likmos (winnowing fan); to winnow, scatter as chaff
- NT Occurrences: Mat 21:44; Luk 20:18
- LXX Usage: Isa 17:13; Jer 31:10; Dan 2:44; Amos 9:9
- Translation: "it will grind...to powder" (all 4 occurrences)
- CRITICAL: Only 2 NT uses, both in the stone parable. The LXX of Dan 2:44 uses this same verb, creating a direct vocabulary link between Daniel's stone and Jesus' stone parable.
