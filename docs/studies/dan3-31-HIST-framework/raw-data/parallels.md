# Cross-Testament Parallels — Raw Data

## Key parallel relationships established in prior studies:

### Dan 2:44 (Stone kingdom) → NT parallels
- Matt 21:42-44 (likmao = winnowing/chaff = Dan 2:35)
- 1 Pet 2:4-8 (stone chain: Psa 118:22, Isa 8:14, Isa 28:16)
- Acts 4:11 (stone which builders rejected)
- Rev 2:27; 12:5; 19:15 (iron rod / Psa 2:9)

### Dan 7:9-14 (Judgment scene) → NT parallels
- Rev 4-5 (9+ shared elements: thrones, seated Judge, white garments, fire, myriads, books, Son of Man approach, kingdom reception, universal worship)
- Rev 14:7 (krisis = judgment announced)
- Matt 26:64 (Jesus combines Psa 110:1 + Dan 7:13)

### Dan 8:14 (nitsdaq) → NT parallels
- Rev 14:7 (krisis + creation catalogue)
- Rev 15:3, 16:7, 19:2 (vindication quartet: dikaiai kai alethinai)

### Dan 9:24-27 → NT parallels
- Mark 1:15 (time fulfilled)
- Gal 4:4 (fulness of time)
- Acts 10:38 (anointing = mashiach)
- Rom 15:8 (confirm/strengthen covenant = gabar beriyth)
- Heb 9:26 (put away sin = Dan 9:24 purposes)

### Dan 7:25 / Rev 13:5 → Verbatim Greek quotation
- stoma laloun megala — identical phrase in Dan 7:8 LXX and Rev 13:5
- 42 months (Rev 13:5) = time, times, half a time (Dan 7:25)

### Dan 2:28 → Rev 1:1
- ha dei genesthai ("things which must come to pass") — verbatim LXX quotation
- Rev 22:6 repeats, forming inclusio around Revelation

### Sealed-to-Unsealed Arc
- Dan 12:4 "seal the book" → Rev 22:10 "seal not the sayings"
- Structural arc spanning the two prophetic books
