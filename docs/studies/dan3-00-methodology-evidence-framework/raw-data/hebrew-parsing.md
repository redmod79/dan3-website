# Raw Hebrew Parsing Data

## ISA 8:20
Hebrew: le-torah we-li-te'udah 'im-lo' yo'meru ka-ddavar hazzeh 'asher 'en-lo shachar

Key words:
- torah (instruction/law) - Noun.fs.Abs
- te'udah (attestation/testimony) - Noun.fs.Abs
- 'im lo' yo'meru (if not they-say) - Conj + Neg + Verb.Qal.Impf.3mp
- ka-ddavar hazzeh (according-to-the-word the-this) - Prep + Noun.ms + DemPron
- 'en-lo shachar (there-is-no to-him dawn/light) - NEG + Prep.3ms + Noun.ms

Structure: "To the torah and to the attestation: if they do not speak according to this word, [it is] because there is no dawn/light in them."

## ISA 28:10
Hebrew: ki tsav la-tsav tsav la-tsav qav la-qav qav la-qav ze'er sham ze'er sham

Key words:
- tsav (H6673 - precept/command) - Noun.s.Abs - repeated 4 times
- qav (H6957 - line/measuring line) - Noun.s.Abs - repeated 4 times
- ze'er (little) - Noun.ms.Abs
- sham (there) - Adv

NOTE: The Hebrew words tsav and qav are monosyllabic, possibly onomatopoeic. Some scholars see them as mockery of Isaiah's teaching (babble), others as a genuine description of the incremental method. The KJV renders "precept upon precept, line upon line."

## NUM 14:34
Hebrew: be-mispar ha-yamim 'asher tarttem 'et-ha-'arets 'arba'im yom YOM LA-SHANAH YOM LA-SHANAH tis'u 'et-'awonotekhem 'arba'im shanah

Key phrase: YOM LA-SHANAH = "day to/for the year" (repeated twice)
- yom (H3117) - day - Noun.ms.Abs
- la-shanah (to the year) - Prep + Art + Noun.fs.Abs
- Structure: "each day for a year, each day for a year"

Context: 40 days spying -> 40 years wandering. The formula is RETROSPECTIVE (looking back at days already spent).

## EZE 4:6
Hebrew: ... 'arba'im yom YOM LA-SHANAH YOM LA-SHANAH netattiv lak

Same formula: YOM LA-SHANAH = "day for a year"
- netattiv (I have appointed/given it) - Verb.Qal.Perf.1s + 3ms suffix
- lak (to you) - Prep + 2ms

Context: Ezekiel lies on his side 40 days for 40 years of Judah's iniquity. The formula is PROSPECTIVE (God assigns the equivalence).

## DAN 8:16
Hebrew: wa-'eshma' qol-'adam ben 'ulay wayyiqra' wayyomer Gavri'el HAVEN le-hallaz 'et-ha-mar'eh

Key words:
- Gavri'el (Gabriel) - PropN.ms
- HAVEN (make understand!) - Verb.Hiphil.Impv.2ms of [H995](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h995/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (bin = understand)
- le-hallaz (to this one) - Prep + Art + DemPron
- ha-mar'eh (the vision/appearance) - Noun.ms.Abs (H4758)

NOTE: The Hiphil imperative of BIN = "cause to understand" - a CAUSATIVE command. God is ordering Gabriel to make Daniel understand. This is a direct divine mandate for interpretation.
