KEY PREPOSITIONS:
- 'im (עִם) = "with" — the clouds accompany, they are the mode of conveyance
- we-'ad (וְעַד) = "and to/unto" — direction is TOWARD the Ancient of Days
- meta (מְטָה) = "arrived" (Peal Perf 3ms of meta') — the Son of Man REACHED the Ancient of Days
- haqrebuhi (Haph'el Perf 3mp + 3ms suffix) = "they brought HIM near" — causative: others presented him before the Ancient. The 3ms object suffix indicates an INDIVIDUAL.

DIRECTION OF MOVEMENT: The Aramaic prepositions (we-'ad = toward; meta' = arrived at; qodamohi = before him) all indicate the Son of Man moves TOWARD the Ancient of Days, not toward earth. This is a challenge for FUT's Second Coming reading.

- 'iddan (a time) + we-'iddanin (and times [plural/dual]) + u-felag 'iddan (and half a time) = 3.5 iddan
