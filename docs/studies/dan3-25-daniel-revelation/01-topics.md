## Relevance Notes for Analysis Agent

Key cross-references connecting Daniel and Revelation through these topics:
- **BLASPHEMY**: DAN 7:25; 11:36,37 directly grouped with REV 13:1,5,6; 16:9,11,21; 17:3
- **DRAGON**: Symbolical in EZK 29:3; 32:2 and REV 12; 13; 16:13; applied to Satan REV 20:2
- **LION**: Symbolical in DAN 7:4 and REV 4:7; 5:5; 13:2
- **LEOPARD**: Figurative in DAN 7:6 (no Revelation entry but Rev 13:2 reference is via LION topic)
- **SEAL**: DAN 12:9 and REV 5:1 grouped under "figurative of secrecy"
- **THRONE**: God's throne PSA 9:4,7 through REV 14:3,5; Christ's throne MAT 19:28 through REV 22:3
- **JUDGMENT**: DAN 7:9,10 and REV 1:7; 6:15-17; 11:18; 20:11-15; 22:12 all under "The General"
- **ANTICHRIST**: 2TH 2:3-12 grouped with MAT 24 and 1JN texts; destruction in REV 19:20; 20:10,15
- **SATAN**: Great red dragon REV 12:3; old serpent REV 12:9; 20:2; synagogue of REV 2:9; 3:9
