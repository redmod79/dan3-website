## [H6663](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6663/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} tsadaq LXX -> Revelation Vindication Quartet Summary

The forensic chain from Dan 8:14 to Revelation's four vindication declarations:

1. **Rev 14:7** -- "the hour of his judgment [kriseos, G2920] is come" (cf. Dan 7:10 LXX krisis)
2. **Rev 15:3** -- "just [dikaiai, G1342] and true are thy ways" (cf. tsadaq -> dikaios)
3. **Rev 16:5-7** -- "Righteous [Dikaios] art thou... true and righteous [dikaiai] are thy judgments [kriseis]"
4. **Rev 19:1-2** -- "true and righteous [dikaiai] are his judgments [kriseis]"

All four use Greek derivatives of the same semantic field as [H6663](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6663/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} tsadaq: dikaios (G1342), krisis (G2920). The LXX mapping confirms tsadaq's primary Greek equivalent is dikaioo (G1344), whose adjective form dikaios (G1342) appears in all four Revelation passages.
