# Word Studies

## [H8269](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8269/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- sar (שַׂר)
- **Original:** שַׂר
- **Transliteration:** sar
- **Part of Speech:** Masculine noun
- **Definition:** A head person (of any rank or class): captain, chief, general, governor, keeper, lord, master, prince, ruler, steward
- **BLB Count:** 421 occurrences
- **Key Translations:** "the princes" (65x, 15%), "the captains" (33x, 7.6%), "captains" (21x), "princes" (20x), "captain" (19x), "the prince" (9x), "chief" (14x)
- **Daniel Occurrences:** Dan 1:7,8,9,10,11,18; 8:11,25; 9:6,8; 10:13(2x),20(2x),21(2x); 11:5; 12:1
- **Key Verses in Daniel 10-12:**
  - Dan 10:13 -- "sar malkut paras" (prince/chief of the kingdom of Persia); "Michael echad ha-sarim ha-rishonim" (Michael, one of the chief princes)
  - Dan 10:20 -- "sar paras" (prince of Persia); "sar yavan" (prince of Greece)
  - Dan 10:21 -- "Michael sarkhem" (Michael your prince)
  - Dan 11:5 -- "sar" used of a military commander under the king of the south
  - Dan 12:1 -- "Michael ha-sar ha-gadol" (Michael the great prince)
- **Title Progression (PRET vs. HIST significance):**
  - 10:13: echad ha-sarim ha-rishonim ("one of the chief princes") -- PRET reads this as placing Michael within a category of multiple angelic princes
  - 10:21: sarkhem ("your prince") -- possessive, Michael as Israel's patron
  - 12:1: ha-sar ha-gadol ("the great prince") -- superlative, highest rank
- **LXX Connection:** LXX uses archon/archontos for sar in Daniel

## [H5057](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5057/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- nagiyd (נָגִיד)
- **Original:** נָגִיד
- **Transliteration:** nagiyd
- **Part of Speech:** Masculine noun
- **Definition:** A commander (occupying the front), civil, military, or religious; captain, chief, excellent thing, governor, leader, noble, prince, ruler
- **BLB Count:** 44 occurrences
- **Key Translations:** "ruler" (9x, 20%), "the ruler" (8x, 17.8%), "captain" (3x), "prince" (2x)
- **Daniel Occurrences:** Dan 9:25,26; 11:22
- **Key Verses:**
  - Dan 9:25 -- "mashiach nagiyd" (Messiah the Prince/Ruler)
  - Dan 9:26 -- "nagiyd" (the prince that shall come)
  - Dan 11:22 -- "negiyd berith" (prince of the covenant) -- PRET identifies as Onias III (high priest murdered c. 170 BC, cf. 2 Macc 4:33-38)
- **Distinction from sar:** nagiyd is used for appointed/designated leaders; sar is broader (any head person). In Dan 11:22, negiyd berith is a religious leader (high priest), not a military chief.

## [H6884](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6884/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- tsaraph (צָרַף)
- **Original:** צָרַף
- **Transliteration:** tsaraph
- **Part of Speech:** Verb
- **Definition:** A primitive root; to fuse (metal), refine (literally or figuratively): cast, refine, founder, goldsmith, melt, pure, purge away, try
- **BLB Count:** 33 occurrences (36 in translation DB)
- **Key Translations:** "tried" (2x), "pure" (2x), "founder" (2x), "refiner's" (1x), "a refiner" (1x)
- **Purification Triad Member (with [H1305](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1305/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}, H3835):**
  - Dan 11:35 -- litsroph (Qal Inf.Con., "to refine")

- **Other Key Occurrences:** Isa 1:25; 48:10; Jer 6:29; 9:6; Zec 13:9; Mal 3:2,3; Psa 12:7; 17:3; 26:2; 66:10; 105:19; 119:140
- **Structural Significance:** Appears in both Dan 11:35 and 12:10, creating a vocabulary bracket. Only prophetic uses outside Daniel are in Isaiah, Jeremiah, Zechariah, and Malachi -- always in contexts of God testing/purifying his people.

## [H3835](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3835/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- laban (לָבַן)
- **Original:** לָבַן
- **Transliteration:** laban
- **Part of Speech:** Verb
- **Definition:** A primitive root; to be (or become) white; also as denominative: to make bricks
- **BLB Count:** 8 occurrences
- **Key Translations:** "let us make" (1x, brickmaking), "white" (1x), "whiter" (1x), "made white" (1x), "are made white" (1x)
- **Purification Triad Member:**
  - Dan 11:35 -- v'lalben (Hiphil Inf.Con., "and to make white")

## [H1860](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1860/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- dera'on (דְּרָאוֹן)
- **Original:** דְּרָאוֹן
- **Transliteration:** dera'on
- **Part of Speech:** Masculine noun
- **Definition:** From an unused root meaning to repulse; an object of aversion: abhorring, contempt
- **BLB Count:** 2 occurrences (HAPAX PAIR)
- **Translations:** "an abhorring" (Isa 66:24), "contempt" (Dan 12:2)
- **Key Verses:**
  - Dan 12:2 -- "l'dir'on olam" (to everlasting contempt)
  - Isa 66:24 -- "dera'on l'kol basar" (an abhorring unto all flesh)
- **Significance:** This is a true hapax pair -- dera'on occurs only in these two verses. The parallel construct chains (chayyey olam // dera'on olam in Dan 12:2; dera'on l'kol basar in Isa 66:24) establish that olam carries identical temporal force in both outcomes. The Isa 66:24 connection links Dan 12:2 to an eschatological, universal-judgment context that transcends any Maccabean framework.

## [H8548](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8548/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- tamid (תָּמִיד)
- **Original:** תָּמִיד
- **Transliteration:** tamid
- **Part of Speech:** Masculine noun
- **Definition:** Properly, continuance (indefinite extension); used attributively as constant/continual; elliptically, the regular (daily) sacrifice: always, continual, daily, perpetual
- **BLB Count:** 104 occurrences (105 in translation DB)
- **Key Translations:** "continually" (53x, 50.5%), "the continual" (17x), "continual" (9x), "the daily" (7x)
- **Daniel Occurrences:** Dan 8:11,12,13; 11:31; 12:11(2x)
- **Key Verses:**
  - Dan 8:11 -- "mimmennu hurim ha-tamid" (from him the daily was taken away) -- verb: rum (H7311, Hophal)
  - Dan 11:31 -- "v'hesiru ha-tamid" (and they shall take away the daily) -- verb: sur (H5493, Hiphil)
  - Dan 12:11 -- "me-eth husar ha-tamid" (from the time the daily was taken away) -- verb: sur (H5493, Hophal)
- **Verb Difference:** Dan 8:11 uses rum (H7311, "lift up/exalt") while 11:31 and 12:11 use sur (H5493, "turn aside/remove"). This vocabulary difference is significant: PRET argues 11:31/12:11 describe the same event (Antiochus removing the daily sacrifice), while 8:11 may describe the same event differently. HIST argues the different verbs signal different agents/events.

## [H8262](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8262/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- shaqats (שָׁקַץ) [Related term]
- **Original:** שָׁקַץ
- **Transliteration:** shaqats
- **Part of Speech:** Verb
- **Definition:** To abominate, make detestable
- **Note:** Root of shiqquts (H8251). The verbal root means "to make detestable/abominable." The noun form shiqquts specifically denotes idolatrous objects or practices, distinguishing it from to'evah (general abomination) and pesha (transgression, used in Dan 8:13).
