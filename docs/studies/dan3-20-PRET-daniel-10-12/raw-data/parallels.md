## Key Cross-Testament Findings

1. **Dan 12:2 -> Isa 26:19 (strongest OT parallel, 0.449):** Both use resurrection-from-dust language. Isa 26:19 is the closest OT conceptual parallel to bodily resurrection.

2. **Dan 12:2 -> Mat 25:46 (strong NT parallel, 0.334):** Both use dual-outcome eternal language (eternal life vs. eternal punishment/contempt).

3. **Dan 11:35 <-> Dan 12:10 (mutual strongest parallel, 0.402):** Purification triad bracket confirmed by cross-reference analysis.

4. **Dan 12:11 -> Dan 11:31 (strongest OT parallel, 0.511):** Tamid removal + shiqquts placement link is the strongest cross-reference in the dataset.

5. **Dan 12:1 -> Jer 30:7 (0.409):** "Time of trouble such as never was" echoes Jeremiah's "time of Jacob's trouble" language.

6. **1 Thess 4:16 -> Exo 19:19 (0.370):** Theophany trumpet connection between archangel's voice and Sinai trumpet.
