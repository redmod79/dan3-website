## Key Findings

### Purification Triad Co-occurrence
- [H6884](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6884/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (tsaraph) + [H1305](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1305/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (barar) + [H3835](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3835/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (laban) appear together ONLY in Dan 11:35 and Dan 12:10
- No other verse in the Hebrew Bible contains all three

### Hapax Pair
- [H1860](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1860/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (dera'on) occurs ONLY in Dan 12:2 and Isa 66:24

### Tamid Verb Distinction
- Dan 8:11: rum (H7311) -- Hophal "was lifted/taken away"
- Dan 11:31: sur (H5493) -- Hiphil "they took away"
- Dan 12:11: sur (H5493) -- Hophal "was taken away"
- Different verbs for the same concept of tamid removal

### Ma'oz Concentration
- [H4581](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4581/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (ma'oz) appears 6 times in Dan 11 alone (out of 37 total OT uses)
- Dan 11:1,7,10(2x),19,31,38(2x),39(2x) -- internal vocabulary thread

### Maskilim Chain
- [H7919](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h7919/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (sakal) Hiphil Participle appears in Dan 11:33, 11:35, 12:3, 12:10
- Bridges Maccabean persecution section to resurrection promise

### Archangel Rarity
- [G743](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g743/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (archaggelos) occurs ONLY in 1 Thess 4:16 and Jude 1:9
- Both refer to Michael
