# Topics Research

## Question
Does the Bible teach one unified people of God or two separate programs for Israel and the Church? Comprehensive test of FUT's Israel/Church distinction across both testaments.

## Topics

### CHURCH
**(Hebrew: qahal, 'edah; Greek: ekklesia)**
The people of God; the collective body of believers. (Also, CONGREGATION, ASSEMBLY, THE BODY OF CHRIST.)

**Key Designations (selected for this study):**
- Called THE CONGREGATION in the O.T.: EXO 12:3,6,19,47; 16:1,2,9,10,22; LEV 4:13,15; 10:17; 24:14
- Called CHURCH in the N.T.: MAT 16:18; 18:17; ACT 2:47; 1CO 11:18; 14:19,23,28,33,34; 15:9; GAL 1:13
- ASSEMBLY OF THE SAINTS: PSA 89:7
- BODY OF CHRIST: EPH 1:22,23; COL 1:24
- BRIDE OF CHRIST: REV 21:9
- FOLD OF CHRIST: JHN 10:16
- HABITATION OF GOD: EPH 2:22
- HEAVENLY JERUSALEM: GAL 4:26; HEB 12:22
- HOUSEHOLD OF GOD: EPH 2:19
- ISRAEL OF GOD: GAL 6:16
- LAMB'S WIFE: REV 19:7-9; 21:9

- FLOCK OF GOD: EZK 34:15; 1PE 5:2

**Unity of:** PSA 133:1; JHN 10:16; 17:11,21-23; ROM 12:4,5; 1CO 10:17; 12:5,12-27; GAL 3:26-28; EPH 1:10; 2:14-19,21; 3:6,15; 4:4-6,12,13,16,25; COL 3:11,15

**Note:** Nave's explicitly lists "ISRAEL OF GOD: GAL 6:16" as one of the church's designations, and the OT/NT linguistic overlap is noted: "Called THE CONGREGATION in the O.T. ... Called CHURCH in the N.T."
