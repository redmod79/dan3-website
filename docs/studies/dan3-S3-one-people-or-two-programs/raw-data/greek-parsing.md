Greek: μὴ κατακαυχῶ τῶν κλάδων· εἰ δὲ κατακαυχᾶσαι, οὐ σὺ τὴν ῥίζαν βαστάζεις ἀλλὰ ἡ ῥίζα σέ.

Key: "the root bears YOU" -- ἡ ῥίζα σέ (he rhiza se) -- the root supports the branches, not vice versa.

### v.20
Greek: καλῶς· τῇ ἀπιστίᾳ ἐξεκλάσθησαν, σὺ δὲ τῇ πίστει ἕστηκας.

Key terms:
- ἀπιστίᾳ (apistia) [G570](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g570/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- unbelief, Dat Sg F (instrumental dative -- "by means of unbelief")
- πίστει (pistei) [G4102](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4102/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- faith, Dat Sg F (instrumental dative -- "by means of faith")
- ἕστηκας (hestēkas) [G2476](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2476/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- you stand, Perf Act Ind 2S (perfect tense = ongoing state)

The contrast is symmetrical: unbelief (apistia) vs. faith (pistis), both in the dative of means. The criterion for inclusion/exclusion is identical for natural and grafted branches.

## Galatians 3:16
Greek: τῷ δὲ Ἀβραὰμ ἐρρέθησαν αἱ ἐπαγγελίαι καὶ τῷ σπέρματι αὐτοῦ. οὐ λέγει Καὶ τοῖς σπέρμασιν, ὡς ἐπὶ πολλῶν, ἀλλ' ὡς ἐφ' ἑνός Καὶ τῷ σπέρματί σου, ὅς ἐστιν Χριστός.

Key: σπέρματι (Dat Sg N) vs σπέρμασιν (Dat Pl N) -- Paul's singular/plural argument. The relative pronoun ὅς (hos, Nom Sg M) identifies the seed as masculine = a person = Christ.

## Galatians 3:28-29
### v.28
Greek: οὐκ ἔνι Ἰουδαῖος οὐδὲ Ἕλλην, οὐκ ἔνι δοῦλος οὐδὲ ἐλεύθερος, οὐκ ἔνι ἄρσεν καὶ θῆλυ· πάντες γὰρ ὑμεῖς εἷς ἐστε ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ.

Key: εἷς ἐστε (heis este) -- "you are one" (εἷς is masculine singular, not neuter ἕν; the believers are one person/entity in Christ, not merely one thing)

### v.29
Greek: εἰ δὲ ὑμεῖς Χριστοῦ, ἄρα τοῦ Ἀβραὰμ σπέρμα ἐστέ, κατ' ἐπαγγελίαν κληρονόμοι.

Key: σπέρμα ἐστέ (sperma este) -- "you are seed" (predicate nominative); κατ' ἐπαγγελίαν κληρονόμοι -- "according to promise, heirs" (not according to ethnicity)

## John 10:16
Greek: καὶ ἄλλα πρόβατα ἔχω ἃ οὐκ ἔστιν ἐκ τῆς αὐλῆς ταύτης· κἀκεῖνα δεῖ με ἀγαγεῖν, καὶ τῆς φωνῆς μου ἀκούσουσιν, καὶ γενήσεται μία ποίμνη, εἷς ποιμήν.

Key terms:
- αὐλή (aule) [G833](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g833/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- fold/courtyard -- NOT the same as ποίμνη
- ποίμνη (poimne) [G4167](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4167/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- flock (the end result: one flock)
- ποιμήν (poimen) [G4166](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- shepherd
- μία (mia) [G1520](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1520/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- one (Nom Sg F)
- εἷς (heis) [G1520](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1520/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- one (Nom Sg M)
- δεῖ (dei) [G1163](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1163/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- it is necessary (divine necessity)

Note: KJV translates both aule and poimne as "fold," but they are different Greek words. The "other sheep" are not of this aule (enclosure/courtyard = Jewish fold) but will become one poimne (flock) with one poimen (shepherd).
