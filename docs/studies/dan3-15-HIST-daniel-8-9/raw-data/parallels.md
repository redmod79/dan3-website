### Key Findings from Parallels

1. **Dan 8:14 erev-boqer links to Genesis creation formula** (Gen 1:5,8 are top OT matches), NOT to DOA formula. Confirms [H4](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} directive.

2. **Dan 9:24 links to Lev 16:16** (0.427) — DOA vocabulary overlap (cover/kaphar + holiness/qodesh + iniquity/avon + rebellion/pesha). Confirms DOA connection.

3. **Dan 9:27 links to 2Chr 36:21** (0.460) — cease/shabat + destruction, connecting 70 weeks to sabbath-year violation and exile.

4. **Mat 24:15 links to Dan 9:2** (0.406) — "daniel, prophet, understand." Jesus's reference to Daniel validates the biyn chain (understand/noeo).

5. **Dan 9:11 links to Deut 29:20** (0.494) — "covenant, curse, instruction, write." Confirms Dan 9 prayer invokes the Levitical covenant-curse framework (Lev 26 / Deut 28-29).
