# Topics Research

## Question
How does historicism read Daniel 7, and what is the textual basis for identifying the little horn as a centuries-long religio-political power?

## Topics

### HORN
- Used to contain anointing oil (1SA 16:1; 1KI 1:39); used for trumpet
- **Figurative**: divine protection (2SA 22:3); power (1KI 22:11; PSA 89:24; 92:10; 132:17)
- **Symbolical**: DAN 7:7-24; 8:3-9,20; AMO 6:13; MIC 4:13; HAB 3:4; ZEC 1:18-21; REV 5:6; 12:3; 13:1,11; 17:3-16 — Horns consistently represent political/military power throughout OT and NT symbolic usage

### JUDGMENT
- **The General**: 1CH 16:33; PSA 9:7; 50:3-6; 96:13; 98:9; ECC 3:17; 11:9; 12:14; EZK 18:20-28; DAN 7:9,10; AMO 4:12; MAT 3:12; 7:22,23; 12:36,37; 13:30,40-43; 16:27; 25:1-46; JHN 5:22; 12:48; ACT 10:42; 17:31; ROM 2:5-12; 1CO 3:13; 4:5; 6:2; 2CO 5:10; 2TH 1:7,8; HEB 6:2; 9:27; 10:27; 1PE 4:5,7; 2PE 2:4,9; 3:7,10-12; REV 1:7; 6:15-17; 11:18; 20:11-15; 22:12
- **According to Works**: JOB 34:11; PSA 62:12; ISA 3:10,11; JER 17:10,11; ROM 2:5-12; 1CO 3:8,12-15; REV 2:23; 20:12,13

### ANCIENT OF DAYS
- An appellative of Jehovah: DAN 7:9,13,22 — only three occurrences, all in Daniel 7

### THRONE
- **Of God**: 2CH 18:18; PSA 9:4,7; 11:4; 47:8; 89:14; 97:2; 103:19; ISA 6:1; 66:1; MAT 5:34; 23:22; HEB 8:1; 12:2; REV 14:3,5
- **Of Christ**: MAT 19:28; 25:31; ACT 2:30; REV 1:4; 3:21; 4:2-10; 7:9-17; 19:4; 21:5; 22:3
- Key connection: PSA 89:14 and 97:2 describe God's throne with "righteousness and judgment" — matching Dan 7:9-10 judgment scene

### SABBATH
- **Foundational texts**: GEN 2:2,3; EXO 20:8-11; 31:13-17
- **As sign**: EZK 20:12,13,16,20,21,24 (sign between God and His people)
- **Sabbath blessing**: ISA 56:2,4-7; 58:13,14
- **Sabbath violation rebuked**: NEH 13:15-22; JER 17:21-27; EZK 22:8; 23:38
- **NT continuity**: MAT 12:1-8; MRK 2:27,28; ACT 13:14,42,44; 16:13; 17:2; 18:4; HEB 4:4,9
- Directly relevant to Dan 7:25 "think to change times and laws"

### COMMANDMENTS
- **General**: EXO 20:3-17; DEU 5:6-21 (Decalogue)
- **OF MEN** (contrast category): ISA 29:13; MAT 15:3-20; MRK 7:2-23; 1TI 4:1-3
- The contrast between God's commandments and human commandments is directly relevant to evaluating Dan 7:25

### ROMAN EMPIRE
- Augustus Caesar LUK 2:1; Tiberius Caesar LUK 3:1; Claudius Caesar ACT 18:2; Nero PHP 4:22
- Citizenship rights: ACT 16:37; 22:25-29; right of trial ACT 25:16; right of appeal ACT 25:10,21
- NT canonical evidence that Rome was the ruling power during the time of Christ and the apostles

### PERSECUTION
- **Of the Righteous**: PSA 44:15-18,22; 119:85-87,95,157; ISA 26:20; MAT 5:10-12; 10:16-18,21-23; 24:8-10; JHN 16:1,2; ACT 8:1,4; ROM 8:17,35-37; 2CO 4:8-12; 2TI 3:12; HEB 10:32-34; 11:33-38; REV 2:3,10,13; 6:9-11; 7:13-17; 12:11; 17:6; 20:4
- Directly parallels Dan 7:21 (horn making war with saints) and 7:25 (wear out the saints)

### DRAGON
- Applied to Pharaoh (ISA 51:9); to Satan (REV 20:2)
- Symbolical: EZK 29:3; 32:2; REV 12; 13; 16:13
- REV 12:3 — great red dragon with seven heads and ten horns (parallel to Dan 7's fourth beast)

### ATONEMENT (DAY OF)
- **Time**: EXO 30:10; LEV 23:27; 25:9; NUM 29:7
- **How observed**: LEV 16:2-34; 23:27-32; NUM 29:7-11; HEB 5:3; 9:7,19,22
- **Key ritual elements**: white linen garments (16:4), fire/incense (16:12-13), cloud on mercy seat (16:2), blood sprinkled (16:14-15), no one present during atonement (16:17), records of sin (16:16)
- These elements parallel the Dan 7:9-14 judgment scene: white garments, fire, cloud, books/records, exclusion

### CHURCH AND STATE
- Ecclesiastical superior: 1SA 10:1; 15:1-35; 16:1-13; 2KI 9:1-26
- Attempted usurpation reproved: 1SA 13:8-14; 2CH 26:16-21
- State subverting religion: Jeroboam (1KI 12:26-33); Manasseh (2CH 33:2-9)
- Relevant to Dan 7:24-25 — the horn as "diverse" (religious + political) power

### LEOPARD
- Fierceness: JER 5:6; HOS 13:7; HAB 1:8
- FIGURATIVE: DAN 7:6 — the third beast
- Taming of, triumph of the gospel: ISA 11:6

### KINGDOM OF HEAVEN
- Parables: MAT 13:24-50; 18:23-35; 22:2-14; 25:1-30
- "My kingdom is not of this world" JHN 18:36
- Not meat and drink: ROM 14:17
- Connects to Dan 7:14,18,22,27 — the everlasting kingdom given to saints

### DANIEL
- Jewish captive, educated at king's court (DAN 1)
- Interprets visions (DAN 2; 4; 5)
- Prophecies: DAN 7; 8; 9; 10; 11; 12; referenced by Jesus (MAT 24:15)

### COURT
- Ecclesiastical: 1CH 26:29-32; 2CH 19:8-11
- Civil composition and procedure: EXO 18:25,26; DEU 1:15-17; 17:8-13
- Relevant to Dan 7:10,26 — "the judgment was set" / "the court shall sit"

## Related Topics Discovered
- **SAINTS/RIGHTEOUS**: cross-reference from SAINTS topic; relevant to Dan 7:18,22,25,27
- **DOMINION / MAN, DOMINION OF**: relevant to Dan 7:14,27 kingdom language
- **PROPHECY**: extensive messianic fulfillment connections, ISA 9:7 with DAN 7:14,27
