# Raw Greek Parsing Data

## 2 Thessalonians 2:3
- apostasia (G646) N-NSF = "the falling away/apostasy"
- anthropos tes anomias (G444+G458) = "the man of lawlessness/sin"
- ho huios tes apoleias (G5207+G684) = "the son of perdition/destruction"

## 2 Thessalonians 2:4
- ho antikemenos (G480) V-PNP-NSM Pres M/P Ptcp = "the one opposing" (present middle participle)
- huperairomenos (G5229) V-PPP-NSM Pres Pass Ptcp = "exalting himself" (present passive participle)
- epi panta legomenon theon = "above all that is called God" — panta (G3956) acc sg
- e sebasma (G4574) N-ASN = "or object of worship"
- hoste auton eis ton naon tou theou kathisai = "so that he sits in the temple of God" — kathizo (G2523) Aor Act Inf
- apodeiknunta heauton hoti estin theos = "showing himself that he is God"
- **Key parallel to Dan 11:36:** hyperairomenos epi panta legomenon theon maps to yitromem...al kol el

## 1 Thessalonians 4:16
- autos ho kurios = "the Lord himself" — emphatic
- en keleusmati (G2752) N-DSN = "with a shout/command"
- en phone archangelou (G743) N-GSM = "with the voice of the/an archangel" — genitive singular, anarthrous
- en salpingi theou (G4536) = "with the trumpet of God"
- katabesetai (G2597) V-FDI-3S Fut Mid Ind = "shall descend"
- hoi nekroi en Christo anastesontai (G450) V-FMI-3P Fut Mid Ind = "the dead in Christ shall rise"
- **Key question:** Is "voice of archangel" appositional (the Lord IS the archangel) or instrumental (accompanied BY archangel's voice)?

## Matthew 24:15
- to bdelygma (G946) N-ASN = "the abomination" — neuter singular with article
- tes eremoseos (G2050) N-GSF = "of desolation" — genitive
- to hrethen dia Daniel tou prophetou = "spoken of through Daniel the prophet" — rheo Aor Pass Ptcp
- hestos (G2476) V-2RAP-ASN Perf Act Ptcp = "standing" (perfect = established state)
- en topo hagio = "in the holy place"
- ho anaginoskon noeito (G3539) V-PAM-3S Pres Act Impv = "let the reader understand"
- **Jesus treats Daniel as prophetic:** dia + genitive = "through" Daniel = instrumental authorship

## Jude 1:9
- ho de Michael ho archangelos (G3413 + G743) = "but Michael THE archangel"
- ho archangelos: articular = THE archangel (unique, not "an archangel")
- hote to diabolo diakrinomenos (G1252) V-PMP-NSM Pres Mid Ptcp = "when contending with the devil"
- dielegeto (G1256) V-INI-3S Impf M/P Ind = "was disputing" — imperfect = ongoing action
- peri tou Mouseos somatos = "about the body of Moses"
- ouk etolmesen (G5111) V-AAI-3S Aor Act Ind = "dared not"
- krisin epenegkein blasphemias = "bring a railing judgment/accusation"
- alla eipen Epitimesai soi kurios = "but said, 'The Lord rebuke you'" — epitimao (G2008) Aor Act Opt = optative of wish

## Revelation 12:7
- egeneto polemos en to ourano = "there was war in heaven"
- ho Michael kai hoi angeloi autou = "Michael and his angels" — articular Michael
- tou polemesai (G4170) V-AAN Aor Act Inf = "to make war"
- meta tou drakontos = "with the dragon"
- ho drakon epolemesen kai hoi angeloi autou = "the dragon fought and his angels"

## Revelation 13:5
- edothe auto stoma laloun megala kai blasphemias = "a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies was given to it"
- stoma laloun megala: cf. Dan 7:25 "shall speak great words" — vocabulary chain across testaments
- edothe auto exousia poiesai menas tesserakonta duo = "authority was given to it to act for 42 months"
- 42 months = 1260 days = time, times, half a time (Rev 12:6,14; Dan 7:25; 12:7)

## Revelation 13:6
- enoixen to stoma autou eis blasphemias pros ton theon = "opened its mouth in blasphemies against God"
- blasphemesai to onoma autou kai ten skenen autou = "to blaspheme his name and his tabernacle"
- tous en to ourano skenountas = "those dwelling in heaven" — skene/skenoo vocabulary
