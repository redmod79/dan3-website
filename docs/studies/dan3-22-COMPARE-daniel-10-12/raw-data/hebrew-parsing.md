# Raw Hebrew Parsing Data

## Dan 10:1
- biyn (H995) Qal Perf 3ms = "understood" + binah (noun) = "understanding"
- mar'eh (H4758) = "sight/vision" — same term as Dan 8:26-27 linking this vision back to Dan 8
- davar (H1697) = "word/matter" revealed, not chazon (vision)

## Dan 10:13
- Michael echad ha-sarim ha-rishonim: echad (H259) Noun.s.Cst + ha-sarim (H8269) Noun.mp.Abs + ha-rishonim (H7223) Adj.mp.Abs
- Partitive: "one of the chief/first princes" OR superlative: "the first of the chief princes"
- amad (H5975) Qal Ptcp = "standing" (opposing the speaker)

## Dan 10:14
- acharit ha-yamim: acharit (H319) Noun.fs.Cst + ha-yamim = "the latter days"
- lehivin-kha: biyn Hiphil InfCon = "to cause you to understand" (biyn chain)
- chazon (H2377) = "vision" + la-yamim = "for many days"

## Dan 11:16
- kir'tsono: ki (prep "as") + ratson (H7522) Noun.ms.Abs + 3ms suffix = "according to his pleasure/will"
- ha-ba (H935) Qal Ptcp = "the one coming" (against him)
- ya'amod (H5975) Qal Impf 3ms = "shall stand" in erets ha-tsevi = "the glorious land"
- kalah (H3617) Noun.fs = "destruction" in his hand

## Dan 11:22
- zero'ot ha-sheteph: zero'ot (H2220) Noun.fp.Cst = "arms of" + sheteph (H7858) Noun.ms = "the flood"

- yishshaberu (H7665) Niphal Impf 3mp = "shall be broken"
- negid (H5057) Noun.ms.Cst = "prince/ruler of" + berith (H1285) Noun.fs = "covenant"
