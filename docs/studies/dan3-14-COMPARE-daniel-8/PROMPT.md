### Focus Areas

1. **The mehem antecedent: grammar textbook evidence for Dan 8:9 origin.** Tool discoveries show [H4704](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4704/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} mits'eirah is a hapax legomenon (1 occurrence, only Dan 8:9), and the HORN topic links Dan 8:3-9,20 as a unit. The three positions disagree on whether the horn arises from the winds (ruchoth, feminine) or the horns (qeranoth, feminine) or the compass points (implicit masculine). The plan specifies GKC 135o (constructio ad sensum) as the key grammar reference. The research agent should: retrieve Dan 8:8-9 with full Hebrew parsing (hebrew_parser.py), search semantic_grammar.py for "constructio ad sensum" and "gender agreement pronoun antecedent" in GKC and Waltke-O'Connor, parse the clause structure of 8:8-9, and trace the pronoun chain mehem -> achat -> mits'eirah.

2. **The gadal/yether three-stage progression: does the text REQUIRE the horn to exceed both named empires?** Tool discoveries show [H1431](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1431/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} gadal appears 115 times with meanings spanning "grow great, magnify, exceed" and appears in Dan 8:4,8,9,10,11,25. [H3499](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3499/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} yether (101 occ.) means "surplus, excess, preeminence" and appears in Dan 8:9. The prior word study (daniel-8-great-progression) established the stem shift Hiphil->Qal and directional meaning. The research agent should: run search_strongs.py --verses [H1431](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1431/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} to get all translation variants, run search_strongs.py --verses [H3499](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3499/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} to see all usages and determine if yether ever means anything LESS than surplus/exceeding, retrieve Dan 8:4,8,9 with hebrew_parser.py to verify the stem forms (Hiphil vs Qal), and retrieve the directional indicator verses (8:4 three directions, 8:9 three directions) to confirm territorial meaning.

4. **2300 erev-boqer: full days vs. 1150 half-days, and the historical math test.** The plan requires grammatical analysis of the compound erev-boqer against Gen 1:5,8 (creation day formula) and the DOA tamid formula (Num 28:3-4 erev/boqer as separate sacrifices). This is verifiable biblical/historical data the research agent should lay out.
