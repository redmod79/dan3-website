# Raw Strong's Lookup Data

## [H1431](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1431/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} gadal -- to be/make great
- Transliteration: gawdal
- POS: verb
- BLB Count: 115
- Definition: a primitive root; properly, to twist; to be (causatively make) large (in various senses, as in body, mind, estate or honor, also in pride) -- advance, boast, bring up, exceed, excellent, be(-come, do, give, make, wax), great(-er, come to... estate, things), grow(up), increase, lift up, magnify(-ifical), be much set by, nourish (up), pass, promote, proudly (spoken), tower.
- Key Daniel occurrences: Dan 1:5; 8:4; 8:8; 8:9; 8:10; 8:11; 8:25; 8:25; 11:36; 11:37
- Translated as "great" 9 times including: Gen 12:2; Gen 26:13; Dan 8:8; Dan 8:9
- Stem forms in Dan 8: Hiphil (8:4, 8:8, 8:11, 8:25), Qal (8:9, 8:10)

## [H3499](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3499/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} yether -- surplus, excess, preeminence
- Transliteration: yeh-ther
- POS: masculine noun
- BLB Count: 101
- Definition: from yathar; properly an overhanging, i.e. (by implication) an excess, superiority, remainder; also a small rope -- abundant, cord, exceeding, excellancy(-ent), what they leave, that hath left, plentifully, remnant, residue, rest, string, with.
- Dan 8:9 is the ONLY Daniel occurrence
- Translated "exceeding" 1 time: Dan 8:9
- Semantic range: remainder/residue/surplus/excellency -- always implies "more than" or "above"

## [H6663](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6663/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} tsadaq -- to be/make right (forensic)
- Transliteration: tsaw-dak
- POS: verb
- BLB Count: 41 (54 KJV occurrences)
- Definition: a primitive root; to be (causatively, make) right (in a moral or forensic sense) -- cleanse, clear self, (be, do) just(-ice, -ify, -ify self), (be turn to) righteous(-ness).
- Translation spread: "be justified" (4x), "righteous" (2x), "justify" (2x), "just" (2x), "be cleansed" (1x, Dan 8:14), "righteousness" (1x, Dan 12:3)
- LXX mapping: [G1344](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1344/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} dikaioo (21x, PMI 8.73) = "to render just"; [G1342](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1342/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} dikaios (22x) = "equitable"; [G2919](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2919/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} krino (8x) = "judge/decide"
- Niphal form: ONLY Dan 8:14 (nitsdaq) -- the sole Niphal occurrence in entire OT
- Hiphil forms: Isa 53:11 (yatsdiq); Dan 12:3 (matsdiqey)
- Daniel occurrences: Dan 8:14 (Niphal); Dan 12:3 (Hiphil ptcp)
- Key forensic passages: Gen 38:26; 44:16; Exo 23:7; Deut 25:1; 2Sam 15:4; 1Ki 8:32; Job 9:2,15,20; 33:32; Psa 51:6; 82:3; 143:2; Pro 17:15; Isa 5:23; 43:9,26; 45:25; 50:8; 53:11

- Daniel occurrences: Dan 8:11; 8:12; 8:13; 11:31; 12:11

## [H2195](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2195/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} za'am -- indignation, fury

## [H2852](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2852/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} chathak -- to cut off/determine
- Transliteration: khaw-thak
- POS: verb
- BLB Count: 1 (HAPAX LEGOMENON)
- Definition: a primitive root; properly, to cut off, i.e. (figuratively) to decree -- determine.
- ONLY occurrence: Dan 9:24 (Niphal: nechtakh = "are determined/cut off")
- The hapax status and root meaning "cut off" is significant for the Dan 8-9 connection argument: 70 weeks "cut off" implies a larger period from which they are cut
