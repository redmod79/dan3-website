## 2 Thessalonians 2:3
```

καὶ ἀποκαλυφθῇ ὁ ἄνθρωπος τῆς ἀνομίας, ὁ υἱὸς τῆς ἀπωλείας,

Key parsing:
- ἐξαπατήσῃ (exapataō, G1818) — Aor Act Subj 3s — "deceive" (subjunctive = conditional)
- ἔλθῃ (erchomai, G2064) — 2nd Aor Act Subj 3s — "come" (apostasy must COME first)
- ἀποστασία (apostasia, G646) — Nom Sg F — "falling away/rebellion/apostasy"
- ἀποκαλυφθῇ (apokalyptō, G601) — Aor Pass Subj 3s — "be revealed" (passive divine unveiling)
- ἄνθρωπος τῆς ἀνομίας — "the man of lawlessness" (genitive of quality)
- υἱὸς τῆς ἀπωλείας — "the son of perdition/destruction" (G684 apoleia)
  - Same phrase as John 17:12 (Judas) — exclusive two-person chain
```

## Revelation 17:12
```
Greek: καὶ τὰ δέκα κέρατα ἃ εἶδες δέκα βασιλεῖς εἰσιν,
οἵτινες βασιλείαν οὔπω ἔλαβον, ἀλλὰ ἐξουσίαν ὡς βασιλεῖς μίαν ὥραν λαμβάνουσιν μετὰ τοῦ θηρίου.

Key parsing:
- δέκα κέρατα... δέκα βασιλεῖς εἰσιν — "ten horns... are ten kings" (present tense equation)
- οὔπω (oupō, G3768) — adverb "not yet" — CRITICAL for FUT
  → "who have not yet received a kingdom" — from John's vantage point (~95 AD), these kings are FUTURE
- ἔλαβον (lambanō, G2983) — 2nd Aor Act Ind 3p — "received" (constative aorist)
- λαμβάνουσιν (lambanō, G2983) — Pres Act Ind 3p — "they receive" (futuristic present)
- μίαν ὥραν — "one hour" — extremely brief co-reign with the beast
- μετὰ τοῦ θηρίου — "with the beast" — simultaneous with the beast's rule
```

- Πεπλήρωται (plēroō, G4137) — Perf Pass Ind 3s — "has been fulfilled" (perfect = completed state with ongoing results)

- μετανοεῖτε (metanoeō, G3340) — Pres Act Impv 2p — "repent!" (present imperative = ongoing)

→ FUT weakness: Both perfects indicate completed action — the kingdom is already inaugurated
