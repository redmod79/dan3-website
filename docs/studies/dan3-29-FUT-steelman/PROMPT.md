## Prior Research Summary

Across them, ~70+ distinct FUT arguments have been identified, ranked in four tiers by evidential strength. The reference agent compiled these into a tiered synthesis:

**Tier 1 (Strongest):** Three-author NT convergence (Jesus, Paul, John all treat Daniel's figures as future); Rev 13:5 verbatim quotation of Dan 7:8; eth qets chain terminating at bodily resurrection (Dan 12:2); four-kingdom schema shared with HIST.

**Tier 4 (Framework):** Gap thesis / Israel-Church distinction; pretribulation rapture; Anderson-Hoehner calculation; Third Temple requirement; clay = democracy; 360-day prophetic year.

**Honest weaknesses** (16+) are compiled and ranked: Critical (gap thesis lacks textual basis, Israel/Church distinction faces 6 NT counter-passages, Mark 1:15 peplērotai), Severe (Dan 7:13 direction toward God, kingdom-already-present texts, stone/cornerstone chain, la-rabbim echo), Moderate (break at 11:36 inferred, kir'tsono continuity, "mystery already works").
