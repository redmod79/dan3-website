## Additional Verse Parsing Data

### Revelation 11:9 — Greek word data
"And they of the people and kindreds and tongues and nations shall see their dead bodies three days and an half, and shall not suffer their dead bodies to be put in graves."
- [G2250](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2250/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (hemera) = "days" — 21x in Revelation
- [G2255](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2255/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (hemisu) = "an half" — 3x in Revelation (also in 11:11, 12:14)
- Three days and a half (treis hemeras kai hemisu) — uses hemeras (days), NOT kairon (times)
- FUT argues this is literal 3.5 days proving the period in Rev 11:2-3 is also literal

### Revelation 11:11
"And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them."
- [G2250](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2250/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (hemera) = "days" — same word as 11:9
- [G2255](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2255/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (hemisu) = "an half"

### Genesis 29:27 — Hebrew word data
"Fulfil her week, and we will give thee this also for the service which thou shalt serve with me yet seven other years."
- [H7620](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h7620/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (shabuwa) = "week" — the week of Laban = 7 years of service
- [H8141](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8141/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (shanah) = "years" — seven other years
- [H4390](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4390/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (male') = "fulfil" — complete the week
- KEY: This is the primary OT precedent for shabuwa meaning a period of 7 years (not 7 days)
- Laban says "fulfil her week" meaning serve 7 more years — the week IS seven years
