# Raw Greek Parsing Data

## Revelation 12:14
Greek: καιρὸν καὶ καιροὺς καὶ ἥμισυ καιροῦ

- καιρὸν (kairon) = καιρός [G2540](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2540/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-ASM] Acc Sg M = "time/season" (accusative)
- καὶ (kai) = and
- καιροὺς (kairous) = καιρός [G2540](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2540/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-APM] Acc Pl M = "times" (plural accusative)
- καὶ (kai) = and
- ἥμισυ (hemisu) = ἥμισυς [G2255](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2255/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [A-ASN] Acc Sg N = "half"
- καιροῦ (kairou) = καιρός [G2540](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2540/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-GSM] Gen Sg M = "of time" (genitive)
- This is the Greek equivalent of Daniel 7:25's Aramaic: iddan ve-iddanin uflag iddan
- kairos (G2540) used in LXX to translate both iddan (Dan 7:25) and mo'ed (Dan 12:7)
- ἐδόθησαν (edothēsan) = δίδωμι [G1325](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1325/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [V-API-3P] Aor Pass Ind = "were given" — divine passive (edothe)
- τρέφεται (trephetai) = τρέφω [G5142](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5142/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [V-PPI-3S] Pres Pass Ind = "is nourished" — preservation context

## Revelation 11:2
Greek: μῆνας τεσσεράκοντα δύο

- μῆνας (mēnas) = μήν [G3376](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3376/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-APM] Acc Pl M = "months"
- τεσσεράκοντα (tesserakonta) = [G5062](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5062/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} = "forty"
- δύο (duo) = [G1417](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1417/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} = "two"
- = 42 months
- Context: HOSTILE — Gentiles trample the holy city
- ἐδόθη (edothē) = δίδωμι [G1325](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1325/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [V-API-3S] Aor Pass Ind = "was given" — divine passive

## Revelation 13:5
Greek: μῆνας τεσσεράκοντα δύο

- IDENTICAL expression to Rev 11:2: mēnas tesserakonta duo = 42 months
- Context: HOSTILE — beast's authority to continue/act
- ἐδόθη (edothē) = δίδωμι [G1325](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1325/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [V-API-3S] Aor Pass Ind = "was given" — SAME divine passive as Rev 11:2

Note on contextual differentiation pattern:
- 42 months (mēnas): Rev 11:2 (Gentile trampling — HOSTILE), Rev 13:5 (beast authority — HOSTILE)
- Both use edothe (divine passive) — the hostile activity is divinely PERMITTED
- 1260 days (hēmeras): Rev 11:3 (witnesses prophesy — PRESERVATION), Rev 12:6 (woman nourished — PRESERVATION)
- time, times, half a time (kairon): Rev 12:14 — Danielic cosmic-conflict framework

## Mark 1:15
Greek: Πεπλήρωται ὁ καιρὸς καὶ ἤγγικεν ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ Θεοῦ

- Πεπλήρωται (Peplērōtai) = πληρόω [G4137](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4137/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [V-RPI-3S] **Perf Pass Ind** = "has been fulfilled/completed"
  - PERFECT tense: completed action with present results
  - PASSIVE voice: the kairos was fulfilled by divine action, not human
- ὁ καιρὸς (ho kairos) = [G2540](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2540/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} "the time/appointed time"
- ἤγγικεν (ēngiken) = ἐγγίζω [G1448](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1448/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [V-RAI-3S] Perf Act Ind = "has drawn near"
- Jesus declares THE kairos (articular — the specific appointed time) has been FULFILLED
- This is the NT confirmation that Daniel's prophetic timetable (70 weeks) reached its appointed end

## Galatians 4:4
Greek: ὅτε δὲ ἦλθεν τὸ πλήρωμα τοῦ χρόνου

- τὸ πλήρωμα (to plērōma) = πλήρωμα [G4138](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4138/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-NSN] = "the fullness/completion"
- τοῦ χρόνου (tou chronou) = χρόνος [G5550](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5550/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-GSM] = "of the time/chronological period"
- Note: Paul uses chronos (chronological time) rather than kairos (appointed time) — emphasizing that a specific chronological period was completed
- ἐξαπέστειλεν (exapesteilen) = [G1821](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1821/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} Aor Act Ind = "sent forth" — God's decisive action at the completion of the time period

## 2 Peter 3:8
Greek: μία ἡμέρα παρὰ Κυρίῳ ὡς χίλια ἔτη καὶ χίλια ἔτη ὡς ἡμέρα μία

- μία ἡμέρα (mia hēmera) = "one day" — ἡμέρα [G2250](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2250/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-NSF]
- παρὰ Κυρίῳ (para Kyriō) = "with the Lord" — dative
- ὡς (hōs) = "as/like" — comparative particle
- χίλια ἔτη (chilia etē) = "a thousand years" — ἔτος [G2094](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2094/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-NPN]
- Bidirectional: one day = 1000 years AND 1000 years = one day
- Context: God's patience, not a chronological conversion formula
- Echoes Psalm 90:4

## Revelation 12:5-6 (time compression passage)
Greek (12:5): ἔτεκεν (eteken) = τίκτω [G5088](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5088/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [V-2AAI-3S] Aor Act Ind = "she bore" (aorist — point action)
- ἡρπάσθη (hērpasthē) = ἁρπάζω [G726](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g726/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [V-API-3S] Aor Pass Ind = "was caught up" (aorist — point action)
- Christ's entire earthly life compressed into TWO aorist verbs: birth → caught up to God
Greek (12:6): 1260 days follows immediately — the time-compression argument

## Luke 4:25
Greek: ἐπὶ ἔτη τρία καὶ μῆνας ἕξ

- ἐπὶ ἔτη τρία = "for three years" — ἔτος [G2094](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2094/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-APN]
- καὶ μῆνας ἕξ = "and six months" — μήν [G3376](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3376/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [N-APM]
- PLAIN LITERAL time expression: "three years and six months"
- CONTRAST with prophetic formulations: Dan 7:25 "time, times, half a time"; Rev 12:14 "kairon kai kairous kai hemisu kairou"
- Same period (3.5 years) but expressed in LITERAL language vs. SYMBOLIC language
- James 5:17 uses same plain expression: "three years and six months"
