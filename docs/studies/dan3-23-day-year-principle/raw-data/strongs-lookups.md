## [H2852](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2852/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - chathak (חָתַךְ) — "to cut off, determine"
- Transliteration: châthak
- POS: verb
- BLB Count: 1 occurrence — HAPAX LEGOMENON
- Definition: "a primitive root; properly, to cut off, i.e. (figuratively) to decree: determine"
- ONLY occurrence: Dan 9:24 — "Seventy weeks are determined [nechtak]"
- Root meaning "to cut off" — Niphal passive: "are cut off"
- KJV translates as "determined" but root etymology = "cut off"
- This is critical for the HIST argument that 70 weeks are "cut off from" a larger period (2300 days)
