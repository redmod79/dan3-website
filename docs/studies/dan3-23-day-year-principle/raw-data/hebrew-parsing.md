# Raw Hebrew Parsing Data

## Numbers 14:34
Hebrew: בְּמִסְפַּר הַיָּמִים אֲשֶׁר־תַּרְתֶּם אֶת־הָאָרֶץ אַרְבָּעִים יוֹם יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה תִּשְׂאוּ אֶת־עֲוֹנֹתֵיכֶם אַרְבָּעִים שָׁנָה וִידַעְתֶּם אֶת־תְּנוּאָתִי

Key formula: **יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה** (yom lashshanah yom lashshanah)
- יוֹם (yom) = Noun.ms.Abs = day
- לַ (la) = Prep "to" + Art "the"
- שָּׁנָה (shanah) = Noun.fs.Abs = year
- Formula repeated twice: "day for the year, day for the year"
- Direction: 40 DAYS of spying → 40 YEARS of wandering (day→year)

## Ezekiel 4:6
Hebrew: אַרְבָּעִים יוֹם יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה נְתַתִּיו לָךְ

Key formula: **יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה** (yom lashshanah yom lashshanah)
- IDENTICAL formula to Num 14:34
- נְתַתִּיו (netattiyw) = Verb.Qal.Perf.1s.+3ms (natan) = "I have given/appointed it to you"
- Direction: Each YEAR of punishment → one DAY of lying (year→day, REVERSED from Num 14:34)
- 40 years of Judah's iniquity = 40 days lying on right side

## Daniel 8:14
Hebrew: עַד עֶרֶב בֹּקֶר אַלְפַּיִם וּשְׁלֹשׁ מֵאוֹת וְנִצְדַּק קֹדֶשׁ

Key construction: **עֶרֶב בֹּקֶר** (erev boqer)
- עֶרֶב (erev) = Noun.ms.Abs = evening
- בֹּקֶר (boqer) = Noun.ms.Abs = morning
- Both are bare masculine singular absolute nouns in ASYNDETIC juxtaposition (no conjunction, no prepositions, no verbs)
- אַלְפַּיִם (alpayim) = Noun.d.Abs = thousand (dual = 2000)
- וּשְׁלֹשׁ מֵאוֹת = and three hundred
- וְנִצְדַּק (venitsdaq) = Verb.Niphal.Perf.3ms (tsadaq) = "shall be justified/cleansed"
- קֹדֶשׁ (qodesh) = Noun.ms.Abs = holiness/sanctuary

**CONTRAST with Gen 1:5**: Gen 1:5 has "vayyehi erev vayyehi boqer" (and there was evening and there was morning) — with verbs (hayah) and conjunction (vav). Dan 8:14 is BARE: no verbs, no conjunction.

## Daniel 8:26
Hebrew: וּמַרְאֵה הָעֶרֶב וְהַבֹּקֶר אֲשֶׁר נֶאֱמַר אֱמֶת הוּא וְאַתָּה סְתֹם הֶחָזוֹן כִּי לְיָמִים רַבִּים

- מַרְאֵה (mar'eh) = Noun.ms.Cst = sight/vision/appearance
- הָעֶרֶב (ha-erev) = Art + evening
- וְהַבֹּקֶר (veha-boqer) = and + Art + morning
- Note: Here erev and boqer have ARTICLES (ha-) and conjunction (ve-), unlike the bare construction in 8:14
- סְתֹם (setom) = Verb.Qal.Impv.2ms (satam) = "shut up/close/stop up"
- הֶחָזוֹן (he-chazon) = Art + vision
- כִּי לְיָמִים רַבִּים = "because/for many days" (yamim rabbim)

## Daniel 8:27
Hebrew: וַאֲנִי דָנִיֵּאל נִהְיֵיתִי וְנֶחֱלֵיתִי יָמִים

- נִהְיֵיתִי (nihyeytiy) = Verb.Niphal.Perf.1s (hayah) = "I was overcome/done"
- וְנֶחֱלֵיתִי (venecheleitiy) = Verb.Niphal.Perf.1s (chalah) = "I became weak/sick"
- וָאֶשְׁתּוֹמֵם (va-eshtomem) = Verb.Hithpael.Wayq.1s (shamam) = "I was astonished/devastated/desolate"
  - Hithpael of shamam: reflexive/intensive of "be desolate" — implies overwhelming devastation
- הַמַּרְאֶה (ha-mar'eh) = the vision/appearance (NOT chazon — uses mar'eh, same root as 8:26)
- וְאֵין מֵבִין (ve-ein mevin) = "and none understood" — Hiphil.Ptcp of biyn

## Daniel 9:24
Hebrew: שָׁבֻעִים שִׁבְעִים נֶחְתַּךְ עַל־עַמְּךָ

- שָׁבֻעִים (shabuim) = Noun.mp.Abs (shabuwa) = weeks — NO yamim qualifier
- שִׁבְעִים (shib'im) = seventy
- נֶחְתַּךְ (nechtak) = Verb.Niphal.Perf.3ms (chathak, H2852) = "are determined/cut off"
  - HAPAX LEGOMENON — occurs only here in the entire Hebrew Bible
  - Root means "to cut off" — Niphal passive: "are cut off/decreed"
- וְלַחְתֹּם (velachtom) = Verb.Qal.InfCon (chatham, H2856) = "to seal"
- חָזוֹן וְנָבִיא = "vision and prophet"
- וְלִמְשֹׁחַ קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים = "and to anoint holy of holies"

## Daniel 10:2
Hebrew: שְׁלֹשָׁה שָׁבֻעִים יָמִים

- שְׁלֹשָׁה (sheloshah) = three
- שָׁבֻעִים (shabuim) = Noun.mp.Abs = weeks
- יָמִים (yamim) = Noun.mp.Abs = days
- CRITICAL: "three weeks OF DAYS" — yamim qualifier PRESENT, distinguishing from Dan 9:24 which has shabuim WITHOUT yamim

## Daniel 9:25
Hebrew: לְהָשִׁיב וְלִבְנוֹת יְרוּשָׁלַם עַד־מָשִׁיחַ נָגִיד

- לְהָשִׁיב (lehashib) = Verb.Hiphil.InfCon (shuv, H7725) = "to restore/return"
- וְלִבְנוֹת (velivnot) = Verb.Qal.InfCon (banah, H1129) = "to build"
- מָשִׁיחַ (mashiach) = Noun.ms.Abs = anointed one
- נָגִיד (nagid) = Noun.ms.Abs = prince/leader/chief

## Daniel 7:25 (Aramaic)
Aramaic: עִדָּן וְעִדָּנִין וּפְלַג עִדָּן

- עִדָּן (iddan) = Noun.ms.Abs = time [BDB: "a set time, technically a year"]
- וְעִדָּנִין (ve-iddanin) = and + Noun.mp.Abs = times (plural)
- וּפְלַג (uflag) = and + Noun.ms.Cst = half
- עִדָּן (iddan) = time
- לְהַשְׁנָיָה (lehashnayyah) = Verb.haf.InfCon (shna, H8133) = "to change"
- זִמְנִין (zimnin) = Noun.mp.Abs (zeman) = times/seasons
- וְדָת (vedat) = and + law
- COMPARE Dan 2:21: מְהַשְׁנֵא עִדָּנַיָּא (mehashneh iddanayya) — same Haphel of shna + iddan: God "changes times and seasons"

## Daniel 2:21 (Aramaic)
Aramaic: מְהַשְׁנֵא עִדָּנַיָּא וְזִמְנַיָּא

- מְהַשְׁנֵא (mehashneh) = Verb.haf.Ptcp.ms.Abs (shna) = "changing" — SAME Haphel form as Dan 7:25
- עִדָּנַיָּא (iddanayya) = Noun.mp.emph = the times
- וְזִמְנַיָּא (vezimnayya) = and + Noun.mp.emph = the seasons
- God changes times and seasons — the little horn in 7:25 "thinks to change" (same verb) — divine prerogative usurped

## Daniel 4:16 (Aramaic)
- אֶשְׁתּוֹמַם (eshtomam) = Verb.etpa.Perf.3ms (shamam) = "was astonished" — same root as Dan 8:27
- Note: Dan 4:16 does NOT contain iddan — the "seven times" (shib'ah iddanin) is in 4:13,23,25,32

## Daniel 12:7
Hebrew: לְמוֹעֵד מוֹעֲדִים וָחֵצִי

- לְמוֹעֵד (lemo'ed) = Prep + Noun.ms.Abs (mo'ed, H4150) = "for an appointed time"
- מוֹעֲדִים (mo'adim) = Noun.mp.Abs = appointed times (plural)
- וָחֵצִי (vachetsi) = and + half
- This is the HEBREW equivalent of Daniel 7:25's Aramaic "iddan ve-iddanin uflag iddan"
- mo'ed = "appointed time/season/feast" vs. iddan = "time/year"

## Daniel 12:11
Hebrew: יָמִים אֶלֶף מָאתַיִם וְתִשְׁעִים

- יָמִים (yamim) = Noun.mp.Abs (yom) = days — explicit yamim used for 1290
- הַתָּמִיד (ha-tamid) = Art + Noun.ms.Abs (tamid, H8548) = "the daily/continual"

## Genesis 1:5
Hebrew: וַיְהִי־עֶרֶב וַיְהִי־בֹקֶר יוֹם אֶחָד

- וַיְהִי (vayyehi) = Verb.Qal.Wayq.3ms (hayah) = "and there was"
- עֶרֶב (erev) = Noun.ms.Abs = evening
- וַיְהִי (vayyehi) = "and there was"
- בֹקֶר (boqer) = Noun.ms.Abs = morning
- יוֹם אֶחָד (yom echad) = day one
- CRITICAL CONTRAST with Dan 8:14: Gen 1:5 uses VERBS (vayyehi x2) and CONJUNCTION (vav) with erev-boqer; Dan 8:14 has BARE nouns in asyndetic juxtaposition

## Leviticus 25:8
Hebrew: שֶׁבַע שַׁבְּתֹת שָׁנִים שֶׁבַע שָׁנִים שֶׁבַע פְּעָמִים

- שֶׁבַע שַׁבְּתֹת שָׁנִים = "seven sabbaths of years"
- שֶׁבַע שָׁנִים שֶׁבַע פְּעָמִים = "seven years seven times"
- = 49 years → Jubilee framework
- shabbetot shanim = "sabbaths of years" — the concept of year-weeks (seven-year cycles)

## Daniel 8:17
Hebrew: כִּי לְעֶת־קֵץ הֶחָזוֹן

- לְעֶת (le-et) = to + time
- קֵץ (qets, H7093) = end
- הֶחָזוֹן (he-chazon) = the vision
- "for at the time of the end [shall be] the vision"

## Daniel 8:19
Hebrew: בְּאַחֲרִית הַזָּעַם כִּי לְמוֹעֵד קֵץ

- בְּאַחֲרִית (be-acharit, H319) = "in the end/latter part of"
- הַזָּעַם (ha-za'am) = "the indignation"
- לְמוֹעֵד (lemo'ed) = "for an appointed time of"
- קֵץ (qets) = "end"
- "in the last end of the indignation, for at the appointed time the end"
