## [H4437](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4437/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} — malkuw (kingdom, Aramaic)
- Transliteration: malkuw; Pronunciation: mal-koo; POS: feminine noun
- Definition: corresponding to; dominion (abstractly or concretely): kingdom, kingly, realm, reign
- 65 total occurrences, 30 unique translations
- Top translations: "the kingdom" (9), "kingdom" (8), "in the kingdom" (7), "a kingdom" (3)
- BDB Usage: 1. royalty, kingship, kingly authority (Dan 4:23,26,28,31,33); 2. organized (world-)kingdom (Dan 2:39x2, 2:40,41,42,44; 7:23x2, 7:24,27); of specific kings (Dan 2:37; 5:18,28; 6:1); of God (Dan 3:33; 7:27; 2:44x2; 4:31; 6:27); of Messiah (Dan 7:14x2); of saints (Dan 7:18x2, 7:22,27); 3. realm (territorial); 4. reign (time of reigning)
- Verses where translated "kingdom": Dan 2:39, 2:39, 2:40, 4:3, 6:1, 7:23, 7:24, 7:27
