## Revelation 1:1 — dei genesthai + esemainen
```
Ἀποκάλυψις Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ, ἣν ἔδωκεν αὐτῷ ὁ Θεός, δεῖξαι τοῖς δούλοις αὐτοῦ ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι ἐν τάχει, καὶ ἐσήμανεν ἀποστείλας διὰ τοῦ ἀγγέλου αὐτοῦ, τῷ δούλῳ αὐτοῦ Ἰωάνῃ

Key terms:
- Ἀποκάλυψις (apokalypsis) G602 [N-NSF] — "Revelation" (Nominative)
- δεῖ (dei) G1163 [V-PAI-3S] — "must" (Present Active Indicative, 3rd Sg) — divine necessity
- γενέσθαι (genesthai) G1096 [V-2ADN] — "come to pass" (Aorist Middle Infinitive of ginomai)
- ἐν τάχει (en tachei) G1722+G5034 — "shortly/with speed"
- ἐσήμανεν (esemainen) G4591 [V-AAI-3S] — "signified" (Aorist Active Indicative of semaino)
- ἀποστείλας (aposteilas) G649 [V-AAP-NSM] — "having sent" (Aorist Active Participle)
- ἀγγέλου (angelou) G32 [N-GSM] — "angel" (Genitive)

NOTE: "ha dei genesthai" (ἃ δεῖ γενέσθαι) = "things which must come to pass"
This echoes Dan 2:28 LXX verbatim: "what must be in the latter days"
esemainen (G4591, semaino) = "communicated by signs" — all 6 NT uses mean symbolic communication
```

## Matthew 21:44 — likmao (grinding to powder)
```

Key terms:
- πεσὼν (peson) G4098 [V-2AAP-NSM] — "having fallen" (Aorist Active Participle of pipto)
- λίθον (lithon) G3037 [N-ASM] — "stone" (Accusative)
- συνθλασθήσεται (synthlasthesetai) G4917 [V-FPI-3S] — "will be broken" (Future Passive Indicative of synthlao)
- λικμήσει (likmesei) G3039 [V-FAI-3S] — "will grind to powder/winnow" (Future Active Indicative of likmao)

NOTE: G3039 likmao appears only HERE and Luk 20:18 in the NT
LXX uses likmao in Dan 2:44 — direct lexical link between Mat 21:44 and Dan 2
Two-phase destruction: (1) fall ON the stone = broken; (2) stone falls ON = ground to powder
This mirrors Dan 2:34-35 two-phase action
```
