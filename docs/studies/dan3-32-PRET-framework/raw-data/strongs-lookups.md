# Raw Strong's Lookups

## [H1860](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1860/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} dera'on
Transliteration: der-aw-one. Masculine noun. 2 occurrences total. "an object of aversion: abhorring, contempt." Only in Dan 12:2 and Isa 66:24.

## [H7919](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h7919/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} sakal
70 occurrences. Key translations: wise (5x), understand (3x), prosper (3x). Hiphil participle form maskilim used in Dan 11:33; 11:35; 12:3; 12:10.

## [H8548](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8548/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} tamid
105 occurrences. Key translations: continually (53x), the continual (17x), the daily (7x). Pentateuchal tamid: Exo 29:38-42; Num 28:3-6. Daniel tamid chain: 8:11; 8:12; 8:13; 11:31; 12:11.

## [H4438](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4438/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} malkuwth
Used in Dan 8:22 for Greek successor "kingdoms" (malkuyot, plural). Same word family as [H4437](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4437/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} malkuw.

## [H4437](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4437/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} malkuw (Aramaic)
Aramaic kingdom. Dan 2:44; 7:14,18,27. Le-'alamayya 'almayya triple declaration.

## [H319](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h319/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} 'achariyth
"End, latter end, future." Dan 8:23 be-acharit malkutam timestamps horn within Greek successor era.
