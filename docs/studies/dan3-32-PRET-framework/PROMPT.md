### Focus Areas

1. **PRET's hermeneutical logic as a system:** WHAT — Map the complete PRET interpretive method: single-referent (Antiochus IV), literal time periods, Greek-era scope cap. WHY — Prior studies showed PRET's individual claims but not the logical architecture that generates them. HOW — Read dan3-28 PRET steelman for the complete framework; identify the core axioms from which all claims derive.

2. **The temporal scope cap — PRET's fundamental design choice:** WHAT — PRET's master commitment is that Daniel's prophecies terminate in the Maccabean era (c. 168-164 BC). Everything else flows from this. WHY — The eth qets chain, dera'on hapax, Dan 12:13, and NT development all extend beyond this cap. HOW — Trace the eth qets chain across Dan 8:17; 11:35; 11:40; 12:4; 12:9 and show how it forces eschatological scope. Retrieve Dan 12:2,13 and Isa 66:24 texts.

3. **PRET's bimodal performance — why Dan 8 succeeds and Dan 7 fails:** WHAT — PRET achieves 5 I-A(1) HIGH in Dan 8 but 0 HIGH in Dan 7. WHY — Dan 8 has explicit angel-given timestamps (be-acharit malkutam) while Dan 7 has everlasting-kingdom language. HOW — Compare specification-match profiles from dan3-10 (Dan 7) and dan3-14 (Dan 8).

4. **The progressive degradation pattern — is it accidental or structural?** WHAT — PRET match quality degrades from I-A(1) HIGH (Dan 11:21-35) to I-D LOW (Dan 11:40-45). WHY — dan3-22 documented this but didn't analyze whether it's a coincidence or reveals a framework problem. HOW — Compile the degradation sequence from dan3-22 data.

5. **The ~20+ ALL-item constraints — why so many hit PRET?** WHAT — More position-neutral constraints hit PRET than any other position (~20+ vs ~10+ FUT vs 0 HIST). WHY — These span scope (eth qets, dera'on), scale (gadal/yether), duration (bela Pa'el), literary unity (haben+mar'eh), and NT development. HOW — Categorize the constraints by TYPE to determine whether they cluster around one vulnerability or represent independent failures.

6. **The three FATAL weaknesses — detail problems or framework problems?** WHAT — F1: Dan 11:40-45 five-specification failure; F2: Dan 12:2 eschatological scope; F3: Dan 12:13 personal resurrection promise. WHY — If these are detail problems, PRET could theoretically be repaired. If framework problems, the system is structurally non-viable. HOW — Test whether any modification to PRET's core axioms could solve all three simultaneously.

7. **Where PRET is genuinely strongest — permanent contributions:** WHAT — Dan 8 specification matches, Dan 11:2-35 identifications, Dan 8/11 five-point verbal correspondence, shiqquts/ba'al shamem wordplay. WHY — Other positions must account for these genuine textual correspondences. HOW — Compile PRET's HIGH-confidence items and evaluate what they establish about Daniel's text.

8. **The 0/15 counter-argument scorecard — systemic or specific?** WHAT — PRET has 0 adequate responses to 15 counter-arguments (vs HIST 5/8, FUT 1/10). WHY — This could mean PRET lacks competent defenders, or the arguments are unanswerable at the system level. HOW — Categorize the 15 counter-arguments by type (scope, scale, duration, literary, NT) and check whether all derive from the same framework vulnerability.
