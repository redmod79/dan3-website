## Question

## Related Topics Discovered

### ABRAHAM
From Nave's entry: GEN 12:1-3; 15:6; 17:5; 22:16; ROM 4:1-22; 4:13,16-18; GAL 3:6-18,29; HEB 6:13,17,18; 7:1-10; 11:8-10,17; JAS 2:21-24

The Abrahamic covenant is repeatedly interpreted by NT authors as including Gentile believers. Abraham is called "the father of many nations" (Gen 17:5) and Paul interprets this as applying to all who share Abraham's faith, not just his ethnic descendants (Rom 4:11-17; Gal 3:7,29).

### ISRAEL
From Nave's entry: ISA 49:3 (Messiah called "Israel"); GEN 32:24-32; REV 21:12

The term "Israel" is applied to the Messiah himself in Isa 49:3, suggesting the identity "Israel" transcends ethnic boundaries and centers on the Messianic calling.
