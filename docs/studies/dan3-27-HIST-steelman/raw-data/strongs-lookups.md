# Raw Strong's Lookups

## [H1855](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1855/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - d'qaq (break in pieces)
- Transliteration: dᵉqaq
- POS: verb
- BLB Count: 10
- Definition: (Aramaic) corresponding to; to crumble or (trans.) crush: break to pieces.
- BDB: Pe. Perfect 3mp daquw Dan 2:35. Haph. break in pieces: Perfect 3fs haddeqet Dan 2:34,45; 3mp haddiquw Dan 6:24. Imperfect 3fs taddiq Dan 2:40 (absolute), Dan 2:44 (accusative); suffix taddᵉqinnah Dan 7:23; Participle active mehaddeq Dan 2:40 (accusative), feminine maddᵉqah Dan 7:7, Dan 7:19.
- 13 total occurrences; translations: brake in pieces, breaketh in pieces, broken to pieces, etc.
- KEY: Appears exclusively in Dan 2 (2:34,35,40,44,45) and Dan 7 (7:7,19,23) and Dan 6:24 -- binding chapters 2 and 7 together

## [H6663](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6663/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - tsadaq (be just/righteous/cleansed)

## [H2852](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2852/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - chathak (determine/cut off) -- HAPAX
- Transliteration: khaw-thak
- POS: verb
- BLB Count: 1
- Definition: a primitive root; properly to cut off, i.e. (figuratively) to decree: determine
- ONLY occurrence: Dan 9:24
- KEY: Daniel used charats (H2782) 3x elsewhere for "determine" (Dan 9:26,27; 11:36). His deliberate choice of chathak (="cut off") in 9:24 is an authorial signal that the 70 weeks are "cut off" from a larger period (the 2300 days of Dan 8:14)

## [H5732](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5732/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - 'iddan (time/year)
- Transliteration: id-dawn
- POS: masculine noun (Aramaic)
- BLB Count: 13
- Definition: (Aramaic) from a root corresponding to that of; a set time; technically, a year: time
- BDB: 1. in general, time as duration (Dan 2:8, 7:12). 2. definite time = year: "seven 'iddanin = seven years" (Dan 4:13,20,22,29); 'iddan we-'iddanin u-felag 'iddan (Dan 7:25, i.e. 3.5 years)
- KEY: BDB explicitly glosses 'iddan = year in Dan 4. This establishes the day-year principle: if 'iddan = year in Dan 4 (literal), the same word in Dan 7:25 should also = year.
