## Daniel 2:38 -- anteh-hu emphatic pronoun
```
וּבְכָל־דִּ֣י דָֽיְרִ֣ין בְּֽנֵי־אֲ֠נָשָׁא חֵיוַ֨ת בָּרָ֤א וְעֹוף־שְׁמַיָּא֙ יְהַ֣ב בִּידָ֔ךְ וְהַשְׁלְטָ֖ךְ בְּכָלְּהֹ֑ון אַנְתְּ־ה֔וּא רֵאשָׁ֖ה דִּ֥י דַהֲבָֽא׃

Key parsing:
- אנתה (anteh) = PersPron.2ms "you"
- ה֔וּא (hu') = PersPron.3ms "he"
- Together: anteh-hu = emphatic "YOU (are) HE" = "Thou art this head of gold"
- רֵאשָׁ֖ה (re'shah) = Noun.ms "head"
- דַהֲבָֽא (dahaba') = Noun.ms "gold"
- יְהַ֣ב (yehab) = Verb.peal.Perf.3ms "give" -- God GAVE authority
- הַשְׁלְטָ֖ךְ (hashletakh) = Verb.haf.Perf.3ms+2ms "caused you to rule"
```
NOTE: anteh-hu is E-tier identification -- the text explicitly names Babylon.

## Daniel 7:25 -- bela, shanah, sbar, dat, 'iddan
```
וּמִלִּ֗ין לְצַ֤ד עִלָּאָה֙ יְמַלִּ֔ל וּלְקַדִּישֵׁ֥י עֶלְיֹונִ֖ין יְבַלֵּ֑א וְיִסְבַּ֗ר לְהַשְׁנָיָה֙ זִמְנִ֣ין וְדָ֔ת וְיִתְיַהֲב֣וּן בִּידֵ֔הּ עַד־עִדָּ֥ן וְעִדָּנִ֖ין וּפְלַ֥ג עִדָּֽן׃

Key parsing:
- מִלִּ֗ין (millin) = Noun.fp.Abs "words" -- the horn's distinctive speech
- יְמַלִּ֔ל (yemallil) = Verb.pael.Impf.3ms "speak" -- Pael (intensive) = great/bombastic speech
- יְבַלֵּ֑א (yeballe') = Verb.pael.Impf.3ms "wear out" -- HAPAX, Pael imperfect = sustained attrition
- יִסְבַּ֗ר (yisbar) = Verb.peal.Impf.3ms "intend/think" -- NOT "succeed" but "plan/attempt"
- לְהַשְׁנָיָה֙ (lehashnayyah) = Verb.haf.InfCon "to change" -- Haphel (causative) of shanah
- זִמְנִ֣ין (zimnin) = Noun.mp.Abs "times" -- appointed times/seasons
- דָ֔ת (dat) = Noun.fs.Abs "law" -- absolute form (no possessive) = THE law (God's)
- עִדָּ֥ן (iddan) = Noun.ms.Abs "time" -- year (per BDB and Dan 4 usage)
- עִדָּנִ֖ין (iddanin) = Noun.mp.Abs "times" -- plural
- פְלַ֥ג (pelag) = Noun.ms.Cst "half"
```
NOTES:
- bela Pael imperfect = prolonged wearing action, not sudden destruction
- sbar = "intend/think" -- indicates attempted, not necessarily accomplished change
- dat in absolute = God's law (cf. Dan 6:5 where dat is used for God's law)
- 'iddan + 'iddanin + pelag 'iddan = 1 + 2 + 0.5 = 3.5 years/times

## Daniel 8:9 -- gadal with yether (exceeding great)
```
וּמִן־הָאַחַ֣ת מֵהֶ֔ם יָצָ֥א קֶֽרֶן־אַחַ֖ת מִצְּעִירָ֑ה וַתִּגְדַּל־יֶ֛תֶר אֶל־הַנֶּ֥גֶב וְאֶל־הַמִּזְרָ֖ח וְאֶל־הַצֶּֽבִי׃

Key parsing:
- מִצְּעִירָ֑ה (mits'eirah) = Noun.fs.Abs "youth/smallness" -- HAPAX, extreme littleness at origin
- וַתִּגְדַּל (vattigdal) = Verb.Qal.Wayq.3fs "became great" -- QAL stem (intransitive)
- יֶ֛תֶר (yether) = Noun.ms.Abs "remainder/excess" -- adverbial modifier = "exceedingly"
```
NOTE: Three-stage gadal escalation:
1. Dan 8:4 -- ram gadal (became great) -- no modifier
2. Dan 8:8 -- goat gadal ad me'od (became very great) -- me'od modifier
3. Dan 8:9 -- horn gadal yether (became exceedingly great) -- yether modifier
The horn MUST surpass both the named empires (Medo-Persia and Greece).

## Daniel 9:24 -- six infinitives, chathak
```
שָׁבֻעִ֨ים שִׁבְעִ֜ים נֶחְתַּ֥ךְ עַֽל־עַמְּךָ֣׀ וְעַל־עִ֣יר קָדְשֶׁ֗ךָ לְכַלֵּ֨א הַפֶּ֜שַׁע וּלְהָתֵ֤ם חַטָּאת֙ וּלְכַפֵּ֣ר עָוֹ֔ן וּלְהָבִ֖יא צֶ֣דֶק עֹֽלָמִ֑ים וְלַחְתֹּם֙ חָזֹ֣ון וְנָבִ֔יא וְלִמְשֹׁ֖חַ קֹ֥דֶשׁ קָֽדָשִֽׁים׃

Key parsing:
- שָׁבֻעִ֨ים שִׁבְעִ֜ים (shabu'im shiv'im) = "seventy weeks/sevens"
- נֶחְתַּ֥ךְ (nechtak) = Verb.Niphal.Perf.3ms of חתך (chathak) = "be cut off/determined" -- HAPAX
- Six infinitive constructs with ל (le-):
  1. לְכַלֵּ֨א (lekhalle') = Piel InfCon of כלה = "to finish/restrain" -- the transgression (pesha)
  2. לְהָתֵ֤ם (lehatem) = Hiphil InfCon of תמם = "to seal up/complete" -- sins (chatta'ot)
  3. לְכַפֵּ֣ר (lekhapper) = Piel InfCon of כפר = "to cover/atone" -- iniquity (avon)
  4. לְהָבִ֖יא (lehavi') = Hiphil InfCon of בוא = "to bring in" -- everlasting righteousness (tsedeq olamim)
  5. לַחְתֹּם֙ (lachtom) = Qal InfCon of חתם = "to seal" -- vision and prophecy (chazon ve-navi')
  6. לִמְשֹׁ֖חַ (limeshoach) = Qal InfCon of משׁח = "to anoint" -- most holy (qodesh qodashim)
```
NOTES:
- chathak (H2852) ONLY occurs here -- Daniel's deliberate word choice vs. charats (H2782)
- DOA triad: pesha + chatta'ot + avon = same three sin categories as Lev 16:21 (Day of Atonement)
- The upgrade pattern: the first three infinitives REMOVE sin; the last three ESTABLISH God's program
- kaphar-to-tsedeq: from "covering" sin to "everlasting righteousness" -- an upgrade from Levitical system

```
וּתְלָתָ֥ה מַלְכִ֖ין יְהַשְׁפִּֽל

Key parsing:
- יִשְׁנֵא֙ (yishneh) = Verb.peal.Impf.3ms of שׁנה (shanah) = "be different" -- diverse from the first
- יְהַשְׁפִּֽל (yehasphil) = Verb.haf.Impf.3ms of שׁפל (shaphal) = "bring low/subdue"
```
NOTE: yehasphil (Haphel imperfect) = "will bring low/subdue" -- causative + ongoing. This is the third verb (angel's interpretation): the horn WILL subdue three kings. The Haphel imperfect indicates ongoing action, not a single past event. yishneh ("be different") uses the same root shanah that appears in 7:25 lehashnayyah ("to change").

## Daniel 2:39 -- batarakh succession markers
```
וּבָתְרָ֗ךְ תְּק֛וּם מַלְכ֥וּ אָחֳרִ֖י ... וּמַלְכ֨וּ תְלִיתָאָ֤ה

Key parsing:
- בָתְרָ֗ךְ (batrakh) = Noun.ms.Abs+2ms "after you" -- from באתר (baatar) = "after"
- תְּק֛וּם (tequm) = Verb.peal.Impf.3fs "will arise"
- מַלְכ֥וּ (malku) = Noun.fs.Abs "kingdom"
- אָחֳרִ֖י (ochorey) = Adj.fs.Abs "another"
- אֲרַ֣עא (ar'a) = Noun.fs.Abs "earth" -- "inferior to you" (lit. "earthward from you")
- תְלִיתָאָ֤ה (telita'ah) = Adj.fs.Abs "third" -- explicitly numbered
```
NOTE: u-vatrakh ("and after you") establishes gap-free temporal succession. The third kingdom is numbered telitaya ("third"). These markers create an unbroken chain that does not permit gaps, rearrangement, or parenthetical insertions, directly countering the futurist gap thesis.

## Daniel 2:43 -- la lehevon dabqin (will not adhere)
```
וְלָֽא־לֶהֱוֹ֥ן דָּבְקִ֖ין דְּנָ֣ה עִם־דְּנָ֑ה

Key parsing:
- מִתְעָרְבִ֤ין (mit'arbin) = Verb.htpa.Ptcp.mp "mixing themselves" -- Hitpael = reflexive
- לֶהֱוֹן֙ (lehevon) = Verb.peal.Impf.3mp "they will be"
- בִּזְרַ֣ע אֲנָשָׁ֔א (bizera' anasha) = "in/with the seed of men"
- לָֽא (la) = Neg "not"
- דָּבְקִ֖ין (dabqin) = Verb.peal.Ptcp.mp of דבק (debaq) = "adhering/cleaving"
- דְּנָ֣ה עִם־דְּנָ֑ה (denah 'im denah) = "this with this"
- חֲסַ֣ף (chasaph) = Noun.ms.Cst "clay/ceramic"
- פַּרְזְלָא (parzela) = Noun.ms "iron"
```
NOTE: la lehevon dabqin = "they will NOT be adhering" -- a falsifiable prediction. Iron and clay mix (marriage alliances, political unions) but do not permanently fuse. This has been tested across 1500+ years: Charlemagne, Napoleon, Hitler, EU -- all failed to permanently reunify Europe's divided territory.

## Daniel 2:21 -- sovereignty hymn
```
מְהַשְׁנֵ֤א עִדָּנַיָּא֙ וְזִמְנַיָּ֔א מְהַעְדֵּ֥ה מַלְכִ֖ין וּמְהָקֵ֣ים מַלְכִ֑ין

Key parsing:
- מְהַשְׁנֵ֤א (mehashne) = Verb.haf.Ptcp.ms of שׁנה = "changing" -- Haphel (causative) Participle
- עִדָּנַיָּא֙ ('iddanayya) = Noun.mp "times" -- emphatic plural
- זִמְנַיָּ֔א (zimnanayya) = Noun.mp "seasons" -- emphatic plural
- מְהַעְדֵּ֥ה (meha'ddeh) = Verb.haf.Ptcp.ms of עדה = "removing" -- Haphel Participle
- מְהָקֵ֣ים (mehaqem) = Verb.haf.Ptcp.ms of קום = "setting up" -- Haphel Participle
```
NOTE: God is the one who mehashne (changes) times and seasons -- the SAME ROOT shanah that appears in Dan 7:25 where the horn lehashnayyah (thinks to change) times and law. God genuinely changes; the horn merely THINKS to (sbar = "intend"). The sovereignty hymn is the theological foundation for all four vision cycles.

## Daniel 9:27 -- gabar beriyth, la-rabbim
```
וְהִגְבִּ֥יר בְּרִ֛ית לָרַבִּ֖ים שָׁב֣וּעַ אֶחָ֑ד

Key parsing:
- הִגְבִּ֥יר (higbir) = Verb.Hiphil.Perf.3ms of גבר (gabar) = "make strong/prevail"
- בְּרִ֛ית (beriyth) = Noun.fs.Abs "covenant"
- לָרַבִּ֖ים (la-rabbim) = Prep+Art+Adj.mp "to/for the many"
- שָׁב֣וּעַ אֶחָ֑ד (shabua' echad) = "one week"
- יַשְׁבִּ֣ית (yashbith) = Verb.Hiphil.Impf.3ms of שׁבת (shabath) = "cause to cease"
- זֶ֣בַח (zebach) = Noun.ms "sacrifice"
- מִנְחָ֗ה (minchah) = Noun.fs "grain offering/present"
- כְּנַ֤ף (kenaph) = Noun.fs.Cst "wing" -- "upon the wing of abominations"
- שִׁקּוּצִים֙ (shiqqusim) = Noun.mp "abominations/idols"
- מְשֹׁמֵ֔ם (meshomem) = Verb.Piel.Ptcp.ms "desolating" -- the desolator
- נֶ֣חֱרָצָ֔ה (necheratsah) = Verb.Niphal.Ptcp.fs "decreed/determined"
- תִּתַּ֖ךְ (tittak) = Verb.Qal.Impf.3fs of נתך (nathak) = "be poured out"
```
NOTE: gabar beriyth (Hiphil = "make the covenant prevail/be strong") is deliberately NOT karath beriyth ("cut a covenant" = make NEW covenant). He STRENGTHENS/CONFIRMS the existing covenant. la-rabbim ("for the many") echoes Isa 53:11-12 "justify many" and "bare the sin of many" -- Messianic language. The subject of higbir is debated: HIST reads it as Messiah confirming God's covenant, supported by (1) la-rabbim Isaianic language, (2) gabar vs karath word choice, (3) the subject being "Messiah the Prince" from 9:25-26.

## Daniel 11:31 -- tamid + shiqquts vocabulary
```
וְהֵסִ֣ירוּ הַתָּמִ֔יד וְנָתְנ֖וּ הַשִּׁקּ֥וּץ מְשֹׁומֵֽם

Key parsing:
- זְרֹעִ֖ים (zero'im) = Noun.mp "arms" -- "arms from him shall stand"
- חִלְּל֞וּ (chillelu) = Verb.Piel.Perf.3p of חלל (chalal) = "profane/defile"
- הַמִּקְדָּ֤שׁ (hammikdash) = Art+Noun.ms "THE sanctuary"
- הַמָּעֹוז֙ (hamma'oz) = Art+Noun.ms "THE fortress/stronghold"
- הֵסִ֣ירוּ (hesiru) = Verb.Hiphil.Perf.3p of סור (sur) = "removed" (Hiphil causative)
- הַתָּמִ֔יד (hattamid) = Art+Noun.ms "THE continual" (= the daily)
- נָתְנ֖וּ (natenu) = Verb.Qal.Perf.3p of נתן (natan) = "placed/set up"
- הַשִּׁקּ֥וּץ (hashiqquts) = Art+Noun.ms "THE abomination"
- מְשֹׁומֵֽם (meshomem) = Verb.Piel.Ptcp.ms "desolating"
```
NOTE: Identical tamid + shiqquts vocabulary to Dan 8:11-13 and 12:11. Three chapters, one vocabulary, one power. The compound "sanctuary-fortress" (mikdash-ma'oz) is unique to 11:31.

## Daniel 12:11 -- tamid + shiqquts + time period
```
וּמֵעֵ֙ת֙ הוּסַ֣ר הַתָּמִ֔יד וְלָתֵ֖ת שִׁקּ֣וּץ שֹׁמֵ֑ם יָמִ֕ים אֶ֖לֶף מָאתַ֥יִם וְתִשְׁעִֽים

Key parsing:
- מֵעֵ֙ת֙ (me'eth) = Prep+Noun.s.Cst "from the time"
- הוּסַ֣ר (husar) = Verb.Hophal.Perf.3ms of סור = "was removed" (Hophal passive)
- הַתָּמִ֔יד (hattamid) = Art+Noun.ms "THE continual"
- לָתֵ֖ת (lateth) = Prep+Verb.Qal.InfCon of נתן = "to set up"
- שִׁקּ֣וּץ שֹׁמֵ֑ם (shiqquts shomem) = "the abomination that maketh desolate"
- יָמִ֕ים (yamim) = Noun.mp "days"
- אֶ֖לֶף מָאתַ֥יִם וְתִשְׁעִֽים (eleph matayim vetish'im) = "1290"
```
NOTE: Same tamid + shiqquts as 8:11-13 and 11:31. The 1290 days extends 30 beyond the 1260 (= time, times, half time). The Hophal passive husar ("was taken away") = divine passive, just as the tamid was divinely removed in judgment.
