# Topics Research

## Question
What is the complete, strongest text-based case for historicism across all of Daniel? Compile all HIST items from dan3-03 through dan3-26 as one coherent narrative from Daniel 2 through Daniel 12.

**Relevance:** Dan 7:9-10 is directly listed under JUDGMENT THE GENERAL. The judgment scene vocabulary (dina yetib, sifrin petichu) is the forensic foundation for Dan 8:14 nitsdaq. Rev 14:7 "hour of his krisis" bridges the Daniel judgment to the NT.

### SANCTUARY (Score: 0.58)
EXO 25:8; LEV 19:30; 26:2; LAM 2:7,20; EZK 11:16; 42:20; **HEB 8:2,5; 9:2**

**Relevance:** Heb 8:2,5 establishes that the earthly sanctuary was a copy of the heavenly original. This is foundational for the HIST argument that Dan 8:14's qodesh refers to the heavenly sanctuary, and that nitsdaq indicates a heavenly forensic process (investigative judgment) typified by the earthly Day of Atonement.

### HORN (Score: 0.43)

### RESURRECTION (Score: 0.58)
JOB 14:12-15; 19:25-27; PSA 16:9,10; ISA 25:8; 26:19; EZK 37:1-14; **DAN 12:2,3,13**; HOS 13:14; JHN 5:21,25,28,29; 1TH 4:14,16; REV 1:18; 20:4-6

**Relevance:** Dan 12:2-3 is the clearest OT resurrection text. The "voice of the archangel" in 1 Thess 4:16 converges with Michael "standing up" in Dan 12:1, and the Son's voice raising the dead in John 5:25,28-29.

### MICHAEL (Score: 0.39)

- Message to Daniel: DAN 10:13,21; 12:1
- Contention with the devil: Jude 1:9

**Relevance:** Michael progression: "one of the chief princes" (10:13) -> "your prince" (10:21) -> "the great prince" (12:1). The archangel voice at resurrection (1 Thess 4:16) + Son's voice raising dead (John 5:25,28) = Michael = Christ identification in the HIST framework.

### PERSECUTION (Score: 0.41)

**Relevance:** Dan 7:25 "wear out the saints" (bela, Pael imperfect = sustained attrition). Rev 13:7 "make war with the saints and overcome them." The persecution of saints is a specification shared by Daniel 7 and Revelation 13.

### ABOMINATION (Score: 0.42)

### STONES (Score: 0.42)
DAN 2:34,45; ISA 8:14; 28:16; ZEC 3:9; MAT 21:42-44; LUK 20:17,18; ACT 4:11; 1PE 2:4,6-8; EPH 2:20; 1CO 3:11; 10:4

The stone/stumbling-stone tradition (Isa 8:14, 28:16, 1 Pet 2:4-8) converges on Christ as both cornerstone and destroying stone.

### PROPHECY (Score: 0.64)
DAN 7:14,27; 9:2,26,27; 11:30-45; ISA 9:7; MAT 5:18; ACT 13:27,29

**Relevance:** Highest-scoring topic. Daniel's visions are the backbone of biblical eschatological prophecy. The progressive revelation pattern (Dan 2 -> 7 -> 8-9 -> 10-12) adds detail with each cycle.

### ANCIENT OF DAYS (Score: 0.44)
DAN 7:9,13,22

**Relevance:** Title appears only in Daniel 7. The Ancient of Days presides over the judgment scene (7:9-10), and the Son of Man approaches Him (7:13). This is the HIST pre-advent judgment.

### KINGDOM OF HEAVEN (Score: 0.50)

**Relevance:** Dan 2:44 ("the God of heaven shall set up a kingdom which shall never be destroyed") and Dan 7:14,27 (everlasting dominion given to Son of Man and saints) are the OT foundation for the NT kingdom of heaven teaching.

### COVENANT
Nave's explicitly places DAN 9:27 under COVENANT instances with the note "To be confirmed." Key covenant chain: Abrahamic (Gen 12,15,17) -> Mosaic/Sinai (Exo 34:28) -> Davidic (2 Sam 7:12-16) -> New Covenant (Jer 31:31-34 -> Heb 8:8-12) -> Dan 9:27 Messiah "confirms" (gabar) the covenant.
**Relevance:** beriyth vocabulary through Dan 9:4,27 and 11:22,28,30,32 connects Messianic covenant fulfillment with the horn's attacks on the "holy covenant." The Second Covenant (JER 31:31-34; HEB 8:4-13) is what Dan 9:27 confirms.

### SABBATH
Exo 20:8-11 creation language ("made heaven and earth, the sea") echoed verbatim in Rev 14:7. The Fourth Commandment is the ONLY commandment that is both law AND time, making it the target of Dan 7:25's "think to change times and laws."
**Relevance:** Luke 23:56 "rested the sabbath day according to THE commandment" (kata ten entolen) AFTER the crucifixion; Matt 24:20 future Sabbath awareness; Isa 66:22-23 new-earth Sabbath. This creation-to-new-creation arc identifies the specific law of Dan 7:25.
