## [H1855](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1855/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - d'qaq (crush/break in pieces)
- Transliteration: dᵉqaq
- Part of Speech: verb
- 13 total occurrences, 13 unique translations
- BDB: Aramaic, corresponding to [H1854](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1854/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}; to crumble or crush; "break to pieces"
- Full BDB: Peal Perfect 3mp daquw Dan 2:35. Haph. break in pieces: Perfect 3fs haddeqet Dan 2:34, 45; 3mp haddiqu Dan 6:24; Imperfect 3fs taddiq Dan 2:40 (absolute), Dan 2:44 (accusative); suffix taddqinnah Dan 7:23; Participle active mehaddeq Dan 2:40, feminine maddqah Dan 7:7, 7:19
- KEY OCCURRENCES: Dan 2:34,35,40,44,45 AND Dan 7:7,19,23 -- same verb in both vision chapters
- Verse: Dan 7:19 translated "brake in pieces"

## [H772](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h772/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - ara' (earth/inferior, Aramaic)
- Transliteration: 'ara'
- Part of Speech: masculine noun (feminine in BDB)
- 21 total occurrences
- BDB: earth (= Biblical Hebrew [H776](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h776/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} eretz); emphatic ar'a
- Dan 2:39: ar'a minnakh = "earthward from you" = inferior to you (Ketiv)

- KEY: In Dan 2:39 this word may mean "inferior" (KJV) or "earthy/earthward" -- establishing the metal-degradation pattern

## [H6151](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6151/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} - arab (mix/mingle, Aramaic)
- Transliteration: 'arab
- Part of Speech: verb
- 4 total occurrences (ALL in Dan 2:41,43)
- BDB: mix (corresponding to Biblical Hebrew H6148); Pa. Passive Participle me'arav "mixed with" (Dan 2:41,43); Hithpa. Participle mit'arav "mixing themselves" (Dan 2:43 x2)
- Semantic range: to commingle, mingle self, mix
- NOTE: CONFINED ENTIRELY TO DANIEL 2. The mixing verb is reflexive in Dan 2:43 (Hithpaal = they mix THEMSELVES), suggesting voluntary/mutual mixing, not forced combination
